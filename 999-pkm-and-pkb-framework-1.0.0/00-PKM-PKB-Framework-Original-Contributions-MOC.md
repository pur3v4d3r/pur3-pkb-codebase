# PKM/PKB Framework — Original Contributions Registry

> [!abstract] Purpose
> Complete index of all 30 original frameworks, principles, and models developed across the series — intellectual contributions that emerge at the intersection of disciplines and cannot be found in any single source. Each entry is flagged as original analytical synthesis and should be treated as a well-grounded design hypothesis, not an established empirical finding.

---

## Epistemic Note

> [!methodology-and-sources] Status of Original Contributions
> Every contribution listed here represents Claude's cross-domain analytical synthesis — an integration across disciplinary evidence that no single tradition states explicitly. These are design hypotheses grounded in convergent scientific evidence, not empirical findings validated through original research. They should be tested in practice and refined through experience.

---

## Tier 1 Contributions (Reports 01–08): Cognitive Foundations

### R01: [[Cognitive-Alignment-Principle]]

**Three-dimensional PKB design framework** requiring structural correspondence between external PKB organization and internal knowledge architecture across: (1) *Relational Alignment* — relationships, not containers, as primary organizational unit; (2) *Depth Alignment* — concept nodes organized by deep structural principles, not surface topics; (3) *Construction Alignment* — deliberate affordances for accommodation, not just assimilation.

*Synthesizes:* Schema Theory + Semantic Networks + Expert Knowledge Organization + Faceted Classification + Constructivism
*Design Translation:* PKB should be a semantic network of principle-organized concept nodes connected by typed relationships, not a hierarchy of topic folders.

---

### R02: [[Load Profile Framework]]

**Four-dimensional note classification** respecting cognitive constraints: every note carries an intrinsic load profile (inherent complexity), extraneous load profile (design-imposed complexity), germane load profile (schema construction demand), and functional profile (archival vs. learning purpose). Note design should minimize extraneous load, calibrate intrinsic load to expertise level, and maximize germane load.

*Synthesizes:* Cognitive Load Theory + Instructional Design + Information Architecture
*Design Translation:* Create distinct note templates for different load profiles — archival notes (low germane) vs. learning notes (high germane).

---

### R03: [[Schema-Progressive-Architecture]]

**Three-layer note organization model:** (1) *Epitome Notes* — schema seeds providing simple, coherent orienting overviews; (2) *Elaboration Notes* — schema extensions adding progressive complexity with explicit elaboration relationship metadata; (3) *Accommodation Notes* — records of schema restructuring events with before/after structure.

*Synthesizes:* Schema Theory + Constructivism + Elaboration Theory
*Design Translation:* Tag notes by type (`#epitome`, `#elaboration`, `#accommodation`); use `elaborates::`, `challenges::`, `integrates::` metadata fields.

---

### R04: [[Metacognitive PKB]]

**PKB reconceived as external metacognitive scaffold** — not passive storage but active monitoring infrastructure. The PKB's primary function is to compensate for known limitations in human metacognitive monitoring (delayed JOL accuracy, illusion of knowing, miscalibration).

*Synthesizes:* Metacognition (Flavell) + SRL (Zimmerman) + Reflective Inquiry (Dewey)
*Design Translation:* Embed reflection prompts, calibration exercises, and self-assessment protocols as structural features of notes and review workflows.

---

### R05: [[Fourfold-Motivational-Architecture]]

**Integrated motivation framework** addressing PKB engagement simultaneously at four levels: (1) Basic psychological needs via SDT (autonomy, competence, relatedness); (2) Goal orientation via AGT (mastery over performance); (3) Resilience under adversity via Stoic praxis; (4) Optimal engagement calibration via Flow/LXD.

*Synthesizes:* Self-Determination Theory + Achievement Goal Theory + Stoic Philosophy + Learning Experience Design
*Design Translation:* Autonomy-supporting organization, competence visibility systems, mastery-oriented note framing, Stoic resilience protocols.

---

### R06: [[Retrieval-Oriented Note Architecture (RONA)]]

**Notes designed as retrieval training instruments from creation** — not just containers for information but tools that practice retrieval when engaged. Notes should include self-testing elements, cue-response structures, and retrieval prompts embedded in their design.

*Synthesizes:* Testing Effect + Encoding Specificity + Desirable Difficulties + Spaced Repetition
*Design Translation:* Add retrieval prompts to note templates; design notes that require recall before re-exposure.

---

### R07: [[External Epistemic Vigilance System (EEVS)]]

**Four-mechanism PKB architecture** compensating for biological epistemic limitations: (1) Source evaluation workflows; (2) Argument mapping templates; (3) Bias-checking protocols; (4) Calibration feedback loops.

*Synthesizes:* Critical Thinking + Epistemic Vigilance + Socratic Questioning + Metacognitive Calibration
*Design Translation:* Source evaluation templates, argument mapping note types, questioning protocols embedded in note creation.

---

### R08: [[Experience Processing Architecture (EPA)]]

**Four-stage PKB workflow** for transforming experience into knowledge: (1) *Capture* — raw experience recording; (2) *Reflect* — structured reflection using Dewey's inquiry model; (3) *Conceptualize* — abstract principle extraction; (4) *Experiment* — testing principles in new contexts.

*Synthesizes:* Dewey's Reflective Inquiry + Kolb's Learning Cycle + Pragmatist Epistemology + SECI Model
*Design Translation:* Experience capture templates, reflection workflows, learning journals with four-stage structure.

## Tier 2 Contributions (Reports 09–18): Advanced Design

### R09: [[Cognitive-Architecture-Isomorphism-Principle]]

**PKB structure should mirror five properties of long-term memory:** (1) hierarchically associative structure, (2) multiple abstraction levels, (3) contextually embedded encoding, (4) time-sensitive consolidation, (5) affordance-sensitive activation.

---

### R10: [[Scaffolding-Fading Design]]

**PKB structure should evolve dynamically with user expertise** — high scaffolding for novice domains (templates, prompts, structure) that fades as expertise develops, preventing the Expertise Reversal Effect.

---

### R11: [[Contextual Bridging Architecture (CBA)]]

**Four-layer note design for knowledge transfer** across contexts: (1) Abstract principle layer; (2) Context-specific instantiation layer; (3) Cross-context comparison layer; (4) Application planning layer.

---

### R12: [[Structural-Metacognition-Principle]] + [[Embedded-Monitoring-Architecture]]

**Structural redesign to correct metacognitive monitoring failures** — monitoring and self-assessment embedded as required structural features of notes and review processes, not optional add-ons.

---

### R13: [[Epistemic-Emotional-Architecture]]

**Three-layer PKB design treating PKB as appraisal machine:** (1) Emotion-awareness layer (recognizing emotional responses to learning); (2) Regulation layer (Stoic-informed response management); (3) Resilience layer (systematic frustration management).

---

### R14: [[Inquiry-First PKB Architecture]] + [[Aporia-to-Schema-Pipeline]]

**Fundamental unit is inquiry node, not assertion** — the PKB organized around questions driving investigation rather than answers awaiting storage. Questions evolve through Socratic aporia toward schema construction.

---

### R15: [[Progressive-Parameterized-Tagging]] + [[Organization-Learning-Loop]]

**Hybrid tag architecture** balancing imposed structure (hierarchical domain/subdomain tags) with emergent structure (practitioner-generated folksonomy). Organization itself becomes a learning process as tag evolution reflects conceptual development.

---

### R16: [[Reconstruction-Imperative]]

**Four-dimensional framework unifying desirable difficulties research:** Every PKB engagement should require reconstruction (retrieval) rather than recognition (re-reading). Dimensions: temporal (spacing), modal (generation), sequential (interleaving), structural (elaboration).

---

### R17: [[Articulatory-Construction-Principle]] + [[Cognitive-Event-Model]]

**Notes as records of cognitive construction events** — each note documents a thinking event (what the writer understood, struggled with, connected, resolved) rather than an information summary.

---

### R18: [[Epistemic Accuracy Architecture]]

**Four-dimension framework for tracking understanding quality:** (1) Correctness — factual accuracy; (2) Confidence Calibration — alignment between certainty and actual knowledge; (3) Application Competence — ability to use knowledge in context; (4) Explanation Clarity — ability to teach the concept.

---

## Tier 3 Contributions (Reports 19–26): Advanced Synthesis

### R19: [[Motivational-Compounding-Model]] + [[Automaticity-Autonomy Paradox]]

**Long-term PKB motivation as compound interest** across three mechanisms: skill compounding, interest deepening, and identity integration. Paradox: as PKM practices become automatic (reducing effort), they risk losing the autonomy-satisfying quality of deliberate choice.

---

### R20: [[RENS Framework]] + [[Cognitive Orphaning]]

**Retrieval-Enhanced Network Strengthening** — each retrieval event strengthens not just the target memory but the network connections surrounding it. **Cognitive Orphaning** — notes disconnected from the retrieval network progressively decay in accessibility and utility.

---

### R21: [[Dialectical Note Triad (DNT)]]

**Three-node Obsidian structure** (thesis note, antithesis note, synthesis note) for epistemic accountability. Forces the learner to confront opposing positions before claiming understanding.

---

### R22: [[Tacit-Knowledge-Observatory]]

**PKB's proper role in tacit-knowledge-rich domains** is observation and triangulation, not direct capture. Tacit knowledge can be pointed toward through approximation, metaphor, and experiential description — but never fully externalized.

---

### R23: [[Personal Constructed Learning Environment (PCLE)]]

**Seven-component design vocabulary** for PKB as learning environment: Content Architecture, Process Scaffolding, Assessment Integration, Feedback Systems, Social Affordances, Tool Ecosystem, Affective Climate.

---

### R24: [[Knowledge Ecology Model]] + [[Epistemological Architecture Principle]]

**Heutagogical PKB architecture** framing the Pedagogy-Andragogy-Heutagogy continuum as an epistemological shift, not just a pedagogical one. At the heutagogical level, the learner determines not just how to learn but what counts as knowledge.

---

### R25: [[Integration-Paradox]] + [[Small-World-PKB-Principle]]

**Optimal PKB topology mirrors expert semantic networks** — high local clustering (dense connections within knowledge domains) with short global path lengths (bridge connections between domains). The paradox: integration effort feels unproductive in the short term but is the primary determinant of long-term PKB value.

---

### R26: [[Learning Metabolism Framework]] + [[Calibration-Prosthetic]]

**Five-property feedback architecture model:** specificity, timeliness, actionability, calibration accuracy, self-generation capacity. The PKB as calibration prosthetic — compensating for known human miscalibration through embedded comparison mechanisms.

---

## Tier 4 Contributions (Reports 27–30): Meta-Framework

### R27: [[Integrated-Learning-System-Model]] + [[Five-Convergence-Zones]] + [[Twelve-Master-Principles]]

**The series capstone synthesis.** A PKB at full function has three synergistic properties: (1) [[Isomorphic External Memory]], (2) [[Constructive Processing Engine]], (3) [[Self-Regulating Adaptive System]]. Twelve Master Principles organized as Foundational (FP1-4), Derived (DP1-5), and Refinement (RP1-3) — see [[00-PKM-PKB-Framework-Design-Principles-MOC]] for full specification.

---

### R28: [[Relational-Theory-of-PKB-Knowledge]] + [[Epistemic Ecosystem Model]]

**Knowledge as relationship** constituted through four modes of epistemic engagement: Encounter (meeting new information), Challenge (testing against existing understanding), Integration (connecting to knowledge network), Application (using in practice).

---

### R29: [[PKB as Ethical Mirror]] + [[Four Epistemic Duties of the PKB Curator]]

**Virtue epistemology applied to PKB curation.** Four duties: (1) Duty of Honest Representation — no cherry-picking; (2) Duty of Source Accountability — trace claims to evidence; (3) Duty of Intellectual Humility — flag uncertainty; (4) Duty of Generative Sharing — contribute to knowledge commons.

---

### R30: [[Cognitive Partnership Model]] + [[Offloading Quality Distinction]] + [[Schema Construction Problem]]

**The series' final original contribution.** AI should function as [[Socratic Interlocutor]] — generating productive uncertainty, surface tensions, and challenging positions rather than providing smooth answers. The Offloading Quality Distinction: storage/retrieval offloading to AI is beneficial; synthesis/reasoning offloading is harmful. The Schema Construction Problem: AI's most helpful features directly conflict with desirable difficulties research on deep learning.

---

## The Cumulative Arc

The 30 contributions trace an intellectual arc from *what knowledge is* (R01: relational, not propositional) through *how knowledge grows* (R03: construction, not accumulation) to *how knowledge systems self-regulate* (R12, R26: embedded monitoring and feedback) to *what knowledge is for* (R28, R29: understanding and ethical responsibility) to *how knowledge survives AI* (R30: challenge-oriented partnership, not frictionless convenience).
