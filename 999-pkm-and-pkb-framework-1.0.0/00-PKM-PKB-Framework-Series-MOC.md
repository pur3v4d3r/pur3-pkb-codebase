# PKM/PKB Framework — Report Series Navigation

> [!abstract] Purpose
> Complete registry of all 30 reports with summaries, original contributions, dependency mappings, and three recommended reading pathways. This MOC is the primary navigation tool for the report series itself.

---

## Series Architecture: The Four-Tier Knowledge Pyramid

The 30 reports form a **directed acyclic graph (DAG)** organized in four tiers. Each tier builds on the preceding tier's conceptual foundations.

| Tier | Reports | Purpose | Core Question |
|------|---------|---------|---------------|
| **1: Foundations** | 01–08 | Cognitive & architectural foundations | *How does the mind learn?* |
| **2: Integration** | 09–18 | Advanced PKB design guidance | *How should a PKB be designed?* |
| **3: Synthesis** | 19–26 | Sophisticated integration challenges | *How does the system mature?* |
| **4: Meta-Framework** | 27–30 | Framework-level integration | *What does it all mean?* |

---

## Recommended Reading Pathways

### Pathway A: Cognitive Foundations First (Recommended for scientists/researchers)

> Reports 01 → 02 → 06 → 09 → 15 → 17 → 20 → 25 → 27

Follows the cognitive science thread from knowledge architecture through memory mechanisms to PKB design to integration to unified framework.

### Pathway B: Design Translation (Recommended for PKB practitioners)

> Reports 09 → 01 → 02 → 15 → 17 → 12 → 20 → 27 → 30

Starts with the design destination (Report 09), then backtracks to understand the science, then builds toward implementation.

### Pathway C: Pedagogical Pathway (from the Synthesis document)

**Stage 1 — Cognitive Foundations:** Reports 01, 02, 06
**Stage 2 — Design Translation:** Reports 09, 15, 17
**Stage 3 — Regulatory Architecture:** Reports 04, 12, 18, 20
**Stage 4 — Advanced Integration:** Reports 25, 26, 27
**Stage 5 — Meta-Framework:** Reports 28, 29, 30
