# Anomaly

> [!definition] **Anomaly** *(from [[abductive-reasoning-foundational-report-2026-03-06]])*
> An observation or datum that does not fit the expectations generated by an agent's current knowledge or background theory. Anomalies are the triggers of abductive reasoning — they create the occasion for hypothesis generation. Importantly, what counts as anomalous is theory-dependent (Hanson's *theory-ladenness of observation*): the same datum that is anomalous for one thinker may be entirely expected for another with different background knowledge. Developing sensitivity to anomaly — a richer sense of what should be surprising — is a core component of abductive competence development.

Induction is ampliative but not creative (it generalizes from what has already been observed).
