# Clinical Psychology

> [!definition] **Clinical Psychology**
> The scientific discipline and applied profession concerned with the assessment, understanding, prevention, and treatment of psychological distress, dysfunction, and disorder across the lifespan. Clinical psychology integrates scientific research (understanding mechanisms of psychopathology and therapeutic change) with clinical practice (applying that understanding to individuals and groups). It is distinguished from psychiatry by its non-medical training and historically non-pharmacological interventions; from counseling psychology by its traditional emphasis on more severe psychopathology; and from research psychology by its irreducible commitment to the individual case. The contemporary field increasingly blurs these boundaries through integrative and transdiagnostic approaches.
