# Causality Orientations

> [!definition] **Causality Orientations**
> Individual differences in the chronic tendency to interpret and engage with social environments in autonomy-consistent (autonomy orientation), pressure-compliance (control orientation), or helpless-amotivated (impersonal orientation) ways. Causality orientations moderate the relationship between relational conditions and relational motivation outcomes: high autonomy orientation individuals extract more autonomous motivation from relational contexts than objective conditions would predict; high control orientation individuals create controlled motivational experiences even in nominally supportive relationships. *See also*: [[Causality-Orientations-Theory]], [[Autonomy-Orientation]], [[Control-Orientation]]
