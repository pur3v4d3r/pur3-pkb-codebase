# Weak-Sense vs. Strong-Sense Critical Thinking

> [!definition] **Weak-Sense vs. Strong-Sense Critical Thinking** *(from [[critical-thinking-educational-settings-foundational-report-2026-03-05]])*
> Weak-sense CT is the use of CT skills selectively, in service of one's own pre-existing beliefs and interests — deploying analytical tools to win arguments rather than to find truth. Strong-sense CT applies the same rigorous standards to one's own reasoning as to others', including the willingness to discover one's own errors and revise one's beliefs accordingly.
