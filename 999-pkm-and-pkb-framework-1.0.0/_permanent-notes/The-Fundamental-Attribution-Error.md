# The Fundamental Attribution Error

> [!definition] **The Fundamental Attribution Error**
> The **Fundamental Attribution Error** (FAE) is the systematic tendency to overemphasize dispositional (personal) causes and underemphasize situational (environmental) causes when explaining another person's behavior. It manifests as a reflexive tendency to attribute behavior to "what kind of person they are" rather than "what kind of situation they were in." The FAE is most pronounced when the behavior is negative, when the observer has limited information about situational constraints, and when the observer is culturally positioned within an individualistic framework.
