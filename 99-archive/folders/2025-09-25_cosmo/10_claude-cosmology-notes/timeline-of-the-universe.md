# Timeline of the Universe

> *From quantum fluctuations to cosmic acceleration - the complete story of our universe*

## ⏰ The Complete Cosmic Timeline

### 🔥 **The Planck Era** (t = 0 to 10⁻⁴³ seconds)
- **Temperature**: >10³² K
- **Physics**: Quantum gravity dominates
- **Description**: All fundamental forces unified
- **Mystery**: Physics unknown - need theory of quantum gravity
- **Key Point**: Time itself may not exist as we understand it

---

### 🚀 **Grand Unification Era** (10⁻⁴³ to 10⁻³⁶ seconds)
- **Temperature**: 10²⁸ to 10³² K  
- **Physics**: Strong force separates from electroweak
- **Events**: Possible formation of cosmic strings, monopoles
- **Connections**: [[Particle Physics and Cosmology]]

---

### ⚡ **Inflationary Epoch** ([[Cosmic Inflation]]) (10⁻³⁶ to 10⁻³² seconds)
- **Temperature**: 10²⁷ K
- **Key Event**: **Exponential expansion** of space
- **Growth**: Universe expands by factor of 10²⁶ or more
- **Results**: 
  - Solves [[The Horizon Problem]] and [[The Flatness Problem]]
  - Creates quantum fluctuations → seeds for structure
  - Makes universe homogeneous and isotropic
- **End**: Reheating creates hot, dense plasma

---

### 🌊 **Electroweak Epoch** (10⁻³² to 10⁻¹² seconds)
- **Temperature**: 10¹⁵ to 10²⁷ K
- **Physics**: Electromagnetic and weak forces separate
- **Matter**: Quarks, leptons, gauge bosons in thermal equilibrium
- **Symmetry Breaking**: Higgs mechanism gives particles mass

---

### ⚛️ **Quark Epoch** (10⁻¹² to 10⁻⁶ seconds)
- **Temperature**: 10¹² to 10¹⁵ K
- **State**: Quark-gluon plasma
- **Physics**: Strong force acts, but quarks remain free
- **Transition**: Approaching quark confinement

---

### 🔗 **Hadron Epoch** (10⁻⁶ to 1 second)
- **Temperature**: 10¹⁰ to 10¹² K
- **Key Event**: **Quark Confinement** - protons and neutrons form
- **Matter**: Hadrons (protons, neutrons) created from quarks
- **Antimatter**: Most antimatter annihilates with matter
- **Result**: Small matter excess survives ([[Matter-Antimatter Asymmetry]])

---

### 💫 **Lepton Epoch** (1 to 10 seconds)
- **Temperature**: 10⁹ to 10¹⁰ K
- **Dominant**: Leptons (electrons, neutrinos) and photons
- **Key Event**: Neutrino decoupling (t ≈ 1 second)
- **Result**: Cosmic neutrino background forms

---

### ⚛️ **Big Bang Nucleosynthesis** ([[Nucleosynthesis]]) (10 seconds to 20 minutes)
- **Temperature**: 10⁸ to 10⁹ K (cool enough for nuclei!)
- **Key Process**: Protons and neutrons fuse into light elements
- **Products**: 
  - 75% Hydrogen (¹H)
  - 25% Helium (⁴He)
  - Small amounts: Deuterium, ³He, ⁷Li
- **Success**: Predicted abundances match observations
- **Window**: Brief period when conditions are just right

---

### 🌫️ **Photon Epoch** (3 minutes to 380,000 years)
- **Temperature**: 3000 K to 10⁸ K
- **State**: Hot plasma of protons, electrons, photons
- **Physics**: Photons scatter off free electrons (opaque universe)
- **Evolution**: Universe cools but remains ionized
- **Matter**: [[Dark Matter]] begins forming small-scale structure

---

### 💡 **Recombination** ([[Cosmic Microwave Background]]) (t = 380,000 years)
- **Temperature**: ~3000 K (cool enough for atoms!)
- **Key Event**: Electrons combine with protons → neutral hydrogen
- **Result**: **Universe becomes transparent** to photons
- **Legacy**: [[Cosmic Microwave Background]] radiation released
- **Structure**: Tiny density fluctuations preserved in CMB
- **Significance**: Last direct view of early universe

---

### 🌑 **Dark Ages** (380,000 to ~100 million years)
- **Temperature**: 3000 K → 100 K
- **State**: Dark, neutral universe (no stars yet!)
- **Invisible**: No visible light sources
- **Activity**: 
  - [[Dark Matter]] halos grow via gravity
  - Gas accumulates in dark matter wells
  - First tiny density fluctuations amplify
- **End**: First stars begin to form

---

### ⭐ **Cosmic Dawn** (~100-200 million years)
- **Temperature**: ~100 K
- **Key Event**: **First Stars Form** (Population III)
- **Properties**: Massive, short-lived, metal-free stars
- **Impact**: Begin creating heavier elements
- **Light**: First sources of visible light since recombination

---

### 🔥 **Reionization** (~150 million to 1 billion years)  
- **Process**: First stars and quasars ionize neutral hydrogen
- **Result**: Universe becomes ionized again (but stays transparent)
- **Duration**: Extended process, not instantaneous
- **Observational**: 21cm hydrogen line signatures

---

### 🌌 **Structure Formation Era** (1 billion years onward)
- **Process**: Hierarchical structure formation
- **Sequence**: 
  1. Dark matter halos merge and grow
  2. Gas cools and forms stars within halos  
  3. First galaxies appear ([[Galaxy Formation]])
  4. Galaxy mergers create larger structures
  5. [[Cosmic Web Structure]] becomes prominent

---

### 🕳️ **Quasar Era** (~1-3 billion years)
- **Peak Activity**: Supermassive black holes actively feeding
- **Brightness**: Most luminous objects in universe
- **Impact**: Feedback affects galaxy formation
- **Decline**: Gas supply depletes over time

---

### 🌍 **Solar System Formation** (~9 billion years / 4.6 billion years ago)
- **Context**: Universe is already mature
- **Process**: Stellar nursery in Milky Way forms Sun and planets
- **Significance**: Beginning of local cosmic history

---

### 🚀 **Dark Energy Domination** (~5 billion years ago to today)
- **Transition**: [[Dark Energy]] becomes dominant over matter
- **Effect**: **Cosmic acceleration** begins ([[Discovery of Cosmic Acceleration]])
- **Impact**: Structure formation slows down
- **Present**: Universe expanding ever faster

---

### 🔭 **Present Era** (13.8 billion years)
- **Composition**: 68.3% [[Dark Energy]], 26.8% [[Dark Matter]], 4.9% ordinary matter
- **State**: Accelerating expansion continues
- **Activity**: Star formation declining, galaxies evolving
- **Observation**: We study this history through astronomy

---

## 🔮 **Future Evolution**

### Near Future (1-100 billion years)
- Continued cosmic acceleration
- Star formation declines as gas depleted
- Milky Way-Andromeda collision (~4.5 billion years)

### Far Future (10¹² - 10¹⁴ years) 
- Star formation ends
- Brown dwarf cooling, white dwarf cooling
- Only longest-lived stars remain

### Ultimate Fate
- **Heat Death**: If [[Dark Energy]] constant (most likely)
- **Big Rip**: If dark energy strengthens
- **Big Crunch**: If dark energy weakens (unlikely)
