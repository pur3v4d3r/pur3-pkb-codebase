# Social, Cultural & Eudaimonic Psychology MOC

> [!abstract] Scope
> Notes spanning social psychology (identity, perception, prejudice), cross-cultural psychology (WEIRD-sample critiques, cultural variation in needs and motivation), humanistic and positive psychology (eudaimonia, flourishing, well-being), and the social-cognitive constructs (self-esteem, perspective-taking, wise interventions) that bridge these traditions.

> [!principle-point] Organizing Principle
> The self is **socially constituted, culturally varied, and oriented toward eudaimonic ends** that go beyond hedonic satisfaction. This MOC organizes notes along three axes: **social structure**, **cultural variation**, and **flourishing**.

[**Domain-Function**:: Captures the social, cultural, and humanistic dimensions of psychology that ground the vault's individual-cognition focus in its interpersonal and cross-cultural context.]
