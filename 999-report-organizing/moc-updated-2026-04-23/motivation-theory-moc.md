# Motivation Theory MOC

> [!abstract] Scope
> The major contemporary theories of motivation as they bear on learning, achievement, and self-regulation: Self-Determination Theory, Social-Cognitive Theory and self-efficacy, Achievement-Goal Theory, Expectancy-Value Theory, Mindset / Implicit Theories, Attribution Theory, Interest Theory, and Control-Value Theory. Includes integration syntheses across frameworks.

> [!principle-point] Organizing Principle
> Motivation theories converge on three core questions: **Why** (energy / direction — SDT, AGT), **Can I?** (efficacy — SCT, mindset), and **Should I?** (expectancy × value — EVT). This MOC organizes notes along that triad plus an integration layer.

[**Motivation-Domain**:: The empirical and theoretical study of what initiates, directs, sustains, and terminates goal-directed behavior in learning contexts.]
