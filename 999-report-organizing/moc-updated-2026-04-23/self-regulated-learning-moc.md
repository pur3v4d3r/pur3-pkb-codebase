# Self-Regulated Learning MOC

> [!abstract] Scope
> The cyclical, agentive process by which learners set goals, monitor progress, deploy strategies, and reflect on outcomes. Covers the major SRL models (Zimmerman, Winne–Hadwin, Pintrich, Boekaerts), their phases, the volition / action-phase tradition, and applied SRL across PKM, clinical, and digital learning contexts.

> [!principle-point] Organizing Principle
> SRL is **cyclical and recursive**: forethought → performance → self-reflection → forethought. Different theorists slice the cycle differently, but all share a monitoring–control core inherited from [[metacognition-moc|metacognition]].

[**SRL-Definition**:: The process whereby learners proactively transform mental abilities into task-related skills through goal-setting, strategic action, monitoring, and self-evaluation.]
