# Researchers & Theorists MOC

> [!abstract] Scope
> Index of person-notes for the foundational and contemporary researchers whose work is cited throughout the vault. Organized by primary domain of contribution. Each entry should ultimately link to (a) the researcher's biographical note and (b) the major theory / construct most associated with them.

> [!principle-point] Organizing Principle
> Person-notes serve as **attribution anchors**, not biographies. Their value is the wiki-link they provide so that any concept-note saying "this construct comes from X" can connect to a single canonical author identity rather than scattering aliases.

[**Person-Note-Function**:: A person-note is a graph node providing canonical attribution for ideas; downstream concept-notes link to it rather than restating affiliations and credentials.]
