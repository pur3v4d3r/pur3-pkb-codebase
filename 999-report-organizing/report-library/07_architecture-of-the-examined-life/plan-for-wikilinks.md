# **§1 - Executive Overview**

### **The Scale of the Work**

Your Python extraction has surfaced the full universe of conceptual links embedded in the Architecture of the Examined Life series. This document transforms that raw data into an organized, prioritized master plan for populating every one of those notes with substantive content.

The core challenge is strategic sequencing: 718 unique concepts cannot be created at once, nor should they be - the knowledge graph only becomes useful once its highest-connectivity nodes (the concepts that appear in 5+ reports) are populated. Everything else anchors to those nodes.

| **Metric** | **Value** | **Implication** |

| Concepts with definitions | 6   | Already written - highest quality start |
| Co-occurrence clusters | 9   | Coordinate creation of related notes |

### **The Strategic Approach**

This plan organizes work around a single principle: build the knowledge graph from the center outward. The most-referenced concepts form the center - these are the 'hub' notes every other note links to. Creating them first means every subsequent note can immediately link to something real rather than a placeholder.

The plan is organized in three tiers, nine co-occurrence clusters, and five phases. Each phase produces immediately usable output while preparing the foundation for the next phase.
