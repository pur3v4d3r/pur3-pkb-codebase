# PKM/PKB Framework — Theoretical Foundations

> [!abstract] Purpose
> Comprehensive index of the 11+ disciplinary traditions synthesized across the framework, organized by domain with key concepts, primary researchers, evidence strength, and cross-domain connections. This MOC is the reference for understanding *where the science comes from*.

---

## Domain 1: Cognitive Architecture & Memory Science

### 1.1 Knowledge Representation

**[[schema-theory-and-knowledge-organization]]** — Bartlett (1932), Rumelhart (1980), Anderson (1977)
Primary Reports: [[01-foundations-of-knowledge-architecture-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R01]], [[03-constructing-understanding-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R03]], [[09-designing-the-learning-pkb-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R09]]
Evidence Status: Established | Key Concept: Knowledge organized in structured mental templates that filter, organize, and reconstruct information. Hierarchically nested, contextually activated, resistant to disconfirming evidence.

**[[Semantic-Networks]] & [[spreading-activation]]** — Collins & Quillian (1969), Collins & Loftus (1975)
Primary Reports: [[01-foundations-of-knowledge-architecture-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R01]], [[09-designing-the-learning-pkb-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R09]]
Evidence Status: Established | Key Concept: Memory as a web of concept-nodes connected by typed relational links. Retrieval via spreading activation — the direct cognitive science basis for wiki-link PKB architecture.

**[[Prototype-Theory]]** — Rosch (1973, 1975)
Primary Reports: [[01-foundations-of-knowledge-architecture-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R01]], [[15-knowledge-organization-at-scale-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R15]]
Evidence Status: Established | Key Concept: Categories organized around "best examples" with graded membership. Implication: PKB concept notes should be pitched at [[Basic-Level-Categories]] — intermediate specificity.

### 1.2 Working Memory & Cognitive Load

**[[Cognitive Load Theory (CLT)]]** — Sweller (1988), Kalyuga (2011)
Primary Reports: [[02-architecture-of-learning-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R02]], [[10-scaffolding-and-fading-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R10]]
Evidence Status: Established (meta-analytic) | Three load types: [[Intrinsic-Load]] (inherent complexity), [[extraneous-load]] (poor design), [[Cognitive Load Theory (CLT)]] (schema construction effort). Working memory limited to ~4 chunks.

Key effects: [[split-attention-effect]], [[redundancy-effect]], [[Expertise Reversal Effect (Kalyuga, Ayres, Chandler, Sweller, 2003)]] (beneficial scaffolding for novices becomes counterproductive for experts)

### 1.3 Memory & Retrieval

**[[Testing-Effect]]** — Roediger & Karpicke (2006), Rowland (2014)
Primary Reports: [[06-science-of-remembering-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R06]], [[16-desirable-difficulties-by-design-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R16]], [[20-retrieval-enhanced-knowledge-networks-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R20]]
Evidence Status: Established (d = 0.50, 159 experiments) | Retrieving information strengthens memory more than restudying. Rated "high utility" by Dunlosky et al. (2013).

**[[Desirable Difficulties (Robert Bjork, 1994)]]** — Cepeda et al. (2006), Dunlosky et al. (2013)
Primary Reports: [[06-science-of-remembering-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R06]], [[16-desirable-difficulties-by-design-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R16]]
Evidence Status: Established (high utility) | Distributed practice > massed practice for long-term retention.

**[[Desirable Difficulties (Robert Bjork, 1994)]]** — Bjork (1994)
Primary Reports: [[16-desirable-difficulties-by-design-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R16]], [[20-retrieval-enhanced-knowledge-networks-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R20]]
Evidence Status: Established | Learning conditions that impede short-term performance but enhance long-term retention and transfer. Core instances: testing, spacing, interleaving, generation.

**[[Fluency Illusion]]** — Koriat & Bjork, Dunlosky
Primary Reports: [[18-calibration-epistemic-humility-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R18]], [[30-future-pkm-ai-enhanced-knowledge-building-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R30]]
Evidence Status: Established | Metacognitive error where ease of processing is mistaken for genuine understanding. Identified as the universal PKM failure mode, amplified by AI-generated content.

### 1.4 Expert Knowledge Organization

**[[Expert-Knowledge-Organization]]** — Chi, Feltovich & Glaser (1981), Ericsson
Primary Reports: [[01-foundations-of-knowledge-architecture-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R01]], [[15-knowledge-organization-at-scale-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R15]]
Evidence Status: Established | Expert knowledge differs qualitatively from novice knowledge: hierarchically deeper, more cross-connected, organized around structural principles rather than surface features.

## Domain 2: Learning Theory & Instructional Design

### 2.1 Constructivism

**[[constructivism]]** — Piaget (1952), Vygotsky (1978), von Glasersfeld (1995)
Primary Reports: [[03-constructing-understanding-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R03]], [[17-note-making-knowledge-construction-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R17]]
Evidence Status: Established | Knowledge actively constructed through learner-environment interaction, not passively received. Two processes: [[assimilation]] (integrating into existing schemas) and [[accommodation]] (restructuring schemas for incompatible information).

**[[zone-of-proximal-development]]** — Vygotsky
Primary Reports: [[03-constructing-understanding-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R03]], [[10-scaffolding-and-fading-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R10]], [[23-learning-environments-design-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R23]]
Evidence Status: Established | Gap between independent capability and guided capability; learning optimally targets this zone.

### 2.2 Instructional Design

**[[Elaboration-Theory]]** — Reigeluth (1979, 1999, 2013)
Primary Reports: [[03-constructing-understanding-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R03]], [[17-note-making-knowledge-construction-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R17]]
Evidence Status: Established | Complex content structured as progressive elaborations from an [[Epitome]] (simplest complete representation). Key components: epitome, elaboration sequences, synthesizers, cognitive strategies.

**[[Scaffolding and Fading]]** — Wood, Bruner & Ross (1976)
Primary Reports: [[10-scaffolding-and-fading-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R10]], [[24-self-determined-learning-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R24]]
Evidence Status: Established | Structured support systematically withdrawn as competence develops.

### 2.3 Self-Regulation & Metacognition

**[[self-regulated-learning]]** — Zimmerman (2000, 2002)
Primary Reports: [[04-metacognitive-self-regulation-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R04]], [[12-reflective-pkb-metacognitive-monitoring-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R12]]
Evidence Status: Established | Three-phase cyclical process: forethought → performance → self-reflection, across cognitive, metacognitive, motivational, and behavioral dimensions.

**[[metacognitive-monitoring]]** — Flavell (1979), Nelson & Narens (1990)
Primary Reports: [[04-metacognitive-self-regulation-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R04]], [[12-reflective-pkb-metacognitive-monitoring-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R12]], [[18-calibration-epistemic-humility-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R18]]
Evidence Status: Established | Two-level model: object level (cognitive processes) and meta-level (monitoring/control). Key phenomena: [[judgment-of-learning]], [[feeling-of-knowing]], [[calibration]].

### 2.4 Motivation

**[[self-determination-theory]]** — Deci & Ryan (1985, 2000)
Primary Reports: [[05-motivation-architecture-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R05]], [[19-sustaining-lifelong-learning-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R19]], [[24-self-determined-learning-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R24]]
Evidence Status: Established | Intrinsic motivation requires three basic needs: [[autonomy]], [[competence]], [[relatedness]].

**[[achievement-goal-theory]]** — Ames (1992), Elliot (2005)
Primary Reports: [[05-motivation-architecture-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R05]]
Evidence Status: Established | Mastery orientation (learning goals) produces deeper engagement than performance orientation (ego goals).

**[[Interest-Development-Theory]]** — Hidi & Renninger (2006)
Primary Reports: [[19-sustaining-lifelong-learning-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R19]]
Evidence Status: Established | Four phases: triggered situational → maintained situational → emerging individual → well-developed individual.

## Domain 3: Knowledge Management & Information Science

**[[SECI-Model]]** — Nonaka & Takeuchi (1995)
Primary Reports: [[22-tacit-knowledge-limits-of-capture-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R22]], [[09-designing-the-learning-pkb-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R09]]
Evidence Status: Established | Four modes of knowledge conversion: Socialization, Externalization, Combination, Internalization.

**[[tacit-knowledge]]** — Polanyi (1966)
Primary Reports: [[22-tacit-knowledge-limits-of-capture-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R22]]
Evidence Status: Established (philosophical) | "We know more than we can tell." Implication: text-based PKBs inherently cannot capture all knowledge — complementary practices needed.

**[[Faceted-Classification]]** — Ranganathan (1933)
Primary Reports: [[01-foundations-of-knowledge-architecture-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R01]], [[15-knowledge-organization-at-scale-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R15]]
Evidence Status: Established | Multi-dimensional classification preserving all dimensions simultaneously. Combined with the [[Vocabulary-Mismatch-Problem]] (<20% naming agreement, Furnas et al.), provides the strongest case for rich tagging over single-hierarchy folders.

**[[Small-World-Networks]]** — Watts & Strogatz (1998)
Primary Reports: [[25-integration-problem-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R25]]
Evidence Status: Established | Networks combining high local clustering with short global path lengths. Provides the diagnostic for PKB health: effective knowledge networks require small-world topology.

**[[Information Foraging Theory]]** — Pirolli & Card (1999)
Primary Reports: [[01-foundations-of-knowledge-architecture-pkm-framework-2026-03-13|R01]], [[15-knowledge-organization-at-scale-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R15]]
Evidence Status: Established | Users navigate information like animals forage for food, following "information scent."

## Domain 4: Educational Philosophy

### 4.1 Pragmatism

**[[Dewey's-Reflective-Inquiry]]** — Dewey (1910, 1933)
Primary Reports: [[08-reflective-practice-experiential-learning-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R08]], [[14-inquiry-based-knowledge-building-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R14]]
Evidence Status: Established (philosophical) | Problem-initiated systematic investigation: felt difficulty → hypothesis → tested conclusion. Knowledge as warranted assertion tested through consequences, not abstract correspondence.

**[[Kolb's-Experiential-Learning-Cycle]]** — Kolb (1984)
Primary Reports: [[08-reflective-practice-experiential-learning-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R08]]
Evidence Status: Established | Four stages: Concrete Experience → Reflective Observation → Abstract Conceptualization → Active Experimentation.

### 4.2 Socratic & Dialectical

**[[socratic-method]]** — Plato's Dialogues
Primary Reports: [[14-inquiry-based-knowledge-building-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R14]], [[21-dialectical-knowledge-building-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R21]]
Evidence Status: Established (philosophical) | Knowledge through structured questioning that surfaces contradictions. [[aporia]] (productive confusion) precedes insight.

### 4.3 Stoic Philosophy

**[[Stoic Discipline]]** — Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus, Seneca
Primary Reports: [[13-emotional-regulation-resilient-learning-pkm-framework-2026-03-14|R13]], [[19-sustaining-lifelong-learning-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R19]]
Evidence Status: Established (philosophical) | Emotional regulation through distinguishing controllable (response, effort, attention) from uncontrollable (outcomes, others' behavior). Applied to learning resilience.

### 4.4 Epistemology

**[[Extended Mind Thesis (Clark & Chalmers, 1998)]]** — Clark & Chalmers (1998)
Primary Reports: [[28-philosophy-of-personal-knowledge-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R28]], [[30-future-pkm-ai-enhanced-knowledge-building-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R30]]
Evidence Status: Established (philosophical) | Cognitive processes extend beyond the brain when external structures meet coupling conditions. Grounds the PKB as literal cognitive extension, not metaphorical.

**[[virtue-epistemology]]** — Sosa, Zagzebski
Primary Reports: [[28-philosophy-of-personal-knowledge-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R28]], [[29-ethical-pkm-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R29]]
Evidence Status: Established (philosophical) | Knowledge production as exercise of intellectual virtues: curiosity, humility, thoroughness, fairness, courage.

### 4.5 Heutagogy

**[[heutagogy]]** — Hase & Kenyon (2000)
Primary Reports: [[24-self-determined-learning-pkm-framework-2026-03-15|R24]]
Evidence Status: Emerging | Self-determined learning where the learner controls curriculum and methodology, not just pace. Highest point on the [[Pedagogy-Andragogy-Heutagogy Continuum]].
