# Cognitive Science MOC

> [!abstract] Scope
> The architecture and dynamics of human cognition: memory systems, attention, executive function, perception, reasoning, problem solving, and the formal models that describe them. **Excludes** metacognition (see [[metacognition-moc]]) and motivation (see [[motivation-theory-moc]]).

> [!principle-point] Organizing Principle
> Cognition is studied at three levels: **architectural** (what mental systems exist), **functional** (what they do), and **dynamic** (how they interact under load). This MOC organizes notes along that triad.

[**MOC-Domain**:: Foundational cognitive science underpinning all higher-order learning, memory, reasoning, and metacognitive phenomena documented elsewhere in the vault.]
