# PKM & Knowledge Systems MOC

> [!abstract] Scope
> Personal Knowledge Management theory, methods, and tools — including the Zettelkasten tradition, Evergreen-Notes practice, Building-a-Second-Brain framework, Obsidian-specific architecture, and the cognitive-science principles that justify PKM as a learning practice. Bridges to AI-assisted knowledge work where applicable.

> [!principle-point] Organizing Principle
> PKM is **distributed cognition made permanent**: the vault is an external memory whose value is determined not by note count but by **graph connectivity**, **retrieval reliability**, and **fit to the owner's cognitive architecture**.

[**PKM-Definition**:: A discipline integrating note-making, linking, and review practices to externalize knowledge in a form that supports learning, synthesis, and reuse over decades.]
