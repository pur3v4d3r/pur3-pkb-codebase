# Adult & Self-Directed Learning MOC

> [!abstract] Scope
> Adult-learning theory and self-directed learning research: andragogy (Knowles), heutagogy (Hase & Kenyon), the Tough/Brookfield/Garrison SDL traditions, transformative learning (Mezirow), competency-based and workplace learning, learning contracts, and the social-organizational forms (situated learning, communities of practice, professional learning).

> [!principle-point] Organizing Principle
> Adult learning differs from pedagogy along three axes: **autonomy** (learner controls goals), **experience** (prior knowledge is the curriculum), and **relevance** (learning serves immediate purpose). SDL operationalizes this in **process** (how) and **personality** (disposition) traditions.

[**SDL-Definition**:: A process in which learners take primary responsibility for diagnosing learning needs, formulating goals, identifying resources, choosing strategies, and evaluating outcomes — with or without help from others.]
