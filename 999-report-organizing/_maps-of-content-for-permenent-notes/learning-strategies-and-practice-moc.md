# Learning Strategies & Practice MOC

> [!abstract] Scope
> Empirically-validated learning strategies, the cognitive mechanisms behind them, expertise development, transfer of learning, and the design of practice. Covers spaced retrieval, elaboration, interleaving, deliberate practice, worked examples, instructional design principles, and the broader cognitive-science-of-learning literature.

> [!principle-point] Organizing Principle
> Effective learning is **effortful in the right way**. Strategies that feel hard often produce durable learning ([[Desirable-Difficulty]]); strategies that feel easy often produce fluency illusions ([[the-fluency-illusion]]). This MOC organizes notes around the desirable-difficulty axis.

[**Learning-Strategies-Domain**:: The intersection of cognitive psychology and education concerned with how humans efficiently acquire, consolidate, and transfer knowledge and skill.]
