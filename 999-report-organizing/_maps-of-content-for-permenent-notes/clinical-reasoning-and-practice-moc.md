# Clinical Reasoning & Practice MOC

> [!abstract] Scope
> Clinical and medical-domain notes covering diagnostic reasoning, evidence-based medicine, clinical heuristics, therapeutic models, behavior change in healthcare contexts, and the cognitive psychology of expert clinical judgment. Bridges to general dual-process theory, metacognition, and decision research.

> [!principle-point] Organizing Principle
> Clinical reasoning is **dual-process under uncertainty with high stakes** — Type 1 pattern recognition checked by Type 2 deliberate analysis. Failures of metacognitive monitoring (overconfidence, premature closure) drive most diagnostic error.

[**Clinical-Reasoning-Domain**:: The cognitive and metacognitive practices by which clinicians arrive at diagnoses, treatment decisions, and evidence-grounded interventions under uncertainty.]
