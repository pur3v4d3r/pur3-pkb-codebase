# Metacognition MOC

> [!abstract] Scope
> Cognition about cognition — the knowledge, monitoring, and control of one's own mental processes. Covers Flavell's foundational taxonomy, the Nelson–Narens architecture, calibration research, metacognitive feelings, and applied metacognition across reading, problem-solving, clinical reasoning, and PKM.

> [!principle-point] Organizing Principle
> Metacognition has two faces: **knowledge** (what you know about cognition) and **regulation** (what you do with that knowledge). The Nelson–Narens model formalizes regulation as a **monitoring → control loop** between meta-level and object-level processes.

[**Metacognition-Definition**:: The capacity to perceive, evaluate, and regulate one's own cognitive activity — the operating system that supervises learning and problem-solving.]
