## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 5 — Distinction
> **Q:** How was germane cognitive load reconceptualized in Sweller (2010), and why was the reconceptualization necessary?
> **A:** Germane load was originally the third load type (resources devoted to schema construction). Sweller (2010) reconceptualized it as a description of how intrinsic load is processed — not a separate resource pool. The reconceptualization was necessary because (1) germane load was empirically indistinguishable from intrinsic load in measurement, (2) "increase germane load" could justify any instructional intervention regardless of efficacy, and (3) the additivity hypothesis became incoherent with three independent variables and one constraint equation.
> **Source:** Section 4

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 5 — Distinction
> **Q:** What distinguishes the emancipatory and neoliberal readings of self-directed learning?
> **A:** The emancipatory reading (Dewey, Freire, Brookfield) sees SDL as a pathway to critical consciousness and democratic participation. The neoliberal reading sees SDL as the individual's responsibility to maintain employability in a flexible labour market, transferring development costs from institutions to individuals. The same curriculum mechanisms can serve either agenda.
> **Source:** Section 8
> **Difficulty:** Advanced
> **Tags:** #critical-theory #SDL #politics-of-education

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 5 — Distinction
> **Q:** What is the difference between need satisfaction and need frustration according to Vansteenkiste & Ryan (2013)?
> **A:** They are partially independent dimensions, not opposite poles of one continuum. Need satisfaction predicts well-being; need frustration predicts ill-being through distinct mediational pathways. Removing frustration does not automatically produce satisfaction.
> **Source:** Section 7
