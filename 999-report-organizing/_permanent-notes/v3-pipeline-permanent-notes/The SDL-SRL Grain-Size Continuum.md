## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] The SDL-SRL Grain-Size Continuum
> Rather than treating SDL and SRL as categorically distinct constructs or as synonyms, this report proposes that they are best understood as occupying different positions on a grain-size continuum of self-regulated knowledge construction. At the finest grain, one finds the moment-to-moment metacognitive monitoring and control processes that SRL researchers study within individual learning tasks. At intermediate grain, one finds the strategic planning and resource management that characterize self-directed engagement with a bounded learning project. At the coarsest grain, one finds the lifelong learning orientation — the heutagogical capacity to continuously revise one's own frameworks for understanding — that characterizes the most fully developed form of self-direction. This continuum model dissolves the apparent opposition between SDL and SRL by revealing them as complementary perspectives on the same fundamental phenomenon — human beings regulating their own knowledge construction — viewed at different levels of temporal and contextual scope.
> *— [[self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*
