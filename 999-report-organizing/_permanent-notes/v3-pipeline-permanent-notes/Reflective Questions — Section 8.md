## Reflections

> [!reflection] Reflective Questions — Section 8
> If attribution is the "interpretive layer" through which other motivational frameworks operate, what implications does this have for motivational intervention design? Should interventions target attributions directly (attribution retraining), the upstream beliefs that constrain attributions (mindset interventions), or the environmental conditions that shape the causal search (SDT-informed autonomy support)? Might the most effective interventions target multiple levels simultaneously?
> *— [[attribution-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!reflection] Reflective Questions — Section 8
> 1. How might the Scaffolding-Sovereignty Progression be adapted for learners from cultures that prioritise collective rather than individual self-direction?
> 2. Is self-directed learning an inherently emancipatory practice, or can it serve as a mechanism for shifting institutional responsibilities onto individuals?
> 3. Given the measurement problem, how should curriculum designers evaluate the success of their efforts to cultivate lifelong learning capacity?
> *— [[curriculum-design-for-lifelong-learning-and-self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!reflection] Reflective Questions — Section 8
> 1. Does the consistency of SDT's mediational model across domains mean that the same intervention strategies should work everywhere, or do domain-specific factors require tailored application?
> 2. In digital learning environments, what are the specific affordances that correspond to autonomy support, competence scaffolding, and relatedness facilitation?
> 3. How does SDT's framework apply to environments where participants cannot leave — prisons, compulsory education, mandatory treatment programs — where autonomy is structurally constrained?
> *— [[self-determination-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!reflection] Reflective Questions — Section 8
> 1. To what extent does your own self-directed learning practice embody technical self-direction (efficient management of learning processes) versus critical self-direction (questioning the assumptions that shape what you consider worth learning)?
> 2. How do the critiques of cultural and socioeconomic bias in SDL theory apply to your own learning context? What conditions enable your self-direction that might not be available to others?
> *— [[self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*
