## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] four-component-instructional-design-4cid — Design Methodology for Complex Learning
> **Description:** A dedicated treatment of van Merriënboer's 4C/ID model as the most comprehensive translation of CLT principles into instructional design methodology. Coverage would include the four components (learning tasks, supportive information, procedural information, part-task practice), the ten steps methodology, empirical evidence for effectiveness, comparison with alternative design models, and application examples across domains.
>
> **Connection to this report:** Section 6 introduces 4C/ID as CLT's most systematic practical extension, but the model's complexity warrants independent, detailed treatment. The current report covers the CLT logic underlying 4C/ID but not the full design methodology.
