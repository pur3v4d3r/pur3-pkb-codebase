## Reflections

> [!reflection] Questions to Carry Forward
> 1. What features of a task make it a good candidate for the application of Goal Setting Theory's prescriptions, and what features make it a poor candidate?
> 2. Why might an inductive theory be more vulnerable to misapplication than a deductive one of comparable empirical support?
> 3. If commitment mediates the effect of goal source, what does this imply about the meaningfulness of the distinction between assigned and self-set goals?
> *— [[goal-setting-theory-locke-latham-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> [!reflection] Questions to Carry Forward
> 1. Which of the four mechanisms is most likely to fail in your own most demanding goal pursuits, and why?
> 2. How might a performance goal set on a task lacking adequate strategy be transformed into a learning goal that re-engages the strategic function productively?
> 3. If the four mechanisms compound, what does this imply about the relative impact of small improvements in each versus large improvements in only one?
> *— [[goal-setting-theory-locke-latham-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> [!reflection] Questions to Carry Forward
> 1. In a goal pursuit you have observed fail despite high effort, which moderator was most likely below threshold?
> 2. Why might commitment be the only moderator the theory describes as a precondition rather than a calibration?
> 3. How might the dependence of the strategic function on task complexity explain why goal-setting interventions in creative or learning-intensive domains produce more variable effects than interventions in production-oriented domains?
> *— [[goal-setting-theory-locke-latham-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> [!reflection] Questions to Carry Forward
> 1. In a goal pursuit that has sustained itself across many cycles in your own experience, what is the feedback edge that has kept commitment renewed?
> 2. How might the cycle's amplification dynamics explain why interventions that "work" in early stages of an organizational change program sometimes produce escalating pathology in later stages?
> 3. What does the conceptual identity between deliberate practice cycles and goal-setting cycles imply about the conditions under which expertise development can be expected to occur?
> *— [[goal-setting-theory-locke-latham-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> [!reflection] Questions to Carry Forward
> 1. In your own experience or observation, can you identify a goal-setting application where the translation window was open and another where it was closed? What distinguished them?
> 2. Why might the same mechanisms that produce desirable performance gains under good conditions necessarily produce pathology under bad ones? What does this imply about the moral weight of choosing whether to apply the theory?
> 3. Is the dark-side critique fundamentally a critique of Goal Setting Theory, or of the conditions under which it has been applied? Does the distinction matter prescriptively?
> *— [[goal-setting-theory-locke-latham-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> [!reflection] Questions to Carry Forward
> 1. In a current goal pursuit, would you describe your commitment as autonomous or controlled, and what does the SDT integration predict about the likely trajectory across cycles?
> 2. For a goal you have committed to but on which you have failed to act consistently, what implementation intention specification might close the gap?
> 3. How does the integration with [[self-regulated-learning]] alter your understanding of what feedback should look like for goals set on complex learning tasks?
> *— [[goal-setting-theory-locke-latham-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*
