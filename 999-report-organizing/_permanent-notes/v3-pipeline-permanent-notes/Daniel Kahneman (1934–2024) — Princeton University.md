## Additional Material

> [!person] Daniel Kahneman (1934–2024) — Princeton University
> Israeli-American psychologist who, with Amos Tversky, founded the heuristics-and-biases programme. Awarded the 2002 Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences for his work integrating psychological insights into economic theory, particularly through prospect theory. His 2011 book *Thinking, Fast and Slow* popularised the System 1 / System 2 framing — though he has noted in technical work that this framing is pedagogical rather than literal. His later collaboration with Gary Klein on the conditions for valid intuition refined the framework's claims about expertise.
> **Key works referenced:** *Thinking, Fast and Slow* (2011); "Judgment under Uncertainty" (with Tversky, 1974); "Conditions for Intuitive Expertise" (with Klein, 2009).
