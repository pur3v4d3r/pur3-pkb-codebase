# Developmental Arc Model (Original to This Report)

> [!definition] Developmental Arc Model (Original to This Report)
> A four-stage model of metacognitive scaffold progression in PKB systems: (1) Structured Dependence — full scaffolding for all regulatory functions; (2) Guided Autonomy — selective fading of mastered functions; (3) Self-Directed Monitoring — independent regulation with occasional scaffold use; (4) Reflective Mastery — learner designs and modifies own scaffolding systems. The arc is domain-specific and non-linear.
>
> **Boundary:** The model describes a developmental trajectory, not a typology of learners. A single learner may occupy different stages across different domains simultaneously. The model does not prescribe timelines; stage transitions depend on practice volume, domain complexity, and individual differences.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** Addresses the scaffold paradox by framing scaffolding as a developmental process with built-in fading, providing a design framework for PKB systems that promote growth toward independence.
>
> **See also:** [[Scaffolded Fading]], [[metacognitive-scaffolding-design-triangle]], [[Lifelong Learning]]
