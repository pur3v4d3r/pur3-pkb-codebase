> - **Key-Term**: [[AGQ-—-Achievement-Goals-Questionnaire]]
> - **Definition**: The AGQ (Achievement Goals Questionnaire) is a psychological assessment tool designed to measure an individual's achievement goals, which are the purposes and intentions that guide their behavior in academic or athletic settings.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The AGQ was developed based on achievement goal theory, which posits that individuals have different orientations towards success, such as focusing on performance (e.g., outperforming others) versus mastery (e.g., improving one's own abilities).

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> Participants are asked to rate their agreement with various statements related to these goals, allowing researchers and practitioners to understand the underlying motivational structures of learners or athletes.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> The questionnaire includes subscales for different types of achievement goals, such as performance-approach, performance-avoidance, mastery-approach, and mastery-avoidance.

## Practical Implications
