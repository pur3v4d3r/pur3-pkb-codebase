# Organismic Metatheory

> [!definition] Organismic Metatheory
> The foundational philosophical position of SDT holding that human beings are inherently active organisms oriented toward psychological growth, integration of experience, and the development of a unified self-structure. This position stands in explicit contrast to mechanistic metatheories that treat the organism as fundamentally reactive and dependent on external stimulation for motivational energy.
>
> **Boundary:** The organismic metatheory does not claim that growth proceeds automatically regardless of conditions. It claims that the *tendency* toward growth is inherent; whether growth *occurs* depends on whether the environment supplies the necessary psychological nutriments.
>
> **See also:** [[Organismic Metatheory]], [[organismic-integration-tendency]], [[humanistic-psychology]], [[eudaimonia]]
