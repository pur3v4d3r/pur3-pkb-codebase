# Integrated Regulation

> [!definition] Integrated Regulation
> The most fully internalized form of extrinsic motivation in OIT. The identified value has been brought into coherence with the rest of the self's values and identifications, eliminating the chronic conflicts characteristic of identification-without-integration. Distinguished from intrinsic motivation by the fact that the behavior is still performed for the value it serves rather than for inherent interest in the activity itself.
