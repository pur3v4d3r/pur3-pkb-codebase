## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Richard M. Ryan
> Co-founder of self-determination theory with Deci. Primary architect of organismic integration theory and the internalization continuum specifically. Co-author of the 2017 *Self-Determination Theory* synthesis volume that consolidated four decades of theoretical and empirical work. Continues active programmatic research and is the central living figure in the framework's contemporary development.
