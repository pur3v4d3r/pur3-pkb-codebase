# performance-avoidance-goal

> [!definition] performance-avoidance-goal
> - **Key-Term**: [[performance-avoidance-goal]]
> - **Definition**: A performance-avoidance-goal is an individual's focus on avoiding failure or negative evaluation rather than achieving success or positive outcomes in a given task or domain.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Performance-avoidance-goals are rooted in achievement goal theory, which distinguishes between different types of goals that individuals pursue. These goals can influence motivation and performance by shaping how learners perceive challenges and setbacks.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> Individuals with high levels of performance-avoidance-goals may be more likely to engage in behaviors aimed at avoiding failure, such as excessive preparation or avoidance of challenging tasks, which can lead to increased anxiety and decreased performance.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> This goal orientation is often contrasted with performance-approach goals, where the focus is on outperforming others rather than avoiding failure.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In educational settings, understanding a student's performance-avoidance-goals can help educators design interventions that reduce anxiety and promote more adaptive goal orientations.

> [!example] Application
> In clinical contexts, recognizing performance-avoidance-goals in patients can inform therapeutic strategies aimed at reducing fear of failure and promoting healthier coping mechanisms.
