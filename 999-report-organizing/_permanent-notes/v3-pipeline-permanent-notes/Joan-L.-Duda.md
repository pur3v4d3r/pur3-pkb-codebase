> - **Definition**: Joan-L.-Duda is a theoretical framework within the Achievement Goal Theory tradition that focuses on understanding how individuals set and pursue goals in various domains, particularly in athletic training and performance development.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The core mechanism of Joan-L.-Duda's theory involves distinguishing between mastery-approach (focusing on learning and improvement) and performance-avoidance (focusing on avoiding failure) goal orientations. These orientations influence how individuals perceive success, effort, and challenges in their pursuit of goals.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this framework is applied to help athletes and learners set appropriate goals that align with their personal values and the context of their activities. It also aids in understanding how different goal orientations can impact motivation, performance, and well-being.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the recognition that individuals may adopt multiple goal orientations simultaneously, and that these orientations can shift over time based on situational factors.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
