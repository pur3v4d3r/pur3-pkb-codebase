> [!reflection] Metacognitive Closing Prompt
> The transfer examples above are unified by a structural insight worth carrying forward: the principles of Goal Setting Theory transfer most productively when the conditions that govern the theory's operation in its original domain are explicitly engineered into the new domain rather than assumed to be present. What this implies for any new application you might consider is that the most important question is not whether goals would help but whether the conditions that make goals helpful can be established and sustained in the context where they would be applied.
