## Open Threads

> [!open-question] Recommended Future Investigations
> > [!topic-idea] **[[Implementation-Intentions-and-Habit-Formation]]**
> > **Description:** A foundational treatment of Gollwitzer's implementation-intention research and its integration with [[habit-formation]] frameworks; examines the volitional machinery that converts goal commitment into reliably executed behavior.
> > **Connection to this report:** Section 6 introduces implementation intentions as a complement to Goal Setting Theory but does not develop the underlying research; a dedicated foundational report would substantially deepen this strand.
> > **Priority:** High.
> > **Suggested report type:** Foundational Report.
> > **Prerequisites:** [[Goal-Setting-Theory-—-Locke-and-Latham]], [[self-regulation]].
>
> > [!topic-idea] **[[Performance-Goals-vs-Mastery-Goals-Reconciling-the-Frameworks]]**
> > **Description:** A dialectical examination of the apparent tension between Goal Setting Theory's performance-goal prescriptions and [[achievement-goal-theory]]'s mastery-orientation findings, working toward an integrative synthesis that specifies the conditions under which each is appropriate.
> > **Connection to this report:** Section 5 touches on this tension but does not develop it dialectically; the dialectical structure would suit the topic's contested nature.
> > **Priority:** High.
> > **Suggested report type:** Dialectical Report.
> > **Prerequisites:** [[goal-setting-theory]], [[mastery-goal-orientation]], [[achievement-goal-theory]].
>
> > [!topic-idea] **[[Goal-Setting-in-Personal-Knowledge-Management]]**
> > **Description:** A practitioner's field guide applying the mature Goal Setting Theory framework — including the translation-window diagnostic — to the design of personal knowledge management workflows, capture practices, and synthesis cycles.
> > **Connection to this report:** Section 7's PKM transfer example outlines the structural fit; a dedicated practitioner's guide would develop the application in operational detail.
> > **Priority:** Medium.
> > **Suggested report type:** Practitioner's Field Guide.
> > **Prerequisites:** [[personal-knowledge-management]], [[goal-setting-theory]].
>
> > [!topic-idea] **[[Engineering-Metrics-and-the-Dark-Side-of-Optimization]]**
> > **Description:** A comparative architecture treatment of metric-design choices in software engineering, evaluating alternative metric systems against their predicted dark-side susceptibilities and translation-window properties.
> > **Connection to this report:** Section 6's far-transfer example identifies the structural fit but does not evaluate alternatives; a comparative architecture would systematically compare metric systems against the diagnostic framework.
> > **Priority:** Medium.
> > **Suggested report type:** Comparative Architecture.
> > **Prerequisites:** [[goal-setting-theory]], [[Software-Engineering-Practice]].
>
> > [!topic-idea] **[[The-Intellectual-Lineage-of-Self-Regulation-Research]]**
> > **Description:** A historical-genealogical treatment tracing the intellectual development of self-regulation research from its mid-twentieth-century roots through contemporary integrations; situates Goal Setting Theory within the broader genealogy that includes [[self-efficacy-theory]], [[self-determination-theory]], [[self-regulated-learning]], and [[goal-intention]] research.
> > **Connection to this report:** This report's Section 6 integrations imply a broader genealogy that the foundational format cannot adequately develop.
> > **Priority:** Medium.
> > **Suggested report type:** Historical-Genealogical Report.
> > **Prerequisites:** [[self-regulation]], [[motivation-theory-foundational-report]].
