## Examples

> [!example] What This Looks Like in Practice
> Consider a graduate student who has formed the goal intention "I want to read more philosophy of mind this semester." Under the Rubicon Model, this intention represents successful crossing into the postdecisional phase — the deliberation about whether to engage with this material is over, and the implemental mind-set has begun to operate. But the goal intention itself does not specify when, where, or in response to what cue the reading will actually occur, which means it does not yet constitute an implementation intention. Adding "When I sit down at my desk after morning coffee, I will open Chalmers and read for forty-five minutes before checking email" transforms the goal intention into an implementation intention by linking a specific situational cue (sitting down with coffee) to a specific behavior (opening Chalmers) in the if-then format that allows the situation itself to later trigger the behavior without requiring a fresh deliberative effort.
> *— [[implementation-intentions-gollwitzer-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*
