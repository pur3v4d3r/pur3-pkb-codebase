> [!reflection] Reflective Questions — Section 6
> 1. In what ways does the 4C/ID model's complexity — four components with multiple parameters — create its own form of extraneous load for the instructional designer? Is there an irony here?
> 2. How might the interface between CLT and motivational frameworks (e.g., [[self-determination-theory]]) be specified — what would a principled boundary look like rather than an ad hoc one?
> 3. If metacognitive regulation consumes working memory resources, does CLT need to expand its architectural model to include a "metacognitive load" type, or would this recapitulate the problems of the original germane load construct?
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*

> [!reflection] Reflective Questions — Section 6
> 1. In your experience, which motivational sources have been most durable across your learning life, and which have been most susceptible to fluctuation?
> 2. How might a curriculum designer create conditions for the transition from maintained situational interest to emerging individual interest in a domain where the learner has no prior connection?
> 3. What role does identity — the learner's sense of becoming a certain kind of person through learning — play in sustaining engagement, and how can curriculum design support identity-level motivation without manipulation?
> *— [[curriculum-design-for-lifelong-learning-and-self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> 3. How might the Ten Steps methodology need to be adapted for self-directed learners designing their own learning programs?

> [!reflection] Reflective Questions — Section 6
> 1. If the fluency illusion is a consequence of the monitoring system's architecture rather than a failure of individual cognition, what does this imply about the universality of overconfidence in learning?
> 2. How does the desirable difficulties framework reframe the relationship between subjective learning experience and actual learning outcome?
> 3. What specific modifications to one's own study or knowledge management practices would be most effective in improving monitoring accuracy, given the mechanisms discussed in this section?

> 3. How effectively does one's current PKB workflow close the loop between reflection and subsequent forethought — or do reflective insights tend to evaporate between sessions?
