# Autonomy (in the SDT technical sense)

> [!definition] Autonomy (in the SDT technical sense)
> The experience of acting from one's own volition rather than under pressure — of being the locus of causality of one's own action. Distinguished from independence (acting without others' input) and from individual choice-making. The autonomy/independence conflation has driven much of the cross-cultural debate; the SDT technical sense is preserved when a person volitionally endorses arrangements (such as deference to in-group judgment) that the casual sense of autonomy would not include.
