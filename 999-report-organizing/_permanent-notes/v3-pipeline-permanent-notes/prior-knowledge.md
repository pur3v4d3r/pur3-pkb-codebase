# prior-knowledge

> [!definition] prior-knowledge
> - **Key-Term**: [[prior-knowledge]]
> - **Definition**: Prior knowledge refers to the existing information, skills, and experiences that an individual brings to a new learning situation, influencing how they interpret and understand new information.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Prior knowledge is foundational in cognitive psychology as it shapes perception, memory, and problem-solving. It enables individuals to make connections between new information and what they already know, facilitating deeper understanding and retention.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, activating prior knowledge involves strategies like self-explanation, where learners articulate their existing knowledge to better integrate new information. This process enhances learning efficiency by leveraging familiar concepts as a basis for new ones.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the role of schema theory in organizing prior knowledge into structured frameworks that can be easily accessed and applied. Sub-variants include explicit vs. implicit prior knowledge and how it varies across different domains.
