# Lifelong Learning

> [!definition] Lifelong Learning
> [[Lifelong Learning|Lifelong learning]] refers to the ongoing, voluntary, and self-motivated pursuit of knowledge and competence across the entire span of a person's life, encompassing formal, non-formal, and informal learning contexts. Unlike education conceived as a bounded phase of preparation for adult life, lifelong learning operates on the premise that the capacity and need for learning does not diminish after formal schooling but continues — and in many respects intensifies — as individuals encounter new roles, new technologies, new domains of knowledge, and new challenges that their prior education could not have anticipated.
>
> **Boundary:** Lifelong learning is not synonymous with continuous formal education, nor with the mere accumulation of credentials. It is distinguished by its voluntariness, its self-motivation, and its integration into the fabric of daily life.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** For curriculum design, lifelong learning establishes the temporal horizon — curriculum must be designed not merely for immediate mastery but for the development of capacities that will sustain learning across decades.
>
> **See also:** [[self-directed-learning]], [[andragogy]], [[heutagogy]], [[personal-learning-environments]], [[lifelong-learning-system-design]]
