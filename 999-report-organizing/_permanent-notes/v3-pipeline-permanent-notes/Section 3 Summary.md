> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> The stability dimension is the primary engine through which causal attributions reshape future expectancy. Stable attributions for failure generate expectations of continued failure, creating self-reinforcing cycles exemplified by learned helplessness. Unstable attributions preserve the possibility of different future outcomes, maintaining motivational engagement. The mechanism connects to implicit theories of intelligence (Dweck's entity vs. incremental theories), to self-efficacy formation (Bandura's mastery experience interpretation), and to explanatory style (Seligman's attributional patterns in depression). Stability functions as the temporal horizon of perceived agency — determining not whether one can act, but whether acting matters.
> *— [[attribution-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> A chunk is a learned associative unit in long-term memory that is structurally complex in storage but functionally simple in use, allowing one slot in working memory to hold what would otherwise require many. Chunks are organized hierarchically, with higher-order chunks composed of lower-order chunks, and they form not through mere exposure but through attentive engagement with configurations as integrated units. Chunks differ from schemas (which are generalized frameworks) and from automatized procedures (which are compiled action sequences); they are recognition units that schemas and procedures both depend on. The Power Law of Practice describes the empirical signature of the chunk-formation process.
> *— [[chunking-and-expertise-development-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> - CLT's effect principles (worked example, split attention, redundancy, modality, expertise reversal) are empirically robust predictions derived from the theory's architectural model.
> - The worked example effect launched CLT's research programme by demonstrating that conventional problem-solving imposes counterproductive extraneous load on novices.
> - The expertise reversal effect introduces dynamic complexity: instructional optimality depends on the interaction between format and learner knowledge, not on format alone.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> The psychological infrastructure for self-directed lifelong learning comprises several interdependent systems: motivational foundations (SDT's autonomy, competence, relatedness), metacognitive capacity (knowledge and regulation of one's own cognition), self-regulatory competence (Zimmerman's forethought-performance-reflection cycle), self-efficacy (belief in one's capacity to learn), and growth mindset (implicit theories about the malleability of ability). These constructs are structurally interconnected — weakness in any one cascades across the others. The key insight for curriculum design is that autonomy and structure are not opposed but complementary: well-structured, autonomy-supportive environments accelerate the internalisation process through which external curriculum structures become internal self-regulatory capacities.
> *— [[curriculum-design-for-lifelong-learning-and-self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> - The 4C/ID model comprises four components — learning tasks, supportive information, procedural information, and part-task practice — each serving a specific cognitive function.
> - Learning tasks provide authentic whole-task practice organized in task classes of increasing complexity, with scaffold support fading within each class.
> - Supportive information supports schema construction for non-recurrent skills; procedural information supports rule automation for recurrent skills.
> - Part-task practice supplements whole-task learning for recurrent skills requiring extreme automaticity, but never replaces the whole-task backbone.
> - The dual-mechanism instructional logic — schema construction and schema automation pursued simultaneously — constitutes the model's generative principle.
> *— [[four-component-instructional-design-model-4cid-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> - Implementation intentions work by establishing a cue-behavior link in memory that subsequently operates with three documented properties: enhanced cue detection, accelerated action initiation, and shielding against interference.
> - Together these properties constitute *strategic automaticity* — automaticity achieved by deliberate planning rather than long repetition.
> - Strategic automaticity differs from ordinary habit-driven automaticity in being plan-dependent, context-bound, and revocable by re-deliberation.
> - The mechanism is best understood as a form of cognitive pre-compilation: high-level deliberation is transformed during plan formation into a low-level executable that runs efficiently at the moment of action.
> *— [[implementation-intentions-gollwitzer-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> The standard of coherence is the implicit configuration of expectations against which the monitoring loop evaluates the current comprehension state. Standards exist at multiple levels (lexical, syntactic, propositional, structural, informational); the standards a reader actually applies determine which breakdowns the monitoring system can detect. Less skilled readers operate at lower standards; skilled readers maintain higher-bandwidth, multi-level monitoring. Standards are set partly before reading begins (which is why pre-reading interventions like advance organizers work) and partly through accumulated feedback from the consequences of past reading. Coherence breaks are not undifferentiated; their accurate *classification* is itself a metacognitive skill, because each kind of break calls for a different regulatory response.
> *— [[metacognition-and-reading-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> The Nelson-Narens framework consists of a meta-level and an object-level connected by monitoring (upward) and control (downward) channels. Three architectural properties drive the framework's predictions: the dominance asymmetry (the meta-level controls the object-level but not vice versa), the imperfection of the meta-level's model (monitoring operates on cues rather than direct access), and the dynamic quality of the model (continuous updating through monitoring feedback). The imperfection of the meta-level's model is what makes the framework scientifically productive, generating testable predictions about systematic monitoring failures.
> *— [[nelson-narens-metacognitive-control-framework-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> Forethought is the assembly of regulatory readiness across all four areas — cognition (goal-setting, task analysis, strategy selection), motivation (deliberate activation of task value and adoption of mastery orientation), behavior (planning of effort allocation and time use), and context (arrangement of the immediate environment). The phase's fundamental logic is the front-loading of regulatory work into the moment when its cost is lowest, before performance has begun to consume the attentional resources that monitoring and control will require. Skipping forethought does not avoid regulatory work; it merely postpones the work to a phase in which it will be more expensive and more likely to fail.
> *— [[pintrich-self-regulation-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> The PKB functions as externalized metacognitive architecture, not merely as an information repository. This claim, grounded in the extended mind thesis, finds practical expression in the mapping of specific PKB features to specific metacognitive functions: note-making scaffolds monitoring, linking scaffolds integration assessment, templates scaffold forethought, and review workflows scaffold reflection. The mechanism of operation is cognitive offloading — the delegation of regulatory work to external artefacts that free working memory for primary learning tasks. The critical caveat is the offloading paradox: short-term regulatory improvement may come at the cost of long-term developmental stagnation if scaffolds are never faded.
> *— [[pkb-metacognitive-scaffolding-for-study-and-planning-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> Three consolidation strategies — spacing, interleaving, and retrieval practice — share the paradoxical property that short-term difficulty produces long-term benefit. Spacing distributes re-encounters to strengthen memory traces through encoding variability and effortful retrieval. Retrieval practice transforms review from passive recognition to active reconstruction, simultaneously strengthening memories and calibrating metacognitive monitoring. Interleaving forces discriminative processing by mixing different knowledge domains within review sessions. Each strategy maps directly onto PKB architectural features: spaced review queues, self-testing protocols, and cross-domain review sessions, respectively.
> *— [[pkb-pkm-cognitive-strategies-for-learning-and-pkb-architecture-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> Knowledge in human memory is organized as an associative network, not a hierarchical filing system. Schema theory, spreading activation, and chunking research collectively imply that PKM systems should be structured as densely linked networks where notes mirror the grain-size of schemas. The PKB-as-externalized-schema-network framing redefines organizational quality in terms of connectivity rather than filing completeness. The link supremacy thesis holds that the linking structure, not the folder hierarchy, is the primary organizational mechanism. Emergent organization — structural patterns that arise from accumulated linking decisions — can reveal conceptual relationships that were not explicitly planned.
> *— [[pkb-pkm-planning-and-cognitive-science-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> Python script execution in VS Code follows a specific chain: the Run button constructs a terminal command that invokes the selected interpreter with the target file, and output flows through stdout and stderr into the terminal pane. Alternative execution pathways — direct terminal invocation, selected-line REPL execution, and launch.json configurations — offer different levels of control over how the script is run. The most productive investment for a beginning developer is understanding this execution chain well enough to diagnose failures by inspecting which interpreter, which file, which arguments, and which working directory are in play at any given moment.

> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> CET explains how external events affect intrinsic motivation through their functional significance — the relative salience of their controlling versus informational aspects. Events experienced as controlling shift the perceived locus of causality from internal to external, undermining autonomy and reducing intrinsic motivation. Events experienced as informationally positive affirm competence and maintain or enhance intrinsic motivation. The undermining effect — demonstrated in Deci's 1971 puzzle experiment and confirmed in extensive meta-analytic work — is CET's signature empirical finding, revealing that external rewards can deplete an existing motivational resource through the mechanism of perceived control.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 3 Summary
> Three motivational systems underpin self-directed learning: autonomy (the experience of volitional choice, not to be confused with independence), self-efficacy (belief in one's capacity to learn, sourced from mastery experiences, vicarious learning, persuasion, and physiological states), and motivational orientation (growth mindset and internal locus of control enabling productive responses to difficulty). These motivational systems are not parallel to but deeply interdependent with the metacognitive architecture of Section 2 — motivation determines whether metacognitive distress signals produce adaptive regulation or defensive withdrawal.
> *— [[self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*
