## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The theory posits that individuals' goals can be categorized based on their content, which includes factors such as personal values, interests, and external pressures. These goal contents shape the individual's motivation and subsequent actions.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, understanding goal contents helps in predicting and influencing behavior by aligning goals with intrinsic motivations, thereby enhancing autonomy and self-determination.
