# Intelligent-Tutoring-Systems

> [!definition] Intelligent-Tutoring-Systems
> - **Key-Term**: [[Intelligent-Tutoring-Systems]]
> - **Definition**: Intelligent Tutoring Systems are computer-based educational tools designed to provide personalized instruction and feedback to learners, adapting their learning experience based on the learner's performance and understanding.
> - **Domain**: systems-thinking
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS) are grounded in cognitive science and artificial intelligence. They aim to simulate human tutoring by providing adaptive and interactive support for students, addressing individual learning needs through real-time assessment of student knowledge and skills.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> These systems work by continuously analyzing the learner's responses and adjusting their instructional strategies accordingly. For example, if a student struggles with a particular concept, an ITS might provide additional explanations or practice problems to help them master it before moving on.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the use of various AI techniques such as rule-based systems, machine learning algorithms, and natural language processing to enhance the tutoring experience.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In educational settings, ITS can significantly improve student engagement and performance by providing personalized feedback and support.

> [!example] Application
> They also help in reducing the workload for educators by automating some aspects of instruction and assessment.
