## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Pause and Consider
> Before continuing, consider how Flavell's taxonomy and Brown's knowledge-regulation distinction represent two fundamentally different ways of organizing the same phenomena. Flavell asks: "What are the components of metacognition?" Brown asks: "What are the processes of metacognition?" Nelson and Narens will ask a third question: "What is the architecture that makes metacognition computationally possible?" How does the architectural question differ from and subsume the other two?
> *— [[nelson-narens-metacognitive-control-framework-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

## Additional Material

> [!active-reading] Pause and Consider
> Before proceeding, reflect on your own most sustained self-directed learning project — one that lasted months or years. What kept you going? Was it interest in the topic, belief in your capacity to master it, a sense of personal choice, or something else? Hold that experience in mind as we examine the motivational substrates that the research literature has identified.
> *— [[self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!active-reading] Pause and Consider
> As you consider Garrison's three-dimensional model, assess your own self-directed learning practice. In which dimension are you strongest — self-management (organizing resources, time, environment), self-monitoring (tracking your own comprehension and adjusting strategies), or motivation (sustaining engagement over time)? Where are you weakest? The model predicts that weakness in any single dimension will constrain the effectiveness of the other two.
> *— [[self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!active-reading] Pause and Consider
> Think of a domain in which you have moved from dependence on external instruction to genuine self-direction. What were the stages of that transition? Was the progression smooth and gradual, or were there threshold moments — experiences of disorientation, reframing, or breakthrough — that marked qualitative shifts in your capacity for self-direction?
> *— [[self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*
