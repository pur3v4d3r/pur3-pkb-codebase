## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Chain-of-Thought-Prompting serves as a cognitive forcing function that prompts individuals to explicitly state the steps of their reasoning. This technique helps in identifying and correcting logical errors or assumptions that might lead to biased conclusions.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
