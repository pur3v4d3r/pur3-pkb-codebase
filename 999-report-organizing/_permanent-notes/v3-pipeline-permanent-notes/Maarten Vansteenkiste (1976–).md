## Additional Material

> [!person] Maarten Vansteenkiste (1976–)
> Ghent University. Leading contemporary SDT researcher who formalized the need-satisfaction/need-frustration distinction (with Ryan, 2013), developed the Basic Psychological Need Satisfaction and Frustration Scale, and led the integration of SDT with achievement goal theory.
>
> **Relationship to other figures:** Student of Lens; collaborator with Ryan and Deci; advanced the "dark side" of need frustration research.
>
> **Key works:** Vansteenkiste & Ryan (2013), Vansteenkiste, Lens, & Deci (2006).
> *— [[self-determination-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*
