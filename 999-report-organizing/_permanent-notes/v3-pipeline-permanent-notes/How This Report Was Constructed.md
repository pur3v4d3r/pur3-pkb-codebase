> **Limitations of the methodology:** The report is a synthesis based on the most cited and methodologically influential works in the Goal Setting Theory literature; it does not include systematic meta-analytic re-evaluation of the underlying primary studies. Coverage favors the empirical mainstream over emerging frontiers (subconscious goals, neural-level analyses, computational models). Cross-cultural and team-level extensions are mentioned but not treated comprehensively.
