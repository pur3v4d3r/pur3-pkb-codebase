> 2. **Recent literature (post-2024) likely under-represented.** As an AI-generated synthesis, the report draws on training data that has a cutoff; the most recent empirical developments in the field may be missing.
> 3. **The original-synthesis contributions are interpretive rather than evidential.** The three-level cascade, the climate-individual asymmetry articulation, and the layered-ecosystem framing are well-motivated by the underlying literature but are not themselves empirical contributions; they should be evaluated as analytical synthesis rather than as new findings.

> **Identified Limitations:**
> 1. The ecological validity gap between laboratory cognitive science findings and complex, self-directed PKB practice remains unresolved. The inferential steps connecting these domains, while theoretically motivated, are untested.
> 2. Individual differences in cognitive strategy effectiveness (working memory capacity, prior knowledge, learning style preferences) are not systematically addressed. The analysis treats cognitive architecture as universal, which is an oversimplification.
> 3. The social and collaborative dimensions of knowledge management (communities of practice, shared knowledge bases, collaborative note-making) receive only brief treatment in the far transfer section rather than sustained analysis.
> 4. Motivational and affective factors that influence learning strategy adoption and persistence are acknowledged but not deeply analyzed.
> 5. The extended mind thesis application, while philosophically grounded, remains contested and should be understood as a productive framing rather than established fact.
>
> **Recommendations for Future Revision:**
> 1. Supplement with empirical studies of PKB practitioners if/when such research becomes available.
> 2. Add a dedicated section on individual differences in cognitive strategy effectiveness.
> 3. Expand treatment of collaborative knowledge management through the lens of distributed cognition.
> 4. Add implementation-specific appendix sections for particular tools (Obsidian, Logseq, Roam Research).
> 5. Commission a Dialectical Report examining the tensions between cognitive engagement and efficiency in PKB practice more thoroughly.
