## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The extended mind hypothesis suggests that our minds are not confined to our brains but can include external devices and resources. Clark argues that these extensions play a crucial role in cognitive processing and problem-solving.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> Clark's work has implications for understanding how we use technology, tools, and environments to enhance our cognitive abilities. This includes the idea that our minds can be extended through writing, computers, and other artifacts.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between mere external storage (like a notepad) and more integrated extensions like prosthetic limbs or even certain types of software.
