## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> This concept seeks to reconcile the motivational constructs proposed by SDT, which emphasizes the role of basic psychological needs in fostering intrinsic motivation, with those from Achievement Goal Theory, which focuses on the types of goals individuals set for themselves (e.g., mastery vs. performance).

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> By integrating these theories, researchers and practitioners can better understand how different goal orientations influence motivation and well-being, leading to more effective interventions in educational, organizational, and clinical settings.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between intrinsic and extrinsic motivation, as well as the role of autonomy-supportive communication in promoting healthy motivational patterns.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In education, this integration can inform teaching strategies that support students' intrinsic motivation by fostering a sense of competence, relatedness, and autonomy.

> [!example] Application

> [!example] Application
