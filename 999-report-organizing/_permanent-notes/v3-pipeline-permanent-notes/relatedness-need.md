# relatedness-need

> [!definition] relatedness-need
> - **Key-Term**: [[relatedness-need]]

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The concept of relatedness-need is rooted in self-determination theory (SDT), which posits that individuals are motivated by three basic psychological needs: autonomy, competence, and relatedness. Relatedness refers to the need for a sense of belonging and connection with others.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this means that learners or athletes who feel connected to their peers, mentors, or communities tend to perform better and experience greater satisfaction in their endeavors. This can be achieved through collaborative learning environments, supportive coaching, and fostering a sense of community within teams or classes.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between perceived relatedness (how individuals perceive their relationships) and actual social interactions. The quality and nature of these connections significantly impact motivation and performance.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application

> [!example] Application
