> **Identified Limitations:**
> - The translation-window framework is offered as a well-motivated synthesis but has not been validated as a discrete construct; readers should treat it as a productive diagnostic frame rather than as an established theoretical structure.
> - The goal-setting–SDT reconciliation is similarly an integrative proposal; the specific predictions it makes warrant empirical investigation.
> - Cross-cultural validation of Goal Setting Theory has produced more nuanced findings than this report covers; the assumption of broad cross-cultural validity should be qualified by careful attention to the cultural specificity of some findings.
> - Team-level and goal-system extensions are mentioned but not developed; substantial bodies of work in these areas warrant their own treatments.
> - The argument-mapping section could be more elaborated; the two included diagrams are illustrative rather than exhaustive.
>
> **Recommendations for Future Revision:**
> - Add a section explicitly addressing cross-cultural and team-level extensions in a future revision.
> - Develop additional argument maps for the moderator-mechanism interactions and the dark-side causal pathways.
> - Conduct empirical literature review specifically targeting the qualitative-commitment integration to refine the Section 6 synthesis.
> - Generate a companion Practitioner's Field Guide to operationalize the diagnostic frameworks for direct field application.
