# Bratman's-Planning-Theory

> [!definition] Bratman's-Planning-Theory
> - **Key-Term**: [[Bratman's-Planning-Theory]]
> - **Definition**: Bratman's Planning Theory is a psychological framework that explains how individuals plan and execute actions, emphasizing the role of intentions in guiding behavior over time.
> - **Domain**: other

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The theory posits that planning involves setting goals, devising strategies to achieve these goals, and then executing those plans. It emphasizes the importance of intentions as mental states that guide action sequences.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, Bratman's Planning Theory helps explain how people make decisions about future actions and stick to them through a series of nested intentions or implementation intentions, which are specific plans for carrying out particular actions under certain conditions.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between planning and acting, as well as the role of reflective equilibrium in balancing different types of intentions. The theory also addresses how external factors can influence internal planning processes.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
