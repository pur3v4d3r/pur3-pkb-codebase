# SDT-AGT-Integration

> [!definition] SDT-AGT-Integration
> - **Key-Term**: [[SDT-AGT-Integration]]
> - **Definition**: SDT-AGT-Integration refers to the synthesis of Self-Determination Theory (SDT) and Autonomous Goal Theory (AGT), which together provide a framework for understanding motivation, particularly in educational and organizational settings by emphasizing autonomy, competence, and relatedness as basic psychological needs.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> This integration builds on SDT's core tenets of intrinsic motivation, the importance of autonomy, competence, and relatedness, while AGT adds a focus on goal orientation and the distinction between autonomous and controlled motivations. Together, they offer a comprehensive model for understanding how individuals are motivated in various contexts.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this integration helps educators and managers design environments that support students' or employees' intrinsic motivation by fostering autonomy-supportive communication, providing opportunities for competence development, and encouraging supportive relationships. It also aids in identifying and addressing motivational conflicts that arise from external pressures or control.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between autonomous and controlled motivations, where AGT emphasizes the importance of aligning goals with personal values to enhance intrinsic motivation.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In education, this integration can lead to more effective teaching strategies by fostering a classroom environment that supports students' autonomy, competence, and relatedness.

> [!example] Application
> In organizational settings, it can help managers create supportive work environments that encourage employees to pursue their goals autonomously, leading to higher job satisfaction and productivity.
