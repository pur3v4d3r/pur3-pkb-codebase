# central-executive

> [!definition] central-executive
> - **Key-Term**: [[central-executive]]
> - **Definition**: The central executive is a component of working memory that coordinates and controls cognitive tasks, such as problem-solving, decision-making, and multitasking.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The central executive acts as the manager or controller within the framework of working memory proposed by Alan Baddeley and Graham Hitch in their model (Baddeley & Hitch, 1974). It is responsible for directing attention to relevant information and integrating it with existing knowledge.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, the central executive helps individuals switch between tasks, suppress irrelevant information, and manage multiple sources of input simultaneously. For example, when driving a car while listening to music, the central executive coordinates these activities by allocating resources appropriately.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include its role in working memory capacity limitations and its interaction with other components like the phonological loop and visuospatial sketchpad.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In e-learning design, understanding the central executive can help create materials that do not overload learners' working memory by breaking down complex tasks into manageable steps.

> [!example] Application
> In multimedia learning, ensuring that visual and auditory information are synchronized to avoid cognitive load can enhance comprehension.
