## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The PKB as Constitutive Metacognitive Architecture
> The central thesis of this report holds that a well-designed PKB functions not as an optional supplement to metacognitive regulation but as a constitutive component of the learner's regulatory architecture. Just as eyeglasses do not merely *assist* vision but become part of the perceptual system for their wearer, PKB scaffolds — when habitually used, reliably consulted, and functionally integrated — become part of the metacognitive system. This is not a metaphor. It is a claim about functional architecture, grounded in the extended mind thesis and supported by evidence that externalised monitoring and planning tools demonstrably improve self-regulatory outcomes.
