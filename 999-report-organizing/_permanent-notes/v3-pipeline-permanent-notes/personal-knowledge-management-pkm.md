# personal-knowledge-management-pkm

> [!definition] personal-knowledge-management-pkm
> - **Key-Term**: [[personal-knowledge-management-pkm]]
> - **Definition**: Personal knowledge management (PKM) is the process of collecting, organizing, and sharing information to enhance an individual's learning and decision-making capabilities over time.
> - **Domain**: learning-science
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Personal knowledge management involves several key activities such as setting up a system for storing and retrieving information, using tools like note-taking apps or digital folders, and regularly reviewing and updating one’s knowledge base. This process helps learners to build a coherent understanding of their field by integrating new information with existing knowledge.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, PKM is applied through various methods including reflective journaling, mind mapping, and collaborative learning platforms. These tools facilitate the organization of diverse sources of information and enable users to access relevant data quickly when needed for problem-solving or decision-making.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances in PKM include the importance of context and personal relevance; what works well for one individual may not be as effective for another. Theoretical roots can be found in cognitive psychology, particularly in theories about memory and knowledge organization.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> PKM enhances an individual's ability to learn from experience by systematically capturing and organizing insights.

> [!example] Application
> It supports better decision-making through the availability of well-organized information that is easily accessible when needed.
