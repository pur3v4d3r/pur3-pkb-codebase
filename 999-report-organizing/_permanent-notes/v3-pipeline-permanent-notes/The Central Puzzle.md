## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Central Puzzle
> Goal commitment, motivational intensity, and behavioral intention — the variables that motivation theory of the 1970s and 1980s assumed would carry behavior through to enactment — are necessary but radically insufficient for action. The space between intending and acting contains its own causal structure, and that structure can be intervened upon directly.
