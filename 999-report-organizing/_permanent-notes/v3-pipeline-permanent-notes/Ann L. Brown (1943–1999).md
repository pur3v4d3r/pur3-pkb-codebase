## Additional Material

> [!person] Ann L. Brown (1943–1999)
> [[ann-brown|Ann Brown]], Professor at UC Berkeley, independently developed an approach to metacognition in children that emphasized the regulatory, strategic dimension — how children learn to plan, monitor, and evaluate their own cognitive activities. Her distinction between knowledge about cognition and regulation of cognition paralleled and complemented the monitoring-control distinction in the Nelson-Narens framework.
>
> **Core contribution:** Established the knowledge-about/regulation-of distinction; pioneered research on metacognition in children; developed reciprocal teaching.
> **Relationship to other figures:** Worked in parallel with Flavell on developmental metacognition; her regulatory emphasis anticipated Nelson and Narens' control channel.
> **Key works:** Brown (1978, 1987).
> *— [[nelson-narens-metacognitive-control-framework-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*
