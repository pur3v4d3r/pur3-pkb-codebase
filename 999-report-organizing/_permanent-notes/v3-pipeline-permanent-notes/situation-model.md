## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The concept of a situation-model is foundational in cognitive psychology and artificial intelligence. It refers to the mental framework or schema that an individual constructs to interpret and navigate through a specific context or scenario, enabling them to make sense of new information within that context.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, a situation-model allows individuals to predict outcomes, understand relationships between elements, and guide their actions in response to the current state. For example, when reading a story, one's situation-model helps interpret characters' motivations and anticipate plot developments based on familiar narrative structures.
