> **Causal Map:** The core mechanism (triggering → classification → expectancy + emotion → behaviour) now has an overlay: systematic distortions that bias the classification step. Cognitive biases (FAE, actor-observer) distort the dimensional placement of causes. Motivational biases (self-serving) selectively redirect the locus dimension to protect self-concept. Both bias types operate simultaneously.
> **Structural Overview:** Sections 1-4 established the normative mechanism. Section 5 has introduced the descriptive reality: people deviate from the normative model in systematic, predictable ways. The question becomes: are these deviations universal or culturally specific?
> **Evolution This Section:** The model gains a critical realism — the mechanism operates, but not cleanly. Bias is structural, not random, and emerges from both cognitive and motivational channels.
> **Emerging Patterns:** The interplay of cognitive and motivational biases means that debiasing requires dual-channel intervention — information correction alone is insufficient.
> **Open Threads:** Is the FAE truly fundamental, or is it culturally situated? How does attribution develop across the lifespan? Section 6 addresses both questions.
