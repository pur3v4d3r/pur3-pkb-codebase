## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The Linda Baker model posits that readers activate their existing knowledge structures when engaging with new information, which can enhance or hinder understanding depending on the alignment between prior knowledge and the text content.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this framework is applied to develop AI systems that assist in reading by providing relevant background information or prompts for self-explanation, thereby improving metacognitive accuracy and learning outcomes.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the role of schema activation and the potential for confirmation bias, where readers may selectively attend to information that confirms their existing beliefs.
