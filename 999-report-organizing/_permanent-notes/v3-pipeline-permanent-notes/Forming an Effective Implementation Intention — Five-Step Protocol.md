## Additional Material

> [!protocol] Forming an Effective Implementation Intention — Five-Step Protocol
> 1. **Verify upstream commitment.** Before forming the if-then plan, confirm that the underlying goal is endorsed and feasible. Mental contrasting (vividly imagining the desired outcome, then surfacing the present obstacles) is the recommended commitment-verification technique. If commitment is not produced, the implementation intention will operate on a weak foundation.
> 2. **Specify the cue with discriminability.** Choose a cue with a discrete onset, a reliable temporal anchor, and a clear external signature. Avoid vague internal states ("when I feel motivated"), generic time references ("when I have time"), or context-dependent conditions whose perceptual signature is unstable.
> 3. **Specify the behavior with executability.** Choose a behavior that is concrete, achievable in the moment of cue arrival, and well within the actor's competence. Avoid behavioral specifications that require sub-deliberation in the moment ("I will do whatever the situation seems to call for") or that exceed the actor's capacity ("I will write a brilliant essay").
> 4. **Rehearse the link.** Visualize the cue arriving and the behavior being enacted in response. The rehearsal strengthens the cue-behavior association in memory and contributes to subsequent automatization.
> 5. **Schedule a review.** Implementation intentions should be periodically reviewed for whether the underlying goal is still endorsed and whether the cue-behavior link is still serving the goal. Drift, decay, and revocation should be deliberately monitored rather than left implicit.
