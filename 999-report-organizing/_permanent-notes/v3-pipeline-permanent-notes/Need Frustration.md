# Need Frustration

> [!definition] Need Frustration
> The active thwarting of a basic psychological need (in contrast to mere need deprivation, the absence of need satisfaction). Empirically distinct from low need satisfaction in its consequences: produces the specific signatures of compensatory behavior, defensive functioning, and ill-being that need-deprivation alone does not produce. Methodologically requires separate measurement from need satisfaction.
