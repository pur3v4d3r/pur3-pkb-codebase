# knowledge-management

> [!definition] knowledge-management
> - **Key-Term**: [[knowledge-management]]
> - **Definition**: Knowledge management is the process of capturing, storing, retrieving, and sharing knowledge within an organization to improve decision-making and performance.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Knowledge management involves various strategies and tools aimed at enhancing the creation, dissemination, and utilization of organizational knowledge. It includes activities such as documenting best practices, fostering a culture of learning, and implementing information systems that support collaboration among employees.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, knowledge management can be applied through methods like creating knowledge repositories, conducting regular training sessions, and encouraging knowledge sharing through social networks or collaborative tools. This helps in reducing the loss of tacit knowledge when experienced employees leave and ensures that critical information is accessible to those who need it.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include distinguishing between explicit and tacit knowledge, where explicit knowledge can be easily codified and shared, while tacit knowledge is more personal and context-dependent. The challenge lies in effectively converting tacit knowledge into a form that can be shared and utilized by others.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> Improving decision-making processes through better access to relevant information.

> [!example] Application
