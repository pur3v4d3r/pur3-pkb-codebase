# Feeling-of-Knowing-—-FOK

> [!definition] Feeling-of-Knowing-—-FOK
> - **Key-Term**: [[Feeling-of-Knowing-—-FOK]]
> - **Definition**: A subjective confidence judgment about the correctness of one's memory, often used to describe the certainty felt when recalling information from memory.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The Feeling-of-Knowing (FOK) is a metacognitive phenomenon where individuals assess their own knowledge and recall accuracy. It plays a crucial role in decision-making processes by influencing how confident one feels about their responses or memories.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, FOK can be observed when someone answers a question and then evaluates the certainty of their answer. This evaluation helps in determining whether to stick with the initial response or seek further information.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> FOK is closely related to memory retrieval monitoring and has been studied extensively in cognitive psychology and metacognition research.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In educational settings, FOK can be used as a tool for self-assessment and learning. Students who feel confident about their answers may benefit from further practice or explanation if they are wrong.

> [!example] Application
> In artificial intelligence, FOK is relevant to the development of self-monitoring systems that assess the reliability of AI outputs.
