# anxiety

> [!definition] anxiety
> - **Key-Term**: [[anxiety]]
> - **Definition**: Anxiety is a feeling of worry, nervousness, or unease about something with an uncertain outcome.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Anxiety is a complex emotional state that often involves physical symptoms such as rapid heartbeat, sweating, and trembling. It can be triggered by anticipation of future events or memories of past experiences.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, anxiety can manifest in various forms, including generalized anxiety disorder (GAD), panic disorder, social anxiety disorder, and specific phobias. Cognitive-behavioral therapy is a common treatment approach that helps individuals identify and challenge anxious thoughts to reduce symptoms.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between normal anxiety responses to stressors and clinical anxiety disorders, which can significantly impair daily functioning.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
