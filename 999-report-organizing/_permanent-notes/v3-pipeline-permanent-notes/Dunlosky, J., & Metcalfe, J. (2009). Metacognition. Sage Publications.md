## Methodology & Sources

> The standard textbook treatment of metacognition as a cognitive psychology research field, covering the core constructs, methods, and findings with particular attention to metacognitive monitoring and control. The book is the most accessible comprehensive overview for readers new to the field.
> *Recommended sections for further reading:* Chapters 4 and 5 on monitoring and control provide the framework that organizes the rest of the book; the chapter on metacognition and education applies the framework to instructional contexts.
