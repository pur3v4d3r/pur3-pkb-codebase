## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 7 — Connection
> **Q:** How are CLT and the desirable difficulties framework reconciled, and what remains unresolved in the reconciliation?
> **A:** CLT addresses load management during initial schema construction; desirable difficulties (spacing, interleaving, retrieval practice) address consolidation and transfer after initial comprehension. They are complementary, not contradictory — operating on different learning phases. What remains unresolved is the principled specification of when to transition from CLT-optimized instruction to difficulty-enhanced practice.
> **Source:** Section 7
> **Difficulty:** Advanced
> **Tags:** #clt #desirable-difficulties #reconciliation #open-question
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 7 — Connection
> **Q:** What are the three basic psychological needs in Self-Determination Theory, and how does each apply to sustained PKM practice?
> **A:** (1) Autonomy — supported by genuine choice in system design and workflow. (2) Competence — supported by visible progress metrics and mastery feedback. (3) Relatedness — supported by community participation and knowledge sharing.
> **Source:** Section 6
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #motivation, #self-determination-theory, #pkm-sustainability
> *— [[pkb-pkm-planning-and-cognitive-science-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 7 — Connection
> **Q:** How do extrinsic vs. intrinsic goal contents relate to well-being according to Goal Contents Theory?
> **A:** The relative priority of extrinsic goals (wealth, fame, image) over intrinsic goals (growth, relationships, community) predicts diminished well-being — not because extrinsic goals are harmful per se, but because pursuing them at the expense of intrinsic goals prevents basic need satisfaction.
> **Source:** Section 5
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
