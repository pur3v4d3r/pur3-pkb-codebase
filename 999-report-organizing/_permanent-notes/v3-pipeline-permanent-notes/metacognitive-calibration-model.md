> [!definition] metacognitive-calibration-model
> - **Key-Term**: [[metacognitive-calibration-model]]
> - **Definition**: The metacognitive calibration model is a framework that describes how individuals adjust their confidence and performance based on their understanding of their cognitive processes, particularly in tasks requiring self-assessment and decision-making.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> This model posits that people use various cues to calibrate their judgments about the accuracy of their own knowledge or skills. It involves both forethought (planning and preparation) and reflection (evaluation after performance).

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this means individuals might adjust their confidence levels based on factors such as prior experience, feedback from others, or the complexity of the task at hand.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the role of metacognitive strategies in enhancing self-regulation and the potential for calibration errors due to overconfidence or underconfidence.
