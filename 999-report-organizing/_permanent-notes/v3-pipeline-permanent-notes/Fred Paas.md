## Additional Material

> [!person] Fred Paas
> Professor of Educational Psychology, Erasmus University Rotterdam, Netherlands. Co-developer of the CLT efficiency framework and pioneer of subjective cognitive load measurement. Paas's development of the nine-point mental effort rating scale remains the most widely used measurement instrument in CLT research.
>
> **Core contributions:** Cognitive load measurement, mental efficiency metrics, instructional efficiency calculations.
>
> **Relationship:** Close collaborator with Sweller; co-authored the foundational 1998 framework paper.
