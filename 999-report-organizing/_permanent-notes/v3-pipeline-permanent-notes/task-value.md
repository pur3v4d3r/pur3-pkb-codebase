> [!definition] task-value
> - **Key-Term**: [[task-value]]

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The concept of task-value is rooted in motivational theories such as Self-Determination Theory (SDT), where individuals are more likely to engage with tasks that they find personally relevant or valuable. This value can be intrinsic, meaning the task itself provides satisfaction, or extrinsic, involving external rewards like grades or recognition.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, understanding and leveraging task-value is crucial for educators and managers in designing activities that align with learners' or employees' goals and interests, thereby enhancing their motivation and performance. For instance, in educational settings, teachers can tailor assignments to reflect students' personal values and interests to increase engagement and learning outcomes.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between intrinsic and extrinsic task-value, as well as how context-specific factors such as social influence or past experiences can shape an individual's perception of a task’s value.
