## Open Threads

> [!open-question] Adaptive 4C/ID: Optimal Real-Time Adjustment Algorithms
> How should task complexity, scaffolding level, and sequencing be adjusted in real time based on learner performance data? The theoretical framework for [[Adaptive-Instruction|adaptive instruction]] within 4C/ID exists (van Merriënboer & Kirschner, 2018, Chapter 15), but the optimal algorithms for real-time adaptation — including the measurement of cognitive load, the detection of schema formation, and the decision rules for complexity progression — remain active research questions, particularly in technology-enhanced learning environments.
