# Elaborative Interrogation

> [!definition] Elaborative Interrogation
> [[Elaborative Interrogation|Elaborative interrogation]] is an encoding strategy in which the learner generates explanations for stated facts or relationships, typically by asking and answering "why" and "how" questions about the material. The strategy's effectiveness derives from its capacity to activate relevant [[prior-knowledge|prior knowledge]], establish connections between new information and existing schemas, and produce distinctive memory traces that support subsequent retrieval. Dunlosky et al. (2013) classified it as a strategy of moderate utility with strong generalization potential across age groups and content domains.
