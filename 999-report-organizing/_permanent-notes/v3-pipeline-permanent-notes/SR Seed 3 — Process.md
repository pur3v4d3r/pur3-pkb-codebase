## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 3 — Process
> **Q:** Name the three phases of Zimmerman's cyclical SRL model and describe how each maps to PKM practice.
> **A:** (1) Forethought — goal setting and strategy selection before a PKM session. (2) Performance — active note-taking, linking, and review with ongoing self-monitoring. (3) Self-reflection — evaluating session outcomes, identifying what worked, adjusting strategy for next session.
> **Source:** Section 5
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #self-regulated-learning, #metacognition, #zimmerman
> *— [[pkb-pkm-planning-and-cognitive-science-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 3 — Process
> **Q:** What is the sequence of regulatory styles on OIT's internalization continuum, from most controlled to most autonomous?
> **A:** External regulation → Introjected regulation → Identified regulation → Integrated regulation. (With amotivation as the absence of regulation and intrinsic motivation as inherent self-regulation.)
> **Source:** Section 4
