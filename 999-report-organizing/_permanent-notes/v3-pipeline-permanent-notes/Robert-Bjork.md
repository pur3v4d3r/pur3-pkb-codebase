# Robert-Bjork

> [!definition] Robert-Bjork
> - **Key-Term**: [[Robert-Bjork]]
> - **Definition**: Robert Bjork is an American psychologist known for his work on memory and learning, particularly the concept of 'desirable difficulty' which suggests that making learning more challenging can enhance long-term retention.
> - **Domain**: other

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Bjork's research focuses on understanding how people learn and remember information. His work has led to the development of several principles aimed at improving educational practices and memory strategies.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> One of his key contributions is the idea that introducing 'desirable difficulties'—such as spacing out study sessions or testing oneself before studying—can lead to better long-term retention compared to easier, more conventional methods like cramming or re-reading materials.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Bjork's theories are grounded in experimental evidence from cognitive psychology and have implications for both educational practices and the design of learning technologies.

## Practical Implications
