# Flavell, J. H. (1979). Metacognition and cognitive monitoring: A new area of cognitive-developmental inquiry. *American Psychologist, 34*(10), 906–911.

> [!definition] Flavell, J. H. (1979). Metacognition and cognitive monitoring: A new area of cognitive-developmental inquiry. *American Psychologist, 34*(10), 906–911.
> The ability to think about and regulate one's own cognitive processes.

## Methodology & Sources

> [!cite] Flavell, J. H. (1979). Metacognition and cognitive monitoring: A new area of cognitive-developmental inquiry. *American Psychologist, 34*(10), 906–911.
> The foundational paper that introduced the construct of metacognition into mainstream psychology and proposed the original tripartite organization (knowledge, experiences, regulation) that subsequent research has refined. Despite its brevity, the paper supplied the conceptual vocabulary on which the entire subsequent literature has been built.
> *Recommended sections for further reading:* The full paper is brief and worth reading in its entirety; the discussion of metacognitive experiences anticipates much of the subsequent calibration literature.
> *— [[metacognition-and-reading-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> Seminal article establishing the field of metacognition research and introducing the tripartite knowledge taxonomy (person, task, strategy). Essential background for the metacognitive knowledge framework discussed in Section 2.
