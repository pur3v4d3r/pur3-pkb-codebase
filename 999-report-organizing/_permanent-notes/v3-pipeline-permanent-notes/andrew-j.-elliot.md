> [!definition] andrew-j.-elliot

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Achievement goal theory posits that individuals pursue different types of goals (e.g., mastery or performance) to enhance their motivation and performance. These goals influence how learners perceive challenges, set standards for success, and respond to feedback.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In educational settings, students with a mastery orientation focus on improving their skills and understanding, while those with a performance orientation aim to outperform others. Elliot's theory helps explain why these different orientations lead to varying outcomes in learning and achievement.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between intrinsic (e.g., interest in the subject matter) and extrinsic (e.g., grades or rewards) motivation, as well as how social context can shape goal orientation.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> Educators can use this theory to design learning environments that promote mastery goals, which are associated with deeper engagement and better long-term outcomes.

> [!example] Application
> In athletic training, understanding performance-oriented versus mastery-oriented athletes can help coaches tailor their strategies to enhance overall performance and well-being.
