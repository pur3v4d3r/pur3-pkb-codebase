## Methodology & Sources

> [!methodology-and-sources] Methodology and Sources
> This report synthesises three primary intellectual traditions:
>
> 1. **Metacognition research** (Flavell, Nelson & Narens, Koriat) — providing the cognitive architecture of monitoring and control
> 2. **Self-regulated learning theory** (Zimmerman, Schunk, Pintrich) — providing the temporal framework of forethought, performance, and self-reflection
> 3. **Scaffolding and distributed cognition** (Vygotsky, Wood/Bruner/Ross, Clark & Chalmers, Risko & Gilbert) — providing the theoretical basis for externalised support and cognitive extension
>
> These traditions are integrated through their application to Personal Knowledge Base systems, a domain that has received limited direct empirical investigation. The integration is therefore largely **analogical** — applying principles established in educational, cognitive, and philosophical research to a context (self-directed PKB use) where direct evidence is sparse.
>
> **Claim Type Taxonomy:**
>
> | Claim Type | Epistemic Status | Example from Report |
> |------------|-----------------|---------------------|
> | Framework descriptions (Flavell's taxonomy, Zimmerman's model) | Established — peer-reviewed, widely cited | "Metacognitive knowledge comprises person, task, and strategy knowledge" |
> | Empirical findings (fluency illusion, desirable difficulties, calibration training) | Established — replicated empirical evidence | "Fluency of processing is systematically misattributed to comprehension" |
> | Feature-function mappings (PKB features → metacognitive functions) | Well-motivated — analogical extension | "Note-making scaffolds comprehension monitoring through generation" |
> | Original frameworks (Calibration-Adaptation Engine, Developmental Arc Model) | Speculative — original to this report | "The Developmental Arc progresses through four stages" |
> | Far transfer applications (clinical reasoning, project management) | Illustrative — plausible analogies, not empirical claims | "Sprint planning scaffolds forethought" |
>
> **Limitations:**
> - The application of educational scaffolding research to self-directed PKB contexts involves an untested assumption: that scaffolding mechanisms operate similarly in formal education and informal self-directed learning.
> - The Calibration-Adaptation Engine and Developmental Arc Model are theoretical proposals. They synthesise established components but have not been validated as integrated systems.
> - Individual differences in metacognitive capacity, technological fluency, and domain expertise are not addressed systematically. All scaffolding recommendations should be understood as modifiable defaults, not universal prescriptions.
