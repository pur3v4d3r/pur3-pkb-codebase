## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Reading plays a crucial role in PKM by enabling individuals to access and process new information from various sources. This foundational activity supports the development of personal knowledge frameworks and enhances cognitive processes such as memory, comprehension, and critical thinking.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, reading is often combined with other PKM strategies like self-explanation, metacognitive calibration, and activation of prior knowledge to deepen understanding and improve retention. For instance, after reading a text, individuals might engage in self-explanation to clarify their thoughts or use metacognitive checks to assess their comprehension.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the importance of active rather than passive reading, the role of context in information processing, and the integration of diverse sources to build comprehensive knowledge structures.
