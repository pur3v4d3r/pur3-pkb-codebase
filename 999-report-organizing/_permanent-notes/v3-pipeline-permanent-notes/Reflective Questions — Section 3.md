> [!reflection] Reflective Questions — Section 3
> When you experience a setback in a domain you care about, do you tend to perceive the cause as something temporary (unstable) or something enduring (stable)? How might deliberately reframing a stable attribution as an unstable one — "I haven't mastered this *yet*" rather than "I can't do this" — change your emotional and motivational response to the setback?

> 3. How does the split-attention/redundancy continuum apply to the design of personal knowledge base notes — when does cross-referencing support understanding, and when does it become noise?

> 2. How might a curriculum designer create conditions for accurate metacognitive calibration without relying on traditional testing?
> 3. To what extent does the psychological infrastructure described here assume a Western, individualistic conception of the self, and how might it need to be modified for learners from collectivist cultural contexts?

> 3. What would go wrong if a designer implemented learning tasks and supportive information but omitted procedural information and part-task practice — or vice versa?

> [!reflection] Reflective Questions — Section 3
> 1. What would change if the meta-level's model were perfect? What predictions would the framework make, and how would the research program differ?
> 2. The dominance asymmetry ensures that the meta-level can control the object-level but not vice versa. Can one think of real cognitive situations in which this asymmetry appears to break down — situations in which object-level processes seem to "commandeer" the monitoring system?
> 3. How does the cybernetic feedback analogy illuminate the monitoring-control loop, and where does the analogy break down?

> 1. Examining one's own PKB workflow, which of the four feature-function mappings (note-making → monitoring, linking → integration, templates → forethought, reviews → reflection) is strongest? Which is weakest or absent?
> 2. The extended mind thesis proposes that external tools can become constitutive parts of cognitive processes. Does one's current relationship with one's PKB meet the criteria for constitutive integration — regular use, reliable access, automatic trust?
> 3. Where in one's current workflow is cognitive offloading most beneficial? Where might it be preventing the development of internal competence?
