## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Situation Model — Final Through Section 8
> **Key Entities:** All prior entities, now complete. Added: cultural critique (Western individualism assumption); socioeconomic privilege critique; technical vs. critical self-direction (Brookfield); Metacognitive Sovereignty Framework (original synthesis — three pillars); SDL-SRL Grain-Size Continuum (original synthesis — dissolving false opposition); measurement limitations (SDLRS critiques); digital reconceptualization imperative
> **Causal Map:** The complete causal architecture of SDL: Basic need satisfaction + self-efficacy → autonomous motivation → metacognitive engagement → monitoring-control loop → self-directed learning behavior → metacognitive knowledge accumulation + self-efficacy growth → enhanced SDL capacity. This cycle operates within and is constrained by cultural context, socioeconomic conditions, and institutional structures.
> **Structural Overview:** The complete report maps SDL from definition (S1) through cognitive mechanism (S2), motivational substrates (S3), theoretical models (S4), SRL interface (S5), developmental trajectory (S6), contextual applications (S7), to critical evaluation (S8). The Metacognitive Sovereignty Framework and SDL-SRL Grain-Size Continuum emerge as original contributions.
> **Evolution This Section:** Added critical evaluation, revealing genuine limitations while proposing integrative syntheses (MSF and grain-size continuum) that address some of the field's unresolved tensions.
> **Final Pattern:** Self-directed learning is a recursive, developmental, context-sensitive capacity that emerges from the interaction of metacognitive skill, motivational alignment, and environmental affordance. It is neither purely cognitive nor purely motivational, neither purely individual nor purely social, neither purely trait nor purely state — it is the dynamic product of their interplay.
