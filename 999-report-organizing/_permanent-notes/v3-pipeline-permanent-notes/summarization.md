# summarization

> [!definition] summarization
> - **Key-Term**: [[summarization]]
> - **Definition**: Summarization is the process of condensing information from a longer text into a shorter, more concise version while retaining the essential meaning and key points.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Summarization involves identifying the main ideas, removing unnecessary details, and rephrasing content to create a condensed version. It can be applied in various contexts such as academic writing, business communications, or information retrieval systems.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, summarization algorithms use natural language processing techniques like text analysis, keyword extraction, and sentence ranking to generate summaries that capture the essence of the original text. Human-generated summaries often involve more nuanced understanding and context awareness.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> There are different types of summarization, including extractive (selecting key sentences) and abstractive (generating new sentences). Theoretical roots include information theory and cognitive psychology.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In academic writing, summarization helps readers quickly grasp the main points of a longer text without having to read it in full.

> [!example] Application
> In business communications, summarization can streamline reports and presentations, making them more accessible and efficient for stakeholders.
