# self-esteem

> [!definition] self-esteem
> - **Key-Term**: [[self-esteem]]
> - **Definition**: Self-esteem refers to an individual's overall evaluation or opinion of their own worth, which can be influenced by personal achievements and the perception of acceptance from others.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Self-esteem is a fundamental aspect of psychological well-being that involves an individual’s subjective judgment about their value as a person. It encompasses beliefs about one's abilities, accomplishments, and overall self-worth.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> The process of developing and maintaining healthy self-esteem often involves attributing successes to internal factors (such as personal effort) and failures to external factors (like situational challenges). This attributional style can be influenced by social interactions and feedback from others, which shape an individual's self-perception over time.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances in the concept of self-esteem include its variability across different domains (e.g., academic, social), its potential for fluctuation based on recent experiences, and its role in motivation and behavior. Theoretical roots can be traced back to cognitive psychology and social learning theory.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In education, fostering self-esteem can lead to improved academic performance as students are more likely to engage with challenging tasks when they believe in their capabilities.

> [!example] Application
> In clinical settings, interventions aimed at improving self-esteem have been shown to be effective in treating depression and anxiety by enhancing an individual's resilience against negative emotions.
