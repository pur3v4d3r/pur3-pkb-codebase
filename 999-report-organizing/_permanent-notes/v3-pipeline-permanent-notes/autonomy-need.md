## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The concept of autonomy-need is rooted in Self-Determination Theory (SDT), which posits that individuals have innate psychological needs to satisfy their basic drives. Autonomy-need specifically pertains to the need to experience volition, or a sense of choice and self-direction.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In educational contexts, fostering autonomy-need involves providing students with opportunities for self-regulation, choice, and responsibility in their learning processes. This can enhance motivation, engagement, and overall academic performance.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Autonomy-need is closely related to the concept of intrinsic motivation, where individuals are driven by internal rewards rather than external incentives.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> Instructors can promote autonomy-need by offering choice in assignments or projects, encouraging self-reflection on learning goals, and providing feedback that supports students' sense of control over their learning.
