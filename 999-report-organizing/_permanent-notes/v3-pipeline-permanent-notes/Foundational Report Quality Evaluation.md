> **Identified Limitations:**
> 1. Cultural coverage is Western-centric with East Asian comparison; attribution research in African, Latin American, Middle Eastern, and Indigenous contexts is absent.
> 2. Neural mechanisms of attribution receive only passing mention in the Tensions section; a neuroscientific treatment would strengthen the mechanistic account.
> 3. The relationship between attribution and SDT's competence and relatedness needs is acknowledged but not fully developed.
> 4. Post-2023 empirical developments are not included.
> 5. The clinical applications of attributional analysis (depression, anxiety, chronic illness) receive summary treatment rather than the systematic coverage they warrant.
>
> **Recommendations for Future Revision:**
> 1. Commission a dedicated Annotated Critical Analysis on cross-cultural attribution to address the Western-centric limitation.
> 2. Add a subsection on neural mechanisms as the cognitive neuroscience evidence matures.
> 3. Develop the attribution-SDT competence/relatedness connection as new research emerges.
> 4. Consider a companion Practitioner's Field Guide focused specifically on attribution retraining protocol design and implementation.
