## Additional Material

> [!section-summary] Level 5 Summary
> At the dynamics level, element interactivity appeared as a clean, unified mechanism generating all CLT effects. At the edge case level, we now see its boundaries. Six edge cases reveal where the framework encounters difficulties: (1) collaborative learning requires collective working memory extension, (2) motivation modulates element interactivity effects beyond what cognitive architecture alone predicts, (3) self-regulated learners generate their own element interactivity, (4) embodied cognition challenges the assumption that element processing is confined to working memory, (5) the creativity paradox questions how genuinely novel schemas form under narrow-limits constraints, and (6) irreducibly high element interactivity situations reveal the need for a developmental model of schema acquisition. These edge cases do not invalidate element interactivity — they map the territory where the mechanism requires supplementation, extension, or reconceptualization. The next level engages the frontier: the active research programs working on these challenges.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-deep-dive-2026-04-12_report]]*

> [!section-summary] Level 5 Summary
> At the previous level, the schema-construction loop appeared as a clean and self-amplifying mechanism. At THIS level, six edge cases — biologically primary knowledge, expertise reversal, germane-load reformulation, isolated elements, productive failure, multimedia exceptions — have shown the loop's domain of validity to be more constrained than the standard exposition suggests, and have driven the theoretical refinements that distinguish modern CLT from its earlier formulations. The loop remains the central mechanism within its domain; the domain itself is now more carefully delimited. The next level will engage the current research frontier where the most active questions about quantification, measurement, and theoretical reconciliation are being worked out.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-element-interactivity-deep-dive-2026-04-20]]*

> [!section-summary] Level 5 Summary
> At dynamics level, we traced how monitoring accuracy propagates through control. At the EDGE CASES level, we've examined seven systematic failure modes: the illusion of knowing (silent comprehension monitoring failure), the [[dunning-kruger-effect]] (meta-ignorance), the delayed JOL anomaly (retrieval as monitoring bootstrapping), domain transfer limitations, emotional contamination, expert calibration reversals, and the hypercorrection effect (monitoring errors as learning resources). Collectively, these edge cases reveal that monitoring accuracy is radically context-dependent — an emergent property of the monitoring conditions, not a stable trait.
>
> **The next level zooms to the frontier:** How are researchers currently attempting to computationally model, neurally map, and technologically augment monitoring accuracy?

> [!section-summary] Level 5 Summary
> Six edge cases reveal the boundaries of the standard internalization mechanism: controlled internalization (identified-looking regulations sustained by hidden contingencies), identification without integration (endorsed values in unresolved conflict), autonomous regulation of harmful content (form-content separation), the cross-cultural autonomy challenge (resolved primarily by correct operationalization of autonomy as volition), pre-reflective internalization in early childhood (mechanism partially distinct from the post-reflective standard model), and need satisfaction through controlling substrates (digital environments as a case the standard framework was not built to handle). Each edge case has driven theoretical refinement; some are largely absorbed, others are still active research frontiers.
>
> Level 6 will move to the frontier — the questions where the field is currently most active, where consensus has not yet formed, and where the most generative future work is being done.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-internalization-continuum-deep-dive-2026-04-21]]*

> [!section-summary] Level 5 Summary
> At dynamics level, the general model produced clean predictions about arousal benefits and task-difficulty moderation. At edge case level, five boundary conditions emerge that qualify or complicate those predictions: (1) WMC variation creates asymmetric sensitivity to dual-task costs; (2) restlessness means sitting ISN'T the free baseline — it has its own costs; (3) ADHD-spectrum profiles amplify the arousal benefit to the point where walking may be quasi-medicinal; (4) the metacognitive blind spot means walking readers may not notice when comprehension has silently failed; (5) hyper-habituation of walking may paradoxically enable mind-wandering. Each edge case reveals something about the topic's deep structure that the general model misses, and together they show that the walking-reading optimization problem is genuinely multidimensional.
> *— [[walking-academic-reading-working-memory-deep-dive-2026-04-13_report]]*
