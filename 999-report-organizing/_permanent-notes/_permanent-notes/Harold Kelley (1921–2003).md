> [!person] Harold Kelley (1921–2003)
> American social psychologist, UCLA. Developed the covariation model (1967) and causal schemata theory (1972), providing the normative input-side account of how people combine consistency, distinctiveness, and consensus information to arrive at internal or external attributions. Kelley's models describe how attributions are *formed*; Weiner's describes what they *do*.
> *— [[attribution-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*
