## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Rabbit Hole: Dynamical Systems Models of the Monitoring-Control Loop
> **What it is:** Formal mathematical modeling of the monitoring-control interaction as a dynamical system, with monitoring accuracy as state variable and study allocation as output.
> **Why follow it:** These models can predict the bifurcation conditions — the threshold of monitoring accuracy below which the system enters the vicious cycle versus the threshold above which the system self-corrects.
> **Time investment:** High. Requires comfort with differential equations and dynamical systems theory.
> **Where to start:** Son & Sethi (2006), "Metacognitive control and optimal learning" — applies optimal control theory to the study-time allocation problem.
> **See also:** [[Self-Regulated-Learning-as-a-Resource-Allocation-Problem-Under-Uncertainty]]
