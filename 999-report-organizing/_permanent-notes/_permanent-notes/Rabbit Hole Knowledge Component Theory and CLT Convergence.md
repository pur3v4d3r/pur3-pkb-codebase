## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Rabbit Hole: Knowledge Component Theory and CLT Convergence
> **What it is:** The potential theoretical convergence between CLT's element interactivity analysis and knowledge component models (Koedinger, Corbett, & colleagues) used in adaptive tutoring systems.
> **Why follow it:** Knowledge component models have already been computationally formalized in ways that element interactivity has not. The cross-pollination could accelerate both programs.
> **Time investment:** ~5 hours (Koedinger's knowledge component framework papers + comparison with CLT)
> **Where to start:** Koedinger, K. R., Corbett, A. T., & Perfetti, C. (2012). The Knowledge-Learning-Instruction framework.
