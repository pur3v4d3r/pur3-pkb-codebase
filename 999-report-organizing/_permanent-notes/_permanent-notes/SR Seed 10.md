## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 10
> **Q:** What is the "Walk-Read-Walk Sandwich" protocol, and what cognitive mechanism does each phase exploit?
> **A:** (1) Pre-walk (15-20 min): schema activation, arousal priming via exercise-cognition effects. (2) Desk reading (45-60 min): deep comprehension and generation effect through active PKB engagement. (3) Post-walk (15-20 min): memory consolidation, retrieval practice, and creative incubation through embodied cognition effects.
> **Source:** Section 8.2–8.3
