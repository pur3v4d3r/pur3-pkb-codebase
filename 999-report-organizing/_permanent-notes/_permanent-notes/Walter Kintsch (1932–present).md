## Additional Material

> [!person] Walter Kintsch (1932–present)
> **Core Contribution:** Developed the Construction-Integration model of text comprehension with Teun van Dijk; distinguished the three levels of text representation (surface code, text base, situation model); provided the most rigorous computational account of how comprehension works.
>
> **Role in the Dialectical Arc:** Primary developer of the alternative framework that displaces the received view; the CI model is both the theoretical ground for the challenges in Phase III and the foundation of the reconstruction in Phase V.
>
> **Relationship to Other Figures/Frameworks:** Built on van Dijk's discourse structure analysis; extended and formalized by Zwaan & Radvansky's Event Indexing Model; related to Bartlett's schema theory as a successor framework.
>
> **Key Works:** *Cognition and Text* (1974); *The Representation of Meaning in Memory* (1974); with van Dijk, "Toward a Model of Text Comprehension and Production" (1978); *Comprehension: A Paradigm for Cognition* (1998).
>
> **Relevance to This Analysis:** The three-level model is the primary theoretical weapon against the received view; the CI model explains the mechanism of situation model failure.
> *— [[situation-model-construction-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-29_report]]*
