# Mastery Goal (Ames, 1992; Dweck's "Learning Goal")

> [!definition] Mastery Goal (Ames, 1992; Dweck's "Learning Goal")
> An achievement goal in which success is defined as developing competence — learning, improving, or mastering the task. The standard of reference is intrapersonal or task-inherent. Associated with deep cognitive processing, persistence in the face of difficulty, intrinsic motivation, help-seeking, and long-term learning. Also known as learning goals (Dweck), task goals (Nicholls), or task-involvement goals.
