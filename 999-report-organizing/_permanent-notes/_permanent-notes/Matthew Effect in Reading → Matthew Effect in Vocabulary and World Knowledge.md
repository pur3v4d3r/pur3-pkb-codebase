## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Matthew Effect in Reading → Matthew Effect in Vocabulary and World Knowledge
> Stanovich's own analysis extended the Matthew Effect beyond reading fluency to vocabulary and world knowledge. Fluent readers encounter more words, across more domains, and build larger vocabularies and richer background knowledge — which makes future reading easier, more productive, and more intrinsically motivating. The structural analogue in any domain of declarative knowledge is identical: early fluency in processing a domain's vocabulary and conceptual schemas determines both the *rate* of future learning (more efficient processing) and the *probability* of future engagement (more enjoyable experience). Early knowledge begets subsequent knowledge acquisition in a compounding asymmetry that is architecturally guaranteed, not merely probabilistically likely.
> *— [[reading-fluency-automaticity-focused-analysis-2026-03-20_report]]*
