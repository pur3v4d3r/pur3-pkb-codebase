## Additional Material

> [!person] Rolf Zwaan (b. 1961)** — Erasmus University Rotterdam
> **Core contribution:** Development of the Event-Indexing Model of situation model construction (with Langston & Graesser, 1995), which specified the five dimensions (time, space, causality, intentionality, entity identity) along which readers track discourse-denoted situations. Also major contributions to the embodied cognition account of language and text comprehension.
>
> **Key works:** Zwaan, Langston & Graesser (1995) *The construction of situation models in narrative comprehension*; Zwaan & Madden (2004) *Updating situation models*
>
> **Intellectual relationship:** Extended Kintsch and van Dijk's situation model concept into a detailed multi-dimensional processing model; influenced by Johnson-Laird's mental model theory.
>
> **See also:** [[rolf-zwaan]], [[situation-model]], [[situation-models-in-text-comprehension]]
