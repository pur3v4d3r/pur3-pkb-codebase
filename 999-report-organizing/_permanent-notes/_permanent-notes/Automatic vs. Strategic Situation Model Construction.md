## Open Threads

> [!tension-identified] Automatic vs. Strategic Situation Model Construction
> **The Tension:** Whether situation model construction is primarily automatic (driven by default processes in skilled reading) or primarily strategic (requiring deliberate metacognitive intervention).
>
> **Position A — Primarily Automatic (Constructionist Theory):** Skilled readers automatically generate causal coherence-preserving inferences as a default; situation model construction is a by-product of skilled fluent reading.
>
> **Position B — Primarily Strategic (Self-explanation literature):** Chi et al. (1994) and related research demonstrate that even skilled readers construct substantially better situation models when using deliberate self-explanation strategies, indicating that automatic processes are not sufficient for deep comprehension.
>
> **Current Evidence:** A middle position is best supported: automatic processes produce a minimally coherent situation model; strategic processes are necessary to extend it to the depth required for inferential transfer.
>
> **This Report's Position:** Strategic processes are necessary for situation model depth that supports transfer; automatic processes establish minimum causal coherence but not explanation-level depth.
>
