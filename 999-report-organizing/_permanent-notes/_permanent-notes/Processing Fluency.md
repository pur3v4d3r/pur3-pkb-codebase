# Processing Fluency

> [!definition] Processing Fluency
> <span style='color: #FFC700;'>Processing fluency</span> is the subjective experience of ease or difficulty associated with cognitive operations. In the metacognitive monitoring context, it serves as a heuristic cue: operations that feel fluent (easy) generate higher confidence and higher [[judgment-of-learning|JOLs]], while operations that feel disfluent (difficult) generate lower confidence. The metacognitive system treats fluency as a signal of learning strength, following the implicit logic: "If processing is easy, I must know this well."
>
> [**Processing-Fluency**:: The subjective experience of ease or difficulty in cognitive operations, serving as the dominant heuristic cue for metacognitive monitoring — high fluency generates high confidence via the implicit inference that easy processing indicates strong learning.]
