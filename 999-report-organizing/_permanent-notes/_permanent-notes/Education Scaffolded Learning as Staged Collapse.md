## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Education: Scaffolded Learning as Staged Collapse
> The pedagogical concept of [[Scaffolding-in-Education|scaffolding]] — providing structured support that is gradually withdrawn as the learner develops independent competence — mirrors the role of the protostellar envelope in star formation. During the early stages of collapse, the envelope provides the mass reservoir from which the protostar grows; as the protostar matures and feedback mechanisms (outflows, radiation) disperse the envelope, the young star must sustain itself through its own internal resources (nuclear fusion). In education, the scaffold provides the resources from which the learner constructs understanding; as the learner develops, the scaffold is withdrawn, and the learner must sustain their engagement through intrinsic motivation and [[self-regulated-learning|self-regulated learning]] strategies. The threshold cascade model applies here as well: initial skill acquisition (deuterium burning) is a soft threshold that does not guarantee sustained competence; [[automaticity|automaticity]] (hydrogen fusion) is a hard threshold that establishes a self-sustaining equilibrium. The structural parallel enriches both domains: it suggests that the timing of scaffold withdrawal in education — too early and the learner "fails to ignite"; too late and the learner develops dependence — may be illuminated by the physics of accretion feedback in star formation.
>
