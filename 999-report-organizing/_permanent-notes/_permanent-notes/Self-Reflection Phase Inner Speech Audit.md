## Additional Material

> [!protocol] Self-Reflection Phase Inner Speech Audit
> After completing a reading session, spend 3–5 minutes with these prompts, attending to the vocabulary you reach for:
>
> **Causal Attribution Audit:**
> "What explains how this session went?" — Notice whether your explanation reaches for ability, effort, strategy, task difficulty, or context. Specifically: is the cause you named stable or unstable? Controllable or uncontrollable?
>
> **Efficacy Update Audit:**
> "What am I taking away about my capability for this kind of material?" — Notice whether your efficacy estimate has changed and why. Is the update warranted by actual performance data, or is it driven by the *emotional valence* of the session?
>
> **Regulatory Register Audit:**
> "What was the dominant motivational register of this session?" — Was it introjected (should/must), identified (want to/because), or intrinsic? What would it take to shift the balance toward the more autonomous end?
>
> **Vocabulary Replacement:**
> For each maladaptive vocabulary item identified, deliberately formulate a replacement using the four-domain vocabulary architecture above. This is not positive thinking; it is accurate attribution reframing.
> *— [[attribution-self-efficacy-inner-speech-vocabulary-foundational-report-2026-03-25_report]]*
