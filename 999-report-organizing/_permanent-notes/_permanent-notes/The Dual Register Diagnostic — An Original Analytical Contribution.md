## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] The Dual Register Diagnostic — An Original Analytical Contribution
> I propose that elaborative interrogation questions can be classified into two registers based on where their answer demands fall in the three-level text representation architecture:
>
> **Register 1 — Textbase Register:** "Why" questions whose answers are recoverable from the propositional content of the text. Example: "Why did the colonists protest the Stamp Act?" when the text explicitly states "the colonists protested because they believed the tax was unjust and unconstitutional." The answer is in the textbase. Reading it more carefully produces the answer; constructing a situation model is not required.
>
> **Register 2 — Situation Model Register:** "Why" questions whose answers require constructing a causal or functional account that integrates textual propositions with prior world knowledge in ways the text does not supply. Example: "Why would a belief that the tax was unjust lead to public protest rather than private compliance?" The text does not explain this; answering requires a model of civic agency, political culture, and the social dynamics of collective action.
>
> The diagnostic question is: **"Can this question be answered by someone who has only read this text, with no other knowledge of the world?"** If yes, it is a Register 1 question and will function at the textbase level. If no — if the answer requires world knowledge not present in the text — it is a Register 2 question and will force situation model engagement.
>
> This diagnostic has immediate practical value: it provides a principled design criterion for constructing EI questions that will actually work, rather than questions that appear to be EI but function as textbase-level prompts.
> *— [[elaborative-interrogation-reading-focused-analysis-2026-03-30_report]]*
