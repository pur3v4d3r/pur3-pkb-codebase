> [!section-summary] Level 6 Summary
> At the edge case level, we identified where element interactivity's explanatory power encounters boundaries. At the frontier level, we now see the active research programs working on those boundaries. The germane load reconceptualization is converging toward a bipartite model (intrinsic + extraneous interactivity only). The measurement problem remains the theory's most significant vulnerability — no method currently measures element interactivity with precision sufficient for quantitative prediction. Neuroimaging offers a potential path to objective measurement. Adaptive instruction represents the theory's most ambitious practical application but requires solving the measurement problem for real-time contexts. The evolutionary educational psychology framework extends element interactivity's theoretical grounding but introduces new questions about the primary/secondary knowledge boundary. The next level will venture beyond the frontier into informed speculation — what element interactivity theory might look like if current research programs succeed.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-deep-dive-2026-04-12_report]]*

> [!section-summary] Level 6 Summary
> At the previous level, the standard account was qualified by edge cases. At THIS level, the *current research frontier* has been engaged through five live questions and four expert debates, revealing CLT as a theory empirically mature in its applications and theoretically reorganizing in its foundations. The element-interactivity construct sits at the center of the reorganization, and the measurement, neural-correlates, and architectural-integration questions are the most consequential open problems. The next level will go beyond the current evidence into informed speculation about where the construct may go under pressure from predictive-processing accounts of cognition and from AI-mediated learning environments.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-element-interactivity-deep-dive-2026-04-20]]*

> [!section-summary] Level 6 Summary
> At the edge cases level, we identified systematic monitoring failures. At the FRONTIER level, we have surveyed three active research programs: **computational modeling** (SDT, Bayesian, and trace-based approaches competing to formalize monitoring), **neural substrates** (anterior PFC as monitoring hub, individual differences in gray matter volume correlating with monitoring sensitivity), and **technology-mediated calibration** (confidence-weighted assessment, externalized monitoring, calibration feedback systems). We also examined individual difference variables (WMC, cognitive reflection, age, expertise) that modulate monitoring accuracy.
>
> The next level extrapolates beyond current evidence into informed speculation about the future of monitoring accuracy research and its transformative implications.
> *— [[metacognitive-monitoring-accuracy-calibration-deep-dive-2026-04-12_report]]*

> [!section-summary] Level 6 Summary
> Five active frontier questions: neural correlates of regulatory style ([[Kenji-Murayama|Murayama]] line), cross-cultural variability in need weighting and satisfying contents, internalization in algorithmically mediated environments, intra-person dynamics revealed by intensive longitudinal designs, and AI-mediated need support. Two expert debates: the completeness of the three-need list (with beneficence as the most credible candidate addition), and the separate-measurement of need frustration vs satisfaction. The frontier has a shape — most active questions concern the framework's extension into conditions outside its formative environment.
>
> Level 7 will move beyond the frontier into informed speculation about what internalization may mean in algorithmically mediated futures whose contours are not yet visible.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-internalization-continuum-deep-dive-2026-04-21]]*

> [!section-summary] Level 6 Summary
> At edge case level, we identified specific boundary conditions. At frontier level, we see the **current limits of knowledge**: (1) the precise dose-response function for acute light exercise and executive function is still being mapped; (2) whether [[embodied-cognition]] contributes a mechanistically distinct facilitation effect is theoretically possible but empirically unresolved for academic reading; (3) movement-integrated learning paradigms are in their infancy; and (4) the specific ecological conditions of the walking reader have essentially no direct empirical study. The frontier picture is one of **reasonable mechanistic confidence in the direction of effects combined with real uncertainty about magnitudes and specific optimization parameters.**
> *— [[walking-academic-reading-working-memory-deep-dive-2026-04-13_report]]*
