## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[Desirable-Difficulties-and-the-Effortful-Encoding-Paradox|Desirable Difficulties and the Effortful Encoding Paradox]]
> Robert Bjork's desirable difficulties research demonstrates that several instructional conditions that increase cognitive effort and impair performance during learning — spacing, interleaving, retrieval practice, generation — consistently improve long-term retention and transfer. This creates productive tension with CLT's load-reduction emphasis. A focused analysis would examine whether desirable difficulties are best understood as germane load operations (increasing productive effort), as a domain where CLT's prescriptions are genuinely wrong, or as a complementary framework that addresses long-term retention rather than initial acquisition. Priority: **High** — the most important theoretical conversation partner for CLT in contemporary learning science.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-06_report]]*
