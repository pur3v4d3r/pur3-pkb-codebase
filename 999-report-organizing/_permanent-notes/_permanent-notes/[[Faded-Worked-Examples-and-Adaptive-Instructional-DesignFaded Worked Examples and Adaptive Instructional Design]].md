> [!evidence] [[Faded-Worked-Examples-and-Adaptive-Instructional-Design|Faded Worked Examples and Adaptive Instructional Design]]
> The faded worked example paradigm represents the most operationally specific and practically implementable application of the expertise reversal effect, with its own substantial empirical literature. A focused analysis would examine the specific conditions under which fading schedules are most effective, the role of embedded assessment in identifying optimal fading pace for individual learners, and the emerging research on automated fading in intelligent tutoring systems and adaptive learning platforms.
