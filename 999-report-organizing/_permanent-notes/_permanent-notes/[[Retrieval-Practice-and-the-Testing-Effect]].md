## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[Retrieval-Practice-and-the-Testing-Effect]]
> **Description:** Retrieval practice is this report's primary recommendation for the PKM retrieval phase, but the full complexity of the testing effect literature — encoding specificity, the role of feedback, the interleaving effect, the interaction with metacognitive monitoring — warrants independent comprehensive treatment. A dedicated foundational report would examine the conditions under which retrieval practice is most effective, the mechanisms by which it produces superior retention, and the most effective integration strategies for PKM practitioners.
> **Connection to This Report:** Section 5 identifies retrieval practice as the schema-consolidating mechanism for the retrieval phase; Section 6 makes it a design principle. But the account is necessarily compressed in this report.
> **Priority:** Critical
> **Suggested Report Type:** Foundational Report — comprehensive encyclopedic treatment of retrieval practice, desirable difficulties, and spaced repetition as an integrated learning science tradition.
> **Prerequisites:** [[working-memory]], [[long-term-memory]], [[spaced-repetition]]
