## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Virtuous and Vicious Cycles
> This dynamic architecture produces both virtuous and vicious cycles. In a virtuous cycle, a learner with moderate self-efficacy engages in accurate metacognitive monitoring, notices genuine progress, attributes that progress to strategic effort, increases self-efficacy, applies more sophisticated metacognitive strategies in the next cycle, and achieves deeper understanding. In a vicious cycle, a learner with low self-efficacy engages in defensive, surface-level monitoring, misses genuine progress (or attributes progress to luck), fails to update self-efficacy upward, continues applying minimal strategies, and achieves superficial understanding that further confirms low self-efficacy. Educational interventions targeting either metacognition or motivation in isolation may fail because they do not break the cycle at multiple points.
> *— [[metacognition-motivation-foundational-report-2026-03-05_report]]*
