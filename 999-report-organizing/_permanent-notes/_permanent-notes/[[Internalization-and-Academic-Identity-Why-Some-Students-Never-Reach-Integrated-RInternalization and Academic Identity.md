## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[Internalization-and-Academic-Identity-Why-Some-Students-Never-Reach-Integrated-R|Internalization and Academic Identity: Why Some Students Never Reach Integrated Regulation]]
> OIT predicts that under supportive conditions, learners will move toward identified and integrated regulation. Yet many students who have experienced reasonably supportive educational environments plateau at identified regulation — they value academic success instrumentally but have not integrated it with a genuine sense of academic identity. A focused analysis would examine: what distinguishes identified from integrated regulation in the academic domain; what conditions promote integration (not merely identification); the role of identity-relevant internalization versus value internalization; and how academic institutions can intentionally foster the integration of learning as a dimension of self. Priority: **medium** — especially relevant for higher education contexts.
> *— [[sdt-focused-analysis-2026-03-06_report]]*
