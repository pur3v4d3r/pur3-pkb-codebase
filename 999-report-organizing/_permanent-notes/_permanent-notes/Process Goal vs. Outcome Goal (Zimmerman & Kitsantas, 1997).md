# Process Goal vs. Outcome Goal** *(Zimmerman & Kitsantas, 1997)*

> [!definition] Process Goal vs. Outcome Goal** *(Zimmerman & Kitsantas, 1997)*
> **Definition:** *Process goals* specify the strategies and behaviors to be implemented during learning. *Outcome goals* specify the end-state to be achieved. Both are useful but serve different functions: outcome goals provide direction; process goals protect self-efficacy during difficulty by providing evaluative criteria that remain under learner control.
> **Operational Indicators:** "I will pause at every section break and write a one-sentence summary" is a process goal. "I will understand this framework well enough to explain it" is an outcome goal. During early skill development with challenging material, process goals are more motivationally protective.
> **Cross-References:** [[goal-setting-theory]], [[self-efficacy]], [[deliberate-practice]]
