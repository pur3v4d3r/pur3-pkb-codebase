> [!person] Ernst von Glasersfeld (1917–2010)
> Austrian-born cybernetician and philosopher of education who developed radical constructivism — the most philosophically rigorous and epistemologically demanding variant of constructivism, drawing on von Foerster and Maturana. Von Glasersfeld's influence has been primarily in mathematics education and in philosophical discussions of educational epistemology.
>
> **Lineage:** Influenced by Piaget, Kant, Maturana, and von Foerster. Influenced Richards, Confrey, and mathematics education constructivists.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Radical constructivism is treated as the epistemologically pure endpoint of the constructivist spectrum, helping to map the philosophical range of the family of theories.
> *— [[constructivist-learning-theory-foundational-report-2026-03-31_report]]*

> [!person] Ernst von Glasersfeld (1917–2010)
> **Position:** Austrian-American cyberneticist and epistemologist; primary theorist of radical constructivism
> **Core contribution:** Radical constructivism — the epistemological position that knowledge is always a viability-driven construction rather than a representation of mind-independent reality; the distinction between viable and veridical knowledge.
> **Intellectual lineage:** Influenced by Piaget, Kant, Heinz von Foerster (cybernetics), Giambattista Vico.
> **Key works:** *Radical Constructivism: A Way of Knowing and Learning* (1995); *The Construction of Knowledge* (1987)
> *See also:* [[radical-constructivism]], [[genetic-epistemology]], [[ernst-von-glasersfeld]]
> *— [[constructivist-learning-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
