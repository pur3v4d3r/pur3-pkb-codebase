## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Self-Regulated Learning: Personal Cognitive Load Monitor
> **Purpose:** Apply CLT to your own learning practice.
>
> - [ ] Before starting: Estimate the intrinsic load — how many interacting elements am I trying to learn simultaneously?
> - [ ] If overwhelmed: Break the material into isolated elements; learn components before interactions
> - [ ] Check for self-imposed extraneous load: Am I studying from multiple scattered sources? (→ Consolidate)
> - [ ] Check for redundancy: Am I re-reading familiar material mixed with novel content? (→ Skim the familiar, focus on the novel)
> - [ ] Apply the modality principle: Can I learn some content by listening while viewing diagrams?
> - [ ] Monitor effort: On a 1-9 scale, how much mental effort am I investing right now? (≤3: too easy, increase challenge; ≥8: too hard, reduce interactivity)
> - [ ] Space and interleave: Am I massing practice or distributing it across sessions?
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-12_report]]*
