## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 6 — Process
> **Q:** What are the four phases of interest development according to Hidi and Renninger?
> **A:** (1) Triggered situational interest (external stimulus, often fleeting), (2) Maintained situational interest (sustained engagement through meaningful activity), (3) Emerging individual interest (self-generated engagement that persists without external support), (4) Well-developed individual interest (deep, self-sustaining engagement with stored knowledge and value). Each phase requires different curricular support.
> **Source:** Section 6
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #motivation #interest-theory #engagement
> *— [[curriculum-design-for-lifelong-learning-and-self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 6 — Process
> **Q:** What are the four stages of the Developmental Arc Model?
> **A:** (1) Structured Dependence — full scaffolding; (2) Guided Autonomy — selective fading; (3) Self-Directed Monitoring — independent regulation with occasional scaffold use; (4) Reflective Mastery — learner designs and modifies own scaffolds.
> **Source:** Section 7.4
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
