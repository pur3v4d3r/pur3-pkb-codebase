> [!diagram] Core Argument Structure
> ```
> FOUNDATION PREMISES
> ├── P1: Schemas are active, relational cognitive structures
> │     (Bartlett 1932, Rumelhart 1980)
> ├── P2: Schemas vary in richness; richer schemas = better processing
> │     (Expertise research: Ericsson, Chase & Simon)
> └── P3: External structures can couple with internal cognitive systems
>       (Clark & Chalmers 1998; Sparrow et al. 2011)
>
>         ↓ ↓ ↓ (synthesis)
>
> INTERMEDIATE CLAIM: Schema-Fidelity
> If an external PKB mirrors schema organization → high schema-fidelity
> ↓
> MAIN THESIS: A high schema-fidelity PKB participates in cognitive processing
>             in analogous ways to internal schematic LTM
>
>         ↓ coupling quality matters for this participation
>         ↓
>
> DESIGN CRITERION: Five structural properties for schema-fidelity:
>   1. Relational primacy
>   2. Hierarchical stratification
>   3. Associative density
>   4. Categorical coherence
>   5. Activation pathway design
>
>         ↓ if achieved ↓
>
> PREDICTED OUTCOME: Bidirectional Enrichment Hypothesis
>   INSIDE-OUT: Richer internal schemas → more accurate PKB construction
>   OUTSIDE-IN: PKB building → self-explanation → richer internal schemas
>   FEEDBACK: Compounding generative cycle over time
>
>         ↓ risk ↓
>
> COUNTERFACTUAL: Substitution pathway (Fluency illusion + Sparrow effect)
>   LOW schema-fidelity PKB → outsource content without external schema structure
>   → reduce retrieval effort → weaken encoding → reduce internal schema development
> ```

> [!diagram] Core Argument Structure
> ```
> THESIS: Walking-reading is a dual-task with a small CE cost, a moderate
>         arousal benefit, and specific optimization opportunities.
>
> SUPPORTING CHAIN:
>
> [1] Walking loads CE minimally (~3-8%) when route is habituated
>     ← CPG automaticity (Level 3)
>     ← MRT low-overlap prediction (Level 2)
>     ← Automaticity gradient analysis (Level 2-3)
>
> [2] Walking provides a genuine arousal benefit via catecholamines
>     ← Arnsten inverted-U model (Level 4)
>     ← Walking sits on ascending limb (Level 4)
>     ← Hypofrontality resolved for walking intensity (Level 4)
>
> [3] The dual-task cost becomes meaningful at difficulty thresholds
>     ← CE bottleneck intensifies with material difficulty (Level 2)
>     ← WMC cliff for individual near capacity (Level 5)
>     ← Fluency-comprehension dissociation masks the cost (Level 5)
>
> [4] The post-exercise window creates an exploitable arbitrage
>     ← Catecholamines persist 15-60 min after exercise stops (Level 4)
>     ← Dual-task cost vanishes instantly (Level 2)
>     ← Temporal asymmetry enables walk-then-sit optimization (Level 7)
>
> CONCLUSION: Optimal protocol cycles between walking (priming) and
>             sitting (processing), exploiting the post-exercise
>             catecholamine window for deep processing.
>
> QUALIFICATION: This conclusion is strongest for readers with moderate-to-
>                high WMC, habituated walking routes, and material of
>                variable difficulty. ADHD-spectrum readers may benefit
>                more from sustained walking. Very high-difficulty material
>                readers should sit regardless.
> ```
