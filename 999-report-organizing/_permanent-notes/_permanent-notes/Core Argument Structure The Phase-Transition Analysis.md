> [!diagram] Core Argument Structure: The Phase-Transition Analysis
> ```
> CENTRAL CLAIM: Phase transition quality > phase content quality
>         as determinant of SRL developmental trajectory
>                          │
>         ┌────────────────┼────────────────┐
>         ▼                ▼                ▼
>   EVIDENCE 1:      EVIDENCE 2:      EVIDENCE 3:
>   Expert-novice    Attribution      Monitoring-control
>   differences      retraining       decoupling research
>   concentrated     effects          = transition failure,
>   in self-         on subsequent    not monitoring
>   reflection       forethought      failure
>         │                │                │
>         └────────────────┼────────────────┘
>                          ▼
>              MECHANISM: Attribution → adaptive
>              vs. defensive inference → self-efficacy
>              updating → next forethought quality
>                          │
>               ┌──────────┴──────────┐
>               ▼                     ▼
>        FAILURE MODE:         IMPLICATION:
>        Degenerate cycle      Self-reflection first
>        (no transition        instructional sequence
>        signal)               + calibration engine
>                              framing
>                          │
>                    FAR TRANSFER:
>                    Deliberate practice,
>                    Double-loop learning,
>                    Monitoring-control gap
>                    in project management
> ```
> **Reading Guide:** The argument flows from the central claim through three converging evidence streams to the mechanism analysis, then bifurcates into the failure mode (degenerate cycle) and the implication (self-reflection first instruction + calibration engine framing). The FAR transfer phase shows the structural portability of the central claim.
> *— [[zimmerman-cyclical-srl-focused-analysis-2026-03-23_report]]*
