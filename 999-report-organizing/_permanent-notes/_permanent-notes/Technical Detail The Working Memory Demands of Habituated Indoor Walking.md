## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Technical Detail: The Working Memory Demands of Habituated Indoor Walking
> Self-paced indoor locomotion on a familiar route loads WM components very differently from academic reading:
>
> **[[phonological-loop]]:** Essentially unloaded. Walking generates no verbal or phonological processing demands.
>
> **[[visuospatial-sketchpad]]:** Lightly loaded — and this is the key finding. In a **familiar, obstacle-free** indoor environment, the spatial processing demands of locomotion are minimal. The walker is not navigating, path-planning, or monitoring for hazards. Route selection is automated. The visuospatial demands are limited to basic proprioceptive-spatial coordination (where am I in the room? when to turn around?) — which, in a habituated walker, is handled by largely [[automaticity|automatic]] processes that barely engage the sketchpad.
>
> **[[episodic-buffer]]:** Unloaded. Walking in a familiar room generates no novel binding demands.
>
> **[[central-executive]]:** **This is where the answer pivots.** The CE demands of walking depend critically on the **automaticity** of the walking behavior:
> - For a healthy adult on flat, familiar ground: CE demand is very low. Locomotion is controlled primarily by subcortical motor circuits (spinal pattern generators, cerebellar coordination) with minimal cortical executive involvement. This is a highly [[automaticity|automated]] motor action.
> - For a person on uneven terrain, navigating obstacles, or in an unfamiliar environment: CE demand increases substantially, as the walker must allocate attention to route planning and hazard monitoring.
> - For a person with impaired automaticity (certain neurological conditions, extreme fatigue): CE demand may be significant even on flat ground.
>
> **Net result for indoor habituated walking:** Very low CE demand, minimal visuospatial demand, zero phonological demand. The task runs primarily on automatic motor programs.
> *— [[walking-academic-reading-working-memory-deep-dive-2026-04-13_report]]*
