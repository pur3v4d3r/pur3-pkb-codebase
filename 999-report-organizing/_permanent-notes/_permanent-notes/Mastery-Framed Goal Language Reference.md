> **INSTEAD OF → USE**
>
> "Get through this report" → "Build understanding of [specific mechanism/claim]"
>
> "Understand Section 3" → "Be able to explain [specific aspect of Section 3] in my own words"
>
> "Take good notes" → "Capture [specific type of insight] that connects to [existing PKB node]"
>
> "Remember the main points" → "Be able to generate [N] implications of [central claim] without the text"
>

>
