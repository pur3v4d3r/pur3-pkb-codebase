## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 10 — Connection
> **Q:** What is Philip Candy's key distinction regarding self-direction, and why did it transform the field?
> **A:** Candy distinguished between self-direction as a personal attribute (dispositional capacity) and self-direction as a mode of instructional organisation (educational method). This distinction demonstrated that self-direction is domain-specific and developmental rather than a fixed personality trait, meaning curriculum design can develop self-direction rather than merely accommodating learners who already possess it.
> **Source:** Section 2
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #SDL #Philip-Candy #conceptual-distinction

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 10 — Connection
> **Q:** How does transfer-appropriate processing connect encoding quality to retrieval success?
> **A:** Transfer-appropriate processing (Tulving) holds that retrieval is most successful when the cognitive operations at retrieval match those at encoding. If a note was created through elaborative processing (asking why, generating connections), then retrieval cues invoking those same elaborative processes will be maximally effective. Shallow encoding produces traces that are difficult to retrieve even with good cues.
> **Source:** Sections 2 & 4
> **Difficulty:** Advanced
> **Tags:** #encoding, #retrieval, #transfer-appropriate-processing
> *— [[pkb-pkm-planning-and-cognitive-science-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 10 — Connection
> **Q:** How does SDT's concept of "controlled motivation" differ from behaviorist accounts of externally motivated behavior?
> **A:** Behaviorism treats externally reinforced behavior as a single category. SDT distinguishes between external regulation (behavior governed by rewards/punishment) and introjected regulation (behavior governed by internal pressures like guilt or contingent self-esteem). Both are controlled, but introjection represents partial internalization — the regulation is inside the person but not experienced as self-endorsed.
> **Source:** Section 4
> **Difficulty:** Advanced
> **Tags:** #sdt #controlled-motivation #behaviorism #distinction
