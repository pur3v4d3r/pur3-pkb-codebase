## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Seed 6 — Connection
> **Q:** What is the specificity-autonomy dialectic and why is it the central design tension in PKM goal-setting?
> **A:** Goal-setting theory shows that specific goals enhance performance (through variance reduction and attention focusing), while SDT shows that autonomy enhances motivational quality. These pull in opposite directions: high specificity can undermine autonomy by constraining choice, while high autonomy can sacrifice the direction-giving benefits of specificity. The resolution is to be specific about *what understanding looks like* while preserving autonomy over *how that understanding is pursued*.
> **Source:** Section 2, 6, 8
> **Difficulty:** Advanced

> [!flashcard] Seed 6 — Connection
> **Q:** How does habit formation connect to Cognitive Load Theory's concept of cognitive liberation?
> **A:** Once a behavior is habitual, it executes via the basal ganglia with minimal working-memory consumption, freeing prefrontal resources for higher-order cognitive work. This is the cognitive load liberation mechanism: habitualization reduces intrinsic load for the routine, allowing germane load to be devoted to learning rather than procedural management.

> [!flashcard] Seed 6 — Connection
> **Q:** What is the Calibration-Action Gap, and how does it differ from the monitoring accuracy problem?
> **A:** The monitoring accuracy problem (calibration problem) is the failure of metacognitive judgments to accurately predict actual performance — monitoring is imprecise. The Calibration-Action Gap is the failure of adaptive control to ensue even when monitoring IS accurate — detection of difficulty does not produce appropriate regulatory response. Three mechanisms: regulatory poverty (no alternative strategies available), motivational interruption (difficulty interpreted as incapacity), regulatory overhead (insufficient working memory to execute corrective response).
> **Source:** Phase IV (original contribution)
> **Difficulty:** Advanced
> **Tags:** #calibration-action-gap, #metacognitive-regulation, #monitoring-regulation-decoupling, #regulatory-poverty
