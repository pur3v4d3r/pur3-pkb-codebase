## Additional Material

> [!section-summary] Level 4 Summary: The Dynamics
> At substructure level, schemas appeared as distributed attractor basins or symbolic node networks, organized in hierarchies. At dynamics level, we can now see schemas in motion:
> - **Activation:** Threshold-competition among partially-activated schemas; winner takes dominance via lateral inhibition; priming biases competition
> - **Three modes of change:** Accretion (adding instances), Tuning (adjusting parameters), Restructuring (changing architecture) — each triggered by different prediction error profiles
> - **Consolidation:** Offline (sleep-based) integration is required for schema formation from episodic instances; [[schema-crystallization-event]] is the threshold-crossing into stable neocortical representation
> - **Automation:** Three-stage trajectory from declarative to autonomous; automation produces tacit knowledge inaccessible to introspection
> - **Equilibration:** The self-regulatory drive toward schema-experience balance; vulnerable to false equilibria that impede restructuring
>
> The next level (edge cases) asks: where do these dynamics fail in revealing ways? What happens at the boundary conditions where schemas produce systematic errors?
> *— [[schema-and-how-they-work-deep-dive-2026-04-08_report]]*
