# [[phonological-loop|Phonological Loop]] (Baddeley & Hitch, 1974)

> [!definition] [[phonological-loop|Phonological Loop]] (Baddeley & Hitch, 1974)
> A component of [[working-memory|working memory]] responsible for maintaining phonological traces of verbal information in an active state for approximately 2 seconds, refreshable through subvocal rehearsal. In reading, the phonological loop maintains the phonological trace of sentences to support syntactic parsing across clausal boundaries.
