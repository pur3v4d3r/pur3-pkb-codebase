## Additional Material

> [!person] Rolf Zwaan (b. 1961) & Gabriel Radvansky (b. 1963)
> Zwaan (Erasmus University Rotterdam) and Radvansky (University of Notre Dame) developed the Event Indexing Model of situation model construction. Their work systematically identified the five dimensions (CATSM) along which readers track situational coherence.
>
> **Key Works:** Zwaan, Langston & Graesser (1995), "The construction of situation models in narrative comprehension"; Zwaan & Radvansky (1998), "Situation models in language comprehension and memory."
>
> **Lineage:** Extended van Dijk & Kintsch's framework by specifying the internal structure of the situation model; influenced by research on mental simulation (Barsalou) and mental models (Johnson-Laird).
>
> **Relationship to Report:** The CATSM dimensions framework, the Causal Spine Bottleneck analysis, and the diagnostic use of dimension-specific comprehension failures all build directly on Zwaan & Radvansky.
> *— [[situation-model-construction-focused-analysis-2026-03-29_report]]*
