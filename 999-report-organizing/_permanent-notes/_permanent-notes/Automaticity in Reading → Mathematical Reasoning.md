## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Automaticity in Reading → Mathematical Reasoning
> The principle of automatization liberating attentional bandwidth operates in mathematical reasoning with the same architectural clarity as in reading. A student who must effortfully reconstruct multiplication facts during multi-step problem solving has their working memory consumed by arithmetic retrieval — leaving insufficient resources for the algebraic or logical reasoning that is the actual point of the task. Research on mathematical fluency shows that students with automatized arithmetic facts produce better performance on complex problem solving *even when calculators are permitted*, because the cognitive benefit of automatization is not merely computation time — it is the working memory freed for higher-order mathematical reasoning. The [[schema-automation]] mechanism is identical: frequent exposure automates fact retrieval as schema chunks, enabling higher-order processing to proceed without a resource bottleneck.
> *— [[reading-fluency-automaticity-focused-analysis-2026-03-20_report]]*
