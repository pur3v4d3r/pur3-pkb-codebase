## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Methodological Self-Awareness
> James's acknowledgment of the book's length and his provision of reading strategies reveals a pedagogical consciousness unusual for the era. He understood that he was writing for multiple audiences simultaneously: students encountering psychology for the first time, colleagues in philosophy and medicine who needed technical depth, and educated general readers interested in scientific psychology. This multiplicity of intended audiences explains the text's variable density—some chapters are accessible introductions, others are technically demanding philosophical arguments.
