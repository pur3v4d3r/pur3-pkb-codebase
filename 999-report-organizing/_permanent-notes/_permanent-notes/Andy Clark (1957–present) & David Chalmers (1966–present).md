## Additional Material

> [!person] Andy Clark (1957–present) & David Chalmers (1966–present)
> Clark (University of Sussex) and Chalmers (New York University) co-authored "The Extended Mind" (1998), arguing that cognitive processes extend into the environment when external tools are appropriately coupled to the agent. Their Parity Principle provides the philosophical basis for treating PKBs as genuinely constitutive of metacognitive processing rather than mere external aids. Clark's solo work on cognitive niche construction and natural-born cyborgs extends this framework further.
>
> **Key works referenced:** "The Extended Mind" (1998); Clark, *Supersizing the Mind* (2008); Clark, *Natural-Born Cyborgs* (2003).
>
