## Additional Material

> [!section-summary] Section III Summary
> Metacognitive monitoring produces the information on which learning decisions are based, but internal monitoring signals — EOL, JOL, FOK, comprehension assessments — are systematically noisy. Fluency illusions, domain-level overconfidence, and affective contamination degrade monitoring accuracy. Well-designed PKM systems can externalise monitoring through spaced repetition (recall assessment), knowledge graph analysis (coherence assessment), and retrieval practice (comprehension assessment), each targeting specific failure modes of internal monitoring. The tension between beneficial externalisation and metacognitive dependency represents a key design challenge.
> *— [[metacognition-and-pkm-foundational-report-2026-04-12_report]]*
