# Comprehension Monitoring Vocabulary (Flavell, 1979; Zimmerman, 2002)

> [!definition] Comprehension Monitoring Vocabulary (Flavell, 1979; Zimmerman, 2002)
> **Definition:** The vocabulary used during in-progress reading to characterize one's current comprehension state. The granularity and structural affordances of this vocabulary determine the precision of metacognitive monitoring available to the reader. Coarse-grained vocabulary ("I get it" / "I don't get it") provides binary monitoring access. Fine-grained vocabulary ("I understand the claim structure but haven't yet mapped the evidence," "I'm missing the conceptual bridge between section two and three," "I've grasped the surface argument but the deeper logic is unclear") provides graduated access that enables targeted self-regulatory responses.
>
> **Cross-References:** [[metacognitive-monitoring]], [[silent-reading-fluency]], [[situation-model]], [[self-regulated-learning]]
