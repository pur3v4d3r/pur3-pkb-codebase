## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] The Prior Knowledge — Situation Model Bootstrap Problem
> The Recht and Leslie finding reveals a deep structural problem: situation model construction requires prior knowledge to bridge implicit causal gaps, but readers typically encounter new content precisely *because* they lack prior knowledge of it. This creates a bootstrapping paradox — you need a partial situation model to build a situation model. The practical resolution is not to avoid reading in domains where you lack knowledge, but to *use pre-reading strategies to build the minimum prior knowledge frame* (through survey, prediction-writing, and schema activation) before content reading. This frame does not need to be detailed; it only needs to provide enough causal and structural anchors to prevent the propositions from floating in isolation.
> *— [[situation-model-construction-focused-analysis-2026-03-29_report]]*
