# [[amotivation]]

> [!definition] [[amotivation]]
> The absence of intentional self-regulation — neither intrinsically nor extrinsically motivated. The person either does not see a connection between their actions and outcomes (helplessness), does not value the activity, or does not feel competent to perform it. Amotivation is associated with the worst psychological outcomes of any regulatory position, including greater wellbeing costs than even external regulation. It is not passive; it involves active disengagement that requires energy to sustain.
