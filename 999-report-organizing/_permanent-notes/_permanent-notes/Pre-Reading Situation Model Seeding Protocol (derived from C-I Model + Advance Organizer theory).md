> [!protocol] Pre-Reading Situation Model Seeding Protocol (derived from C-I Model + Advance Organizer theory)
> *Purpose: construct a low-resolution situation model before full-text reading, reducing working memory demands during the reading itself.*
>
> **Step 1 — Scenario Priming (2-3 min):**
> Write one sentence: *What situation, mechanism, event sequence, or domain is this text going to help me understand?* This is a scenario question, not a topic question. It should name the referent world, not the text's subject heading.
>
> **Step 2 — Structural Survey (5-10 min):**
> Read, in order: abstract or introduction, all section headings and subheadings, conclusion. Do not read body paragraphs. Goal: extract the causal skeleton — the main claims and the logical connectives between them.
>
> **Step 3 — Prior Knowledge Activation (2-3 min):**
> Write 3-5 sentences: what you already know about the described situation or domain, including what causes what, what the main mechanisms are, and what the main controversies are. This is your initial situation model.
>
> **Step 4 — Prediction Generation:**
> Write two specific predictions about what the text will argue, demonstrate, or claim. Predictions commit you to an existing situation model that the text will confirm, disconfirm, or complicate.
> *— [[situation-models-text-comprehension-foundational-report-2026-03-29_report]]*
