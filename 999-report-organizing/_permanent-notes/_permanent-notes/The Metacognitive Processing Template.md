## Additional Material

> [!protocol] The Metacognitive Processing Template
> **Purpose:** Scaffold deep processing of source material through structured metacognitive prompts.
>
> **Use when:** Processing a new article, chapter, or concept that requires integration with existing knowledge.
>
> **Template structure:**
> 1. **Pre-processing assessment** (planning regulation):
>    - What do I already know about this topic? (≥3 existing connections)
>    - What am I trying to learn from this? (specific question)
>    - What processing depth does this warrant? (skim / standard / deep)
> 2. **During processing** (monitoring regulation):
>    - Central claim or thesis: [expressed in own words]
>    - Supporting evidence: [key evidence, with quality assessment]
>    - Points of confusion: [explicit confusion markers]
>    - Connections to existing knowledge: [≥2 wiki-links with explanation]
> 3. **Post-processing evaluation** (evaluative regulation):
>    - Can I explain this to someone else without notes? [attempt]
>    - What changed in my understanding? [before/after delta]
>    - What questions remain? [specific, actionable questions]
>    - How confident am I in my understanding? [1-10, with justification]
>
> **Design principle:** Each prompt is a regulatory instruction that activates monitoring processes the practitioner might otherwise skip.
> *— [[metacognition-and-pkm-foundational-report-2026-04-12_report]]*
