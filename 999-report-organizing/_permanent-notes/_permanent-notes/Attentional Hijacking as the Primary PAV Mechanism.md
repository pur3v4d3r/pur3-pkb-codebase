## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Attentional Hijacking as the Primary PAV Mechanism
> The narrowing of attentional scope under avoidance motivation is the most parsimonious explanation for the PAV-deep processing relationship. Deep processing requires sustained, flexible attention to complex material — following an argument, integrating new information with prior knowledge, generating inferences. This requires attentional resources that avoidance motivation systematically redirects toward threat monitoring. The consequence is not reduced effort but *misdirected effort* — effortful attention deployed on self-protective monitoring rather than epistemic exploration. This explains why PAV learners often report high effort while showing shallow processing: effort is real, but its target is wrong. It also explains why the PAV-performance relationship is worse than the effort investment would predict.
> *— [[achievement-goal-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-24_report]]*
