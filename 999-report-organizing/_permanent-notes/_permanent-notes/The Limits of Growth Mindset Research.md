## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Limits of Growth Mindset Research
> Replications of Dweck's growth mindset intervention studies have produced mixed results, with several large-scale studies finding small or negligible effects on academic outcomes, particularly when the original experimental conditions are not closely replicated. Critics argue that brief mindset interventions cannot overcome structural barriers to learning, and that the theoretical distinction between fixed and growth mindset may be less binary in practice than the conceptual framework implies. The metacognitive implications of mindset research remain robust, but growth mindset interventions should not be oversold as panaceas for educational underachievement.
> *— [[metacognition-lifelong-learning-foundational-report-2026-03-05_report]]*
