## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Why This Distinction Matters for Practice
> The depth-versus-time distinction has direct practical implications that run counter to common intuitions. Students who spend more time with material may actually retain it less well if they spend that time at a shallow processing level (re-reading without elaboration). Conversely, a brief but semantically rich engagement — generating an explanation, connecting to prior knowledge, creating an analogy — can produce superior retention despite consuming less time. This is not a paradox in the LOP framework; it is a prediction.
> *— [[levels-of-processing-foundational-report-2026-03-13_report]]*
