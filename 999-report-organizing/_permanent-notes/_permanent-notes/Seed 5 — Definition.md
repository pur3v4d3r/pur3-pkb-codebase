> [!flashcard] Seed 5 — Definition
> **Q:** What is calibration in the metacognitive sense, and what is the predominant direction of miscalibration in the research literature?
> **A:** Calibration is the alignment between subjective confidence and objective accuracy — a well-calibrated learner who expresses 70% confidence should achieve approximately 70% accuracy. The predominant direction of miscalibration is over-confidence: most learners, in most domains, express confidence that exceeds their actual accuracy.
> **Source:** Phase IV
> **Difficulty:** Basic

> [!flashcard] Seed 5 — Definition
> **Q:** What is a Judgment of Learning (JOL), and what is its relationship to metacognitive monitoring accuracy?
> **A:** A JOL is a prospective metacognitive estimate (made during or after study) of the probability that a studied item will be retrievable on a future test. Monitoring accuracy (calibration) is measured by the correspondence between JOLs and actual performance (via gamma coefficient). Delayed JOLs (taken 24 hours after study) are consistently more accurate than immediate JOLs because they better reflect actual long-term retention rather than current fluency.
> **Source:** Phase II, Phase III
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #judgment-of-learning, #metacognitive-monitoring, #calibration, #nelson-narens-model
> *— [[metacognitive-regulation-foundational-report-2026-03-31_report]]*

> [!flashcard] Seed 5 — Definition
> **Q:** What is the Vocabulary-Comprehension Spiral, and what are its two stable equilibria?
>
> **A:** An original synthesis (this report) describing the bidirectional, mutually amplifying feedback between vocabulary knowledge and reading comprehension. Vocabulary knowledge enables text comprehension; comprehension quality determines inferential context richness for vocabulary acquisition; acquisition quality determines future vocabulary representations. Positive equilibrium: high vocab → rich context → deep processing → richer vocab. Negative equilibrium: thin vocab → impoverished context → shallow processing → minimal gain. The negative equilibrium is self-sustaining and requires external intervention (direct instruction, scaffolded reading) to escape.
>
> **Source:** Phase VI

> **Tags:** #vocabulary-comprehension-spiral, #matthew-effect, #dynamical-systems
