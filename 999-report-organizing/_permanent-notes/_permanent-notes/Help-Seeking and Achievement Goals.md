## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Help-Seeking and Achievement Goals
> Ryan and Pintrich (1997) demonstrated that PAV goals were negatively associated with adaptive help-seeking (asking for the understanding needed to master the material) while being positively associated with avoidant help-seeking (avoiding situations where help might be needed, or asking for answers rather than explanation). MAP goals showed the reverse pattern: positive association with adaptive help-seeking. This mechanism has direct instructional implications: classrooms structured to activate PAV goals — through public comparative evaluation, normative grading curves, or ability tracking — may systematically suppress the very behaviour (asking questions, seeking clarification, exposing confusion) that enables learning.
