## Open Threads

> [!open-question] Topics for Future Research and Note Development
> > [!topic-idea] 1. [[Autonomy-Supportive-Parenting]] — Full Developmental Model
> > **Connection to COT:** The primary developmental antecedent of autonomy orientation. A dedicated note would trace the specific parenting practices (choice provision, perspective-taking, informational feedback, minimizing control) through their longitudinal effects on orientation development, including moderators such as child temperament and cultural context.
> > **Priority:** High — directly instantiates COT's developmental claims.
>
> > [!topic-idea] 2. [[contingent-self-esteem]] — Structure, Dynamics, and Consequences
> > **Connection to COT:** Control orientation's characteristic self-esteem pattern. A dedicated note would examine the construct's measurement (Kernis, Deci, Ryan), its relationship to ego-involvement and narcissism, its role in psychological vulnerability, and interventions targeting the shift from contingent to genuine self-esteem.
> > **Priority:** High — one of COT's most consequential predictions.
>
> > [!topic-idea] 3. [[Need-Frustration-Theory]] — From Absence to Active Thwarting
> > **Connection to COT:** The Vansteenkiste & Ryan (2013) distinction between need satisfaction absence and active need frustration has implications for understanding the developmental pathways to control and impersonal orientations. Active frustration (controlling environments, rejecting relationships) drives orientation development more powerfully than passive absence.
> > **Priority:** Medium-High — extends COT's developmental account.
>
> > [!topic-idea] 4. [[motivational-interviewing]] — SDT-Grounded Mechanisms
> > **Connection to COT:** MI's effectiveness can be understood through COT as an orientation-development intervention. A dedicated note would trace the specific mechanisms through which MI techniques support the shift from control or impersonal orientation toward more autonomous functioning.
> > **Priority:** Medium — important applied connection.
>
> > [!topic-idea] 5. [[Cross-Cultural-Motivation-Research]] — SDT's Universality Claims
> > **Connection to COT:** The cultural boundary conditions of COT's three-orientation structure require dedicated exploration. This note would examine the evidence for and against universality, the role of cultural self-construal, and proposals for culturally contextualizing SDT constructs.
> > **Priority:** Medium — methodologically and theoretically important.
>
> > [!topic-idea] 6. [[Motivational-Affordance-Theory]] — From Ecological to Motivational Psychology
> > **Connection to COT:** The affordance concept, borrowed from Gibson's ecological psychology, provides COT's central mechanism but has not been fully elaborated as an independent construct. A dedicated note would trace the concept from ecological perception through motivational psychology and examine its utility as a general framework for understanding individual differences in environmental perception.
> > **Priority:** Medium — theoretical enrichment.
>
> > [!topic-idea] 7. [[Ego-Depletion-and-Self-Regulation]] — SDT's Alternative Account
> > **Connection to COT:** The ego depletion controversy intersects with SDT's treatment of self-regulation. SDT proposes that autonomous regulation is less depleting than controlled regulation — a claim with direct implications for how orientation profiles influence self-regulatory capacity and vulnerability to depletion.
> > **Priority:** Medium — connects COT to hot-topic self-regulation debates.
> *— [[causality-orientations-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-01_report]]*
