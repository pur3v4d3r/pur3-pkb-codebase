## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Cultural Autonomy Debate
> The claim that autonomy is a universal basic psychological need is the most contested element of SDT's universality thesis. Cross-cultural research by [[Chirkov et al. (2003)]] and others shows that autonomy support predicts internalization and well-being even in collectivist contexts, supporting universality at the level of need function. But critics note that the very salience and conscious valuation of autonomy varies systematically across cultures, that the measurement instruments were developed in WEIRD contexts and may not translate perfectly, and that the expression of autonomy through harmony and social role fulfilment in collectivist cultures may be conceptually distinct from individualistic autonomy in ways SDT has not fully theorised.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-foundational-report-2026-03-11_report]]*
