## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] 4. [[humanistic-psychology]] and SDT's Intellectual Roots
> **Connection**: SDT emerged partly from the humanistic tradition (Rogers, Maslow) while subjecting that tradition's claims to empirical scrutiny. Understanding SDT's relationship to humanistic psychology illuminates both what SDT inherits (the organismic growth tendency, unconditional positive regard, the distinction between being and having) and what it modifies (replacing Maslow's hierarchy with three universal non-hierarchical needs; subjecting Rogers' clinical propositions to experimental test).
> **Depth Potential**: Intellectual history note tracing the SDT-humanistic lineage.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-01 (1)]]*
