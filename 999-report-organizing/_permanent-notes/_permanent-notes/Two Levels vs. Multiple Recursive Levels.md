## Open Threads

> [!tension-identified] Two Levels vs. Multiple Recursive Levels
> **The tension:** Is the meta-level/object-level distinction a two-level architecture, or are multiple recursive meta-levels needed? **Position A:** Nelson and Narrens argued that two levels suffice for the phenomena they wished to explain. **Position B:** Pronevich (2006) and others have argued that meta-metacognition (monitoring one's own monitoring) requires at least three levels. **Current evidence:** Empirical evidence for distinct meta-meta-level processes is limited, but the theoretical argument for recursive levels is compelling. **This report's stance:** Two levels are sufficient for most empirical purposes, but the model should remain open to recursive extension.
> *— [[nelson-narrens-metacognitive-model-foundational-report-2026-04-16_report]]*
