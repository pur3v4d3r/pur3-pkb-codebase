## Additional Material

> [!person] Richard Larson (b. 1941)
> American astrophysicist. Larson performed the first detailed numerical simulations of protostellar collapse (1969) and established the empirical scaling relations (Larson's Laws, 1981) that describe the relationships between size, velocity dispersion, and density in molecular clouds.
>
> **Relationship to other figures:** Larson's collapse simulations preceded and motivated Shu's analytical inside-out model. His scaling relations remain a touchstone for both observational and theoretical work.
>
> **Key works:** *Numerical Calculations of the Dynamics of Collapsing Proto-Star* (1969); *Turbulence and Star Formation in Molecular Clouds* (1981).
> *— [[how-stars-are-created-in-the-universe-foundational-report-2026-04-17_report]]*
