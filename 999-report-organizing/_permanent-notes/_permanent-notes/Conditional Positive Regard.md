# Conditional Positive Regard

> [!definition] Conditional Positive Regard
> The relational pattern in which parental or significant other approval, warmth, and affection are conveyed as contingent on the child's performance, compliance, or achievements. Associated with development of introjected regulatory style through the development of contingent self-worth. Distinguished from unconditional positive regard ([[Unconditional-Positive-Regard]]), which separates care for the person from evaluations of their behavior and supports authentic self-development.
