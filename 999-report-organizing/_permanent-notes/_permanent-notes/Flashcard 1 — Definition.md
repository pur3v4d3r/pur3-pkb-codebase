> [!flashcard] Flashcard 1 — Definition
> **Q:** How did Flavell (1976) define metacognition, and what are its two major components as distinguished by Brown (1987)?
> **A:** Flavell defined metacognition as "cognition about cognition" — thinking about one's own thinking processes. Brown distinguished two components: *knowledge of cognition* (awareness of cognitive processes, strategies, and states — including declarative, procedural, and conditional knowledge) and *regulation of cognition* (executive control including planning, monitoring, and evaluating).
