## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Seed 4 — Theory Connection
> **Q:** How does levels-of-processing theory explain EI's effectiveness? What specifically does EI guarantee that passive re-reading does not?
> **A:** LOP theory holds that encoding durability is a function of semantic depth and elaborative richness. EI structurally guarantees semantic-level processing: generating a why-answer necessarily engages with relational, causal, meaningful features of the proposition. Passive re-reading can remain at the surface-feature level, producing the fluency illusion without deep encoding.
