# Self-Efficacy (Bandura, 1977; 1997)

> [!definition] Self-Efficacy (Bandura, 1977; 1997)
> Domain-specific beliefs about one's capacity to successfully execute the behaviors required to produce a given outcome. NOT equivalent to general confidence or self-esteem. Self-efficacy is the most consistently powerful motivational predictor of SRL engagement, governing both threshold for monitoring sensitivity and persistence against resistance. Dynamically updated through mastery experiences, vicarious learning, social persuasion, and physiological/affective states.
