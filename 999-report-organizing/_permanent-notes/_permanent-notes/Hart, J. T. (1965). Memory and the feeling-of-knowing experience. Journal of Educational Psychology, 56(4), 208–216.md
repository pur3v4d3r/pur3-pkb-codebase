## Methodology & Sources

> [!cite] Hart, J. T. (1965). Memory and the feeling-of-knowing experience. *Journal of Educational Psychology*, 56(4), 208–216.
> Hart's pioneering study provided the first experimental demonstration that feeling-of-knowing judgments have above-chance predictive validity for subsequent recognition performance. This finding established the empirical phenomenon that would become central to the Nelson-Narens framework's analysis of monitoring.
> *— [[nelson-narens-metacognitive-control-framework-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!cite] Hart, J. T. (1965). Memory and the feeling-of-knowing experience. *Journal of Educational Psychology*, 56(4), 208–216.
> The first systematic experimental study of feeling-of-knowing, demonstrating that FOK judgments predict subsequent recognition performance at above-chance levels. Establishes the empirical foundation for the FOK component of the Nelson–Narrens taxonomy.
> *— [[nelson-narrens-metacognitive-model-foundational-report-2026-04-16_report]]*
