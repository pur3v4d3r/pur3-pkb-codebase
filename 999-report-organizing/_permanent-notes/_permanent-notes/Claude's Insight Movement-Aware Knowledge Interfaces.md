## Reflections

> [!claude-insight] Claude's Insight: Movement-Aware Knowledge Interfaces
> The walking reader's practice anticipates a future direction in knowledge work technology: **movement-aware reading interfaces.** Consider a system that:
>
> 1. **Tracks walking vs. sitting state** via phone accelerometer
> 2. **Adjusts reading difficulty** of presented material: more accessible summaries during walking, full-complexity text during sitting
> 3. **Prompts transition cues:** "Comprehension-critical section ahead — consider transitioning to seated reading" based on material difficulty analysis
> 4. **Integrates note-making prompts** that appear when the reader sits down, referencing the material just read during walking
> 5. **Logs walking-reading vs. sitting-reading comprehension** over time to calibrate the individual's specific dual-task cost profile
>
> This kind of interface doesn't exist yet, but the cognitive science documented in this Deep Dive provides the design rationale for it. Each feature addresses a specific mechanism: (1) exploits the post-exercise window, (2-3) manages the difficulty-ceiling switching problem, (4) bridges the walk-read → sit-process transition, (5) enables personalized optimization.
> *— [[walking-academic-reading-working-memory-deep-dive-2026-04-13_report]]*
