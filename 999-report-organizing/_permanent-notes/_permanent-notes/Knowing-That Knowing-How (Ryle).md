# Knowing-That / Knowing-How (Ryle)

> [!definition] Knowing-That / Knowing-How (Ryle)
> Gilbert Ryle's distinction between two types of knowledge: *knowing-that* (propositional knowledge: knowing that Paris is the capital of France) and *knowing-how* (procedural knowledge: knowing how to ride a bicycle). Ryle argued that knowing-how is not reducible to knowing-that — you cannot derive skilled performance from a set of propositions, however complete. This distinction is directly relevant to the tacit knowledge problem: expert skill represents knowing-how that cannot be converted into knowing-that without loss.
