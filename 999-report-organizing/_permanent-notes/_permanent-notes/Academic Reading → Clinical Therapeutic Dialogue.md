> [!cross-domain-connection] Academic Reading → Clinical Therapeutic Dialogue
> This structural principle appears with striking clarity in the therapeutic effectiveness literature. The transition from problem-saturated descriptions ("I am an anxious person") to process-descriptions ("I experience anxiety in these specific contexts") is a core mechanism in narrative therapy and cognitive-behavioral frameworks. Both encode the same structural shift: from trait-stable-uncontrollable to contextual-unstable-potentially-controllable. The [[Attribution-Retraining-Interventions]] evidence and the cognitive-behavioral evidence are structural analogues — different domains, same dimensional reassignment principle. The transfer insight: any domain where self-explanation language is used (therapy, coaching, performance review, peer feedback) is subject to the same vocabulary-modifiability principle.
> *— [[inner-speech-attribution-self-efficacy-focused-analysis-2026-03-25_report]]*
