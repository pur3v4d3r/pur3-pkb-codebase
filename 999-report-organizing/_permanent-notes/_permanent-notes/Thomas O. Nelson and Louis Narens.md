## Additional Material

> [!person] Thomas O. Nelson and Louis Narens
> Thomas Nelson (University of Maryland) and Louis Narens (UC Irvine) developed the formal monitoring-control framework that provides the theoretical backbone for contemporary metacognitive research. Their framework — distinguishing meta-level from object-level processes, monitoring as upward information flow, control as downward information flow — formalized Flavell's earlier more descriptive account into a testable computational architecture. Their 1990 *Psychological Review* paper remains the most influential formal theoretical statement of the metacognitive architecture.
> *— [[metacognitive-development-foundational-report-2026-04-09_report]]*
