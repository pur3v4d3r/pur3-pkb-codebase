# Schema (Piaget, elaborated by Bartlett and Rumelhart)

> [!definition] Schema (Piaget, elaborated by Bartlett and Rumelhart)
> A cognitive structure that organises prior knowledge and experience into a generalised, flexible template that guides the encoding and interpretation of new information. Schemas operate largely below conscious awareness, shaping what is perceived, encoded, and recalled. Expert advantage in memory tasks for domain-specific material is explained by the richer and more hierarchically organised schema networks that domain expertise develops.
>
> **Boundary:** Schemas are not simply categories or storage bins. They are active interpretive structures that fill in missing information from expectations, which is why eyewitness testimony is unreliable and why experts sometimes fail to notice deep structural changes in familiar material (change blindness in expertise).
>
> **See also:** [[schema-theory]], [[chunking]], [[mental-models]], [[long-term-memory]]
