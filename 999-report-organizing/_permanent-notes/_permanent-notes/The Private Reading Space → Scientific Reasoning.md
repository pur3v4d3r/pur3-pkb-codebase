## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] The Private Reading Space → Scientific Reasoning
> The cognitive conditions that private silent reading creates map with surprising precision onto the conditions that produce high-quality scientific reasoning in research contexts. Kahneman (2011) and colleagues working in the dual-process tradition have documented that evaluative, critical thinking is most likely to emerge when the thinker is not under time pressure, is not observed by others, and has sufficient working memory capacity available. These are precisely the conditions of private, self-paced silent reading. The implication is that the cognitive science of critical thinking — developed in the context of logical reasoning and decision-making research — actually provides a deeper account of *why* silent reading enables critical analysis than the reading research literature itself does.
> *— [[silent-reading-critical-analysis-focused-analysis-2026-03-21_report]]*
