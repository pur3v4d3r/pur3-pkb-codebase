## Additional Material

> [!person] Teun A. van Dijk (b. 1943) & Walter Kintsch (b. 1932)
> Van Dijk (University of Amsterdam) and Kintsch (University of Colorado) are the co-developers of the Construction-Integration model and the textbase/situation model distinction. Van Dijk's early work focused on discourse grammar and macro-structural analysis; Kintsch brought computational modeling and experimental psychology. Their collaboration produced *Strategies in Discourse Comprehension* (1983), the foundational text for the three-level architecture analyzed in this report.
>
> **Key Works:** van Dijk & Kintsch (1983), *Strategies in Discourse Comprehension*; Kintsch (1988), "The Role of Knowledge in Discourse Comprehension: A Construction-Integration Model"; Kintsch (1998), *Comprehension: A Paradigm for Cognition*.
>
> **Lineage:** Built on Bartlett's schema theory (1932), Fillmore's case grammar (1968), and propositional theories of meaning. Their work was extended by Zwaan & Radvansky (situation model dimensions), Graesser et al. (inference generation), and Mayer (multimedia learning).
>
> **Relationship to Report:** The three-level architecture (surface code/textbase/situation model) and the Construction-Integration model are the primary theoretical framework for this entire analysis.
