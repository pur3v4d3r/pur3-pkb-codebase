## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Transfer Domain 2: Leadership Communication under Organizational Conditions
> **Structural principle:** Organizational communication layers on top of interpersonal communication dynamics additional complexity: hierarchical power differentials that amplify the threat dimension (leaders' casual evaluative comments carry disproportionate weight); institutional pressures that constrain message authenticity; and audiences large enough that common ground cannot be individually calibrated.
>
> The most critical transfer: leaders whose messages produce compliance-without-understanding (technically "received" but not genuinely comprehended) have failed at the grounding requirement. The Curse of Knowledge bias is particularly dangerous in leadership — experienced leaders forget that new organizational members, new employees, or external stakeholders do not share their implicit institutional knowledge.
>
> Gottman's Four Horsemen apply with modification to organizational leadership: criticism-as-character-attack, contempt-toward-subordinates, and stonewalling-as-withdrawal from feedback are documented patterns in toxic leadership. The communication antidotes (specific complaint, appreciation-culture, taking responsibility) are directly applicable.
>
