## Additional Material

> [!person] Richard deCharms (1927–2010)
> Educational psychologist who developed the origin-pawn concept (1968) — the phenomenological distinction between experiencing oneself as the source versus the object of behavioral regulation. deCharms's work directly inspired Deci and Ryan's Perceived Locus of Causality construct and their attention to the experiential quality of motivation rather than merely its quantity.
> *— [[cognitive-evaluation-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-01_report]]*

> [!person] Richard deCharms (1927–2010)
> Washington University in St. Louis. Developed the Origin-Pawn distinction — the experience of being an origin of one's own behavior versus a pawn moved by external forces — which directly prefigured SDT's concept of perceived locus of causality and the autonomy-control distinction.
>
> **Relationship to other figures:** Directly influenced Deci's early work; the Origin-Pawn framework was integrated into CET.
>
> **Key works:** deCharms (1968), *Personal Causation*.
