## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Frontier Question: Can Monitoring Accuracy Be Formally Modeled?
> **The question:** Can we build computational models that predict monitoring accuracy from first principles — models that specify the monitoring mechanism formally enough to generate quantitative predictions about when monitoring will be accurate and when it will fail?
> **Current best understanding:** Several formal frameworks exist, but none has achieved consensus as the standard model. The field is in a pre-paradigmatic phase of computational modeling.
> **What we don't know:** Whether monitoring accuracy is best modeled as signal detection, Bayesian inference, heuristic computation, or associative activation — and whether different monitoring situations require different formal models.
> **Active research directions:** Three primary computational frameworks are under active development.
> **Predicted resolution timeline:** 10-15 years for mature computational models; the measurement complexity of metacognition slows progress relative to simpler cognitive phenomena.
> **What would change if resolved:** Quantitative prediction of monitoring failures, principled design of calibration interventions, and integration of metacognition into [[Computational-Modeling-of-Metacognitive-Control-uses-cognitive-architectures-lik|computational cognitive architectures]].
