# The Fluency Illusion** *(Bjork, 1994; Koriat & Bjork, 2005)*

> [!definition] The Fluency Illusion** *(Bjork, 1994; Koriat & Bjork, 2005)*
> **Definition:** The systematic misperception that familiarity with information constitutes understanding of it. When reading, the subjective experience of "getting it" is strongly influenced by prior exposure to vocabulary and concepts, even when genuine comprehension is absent. This causes systematic overconfidence in comprehension assessments that do not use retrieval-based testing.
> **Operational Indicators:** The Fluency Illusion is active when a learner reports high comprehension confidence immediately after reading but cannot reconstruct core claims from memory without consulting the text. The Cold Reconstruction protocol specifically circumvents this illusion.
> **Cross-References:** [[fluency-illusion]], [[metacognitive-calibration]], [[the-fluency-trap]], [[the-fluency-metacognition-paradox]]
