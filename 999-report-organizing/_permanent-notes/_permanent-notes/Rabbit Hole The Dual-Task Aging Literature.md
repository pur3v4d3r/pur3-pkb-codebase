## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Rabbit Hole: The Dual-Task Aging Literature
> **What it is:** A substantial literature examines walking-while-performing-cognitive-tasks in older adults, where dual-task costs are larger due to age-related declines in both walking automaticity and executive function. This literature provides the most detailed empirical examination of walking-cognition dual-task dynamics available.
> **Why follow it:** The aging dual-task literature uses paradigms almost identical to walking-while-reading and contains the most precise measurements of dual-task costs available. While the absolute cost magnitudes are higher in older adults, the **mechanistic patterns** (which subsystems compete, how automaticity moderates costs, how task difficulty interacts) are directly informative for younger adults.
> **Time investment:** Moderate — large but well-reviewed literature.
> **Where to start:** Woollacott & Shumway-Cook (2002), "Attention and the control of posture and gait: a review of an emerging area of research"; Yogev-Seligmann et al. (2008)
> **See also:** [[Cognitive-Aging]], [[Dual-Task-Paradigm]], [[executive-function]]
