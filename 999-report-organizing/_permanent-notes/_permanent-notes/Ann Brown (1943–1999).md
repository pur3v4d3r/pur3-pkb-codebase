## Additional Material

> [!person] Ann Brown (1943–1999)
> University of California, Berkeley. Distinguished between knowledge of cognition and regulation of cognition — a distinction that operationalised metacognition for educational research and became the standard framework for intervention design. Pioneered reciprocal teaching as a metacognitive instructional strategy.
>
> **Relationship to other figures:** Complemented Flavell's taxonomy with an emphasis on executive regulation. Her work directly influenced Schraw's tripartite model and Zimmerman's SRL framework.
>
> **Key works:** Brown, A. L. (1987). Metacognition, executive control, self-regulation, and other more mysterious mechanisms. In F. E. Weinert & R. H. Kluwe (Eds.), *Metacognition, Motivation, and Understanding*.
>
> **See also:** [[ann-brown]], [[knowledge-of-cognition]], [[regulation-of-cognition]]
> *— [[metacognition-and-pkm-foundational-report-2026-04-12_report]]*

> [!person] Ann Brown (1943–1999)
> Developmental and educational psychologist, professor at University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign and later at University of California, Berkeley. Brown's contributions to metacognition research span 1975–1997, with major theoretical papers in 1978 ("Knowing when, where, and how to remember") and 1987 ("Metacognition, executive control, self-regulation, and other more mysterious mechanisms"). Also known for the Community of Learners instructional model and reciprocal teaching.
>
> **Lineage:** Influenced by Vygotsky's zone-of-proximal development framework; influenced subsequent educational psychology research on strategy instruction and metacognitive training.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Provides the foundational separation of metacognitive knowledge from metacognitive regulation and the executive control framing that organizes Phase II.
> *— [[metacognitive-regulation-foundational-report-2026-03-31_report]]*

> [!person] Ann Brown (1943–1999)
> Developmental and educational psychologist, University of Illinois and UC Berkeley. Brown's 1978 and 1987 papers established the knowledge-regulation distinction and elevated regulation to co-equal status. Her empirical program demonstrated the production deficiency phenomenon in children's strategic cognition, and her applied work developed reciprocal teaching as a metacognitive scaffolding intervention.
>
> **Lineage:** Influenced by Vygotsky's sociocultural theory; collaborated with Palincsar on reciprocal teaching; influenced Schraw, Pressley, and subsequent strategy instruction research.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Provides the executive/regulatory framing of metacognition and the empirical foundation for understanding regulatory failure as distinct from knowledge failure.
> *— [[metacognitive-regulation-foundational-report-2026-03-31_report]]*
