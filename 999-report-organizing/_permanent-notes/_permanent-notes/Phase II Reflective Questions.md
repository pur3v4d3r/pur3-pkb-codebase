## Reflections

> [!reflection] Phase II Reflective Questions
> 1. How does the construct of "functional significance" challenge the standard assumption that rewards simply increase motivation? What does it require us to believe about how individuals process their own motivational states?
> 2. The distinction between perceived locus of causality and locus of control is technical but consequential. What practical difference would it make if a designer confused the two — for example, in designing a feedback system?
> 3. CET posits that even intrapersonal events (ego involvement) can undermine intrinsic motivation. What does this imply about the relationship between perfectionism, self-imposed standards, and the quality of engagement in a domain?
> *— [[cognitive-evaluation-theory-foundational-report-2026-03-31_report]]*
