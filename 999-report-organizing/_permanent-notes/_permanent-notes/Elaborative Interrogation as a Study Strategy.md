## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Elaborative Interrogation as a Study Strategy
> The elaborative interrogation strategy asks: "Why is this true?" and "How does this fit with what I already know?" Compared to re-reading and summarization, elaborative interrogation while studying produces substantially stronger memory for factual material, particularly for learners who have sufficient prior knowledge to generate meaningful elaborations. The schema-theoretic explanation: elaboration creates new associative links between the new information and existing schemas, providing multiple retrieval routes and deeper integration into the knowledge structure. Students with poor prior knowledge schemas generate fewer meaningful elaborations and benefit less — further evidence of the foundational role of background knowledge in learning.
> *— [[schema-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
