## Additional Material

> [!person] Christopher Wolters (active 1990s–present)
> **Core Contribution:** Developed the primary empirical research program on motivational regulation, providing the most direct operationalization and evidence for Pintrich's motivational area. Created a taxonomy of motivational regulation strategies and developed scales for measuring them.
>
> **Relationship to Other Figures/Frameworks:** Worked within Pintrich's theoretical framework at the University of Michigan. His 2003 paper on motivational regulation is the key empirical reference for the motivational regulation component of Pintrich's model.
>
> **Key Works:** *Regulation of motivation: Contextual and social aspects* (2003); *Advancing achievement goal theory: Using goal structures and goal orientations to predict student motivation, cognition, and achievement* (2004).
>
> **Relevance to This Analysis:** Wolters' work provides the best evidence for and the best taxonomy of motivational regulation strategies — directly relevant to Phase III and Phase V.
> *— [[pintrichs-framework-srl-focused-analysis-2026-03-23_report]]*
