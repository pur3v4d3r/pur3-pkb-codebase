## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Asymmetry Emerges
> A critical dialectical turn has occurred. The Respondent has conceded that the explanation-test functions as a *defeater* — a way of detecting the *absence* of understanding — rather than as a *confirmer* of understanding's presence. This is a significant retreat from the original thesis, which claimed explanation was both necessary and sufficient. The test now plays a negative, eliminative role rather than a positive, constitutive one. This is epistemologically important: it means we have good tools for detecting misunderstanding but that the positive concept of understanding remains underspecified.
> *— [[what-does-it-mean-to-truley-understand-something-2026-03-10_report]]*
