## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Frontier Question 5: AI-Mediated Need Support — Can Artificial Agents Provide the Conditions for Autonomous Internalization?
> **The question:** As AI systems increasingly mediate educational, therapeutic, professional, and social interactions, can they provide the conditions of need support that the SDT framework specifies as necessary for autonomous internalization? And if so, under what design conditions, and with what limitations relative to the human-mediated need support the framework was developed to characterize?
> **Current best understanding:** Very preliminary. Initial work on AI tutoring systems, AI-assisted therapy, and AI coaching has begun to apply SDT analytic frameworks, with mixed early results. The relatedness need raises particular questions: can interactions with AI systems satisfy relatedness in the way human relationships do, or does relatedness specifically require the perception that the other is a conscious being who genuinely cares about one?
> **What we don't know:** Whether AI systems can produce the conditions for genuine autonomous internalization, or whether they at best produce identified-looking regulations that lack the integrative depth of human-mediated internalization; whether the answer depends on the AI's design (more or less autonomy-supportive interaction patterns) or on something more fundamental about the nature of the relational substrate.
> **Active research directions:** Application of [[Autonomy-Support-in-Digital-and-AI-Mediated-Learning-Environments|autonomy support frameworks to AI-mediated environments]]; experimental studies of AI tutor design effects on motivational outcomes; theoretical work on whether the relatedness need can be satisfied by non-conscious agents.
> **Predicted resolution timeline:** This frontier is the youngest of the five and is likely to remain active for at least a decade. The underlying technology is changing rapidly, which both creates and complicates the empirical questions.
> **What would change if resolved:** Substantial implications for educational technology, mental health technology, workplace learning, and any domain in which AI systems are being deployed to support behavior change or learning.
