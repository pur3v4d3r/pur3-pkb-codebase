## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Before-Study Metacognitive Activation Checklist
> Before beginning a serious learning session, check:
> - [ ] Do I have a clear learning goal (what I want to be able to do after this session)?
> - [ ] Do I know what strategy I will use, and why this strategy is appropriate for this goal?
> - [ ] Have I done a predicting/retrieval step first (what do I already know about this)?
> - [ ] Have I identified the aspects of this material I find most confusing or uncertain?
> - [ ] Do I have a way to test understanding during the session, not just at the end?
> - [ ] Am I allowing for productive difficulty, or am I seeking fluent comfort?
> *— [[metacognitive-skills-foundational-report-2026-04-09_report]]*
