## Additional Material

> [!person] Elizabeth Loftus (1944–)
> American cognitive psychologist (University of California, Irvine) who established the misinformation effect through landmark eyewitness testimony research. Her work demonstrated that post-event leading questions alter memory in ways indistinguishable from genuine experience by the subject, and that high-confidence false memories can be implanted for events that never occurred. Loftus has been expert witness in hundreds of legal cases and her research directly influenced police interviewing protocols (the Cognitive Interview). Her work connects laboratory memory science to legal, clinical, and policy contexts. Related figures: [[frederic-bartlett]], [[Marcia-Johnson]].
