## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Malleable Infrastructure Model
> What the evidence suggests about the stability-malleability question is a *malleable infrastructure* model: individuals have dispositional tendencies toward particular goal orientations that are shaped by prior achievement history, implicit theories of intelligence, and parenting and teaching style — but these dispositions are continuously updated by environmental signals. This explains both the individual difference evidence (people differ predictably in their baseline orientations) and the motivational climate evidence (environments powerfully shift goal distributions). The practical implication: both individual-level intervention (e.g., growth mindset interventions that shift implicit theories of intelligence) and environment-level intervention (motivational climate design) are valid levers, and they operate through partially independent pathways.
> *— [[achievement-goal-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-24_report]]*
