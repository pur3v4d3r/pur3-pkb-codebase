# Motivational-Regulatory Asymmetry

> [!definition] Motivational-Regulatory Asymmetry
> An original synthesis proposed in this analysis: the claim that cognitive regulation requires functional motivational architecture as a necessary precondition, but not vice versa. Motivational dysregulation cascades into cognitive regulatory failure (fragile strategy use, degraded monitoring quality, maladaptive persistence); cognitive strategy limitation does not equivalently cascade into motivational dysregulation. This asymmetry implies that motivational architecture is the more foundational of the two regulatory domains for sustained deep engagement with challenging learning tasks.
