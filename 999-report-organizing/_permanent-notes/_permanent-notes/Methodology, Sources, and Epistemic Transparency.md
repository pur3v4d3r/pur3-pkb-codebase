## Methodology & Sources

> [!methodology-and-sources] Methodology, Sources, and Epistemic Transparency
> **Disciplines Synthesised:** This report synthesises four intellectual traditions: (1) cognitive psychology (Baddeley-Hitch working memory model, CLT, schema theory), (2) philosophy of mind (extended mind thesis, functionalism, distributed cognition), (3) cognitive science of embodiment (epistemic actions, interactivity), and (4) educational psychology (desirable difficulties, spaced repetition, generative processing). Integration across these traditions is interpretive and carries the standard risks of cross-disciplinary synthesis — primarily the risk that findings from carefully controlled laboratory conditions are over-applied to the complexly situated practice of PKM.
>
> **Claim Type Taxonomy:**
>
> | Claim Type | Epistemic Status | Example |
> |------------|-----------------|---------|
> | Working memory architecture descriptions | Established — well-replicated empirical finding | "Working memory has a capacity of ~4 chunks" |
> | Cognitive Load Theory distinctions | Established — substantial empirical basis | "Extraneous load reduces germane processing" |
> | Extended Mind Thesis arguments | Well-motivated philosophical framework | "PKB can constitute extended cognition when coupling conditions are met" |
> | Design principles (this report) | Well-motivated practical derivations | "The Extraneous-First Principle for PKM design" |
> | Cognitive Economy of PKM Maturity | Speculative original synthesis | "Phase transition in PKB utility above integration density threshold" |
> | Offloading Fidelity Principle | Novel construct — motivates but does not verify | "High-fidelity external representations reduce translation overhead" |
>
> **Limitations:**
> - All design principles are derived, not empirically tested in PKM contexts. There is very limited empirical research directly studying how PKM system design variables affect practitioner cognition.
> - Individual differences in working memory capacity, prior knowledge, and personality substantially affect which design principles matter most for any given practitioner.
> - The extended mind coupling conditions remain philosophically contested; their operationalisation for empirical research is not yet resolved.
