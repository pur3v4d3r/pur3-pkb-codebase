## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] The Dissemination Gap as a Structural Problem in Applied Science
> The gap between the moderator-conditional technical finding and the categorical popular or policy transmission is not unique to SDT. Stereotype threat effects, growth mindset interventions, ego-depletion, power poses, and many other behavioral science findings have followed the same pattern: nuanced, moderator-conditional effects in careful research; categorical claims in popular dissemination; and eventually a "replication crisis" that is partly a crisis of the categorical version, not necessarily of the conditional version. SDT's undermining effect is an instance of a general structural problem in the science-to-application translation pipeline. The solution is not more careful research — the careful research already exists. It is a different model of dissemination that preserves and transmits the moderator structure rather than collapsing it.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-19_report]]*
