> The largest early meta-analysis, synthesizing 135 studies and reporting a mean effect size of d ≈ 0.44. Often cited as the definitive demonstration of organizer effectiveness, but the definitional heterogeneity problem applies — many included studies used materials that do not meet Ausubel's criteria.
