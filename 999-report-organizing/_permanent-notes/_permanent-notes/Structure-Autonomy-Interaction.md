# Structure-Autonomy-Interaction

> [!definition] Structure-Autonomy-Interaction
> A critical but frequently misunderstood SDT principle: structure and autonomy support are orthogonal dimensions, not opposites. High structure + high autonomy support is optimal (structured autonomy supporting environment). High structure + low autonomy support is controlling. Low structure + high autonomy support may support autonomy but frustrates competence through lack of guidance. Low structure + low autonomy support is neglecting/amotivating.
