# Organismic Integration Tendency** (Deci & Ryan)

> [!definition] Organismic Integration Tendency** (Deci & Ryan)
> The natural growth orientation of the human organism — the intrinsic propensity toward integration, coherence, and expanding competence that the organismic meta-theory posits as the fundamental developmental force in human psychology. BPNT claims that this tendency is *expressed* under need-supportive conditions and *suppressed* (producing defensive adaptation) under need-frustrating conditions, but is never fully extinguished — providing the theoretical basis for the possibility of later developmental repair after early need frustration. **See also:** [[organismic-meta-theory]], [[Self-Organisation]], [[Actualisation-Tendency]]
