# Mentalization

> [!definition] Mentalization
> The capacity to understand behavior — one's own and others' — in terms of underlying mental states: thoughts, feelings, beliefs, wishes, and intentions. Developed theoretically by Peter Fonagy and Anthony Bateman, mentalization is understood as a developmental achievement that requires a sufficiently secure relational environment to emerge robustly. Failures of mentalization — defaulting to concrete, non-reflective, or hyperactivated modes of experiencing — are central to severe personality disorder, attachment disorganization, and trauma. Mentalization-Based Treatment (MBT) explicitly targets the restoration of flexible mentalizing as the primary therapeutic goal, with the therapeutic relationship serving as the context in which mentalizing capacity can be safely practiced and gradually restored.
