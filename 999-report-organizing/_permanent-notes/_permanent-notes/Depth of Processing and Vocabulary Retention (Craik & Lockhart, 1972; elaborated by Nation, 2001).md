## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Depth of Processing and Vocabulary Retention (Craik & Lockhart, 1972; elaborated by Nation, 2001)
> The richness and durability of a lexical memory trace is a function of the depth of cognitive processing applied at encoding and at each subsequent encounter. Phonological processing (attending to the sound of the word) produces weaker traces than semantic processing (attending to meaning), which produces weaker traces than elaborative processing (connecting the word's meaning to prior knowledge, using it in context, generating examples). This principle implies that deliberate vocabulary learning strategies should prioritize elaborative, meaning-focused engagement over rote repetition.
> *— [[vocabulary-building-reading-foundational-report-2026-03-28_report]]*
