# Test-Anxiety-Definition

> [!definition] Test-Anxiety-Definition
> A motivating affect construct in the MSLQ representing the cognitive worry and emotionality components of anxiety about evaluative performance situations. Negatively correlated with self-efficacy and positively correlated with performance-avoidance goal orientation. At high levels, interferes with working memory resources available for cognitive strategy deployment and metacognitive monitoring. *Research status*: The worry component shows stronger relationships with performance outcomes than the emotionality component in MSLQ research — a finding consistent with broader anxiety-cognition research establishing that intrusive worry thoughts compete directly with on-task cognitive processing.
