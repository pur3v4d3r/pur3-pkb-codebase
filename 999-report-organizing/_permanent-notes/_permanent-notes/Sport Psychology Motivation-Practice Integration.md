## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Sport Psychology: Motivation-Practice Integration
> In sport psychology, the SDT-SRL interface recurs with remarkable structural similarity. Research on athlete development distinguishes between intrinsic motivation for sport (SDT level) and deliberate practice engagement quality (SRL level). Athletes with high intrinsic motivation but poor deliberate practice structure improve slowly; athletes with sophisticated practice habits but controlled motivation (training only for external validation) show poorer long-term resilience, burnout risk, and transfer of skills. The field has developed integrated frameworks (Vallerand's Hierarchical Motivation Model; Côté's developmental model) that explicitly attempt what SDT and SRL have not yet achieved together in educational contexts: a unified account of motivational quality and regulatory architecture in skilled performance development. The parallel is not superficial — it is structurally identical.
> *— [[sdt-srl-comparative-synthesis-2026-03-19_report]]*
