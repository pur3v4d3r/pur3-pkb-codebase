## Open Threads

> [!open-question] What is the Minimum Prior Knowledge Threshold for Effective EI?
> **The Question:** At what level of prior knowledge does EI transition from beneficial to neutral to harmful? Is this threshold domain-general or domain-specific?
>
> **What We Know:** Prior knowledge moderates EI effectiveness; accuracy of generated elaborations mediates this effect. Below some threshold, generated elaborations are likely to be inaccurate or tautological.
>
> **What We Don't Know:** There is no validated, prospective measure for determining whether a learner has "sufficient" prior knowledge to deploy EI productively in a given domain. The threshold may differ across content types, learner profiles, and question formats.
>
> **Why It Matters:** Without a threshold measure, the advice to "build prior knowledge before using EI" is operationally hollow — learners cannot assess when they have built enough.
>
> **Research Direction:** Studies comparing EI outcomes across systematically varied prior knowledge levels (using vocabulary size, domain knowledge pretests, or analogical reasoning tests as predictors) and measuring both retention and misconception formation as outcomes.
>
