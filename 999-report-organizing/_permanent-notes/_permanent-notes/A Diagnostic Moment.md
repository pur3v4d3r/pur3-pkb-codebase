## Open Threads

> [!open-question] A Diagnostic Moment
> Think about a text you recently found difficult. Was the difficulty primarily syntactic (long, complex sentences)? Conceptual (unfamiliar ideas)? Or lexical (too many unknown or only-partially-known words)? Being honest about the source of difficulty is the first step toward addressing it strategically. If the difficulty was primarily lexical, the implications are different from if it was conceptual.
> *— [[vocabulary-building-reading-foundational-report-2026-03-28_report]]*
