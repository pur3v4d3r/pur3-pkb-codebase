# Calibration (Nelson, 1996)

> [!definition] Calibration (Nelson, 1996)
> [[calibration]] is the correspondence between the absolute level of confidence expressed in metacognitive judgments and the actual probability of correct performance. A perfectly calibrated individual who expresses 70% confidence recalls 70% of so-rated items. Miscalibration — typically in the direction of overconfidence — is one of the most robust findings in metacognition research.
>
> **Boundary:** Calibration is distinct from resolution. Calibration concerns absolute accuracy; resolution concerns relative accuracy (the ability to discriminate between items that will and will not be recalled).
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** The calibration–resolution distinction is central to the report's proposed Calibration–Regulation Coupling Thesis.
>
> **See also:** [[calibration]], [[metacognitive-calibration]], [[Metacognitive-Monitoring-Accuracy-and-Calibration]]
