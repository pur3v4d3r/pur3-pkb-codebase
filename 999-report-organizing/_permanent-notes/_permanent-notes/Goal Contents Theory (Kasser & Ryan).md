# Goal Contents Theory (Kasser & Ryan)

> [!definition] Goal Contents Theory (Kasser & Ryan)
> A mini-theory within SDT that distinguishes between intrinsic goal contents (personal growth, community contribution, meaningful relationships, health) and extrinsic goal contents (wealth, fame, image, status). The pursuit of intrinsic goals is consistently associated with greater well-being, deeper processing, and more sustainable motivation; the pursuit of extrinsic goals is associated with anxiety, superficial processing, and motivational fragility — even when extrinsic goals are successfully achieved.
>
> **Boundary:** The intrinsic-extrinsic distinction refers to goal *contents* (what is pursued), not goal *regulation* (why it is pursued). A learner can pursue intrinsic contents for controlled reasons (learning for approval) or extrinsic contents for autonomous reasons (earning credentials that genuinely align with values). Contents and regulation interact but are theoretically distinct.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** GCT provides the framework for analysing whether PKM goal objects reflect genuine learning orientations or performative substitutes.
>
> **See also:** [[goal-contents-theory]], [[intrinsic-goal-content]], [[extrinsic-goal-content]], [[self-determination-theory]]
