## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Flashcard 8 — Distinction
> **Q:** What is pseudometacognition in the context of PKB practice?
> **A:** The condition where the external apparatus of metacognitive regulation (dashboards, analytics, gap detectors) is present without corresponding internal regulatory activity. The practitioner observes monitoring data without engaging in the regulatory reasoning that monitoring should inform — the appearance of metacognitive engagement without the substance.
> **Source:** Section 3.3, A.1
> **Difficulty:** Advanced
> **Tags:** #pseudometacognition, #monitoring-control-decoupling, #failure-mode
