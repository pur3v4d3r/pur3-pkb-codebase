## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Lateral Connections (parallel frameworks addressing complementary aspects)
> - [[cognitive-load-theory]] — the cognitive architectural framework that specifies the working memory conditions under which constructivist learning is efficient or impaired; the complementary cognitive science partner to constructivism
> - [[metacognition]] — the component of self-regulated learning most directly connected to constructivist learning: the capacity to monitor and manage one's own construction activities is both a goal and precondition of constructivist learning
> - [[schema-theory]] — the cognitive science account of the knowledge structures that constructivist learning constructs; schema theory is the cognitive mechanism through which constructivist epistemological claims are instantiated
> - [[meaningful-learning-theory]] — Ausubel's framework for the meaningful (as opposed to rote) assimilation of new knowledge into existing schemas; directly operationalizes the constructivist claim about how prior knowledge mediates new learning
> - [[situated-learning-theory]] — the extension of sociocultural constructivism to community and context; addresses the social and material embeddedness of constructivist learning activities
> *— [[constructivist-learning-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
