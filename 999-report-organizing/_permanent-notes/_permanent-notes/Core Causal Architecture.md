## Additional Material

> [!diagram] Core Causal Architecture
> ```
> PRIOR KNOWLEDGE ─────────────────────────────────────────┐
> (domain schemas, vocabulary,                              │
>  genre knowledge, epistemic norms)                        ↓
>                                             SITUATION MODEL QUALITY
> WORKING MEMORY CAPACITY ──────────────────────────────→  (comprehension depth,
> (decoding automaticity,                                    transfer potential)
>  schema-based compression)                                 ↑
>                                                           │
> INFERENCE GENERATION ─────────────────────────────────────┘
> (bridging, elaborative,                                    │
>  causal-logical)                           METACOGNITIVE MONITORING
>                                            (accuracy, sensitivity,
>        ┌──────────────────────────────────  comprehension failure detection)
>        ↓                                                   │
> REGULATION (Fix-up strategies,                             ↓
>  strategy selection, rate                    METACOGNITIVE REGULATION
>  adjustment, re-reading)  ←─────────────── (strategy deployment, effort
>                                              allocation, self-explanation)
>        ↕
> SELF-REGULATED LEARNING CYCLE
> (Forethought → Performance → Self-Reflection → Updated strategy knowledge)
> ```
>
> **Key insight:** The architecture is reciprocal, not sequential. Prior knowledge affects monitoring quality (Matthew Effect-metacognitive interaction trap); strategy deployment builds schemas; metacognitive accuracy drives motivation for domain knowledge investment.
> *— [[developing-reading-comprehension-and-metacognition-for-academic-texts-foundational-report-2026-04-08_report]]*
