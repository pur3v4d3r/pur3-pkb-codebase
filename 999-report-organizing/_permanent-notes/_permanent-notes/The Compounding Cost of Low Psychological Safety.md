## Reflections

> [!claude-insight] The Compounding Cost of Low Psychological Safety
> What makes low psychological safety particularly insidious is that its costs are largely invisible. When people don't speak up, what is lost is the information that would have been shared — and absent information rarely announces its absence. The team that never hears about the engineer's reservation, the early warning sign the junior analyst noticed, or the customer complaint the sales rep dismissed as an outlier proceeds in confident ignorance until the absence compounds into a visible failure. The communicative pattern that produced each silence looked locally fine; the cumulative effect was catastrophic. Building psychological safety is not a soft-skills luxury but a hard-nosed cognitive infrastructure requirement for any organization that depends on accurate information flows.
> *— [[how-to-communicate-effectively-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
