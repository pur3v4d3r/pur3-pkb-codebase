## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] ⚡ Active Reading Checkpoint
> Pause here. Before reading further, attempt to recall: What are the three types of metacognitive knowledge in Paris's taxonomy, and give a concrete example of each from your own learning experience. Do not look back at the previous text. After attempting recall, check your answer against the text above. Was your recall accurate? Was there material you felt you understood but could not generate? This exercise is itself a metacognitive monitoring experience — specifically, a delayed JOL triggered by retrieval attempt rather than re-reading.
