## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Technique: Explicit Goal Setting (Process + Outcome)
> *Why here: Vague goals produce vague self-evaluation. Specific, behavioral process goals give you something concrete to audit after the session. Set both kinds: outcome goals (what you want to understand) and process goals (how you will read).*
>
> **Outcome Goals:**
> 1. Be able to explain, without notes, the two-subsystem structure of *both* the Forethought Phase and the Self-Reflection Phase.
> 2. Be able to describe the "five feedback channels" through which Self-Reflection generates the next Forethought.
> 3. Understand the difference between adaptive and defensive inference — not just as a definition, but as a mechanism.
>
> **Process Goals:**
> 1. Self-explain at the end of each Part (close the report, write in my own words, then re-open).
> 2. Generate at least one [[elaborative-interrogation]] question per major section.
> 3. Pause at every callout labeled `[!key-claim]` and paraphrase it before moving on.
> 4. Flag every concept I cannot yet wiki-link to an existing PKB note (these are knowledge gaps, not just unfamiliar words).
> *— [[optimal-reading-session-worked-example-2026-03-25_report]]*
