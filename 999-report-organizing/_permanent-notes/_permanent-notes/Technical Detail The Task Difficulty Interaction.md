## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Technical Detail: The Task Difficulty Interaction
> The net effect of walking on reading is not static — it changes dynamically as reading difficulty fluctuates. Academic texts are not uniformly difficult; they contain passages of varying complexity, some building on well-established [[schema-theory|schemas]] and some introducing genuinely novel concepts that require heavy [[central-executive]] processing.
>
> The dynamic interaction works as follows:
>
> **During LOW-difficulty reading passages** (familiar concepts, well-established schemas, fluent comprehension):
> - Reading CE demand: LOW
> - Walking CE demand: LOW (habituated)
> - Total CE demand: LOW
> - Available CE headroom: LARGE
> - Arousal benefit: PRESENT (adds to already-adequate processing)
> - **Net effect:** Walking is BENEFICIAL — pure arousal benefit with negligible competition
>
> **During MODERATE-difficulty reading passages** (new concepts building on existing knowledge, moderate inference demands):
> - Reading CE demand: MODERATE
> - Walking CE demand: LOW
> - Total CE demand: MODERATE
> - Available CE headroom: MODERATE
> - Arousal benefit: PRESENT (sustains processing that might flag without arousal support)
> - **Net effect:** Walking is NEUTRAL to SLIGHTLY BENEFICIAL — arousal benefit roughly offsets dual-task cost
>
> **During HIGH-difficulty reading passages** (novel conceptual frameworks, complex argument structures, unfamiliar vocabulary, high inference demands):
> - Reading CE demand: HIGH (near [[working-memory-capacity]] limits)
> - Walking CE demand: LOW (but not zero)
> - Total CE demand: NEAR CEILING
> - Available CE headroom: MINIMAL
> - Arousal benefit: PRESENT but insufficient to compensate for ceiling effects
> - **Net effect:** Walking is DETRIMENTAL — the small CE cost of walking pushes total demand past capacity, and the arousal benefit cannot compensate because the bottleneck is capacity, not arousal
>
> **The critical prediction:** Walking-while-reading is most beneficial for material at or below the reader's current comprehension level, and becomes detrimental precisely when the material is most challenging and most important to comprehend accurately. This is the fundamental tension in the walking reader's practice.
> *— [[walking-academic-reading-working-memory-deep-dive-2026-04-13_report]]*
