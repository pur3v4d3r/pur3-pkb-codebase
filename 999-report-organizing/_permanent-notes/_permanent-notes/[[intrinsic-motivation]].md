# [[intrinsic-motivation]]

> [!definition] [[intrinsic-motivation]]
> Engagement driven by inherent satisfaction in the activity itself — the activity is its own reward. This is the form of motivation that first drove Deci and Ryan's research program, and it remains the paradigm case of self-determined motivation. However, [[cognitive-evaluation-theory]] established that intrinsic motivation is fragile: it can be undermined by the wrong kinds of external events. This fragility is central to understanding the practical implications of the continuum.
