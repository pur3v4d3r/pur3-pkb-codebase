# Defeasibility (Defeasible Justification)

> [!definition] Defeasibility (Defeasible Justification)
> A property of a justification: it is defeasible if there exists possible additional evidence that would weaken or remove it. All empirical justification is thought by fallibilists to be defeasible. Contrast with deductive entailment, which is indefeasible. Introduced into epistemology prominently by Roderick Chisholm and later systematised in the work of John Pollock.
