> [!reflection] Section 1 Reflective Questions
> 1. The shift from the multi-store model to the working memory model was driven partly by neuropsychological case studies of brain-damaged patients. What does this illustrate about the relationship between clinical evidence and theoretical development in cognitive science?
> 2. Working memory capacity correlates with reading comprehension, reasoning, and academic achievement. Does this suggest that working memory capacity is a fundamental cognitive resource, or might the correlation reflect shared variance with other constructs like intelligence or processing speed?
> 3. If working memory is the site of real-time cognitive work, what are the implications for how learners should structure their study environments and pacing strategies?
