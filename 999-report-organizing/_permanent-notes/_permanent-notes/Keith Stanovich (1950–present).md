## Additional Material

> [!person] Keith Stanovich (1950–present)
> **Core Contribution:** Identified the Matthew Effect in reading, showing that reading development is self-amplifying — advantages and disadvantages compound over time through the mechanism of vocabulary and prior knowledge development.
>
> **Role in the Dialectical Arc:** Central to the critical lens challenge in Phase III; the Matthew Effect describes the population-level consequence of the situation model being prior-knowledge-dependent.
>
> **Key Works:** "Matthew Effects in Reading" (1986); *Progress in Understanding Reading* (2000).
>
