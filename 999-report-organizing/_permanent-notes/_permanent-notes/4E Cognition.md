# 4E Cognition

> [!definition] 4E Cognition
> An umbrella term for four related theoretical movements challenging Classical Cognitivism: Embodied cognition (the body shapes cognitive processes constitutively, not merely causally), Embedded cognition (cognitive systems are deeply adapted to and exploit environmental structures), Enacted cognition (cognition is brought forth through sensorimotor coupling, not representation of a pre-given world), and Extended cognition (cognitive processes can literally extend into the environment). The four Es are related but distinct: a theorist can accept embodied and embedded cognition without accepting the stronger extended cognition claim.
