## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Far Transfer: The Monitoring-Control Loop Applied to Organizational Learning
> **Source insight:** Monitoring accuracy propagates through control decisions in self-amplifying or self-correcting loops depending on feedback presence (Level 4).
> **Target domain:** Organizational learning and strategic decision-making.
>
> Organizations, like individuals, have "monitoring" systems (performance metrics, market research, customer feedback) and "control" systems (strategic decisions, resource allocation). The dynamics identified in Level 4 apply: organizations with poor monitoring inputs make poor allocation decisions, which produce poor outcomes, which degrade the information environment, creating organizational analogues of the [[monitoring-gap|monitoring-control trap]]. The Deep Dive's analysis suggests that organizations should invest in monitoring accuracy (quality of information systems) before investing in decision optimization — the decision system can only be as good as the monitoring system that feeds it.
> *— [[metacognitive-monitoring-accuracy-calibration-deep-dive-2026-04-12_report]]*
