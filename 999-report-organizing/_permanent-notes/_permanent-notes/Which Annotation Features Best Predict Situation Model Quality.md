## Open Threads

> [!open-question] Which Annotation Features Best Predict Situation Model Quality?
> **The Question:** Is there empirical evidence establishing which specific annotation behaviors — causal marking, dimensional tracking, question generation, compression — most reliably predict situation model quality on inference testing and transfer tasks?
>
> **What We Know:** Self-explanation (a form of elaborative annotation) robustly improves situation model quality. Elaborative interrogation improves retention relative to re-reading.
>
> **What We Don't Know:** No study has directly compared annotation types (causal chains vs. dimensional flags vs. question annotations vs. compression annotations) for their specific contributions to situation model quality, controlling for time on task.
>
> **Why It Matters:** The DIME Annotation Protocol in this report is theory-derived and well-motivated but awaits direct empirical validation.
>
> **Research Direction:** A within-subjects reading study comparing annotation type conditions on inference posttests and transfer tasks would directly test the DIME protocol.
>
