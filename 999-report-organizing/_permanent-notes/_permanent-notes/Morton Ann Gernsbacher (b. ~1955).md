## Additional Material

> [!person] Morton Ann Gernsbacher (b. ~1955)
> American cognitive psychologist; Professor, University of Wisconsin–Madison. Developed the Structure Building Framework (1990) and the suppression hypothesis. Her research on individual differences in suppression efficiency provided the most direct evidence that comprehension skill is substantially determined by inhibitory capacity.
>
> **Lineage:** Influenced by Kintsch and by associative memory research; influenced work on comprehension differences in autism spectrum conditions.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** The Structure Building Framework's suppression mechanism is incorporated into Phase IV's account of situation model failure modes and individual differences in comprehension.
> *— [[situation-models-text-comprehension-foundational-report-2026-03-29_report]]*
