## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Navigation as Distributed Cognition — Hutchins's Core Findings
> Hutchins's studies of ship navigation demonstrated that: (a) no single crew member possesses all the knowledge required to navigate the vessel; (b) cognitive work is achieved through the coordinated interaction of crew members, physical artefacts (charts, plotting tools, alidades), and encoded procedures; (c) errors at the system level often arise not from individual incompetence but from breakdowns in the distribution of cognitive labour across the system; (d) the physical arrangement of the work environment encodes knowledge in a form that shapes and scaffolds individual cognition. These findings support the view that cognitive analysis must take the entire sociotechnical system as its unit of analysis.
> *— [[extended-mind-theory-foundational-report-2026-03-11_report]]*
