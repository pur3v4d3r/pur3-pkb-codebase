>                    MOLECULAR CLOUD
>                    (cold, dense, turbulent)
>                           │
>               turbulence decay, ambipolar diffusion
>               fragmentation into cores
>                           ▼
>                    DENSE CORE
>                    (M > M_Jeans → unstable)
>                           │
>                    gravitational collapse
>                    (isothermal → adiabatic)
>                           ▼

>               │   (H₂ dissociation threshold)  │

>                          ▼
>               ┌─── SECOND HYDROSTATIC CORE ──┐
>               │   = PROTOSTAR (Class 0)       │
>               │   + Accretion Disk             │
>               │   + Bipolar Outflows           │
>               └──────────┬───────────────────┘
>                          │
>                   envelope dispersal
>                   (outflows, radiation)
>                          ▼
>               ┌─── T TAURI STAR (Class II) ──┐
>               │   Kelvin-Helmholtz contraction │
>               │   Deuterium burning (soft)     │
>               └──────────┬───────────────────┘
>                          │
>                   T_core → 10⁷ K
>                   H fusion ignites (hard)
>                          ▼
>                ┌── MAIN SEQUENCE STAR ──┐
>                │  Hydrostatic equilibrium │
>                │  Thermonuclear thermostat│
>                │  Stable for ~Gyr         │
>                └─────────────────────────┘
>
>     ── OR (if M < 0.08 M☉) ──
>                          ▼
>                    BROWN DWARF
>                    (no sustained H fusion)
>                    (slow cooling, fading)
> ```
