# Formative Feedback

> [!definition] Formative Feedback
> Feedback provided *during* a learning episode, while the learner still has time and opportunity to act on it and close the gap between current and desired performance. Contrasted with summative feedback, which reports performance after the fact. The defining feature is not format or content but *functional timing*: it must arrive when it can still influence the course of learning.

> [!evidence] Formative Feedback
> Feedback provided during the learning process — before summative evaluation — with the purpose of informing revision and improvement rather than rendering final judgment. Distinguished from summative feedback (which assesses final performance) by its temporal position and developmental intent. Growth mindset research provides strong motivational rationale for formative feedback: it operationalizes the belief that current performance is not final, and that improvement through effort and strategy is both possible and expected.
> *— [[growth-mindset-learning-foundational-report-2026-03-05_report]]*
