# Mastery-Approach Goal (MAP)

> [!definition] Mastery-Approach Goal (MAP)
> **Definition:** Striving to develop competence, to learn, to master tasks, and to improve one's own performance. The standard is absolute or self-referential; success means doing better than one's prior performance or achieving task mastery. This is the approach pole of the mastery dimension.
> **Report-Specific Significance:** MAP goals are the most uniformly beneficial goal type in the empirical literature — the one cell of the 2×2 where the evidence across outcomes, populations, and contexts is most consistent.
