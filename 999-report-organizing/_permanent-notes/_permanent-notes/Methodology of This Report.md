> [!methodology-and-sources] Methodology of This Report
> This report synthesises four converging research traditions: (1) the heuristics-and-biases programme founded by Tversky and Kahneman; (2) the heuristic-analytic and default-interventionist research programme of Evans and collaborators; (3) Stanovich's individual-differences and rationality-quotient research; (4) the fast-and-frugal heuristics tradition of Gigerenzer and the Adaptive Behavior and Cognition group. It draws additionally on the Kahneman-Klein synthesis on intuitive expertise, the cognitive-forcing-function literature in clinical reasoning (Croskerry), and the broader philosophical literature on metacognition and self-knowledge.
