## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Level 2: Mechanism — How Schemas Constrain and Generate Cognition
> **Zoom progression:** At Level 1, we saw schemas as organized knowledge templates with variable slots and default values. Level 2 reveals the functional mechanisms by which schemas do their cognitive work: they are not passive templates waiting to be consulted, but active processing constraints that shape perception, comprehension, inference, and memory reconstruction from the moment a context is established.
> **What you'll see at this level:** Top-down constraint on bottom-up processing; the inference generation mechanism; the gap-filling and default value assignment process; how schema selection (schema instantiation) competes for a given context; and how the assimilation-accommodation distinction captures the relationship between schema activation and schema change.
> **Specialist value:** Understanding the mechanism is prerequisite to understanding expertise, learning, and cognitive error. The mechanism, not the surface description, explains why schemas simultaneously enable expert performance and produce systematic cognitive failures.
> *— [[schema-and-how-they-work-deep-dive-2026-04-08_report]]*
