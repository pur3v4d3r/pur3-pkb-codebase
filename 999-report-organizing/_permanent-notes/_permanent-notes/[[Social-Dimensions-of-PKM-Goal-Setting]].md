> [!evidence] [[Social-Dimensions-of-PKM-Goal-Setting]]
> **Description:** An examination of the social, community, and collaborative dimensions of goal-setting in PKM — including learning communities, accountability partnerships, community of practice models, and the relatedness need's role in sustaining long-term knowledge work. Should address both the benefits (motivation, diverse perspectives, social accountability) and risks (social comparison, normative pressure, performance orientation) of social engagement in PKM.
> **Connection to this report:** The relatedness need (Section 2) was acknowledged but not developed; the report focused primarily on intrapsychic processes. Social dynamics represent a significant extension.
