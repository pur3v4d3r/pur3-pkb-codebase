## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Questioner's Second Elenctic Challenge: The Tacit Knowledge Problem
> The Respondent has retreated to a domain restriction: the explanation-test applies to theoretical/conceptual knowledge, not to procedural or perceptual knowledge. But this is a significant concession — it means the explanation-test is not the criterion for understanding *in general*, only for a subset of understanding. The Questioner will now press on whether the boundary between "theoretical" and "tacit" is as clean as the Respondent needs it to be, opening the deeper problem that even our most theoretical understanding may rest on tacit foundations that cannot be articulated.
