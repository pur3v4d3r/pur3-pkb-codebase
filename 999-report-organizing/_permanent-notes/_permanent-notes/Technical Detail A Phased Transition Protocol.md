## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Technical Detail: A Phased Transition Protocol
> For a reader accustomed to 40 miles/week of walking-reading, an abrupt transition to seated reading is likely to fail because:
> - The restlessness that walking manages will reassert
> - The habit is deeply entrenched (months or years of practice)
> - The arousal baseline during seated reading will feel sub-optimal by comparison
>
> **Phased transition (speculative but informed):**
>
> **Week 1-2: Awareness Phase**
> - Continue current practice unchanged
> - Add comprehension checks every 15-20 minutes: pause walking, close eyes, explain the last passage to yourself
> - Notice which passages you can and cannot reconstruct — these are your difficulty ceiling markers
> - No behavior change yet; just calibrate self-awareness
>
> **Week 3-4: Short Sit Periods**
> - After each 25-minute walking-reading block, sit at PKB desk for 10 minutes
> - During the 10-minute sit: re-read the most difficult passage from the walking block, make one atomic note or highlight
> - Return to walking after 10 minutes (whether or not the sitting was productive)
> - **Goal:** Build the habit of transitioning from walking to sitting without triggering resistance
>
> **Week 5-6: Extended Sit Periods**
> - Walking: 25 minutes → Sitting: 20 minutes → Walking: 25 minutes
> - During sitting: full [[active-note-making]] — extract claims, write elaborations, connect to existing PKB notes
> - Sitting periods may feel uncomfortable initially due to restlessness — this is expected and will diminish as the body adapts to the sit-walk-sit rhythm
> - **Goal:** Establish the cycled protocol as the new default
>
> **Week 7+: Optimized Steady State**
> - Walking: 20-30 minutes (first-pass reading, moderate material)
> - Sitting: 30-40 minutes (deep processing, note-making, high-difficulty material)
> - Total daily walking distance will decrease from ~5-6 miles to ~2-3 miles (compensate with separate exercise if desired for physical health)
> - The post-exercise catecholamine window will cover most or all of the sitting period
> - **Metacognitive cue for ending walking phase:** "Am I struggling to hold the thread of this argument?" → transition to sitting
