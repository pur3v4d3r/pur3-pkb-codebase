## Reflections

> [!reflection] Reflective Questions: Section 4
> 1. In which academic disciplines do you have robust genre schemas — can you predict the structural moves of a text before reading it? In which are you reading without these schemas? How does this difference manifest in your comprehension experience?
> 2. The concept of "modal shift" to discursive reading mode implies that you actively choose how to read. How often do you make this conscious mode selection, versus defaulting to whatever reading mode is habitual?
> 3. High-frequency academic vocabulary (the Academic Word List tier) is more broadly learnable than technical vocabulary. What systematic approaches to this vocabulary might be worth developing?
