> [!reflection] Integrating the Navigation
> **Comprehension**: What is the single most important contextual factor that determines which framework to reach for? >
> **Application**: Identify two learning contexts where you would reach for different frameworks. What features of each context drive the different choices?
>
> **Extension**: Does the need for different frameworks for different questions suggest that a genuinely unified theory of motivated self-regulated learning is possible? What would it need to contain?
