## Additional Material

> [!person] John D. Karpicke (b. 1981)
> Karpicke (Purdue University) is a leading researcher on retrieval practice and the testing effect, whose studies have most directly documented the Fluency Illusion in academic reading contexts.
>
> **Key Works:** Karpicke & Roediger (2008), "The Critical Importance of Retrieval for Learning"; Karpicke & Blunt (2011), "Retrieval Practice Produces More Learning than Elaborative Studying."
>
> **Lineage:** Extends Ebbinghaus's memory research and Roediger's retrieval practice work; provides the experimental basis for retrieval-based learning interventions.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Karpicke's research provides the evidence for the Fluency Illusion and the basis for the post-reading free recall intervention.
