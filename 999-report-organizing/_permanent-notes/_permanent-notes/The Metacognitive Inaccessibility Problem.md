## Practical Implications

> [!warning] The Metacognitive Inaccessibility Problem
> Effective monitoring requires that the state being monitored is accessible to conscious inspection. There is substantial evidence from cognitive psychology that many of the motivational processes Pintrich describes — particularly implicit goal activation and affective priming — operate below the threshold of reliable introspective access. A learner cannot monitor what they cannot access. This means that the monitoring column of Pintrich's matrix describes an aspirational target that learners' actual metacognitive access may substantially fall short of — particularly for motivation and affect monitoring, which have less external behavioral correlates than cognitive monitoring.
> *— [[pintrichs-framework-srl-focused-analysis-2026-03-23_report]]*
