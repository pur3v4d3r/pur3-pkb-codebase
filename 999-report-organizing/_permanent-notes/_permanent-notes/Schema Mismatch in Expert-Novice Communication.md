## Examples

> [!example] Schema Mismatch in Expert-Novice Communication
> A cardiologist explaining atrial fibrillation to a patient without medical background cannot assume the patient has a working schema for "arrhythmia," "electrical conduction system," or even "heartbeat regularity." The physician must first activate a simplifying schema ("think of the heart as having a rhythm like a drum..."), then progressively complicate it with technically accurate elaborations. Skipping the schema activation step — proceeding directly to technical description — produces the characteristic "talking past" dynamic in expert-novice communication.
