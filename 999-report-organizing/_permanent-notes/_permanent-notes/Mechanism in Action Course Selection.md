## Examples

> [!example] Mechanism in Action: Course Selection
> A concrete illustration of this mechanism is the course selection behavior documented in Dweck's research. When students with different mindset orientations were given the choice between reviewing material they had already mastered (safe, no risk of failure) versus learning new, challenging material (risky, possibility of confusion), fixed-mindset students disproportionately chose the safe option and growth-mindset students chose the challenging option — even when both groups were told their choice was private and would not affect their grade. The behavior is driven by what difficulty means within each implicit theory, not by external incentives.
> *— [[growth-mindset-learning-foundational-report-2026-03-05_report]]*
