## Open Threads

> [!tension-identified] Guidance vs. Discovery: The Scaffold-Fading Dilemma
> **Position A (Strong Guidance):** Direct instruction and worked examples are categorically more effective than discovery-based approaches for novice learners (Kirschner, Sweller, & Clark, 2006). The 4C/ID model's scaffold-fading trajectory should lean heavily toward guidance, fading slowly and cautiously.
>
> **Position B (Productive Failure):** Research by Kapur (2008, 2016) demonstrates that under specific conditions, initial struggle without guidance produces superior conceptual understanding and transfer compared to direct instruction. The 4C/ID model undervalues the potential of productive failure as a learning mechanism.
>
> **Current evidence:** Both positions are empirically supported within their respective boundary conditions. The critical variables appear to be learner expertise level, domain structure, availability of subsequent consolidation, and outcome measures (procedural accuracy vs. conceptual transfer).
>
> **This report's stance:** The tension is productive rather than resolvable. The 4C/ID model's scaffold-fading trajectory provides a principled starting point, but optimal guidance calibration requires attention to the factors identified by the productive failure literature.
> *— [[four-component-instructional-design-model-4cid-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*
