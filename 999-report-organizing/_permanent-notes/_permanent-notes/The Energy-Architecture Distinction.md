## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Energy-Architecture Distinction
> **Type**: Complementarity
>
> The most productive structural insight from comparing these frameworks is the distinction between motivational energy and regulatory architecture. SDT theorises the quality and source of the energy; SRL frameworks theorise the architecture through which that energy is converted into skilled performance. This is analogous to distinguishing between a fuel and an engine — you need both, and neither alone predicts how far you will travel. A learner with high-quality autonomous motivation but poor regulatory architecture will have rich motivational resources but be unable to channel them into disciplined practice. A learner with sophisticated regulatory architecture but controlled motivation will be able to enact regulatory cycles but will do so under psychological strain, with consequences for persistence, creativity, and well-being. Only by integrating the two accounts do we get a complete explanation of learning quality.
> *— [[sdt-srl-comparative-synthesis-2026-03-19_report]]*
