# Empirically Supported Treatment (EST)

> [!definition] Empirically Supported Treatment (EST)
> A treatment protocol that meets specified criteria for having demonstrated effectiveness in controlled research, typically including at least two independent randomized controlled trials showing superiority to a waiting list, placebo, or alternative treatment, conducted with a clearly defined patient population using a replicable treatment manual. The EST framework was developed by APA Division 12's Task Force on the Promotion and Dissemination of Psychological Procedures. Criticism of the framework focuses on: the artificial conditions of RCTs relative to real-world practice, the neglect of common factors, allegiance effects, and the challenge of applying manualized treatments to the complex, comorbid presentations that dominate clinical practice.
