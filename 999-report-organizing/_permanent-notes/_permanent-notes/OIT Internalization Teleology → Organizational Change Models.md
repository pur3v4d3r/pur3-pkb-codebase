## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] OIT Internalization Teleology → Organizational Change Models
> Stage-based organizational development models (Spiral Dynamics, Laloux's Teal organizations, Kegan's orders of mind as applied to leadership) make the same structural move as OIT: they describe a sequence of developmental stages and claim that development under the right conditions naturally tends toward the highest stage — which reliably looks like autonomous, self-organizing, complexity-embracing functioning that reflects the intellectual values of the theorists and their primary audiences. The SDT reconstruction suggests a more useful frame: describe the sequence without teleology (these stages have been observed to occur in this sequence under certain conditions), specify the conditions required for progression (rather than assuming natural tendency), and resist the implication that higher stages are universally desirable rather than contextually appropriate.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-19_report]]*
