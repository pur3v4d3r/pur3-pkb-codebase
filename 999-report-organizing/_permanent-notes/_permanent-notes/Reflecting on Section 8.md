## Reflections

> [!reflection] Reflecting on Section 8
> 1. The AI learning disruption may represent a genuine threat to the human cognitive development that generative learning builds. But AI could also be deployed as a generative learning partner. What design principles would guide the development of AI educational tools that enhance rather than bypass human generative cognitive processing?
> 2. The implementation gap suggests that publishing research findings about effective learning strategies is insufficient to change practice. What other mechanisms — curriculum reform, teacher training, policy, public communication — would be needed to systematically shift educational practice toward generative approaches?
> 3. GLT was developed primarily in Western, academic educational contexts. To what extent are its core principles universally valid, and to what extent do they reflect assumptions about learning, knowledge, and the learner that are culturally specific? What cross-cultural research would be needed to assess the universality of generative learning principles?
> *— [[generative-learning-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
