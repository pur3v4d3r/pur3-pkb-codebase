## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Depth-Frequency Multiplier → Deliberate Practice in Motor Skill Development
> Anders Ericsson's research on [[deliberate-practice|deliberate practice]] captures this principle in the domain of skill acquisition. Athletes who practice for many hours with low intentionality — going through motions without focused attention to technique correction — acquire less expertise per hour than those who practice with full attentional engagement, immediate feedback, and focused correction of specific errors. The analogy to vocabulary: hours of shallow reading exposure are the equivalent of high-volume but low-intentionality sports practice. The mechanisms are different (procedural vs. declarative memory, motor vs. lexical systems) but the structural principle is identical: depth of processing per encounter multiplies the learning return in a way that frequency alone cannot replicate.
> *— [[reading-vocabulary-focused-analysis-2026-03-28_report]]*
