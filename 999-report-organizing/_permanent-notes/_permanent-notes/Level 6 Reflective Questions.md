## Reflections

> [!reflection] Level 6 Reflective Questions
> 1. The predictive processing reframing of schemas as hierarchical priors is elegant but potentially unfalsifiable. What experiment would you design to distinguish predictions unique to the PP schema account from predictions common to traditional schema theory?
> 2. If transformer attention mechanisms genuinely implement schema-like knowledge, what implications does this have for the old debate between symbolic and connectionist schema architectures? Does transformer success favor the connectionist account?
> 3. The schema-distinctness debate asks whether "schema" is a theoretically coherent category. If you had to break "schema" into 3-4 more specific theoretical terms, what would they be, and how would they differ from each other?
