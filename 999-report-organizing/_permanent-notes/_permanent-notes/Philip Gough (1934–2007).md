## Additional Material

> [!person] Philip Gough (1934–2007)
> **Core Contribution:** Co-developed (with William Tunmer) the Simple View of Reading, which formalized the decomposition of reading comprehension into decoding and linguistic comprehension components.
>
> **Role in the Dialectical Arc:** Primary theoretical resource for the steel-manned received view; the Simple View correctly identifies decoding as necessary and is preserved in the reconstruction while being supplemented.
>
> **Key Works:** With Tunmer, "Decoding, Reading, and Reading Disability" (1986); with Hoover, "The Simple View of Reading" (1990).
>
> **Relevance to This Analysis:** The Simple View's empirical validity for early readers is the core of what survives dialectical scrutiny in the received view.
> *— [[situation-model-construction-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-29_report]]*
