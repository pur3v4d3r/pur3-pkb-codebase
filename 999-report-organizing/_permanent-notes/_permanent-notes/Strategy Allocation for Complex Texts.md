## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Strategy Allocation for Complex Texts
> Not all text deserves equal processing depth. Strategic planning for reading a complex report should explicitly allocate strategies by text region:
> - **Definitional sections** (lexicons, conceptual foundations): Slow, elaborative processing — generate your own examples, test the boundary conditions of each definition, connect to existing PKB nodes
> - **Mechanistic sections** (how things work): Trace causal chains explicitly, generate "what would happen if X were absent?" tests, draw the mechanism in your own notation
> - **Application and implication sections**: Generate specific cases from your own learning context; ask "how would this change what I do on Tuesday?"
> - **Limitations and tensions sections**: These deserve disproportionate attention because they are where the report's epistemic humility lives — and where your calibration of the field's boundaries is sharpened
> *— [[srl-forethought-selfreflection-pkb-report-2026-03-24_report]]*
