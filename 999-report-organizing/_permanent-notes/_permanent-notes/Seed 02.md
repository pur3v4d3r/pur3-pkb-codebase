## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Seed 02
> **Q:** What is the Matthew Effect in reading, and why does it challenge the reading-primary vocabulary hypothesis?
> **A:** Stanovich's (1986) finding that vocabulary-reading development is self-reinforcing in a way that diverges rather than converges: high-vocabulary readers read more fluently, read more, acquire more vocabulary; low-vocabulary readers find reading effortful, read less, acquire less. It challenges the reading-primary hypothesis because the mechanism works well for those who need it least and poorly for those who need it most — making reading-based vocabulary acquisition structurally inequitable.
> **Source:** Phase II, Matthew Effect definition; Phase III Critical Challenge
