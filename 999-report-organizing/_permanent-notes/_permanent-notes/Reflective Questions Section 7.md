## Reflections

> [!reflection] Reflective Questions: Section 7
> 1. The scaffolding model of instruction implies that the goal is not to provide permanent support but to gradually eliminate it as competence develops. Applied to your own reading development, where are you in the I-We-You progression for different types of academic texts? What forms of external scaffolding are you currently depending on that you want to gradually internalize?
> 2. Metacognitive instruction should precede or accompany strategy instruction, not follow it. Reflecting on strategy instruction you have received or practiced: how often was this sequencing followed? What would it look like to re-sequence your own self-directed learning to follow this principle?
> 3. Zimmerman's self-reflection phase — evaluating outcomes and attributing difficulties to appropriate causes — is what converts reading experiences into metacognitive learning. How systematically do you engage in this phase after completing difficult academic reading?
> *— [[developing-reading-comprehension-and-metacognition-for-academic-texts-foundational-report-2026-04-08_report]]*
