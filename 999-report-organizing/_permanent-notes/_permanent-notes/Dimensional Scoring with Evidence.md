>
> **Identified Limitations:**
> 1. **Western research bias:** The constituent theories were developed in WEIRD research contexts. Application to cross-cultural PKM practice is extrapolative.
> 2. **Domain extrapolation:** Moving findings from formal education and organisational psychology to self-directed PKM involves assumptions about transferability that have not been directly tested.
> 3. **Untested proposals:** The motivational grammar framework and specificity-autonomy dialectic are theoretically grounded but empirically unvalidated.
> 4. **Individual differences:** Personality, prior expertise, regulatory capacity, and learning preferences moderate all reported effects but are not systematically addressed.
> 5. **Technology specificity:** While framed for Obsidian PKB practice, the principles are more general than the implementation examples suggest; readers using different tools will need to adapt.
>
> **Recommendations for Future Revision:**
> 1. Add a dedicated subsection on individual differences in goal-setting effectiveness (goal orientation stability, regulatory focus theory, need for cognition)
> 2. Incorporate research on collective goal-setting for community PKM practices
> 3. Develop worked examples using actual Obsidian template syntax (Templater/QuickAdd) for each protocol
> 4. After the motivational grammar framework has been used in practice, add empirical observations about its effectiveness
> 5. Cross-reference with any future Practitioner's Field Guide on implementation intentions to complete the goal lifecycle model
