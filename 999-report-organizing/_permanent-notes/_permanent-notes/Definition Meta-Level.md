## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Definition: Meta-Level
> **Q:** In the Nelson-Narens framework, what is the meta-level?
> **A:** The meta-level is the functional level of cognitive processing that contains a dynamic, imperfect model of the object-level's state, receives information through monitoring, and issues control directives to modify object-level operations.

> [!flashcard] Definition: Meta-Level
> **Q:** In the Nelson–Narrens model, what is the meta-level and what are its two primary functions?
> **A:** The meta-level is the higher-order component that contains a dynamic model of the object-level's state. Its two primary functions are monitoring (receiving information from the object-level through cue-mediated channels) and control (issuing directives that modify object-level processing).
> **Source:** Section I
