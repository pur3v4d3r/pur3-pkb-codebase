## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Basic Psychological Needs as the Structural Conditions for Psychological Integration
> The conjunctive architecture of BPNT becomes theoretically intelligible when we view the three needs not as three independent motivational inputs but as *three distinct structural prerequisites* for a single underlying process: the active, ongoing integration of experience into a coherent self. From this perspective, autonomy satisfaction provides the *authorship* condition — the sense of volitional agency required for experience to be genuinely one's own rather than an imposition from outside; competence satisfaction provides the *efficacy* condition — the sense of effective functioning that makes genuine engagement rather than defensive avoidance possible; and relatedness satisfaction provides the *security* condition — the interpersonal foundation that enables genuine exposure and vulnerability without activating defensive self-protection. These are not three things that help; they are three things that are *constitutive* of the self-organization process itself. Remove any one and the process is not degraded — it is qualitatively disrupted. This reframing explains why compensation fails: you cannot compensate for the absence of authorship with greater efficacy; you cannot compensate for insecurity with greater agency. Each is doing work that the other two cannot do. The conjunctive architecture is not an empirical coincidence but a structural necessity given what psychological self-integration requires.
> *— [[basic-psychological-needs-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-13_report]]*
