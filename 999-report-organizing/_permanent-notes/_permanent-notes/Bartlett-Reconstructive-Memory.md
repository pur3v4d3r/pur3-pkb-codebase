# Bartlett-Reconstructive-Memory

> [!definition] Bartlett-Reconstructive-Memory
> Bartlett demonstrated experimentally that recall is schema-governed reconstruction rather than reproductive playback — errors of recall are systematic, reflecting the assimilation of unfamiliar material to familiar cognitive frameworks.
