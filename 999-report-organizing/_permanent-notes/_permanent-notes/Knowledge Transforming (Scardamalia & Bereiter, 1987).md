# Knowledge Transforming (Scardamalia & Bereiter, 1987)

> [!definition] Knowledge Transforming (Scardamalia & Bereiter, 1987)
> A model of writing process in which the demands of text production (rhetorical problem space) interact dialectically with the demands of understanding (content problem space), producing genuine conceptual change in the writer. Contrasted with [[knowledge-telling|knowledge telling]], where pre-existing knowledge is simply reported without restructuring.
>
> **Boundary:** Knowledge transforming is a process description, not a binary classification. Most writing involves elements of both telling and transforming; the distinction identifies a continuum of cognitive engagement.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** Provides the mechanism by which writing functions as metacognitive monitoring — the "talk back" of text reveals comprehension gaps.
>
> **See also:** [[knowledge-telling-vs.-knowledge-transforming]], [[writing-to-learn]], [[generative-learning-theory]]
