## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Why Mastery Goals Enable Deeper Processing — The Mechanism
> The mechanism connecting mastery orientation to deeper processing is underspecified in much of the achievement goal literature, but Pintrich's framework suggests it operates through the *conditional relationship between monitoring and threat*. A performance-oriented learner treats monitoring signals as diagnostic of ability: a comprehension failure is evidence of inadequacy, which is threatening to self-esteem and activates defensive responses. A mastery-oriented learner treats the same signal as diagnostic of the current strategy: comprehension failure means the strategy is not working, which is simply informative. The threat interpretation activates affect regulation at the expense of cognitive regulation; the informational interpretation enables cognitive strategy adjustment. This mechanism suggests that goal orientation shapes *how monitoring signals are interpreted* before they trigger regulatory responses — a genuinely dynamic interaction between the motivational and cognitive areas.
> *— [[pintrichs-framework-srl-focused-analysis-2026-03-23_report]]*
