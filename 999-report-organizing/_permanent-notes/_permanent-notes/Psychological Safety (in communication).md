# Psychological Safety (in communication)

> [!definition] Psychological Safety (in communication)
> The shared belief within a group that interpersonal risk-taking — speaking up, admitting uncertainty, challenging decisions, sharing bad news — is safe and will not result in punitive consequences. The most important organizational variable predicting honest, complete information sharing in group contexts. Built or destroyed through accumulated communicative choices by group members, particularly leaders. See [[psychological-safety]].
