## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Empirical Testing of the Bidirectional Enrichment Hypothesis
> **Description:** Design longitudinal studies measuring internal schema development in PKB practitioners as a function of PKB schema-fidelity. This would require validated measures of both PKB schema-fidelity and internal schema organization (concept mapping, clinical interview, knowledge structure assessment batteries). The hypothesis generates specific testable predictions that could be investigated with existing methods in educational and cognitive psychology.
> **Connection to this report:** The Bidirectional Enrichment Hypothesis is the central original contribution of this report; it is well-motivated but untested. Empirical investigation would either validate and flesh out the mechanism or identify where the synthesis breaks down.
