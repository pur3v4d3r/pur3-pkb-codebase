## Additional Material

> [!person] Isabel Beck (1934–2021)
> American educational psychologist and reading researcher; professor at the University of Pittsburgh. Known primarily for the Three-Tier Vocabulary Model, which categorizes English vocabulary by frequency and instructional utility. Beck's pedagogical research on "robust vocabulary instruction" — intensive, multi-faceted word study — demonstrated that explicit instruction in Tier 2 vocabulary produced measurable comprehension gains.
>
> **Lineage:** Collaborated with Margaret McKeown and Linda Kucan; influenced by Pearson's comprehension research and Stahl's vocabulary work.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Beck's tier framework provides the applied instructional complement to the incidental acquisition account; referenced in the Applications phase.
> *— [[vocabulary-building-reading-foundational-report-2026-03-28_report]]*
