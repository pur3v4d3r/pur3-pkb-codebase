# Receptive Vocabulary

> [!definition] Receptive Vocabulary
> Word knowledge sufficient for recognition and meaning-extraction in reading or listening contexts, without necessarily enabling production. A reader has receptive knowledge of a word when they can accurately assign meaning to it in context, even if they could not retrieve the word spontaneously or deploy it in writing.
>
> **Boundary:** Receptive knowledge can be highly context-dependent — a reader may understand a word in one context but fail to recognize it in another. True receptive mastery implies cross-contextual recognition stability.
>
> **See also:** [[reading-fluency|Reading Fluency]], [[lexical-automaticity|Lexical Automaticity]], [[verbal-efficiency-theory|Verbal Efficiency Theory]]
