## Additional Material

> [!section-summary] Level 6 Summary: The Frontier
> At edge cases level, schema theory was constrained and enriched by specific boundary conditions. At frontier level, we can see where schema theory is being actively extended:
> - **Systems neuroscience:** Hippocampal-neocortical transfer via replay during sleep; schema availability speeds its own extension ([[prior-knowledge-activation]] as neural facilitator); van Kesteren dissociation between mPFC (congruent) and hippocampal (incongruent) encoding
> - **Predictive processing:** Schemas reframed as hierarchical Bayesian priors; accommodation resistance explained via precision-attenuation; genuine theoretical advance vs. vocabulary substitution still debated
> - **AI connections:** Transformer self-attention as Hopfield-type content-addressable memory with attractor-like schema behavior; interpretability research may resolve the symbolic/distributed debate
> - **Schema distinctness debate:** "Schema" as unified category vs. family of related phenomena; gradual differentiation into more specific theoretical terms
> - **Binding problem:** Neural mechanism of variable-slot binding remains an open neuroscience question; gamma synchrony hypothesis; binding-load connection to Cognitive Load Theory
>
> The final level (speculation) extrapolates beyond current evidence to ask what schema theory might imply for AI, PKM, and the future of knowledge organization.
> *— [[schema-and-how-they-work-deep-dive-2026-04-08_report]]*
