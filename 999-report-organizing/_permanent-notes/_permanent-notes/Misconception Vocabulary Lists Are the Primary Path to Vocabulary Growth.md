## Practical Implications

> [!warning] Misconception: Vocabulary Lists Are the Primary Path to Vocabulary Growth
> Traditional vocabulary study — memorizing word lists, completing fill-in-the-blank exercises — produces weak, shallow word knowledge that does not transfer reliably to reading comprehension or authentic production. This is because list-based study typically engages only shallow definitional processing, produces bare form-meaning pairs without collocational or contextual anchoring, and creates few of the elaborative associations that make words retrievable across varied contexts. List study has a role as a complement to extensive reading, but it is not a substitute for it, and research consistently shows that extensive reading produces superior vocabulary depth for the time invested.
> *— [[vocabulary-building-reading-foundational-report-2026-03-28_report]]*
