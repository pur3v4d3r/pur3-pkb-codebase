## Additional Material

> [!person] Paul Nation (b. 1940)
> Applied linguist at Victoria University of Wellington. The most systematic and comprehensive researcher of vocabulary acquisition, coverage ratios, and word frequency in L1 and L2 contexts. His work established the empirical foundations for the vocabulary threshold and coverage ratio research.
>
> **Key Works:** Nation, I.S.P. (2001). *Learning Vocabulary in Another Language*. Cambridge University Press.
>
> **Lineage:** Built on corpus linguistics tradition (West's General Service List) and L2 acquisition research. His word frequency lists and coverage ratio calculations have become standard tools in vocabulary instruction research.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Nation's coverage ratio evidence provides the empirical foundation for the bootstrapping paradox described in Phase III and the text-selection imperative in Phase V.
> *— [[vocabulary-acquisition-reading-focused-analysis-2026-03-28_report]]*
