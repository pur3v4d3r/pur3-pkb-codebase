# Amotivation (Deci & Ryan)

> [!definition] Amotivation (Deci & Ryan)
> The absence of intentional self-regulation — the person either does not engage with an activity or engages passively, perceiving no contingency between their behavior and outcomes or perceiving themselves as incapable of producing valued outcomes. Amotivation is distinct from low extrinsic motivation (which still involves some regulation); it represents a collapse of the behavior-outcome-value connection entirely. Associated with the highest psychological costs and lowest performance outcomes of all regulatory positions.
