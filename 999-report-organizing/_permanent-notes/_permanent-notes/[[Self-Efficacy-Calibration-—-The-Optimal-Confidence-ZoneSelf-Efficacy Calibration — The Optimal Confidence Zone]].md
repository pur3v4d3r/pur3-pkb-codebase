## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[Self-Efficacy-Calibration-—-The-Optimal-Confidence-Zone|Self-Efficacy Calibration — The Optimal Confidence Zone]]
> Examines the U-shaped relationship between self-efficacy and performance: both very low efficacy (gives up too easily) and very high efficacy (underestimates task demands) produce inferior performance relative to moderately optimistic but calibrated efficacy. Covers the mastery experience cycle in detail, the role of attributed difficulty in efficacy updating, and what "calibrated optimism" actually looks like in developmental terms. Extends Phase IV's recency bias analysis. Priority: MEDIUM.
> *— [[self-regulated-learning-focused-analysis-2026-03-06_report]]*
