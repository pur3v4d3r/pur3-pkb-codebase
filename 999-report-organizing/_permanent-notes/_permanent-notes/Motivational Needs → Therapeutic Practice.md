## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Motivational Needs → Therapeutic Practice
> The frustration-universality / satisfaction-variation principle maps onto a long-standing tension in clinical psychology between manualized treatments (standardized interventions based on universal mechanisms) and individualized treatments (responsive to specific client context). The SDT reconstruction suggests a framework: design the therapeutic environment to reliably prevent frustration of core vulnerabilities (autonomy, competence, relatedness), while allowing the form of need satisfaction to be individually responsive. This resolves the false dichotomy between evidence-based universalism and individualized responsiveness by grounding them in the frustration/satisfaction distinction.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-19_report]]*
