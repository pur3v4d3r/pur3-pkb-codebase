## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[Metacognitive-Therapy-Model]] — Clinical Metacognitive Interventions
> - **Description:** Adrian Wells's Metacognitive Therapy offers the most theoretically developed clinical application of metacognitive theory, targeting metacognitive beliefs (positive beliefs about worry: "Worrying keeps me safe"; negative beliefs about uncontrollability: "I cannot control my thoughts") as the core maintaining mechanism of depression, anxiety, and PTSD. A dedicated Foundational Report would examine the cognitive attentional syndrome (CAS), the self-regulatory executive function (S-REF) model, the core MCT intervention techniques (attention training, detached mindfulness, challenging metacognitive beliefs), and how MCT compares to CBT efficacy in randomized controlled trials.
> - **Connection to this report:** Section 7 mentioned MCT in passing; this would give the clinical metacognitive tradition full treatment.
