## Additional Material

> [!person] Michael Pressley (1951–2006)
> Professor of Educational Psychology and co-director of the Cognitive Strategy Instruction Research Group; among the most influential educational psychologists of the late 20th century in the study of learning strategies. Pressley's research program on cognitive strategies — beginning in the 1970s and extending through the 1990s — established the empirical foundations for EI, keyword mnemonics, reciprocal teaching, and self-regulated reading comprehension. His collaborative work with colleagues including [[Woloshyn,-Pressley-&-Schneider-1992|Woloshyn and Schneider]] directly established the prior knowledge moderator in the EI literature.
>
> **Lineage:** Trained in cognitive developmental psychology; influenced by [[jean-piaget|Piagetian]] constructivism, the information processing tradition, and John Flavell's metacognition research.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Provides the foundational empirical program for EI; the prior knowledge moderator he documented is the central empirical finding this report analyses.
> *— [[elaborative-interrogation-foundational-report-2026-03-30_report]]*

> [!person] Michael Pressley (1951–2006)
> Pressley, working primarily at the University of Maryland and later the University at Albany, was the most productive researcher in the EI tradition and contributed more than any other individual to establishing EI as a validated learning strategy. His research program systematically explored EI's boundary conditions, the prior knowledge moderator, and the generation advantage.
>
> **Key Works:** Pressley, M., McDaniel, M. A., Turnure, J. E., Wood, E., & Ahmad, M. (1987). Generation and precision of elaboration: Effects on intentional and incidental learning. *Journal of Experimental Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition*, 13(2), 291-300.
>
> **Lineage:** Pressley built on the LOP framework and the broader cognitive strategies research tradition (Flavell on metacognition, Palincsar and Brown on reciprocal teaching). His research is the primary empirical foundation for this report.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** The empirical evidence base is primarily the Pressley research program. The analysis develops a mechanistic account that explains why Pressley's empirical patterns — prior knowledge moderation, generation advantage — look the way they do.
> *— [[elaborative-interrogation-reading-focused-analysis-2026-03-30_report]]*
