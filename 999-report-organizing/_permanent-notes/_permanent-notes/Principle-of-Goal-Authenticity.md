# Principle-of-Goal-Authenticity

> [!definition] Principle-of-Goal-Authenticity
> When a person's most central life goals reflect their intrinsic values and authentic interests rather than internalized social comparison scripts, the pursuit of those goals tends to feel more genuinely chosen, more consonant with core identity, and more sustainable across time. Sheldon and Elliot's self-concordance model operationalizes this connection between goal content authenticity and well-being.
