## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Learning Curve of Habit Formation
> Habit formation follows a negatively accelerating learning curve: initial repetitions produce rapid increases in automaticity, with gains slowing as the behavior approaches its automaticity ceiling. Missing occasional repetitions during the early phase does not dramatically impair final habit strength — a finding that counters the perfectionist anxiety many habit-formation attempts are accompanied by.
> *— [[habit-formation-foundational-report-2026-03-28_report]]*
