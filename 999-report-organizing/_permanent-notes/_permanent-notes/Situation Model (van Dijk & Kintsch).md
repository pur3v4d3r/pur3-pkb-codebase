# Situation Model (van Dijk & Kintsch)

> [!definition] Situation Model (van Dijk & Kintsch)
> The mental representation of the state of affairs described by a text, constructed by the reader through active integration of textbase propositions, prior world knowledge, and inferences. A situation model is not a representation of the text but of the domain the text describes; it is the level at which genuine comprehension occurs and the level at which new knowledge becomes integrated with existing knowledge structures.
>
> **Boundary:** A reader can construct a coherent textbase (an accurate internal representation of what the text says) without constructing a situation model (an accurate representation of what the text describes). Test-passing performance can be achieved at the textbase level; genuine knowledge acquisition requires the situation model level.
>
> **See also:** [[reading-comprehension]], [[mental-models]], [[schema-theory]], [[working-memory]]
