> [!evidence] [[Cognitive-Load-Theory-and-SRL-—-The-Resource-Competition-Model|Cognitive Load Theory and SRL — The Resource Competition Model]]

Would examine element interactivity, worked examples as SRL scaffolds, and the conditions under which reducing extraneous load releases capacity for regulatory processes.
