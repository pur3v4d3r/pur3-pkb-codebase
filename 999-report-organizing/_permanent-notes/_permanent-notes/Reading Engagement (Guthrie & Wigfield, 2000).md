# Reading Engagement (Guthrie & Wigfield, 2000)

> [!definition] Reading Engagement (Guthrie & Wigfield, 2000)
> Reading engagement is a term used in the reading motivation literature to refer to the integration of cognitive, motivational, and behavioral dimensions of reading — specifically, the combination of sustained comprehension strategy use, intrinsic motivation for reading, and broad and frequent reading behavior. Engaged readers read more, read across more domains, apply more strategies, and demonstrate steeper reading achievement growth.
>
> **Boundary:** Reading engagement is not equivalent to reading enjoyment; it encompasses strategic processing and persistence under difficulty, not merely positive hedonic affect during reading.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** Engagement is the integrated outcome this report aims to cultivate — not merely enjoyment, not merely competence, but the coherent motivational-cognitive-behavioral complex that produces durable reading development.
>
> **See also:** [[intrinsic-motivation]], [[metacognitive-self-regulation]], [[self-regulated-learning]], [[flow-theory]]
