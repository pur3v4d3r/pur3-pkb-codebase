## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Important Nuance: Resistance Versus Incomprehension
> Accommodation resistance must be distinguished from simple incomprehension. A student may fail to accommodate because:
> (a) They genuinely don't understand the alternative schema well enough to evaluate it (incomprehension, not resistance)
> (b) They understand the alternative but maintain the original schema for motivational, social, or identity reasons (resistance proper)
> (c) They have inadequate sub-schemas to support the alternative schema, so it cannot be "implemented" even if intellectually accepted (sub-schema gap)
>
> Conceptual change instruction that confuses these three will misapply remedies. Case (a) requires better explanation of the alternative; case (b) requires addressing motivation and identity; case (c) requires explicit sub-schema instruction before abstract restructuring is attempted.
> *— [[schema-and-how-they-work-deep-dive-2026-04-08_report]]*
