# Proximal vs. Distal Goals** *(Bandura & Schunk, 1981)*

> [!definition] Proximal vs. Distal Goals** *(Bandura & Schunk, 1981)*
> **Definition:** Proximal goals are achievable within a single session; distal goals are achievable across extended time periods. Research shows that proximal goals are more motivationally effective during skill development because they generate more frequent mastery experiences and provide more timely feedback on progress.
> **Operational Indicators:** A proximal goal specifies what will be achieved *today* — "I will be able to explain this section's central distinction in my own words by the time I close the text." A distal goal specifies a more distant state — "I will deeply understand metacognition."
> **Cross-References:** [[goal-setting-theory]], [[mastery-experience]], [[self-efficacy]]
