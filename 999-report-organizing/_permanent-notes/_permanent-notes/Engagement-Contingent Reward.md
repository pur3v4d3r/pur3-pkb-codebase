# Engagement-Contingent Reward

> [!definition] Engagement-Contingent Reward
> A reward contingent simply on engaging in or starting an activity, without reference to completion or quality. This is the most purely controlling reward type in CET's taxonomy: the contingency "if you do it, you get the reward" makes the external reason for the behaviour maximally salient. Empirically, engagement-contingent and task-contingent rewards show the strongest undermining effects in the Deci/Koestner/Ryan (1999) meta-analysis.
