## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Vocabulary Acquisition → Scientific Literature Literacy
> The multi-dimensionality flattening problem is strikingly visible in scientific reading. A researcher new to a field can often comprehend the approximate meaning of individual sentences in domain papers (the receptive vocabulary analogue) long before they can deploy the domain's conceptual vocabulary accurately in their own arguments (the productive vocabulary analogue). The received view in research training — read widely in the domain, and comprehension and fluency will develop naturally — suffers from exactly the same limitations as the vocabulary reading-primary hypothesis. Active engagement with concepts (seminars, writing, peer explanation) develops productive/generative conceptual fluency faster than passive reading of papers, just as deliberate vocabulary study develops productive word knowledge faster than extensive reading. The structural parallel is exact.
> *— [[vocabulary-building-reading-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-28_report]]*
