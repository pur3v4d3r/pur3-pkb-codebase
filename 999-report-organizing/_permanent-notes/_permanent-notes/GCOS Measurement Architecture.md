## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] GCOS Measurement Architecture
> The GCOS presents respondents with 12 vignettes describing everyday situations (receiving performance feedback, starting a new job, encountering a difficult problem). For each vignette, three response options are provided, each keyed to one orientation. Respondents rate the likelihood of each response on a 7-point scale (the scoring is NOT ipsative — endorsing one response does not lower scores for others). The result is three subscale scores, each reflecting the respondent's tendency toward autonomy, control, and impersonal regulation respectively. Reliability coefficients are typically .70-.82 across subscales, with test-retest stability over several months in the moderate-to-high range.
> *— [[causality-orientations-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-13_report]]*
