# Attribution Theory

> [!definition] Attribution Theory
> **[[attribution-theory]]** is the broad theoretical tradition in social and motivational psychology that studies how people assign causal explanations to events, outcomes, and behaviors — both their own and others'. Originally rooted in Heider's (1958) "naive psychology," the tradition encompasses a family of related models distinguished by their focus on interpersonal attribution (Jones & Davis, Kelley) or intra-personal motivational dynamics (Weiner). The unifying concern is the systematic study of causal reasoning in everyday life and its consequences for affect, motivation, and behavior.
