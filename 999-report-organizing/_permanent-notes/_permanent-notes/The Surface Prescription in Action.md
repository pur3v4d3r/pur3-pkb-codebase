## Examples

> [!example] The Surface Prescription in Action
> A physics instructor teaching circuit analysis might apply the surface-level CLT framework as follows:
> - **Reduce extraneous load:** Integrate circuit diagrams with accompanying explanations rather than placing them on separate pages (split-attention principle)
> - **Manage intrinsic load:** Begin with simple series circuits before introducing parallel circuits (sequencing by complexity)
> - **Promote germane load:** Provide worked examples that demonstrate solution procedures before requiring independent problem-solving
>
> This application is correct, practically useful, and — from the perspective of this Deep Dive — importantly incomplete. The instructor has applied CLT's *effects* without understanding the mechanism that *generates* those effects.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-deep-dive-2026-04-12_report]]*
