## Additional Material

> [!person] George A. Miller (1920–2012)
> Cognitive psychologist whose 1956 paper "The Magical Number Seven, Plus or Minus Two" introduced the concept of chunking to explain the discrepancy between the apparently tiny capacity of immediate memory and the apparently unlimited capacity of human cognition in domains of expertise. Miller's chunk concept became the unit currency of all subsequent expertise research.
> *— [[chunking-and-expertise-development-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> [!person] George A. Miller (1920–2012)
> Professor of Psychology, Princeton University. Established the classical capacity estimate of 7±2 items (1956) and introduced the concept of chunking. Co-founder of cognitive science as a discipline.
>
> **Relationship to other figures:** His capacity estimate was revised downward by Cowan; his concept of chunking was developed further by Simon and became central to Sweller's cognitive load theory.
> *— [[working-memory-foundational-report-2026-04-16_report]]*
