# Adaptive Help-Seeking vs. Avoidant Help-Seeking (Ryan & Pintrich, 1997)

> [!definition] Adaptive Help-Seeking vs. Avoidant Help-Seeking (Ryan & Pintrich, 1997)
> **Definition:** Adaptive help-seeking is the strategic use of assistance to acquire the understanding needed to achieve mastery — asking for explanation, requesting feedback, seeking clarification. Avoidant help-seeking is the avoidance of situations requiring assistance or the solicitation of answers rather than explanations, motivated by the desire to conceal incompetence.
> **Report-Specific Significance:** The distinction operationalizes one of the three core mechanisms through which PAV goals suppress adaptive learning behavior. PAV learners are not less likely to need help; they are less likely to seek the kind of help that produces genuine learning.
