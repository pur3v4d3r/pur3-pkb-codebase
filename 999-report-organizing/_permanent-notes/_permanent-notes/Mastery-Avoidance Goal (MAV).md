# Mastery-Avoidance Goal (MAV)

> [!definition] Mastery-Avoidance Goal (MAV)
> **Definition:** Striving to avoid failure to learn, to avoid forgetting previously mastered material, to avoid performing worse than one's prior standard. The standard is self-referential, but the motivational orientation is avoidant. This is the rarest and most theoretically novel cell of the 2×2.
> **Report-Specific Significance:** MAV goals were [[john-elliot|Andrew Elliot]]'s theoretical contribution — the cell that had no home in earlier frameworks. They appear predominantly in perfectionist individuals and in older adults concerned with maintaining existing competencies.
