## Practical Implications

> [!warning] Limitation 3: The Measurement Confound
> The relative scoring approach of the Aspiration Index — which captures the priority of extrinsic goals *relative to* intrinsic ones — may conflate true goal content preferences with response styles. A person who rates *everything* as highly important will appear to have neither a strongly intrinsic nor strongly extrinsic orientation. And the relative importance score means that a person can "appear" more extrinsic simply by caring deeply about intrinsic goals *less*, rather than by caring more about extrinsic ones. More recent measurement approaches have attempted to address this, but it remains a methodological consideration when interpreting AI scores.
> *— [[goal-contents-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-13_report]]*
