## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Protocol 3: N=1 Self-Experimentation Design
> **Goal:** Determine YOUR specific dual-task cost empirically
> **Method:**
> 1. Select 4 comparable reading materials (similar topic, difficulty, length)
> 2. Randomly assign: 2 to walking-reading, 2 to sitting-reading
> 3. After each session, measure comprehension:
>    - Free recall test (write everything you remember in 5 minutes)
>    - Applied test: make an atomic note from the material within 10 minutes — score note quality
> 4. Compare walking vs. sitting on recall completeness and note quality
>
> **Duration:** 2 weeks (one pair per week)
> **Outcome:**
> - If walking ≈ sitting: your dual-task cost is negligible for your material type and WMC
> - If walking < sitting: dual-task cost is measurable; hybrid protocol recommended
> - If walking > sitting: arousal benefit exceeds dual-task cost; continue walking with metacognitive checks
>
> **Limitation:** N=1, no statistical power; treat as calibration, not proof.
> *— [[walking-academic-reading-working-memory-deep-dive-2026-04-13_report]]*
