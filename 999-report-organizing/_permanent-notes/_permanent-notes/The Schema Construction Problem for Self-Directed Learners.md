## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Schema Construction Problem for Self-Directed Learners
> Consider what happens when a highly motivated, intelligent adult attempts to learn a genuinely unfamiliar domain — say, quantum mechanics, or an unfamiliar legal system — independently from written materials, without a teacher. The difficulty they encounter is not primarily linguistic or motivational. It is schematic: the texts assume the reader possesses schemas that the reader does not yet have, and the materials designed to introduce those schemas themselves assume prior schematic frameworks the reader also lacks. This recursive dependency — schemas presuppose schemas — is [[Schema-Construction-Problem-—-Original-Analytical-Insight|the schema construction problem]] in its most acute form. It is the reason that entry-level expertise in a genuinely foreign domain is almost always facilitated by expert guidance, and why attempts to shortcut this guidance through text alone tend to produce unstable, partially constructed schema networks rather than genuine foundational understanding.
> *— [[schema-theory-foundational-report-2026-03-31_report]]*
