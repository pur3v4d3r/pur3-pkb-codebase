## Open Threads

> [!open-question] Examining Your Planning Habits
> Think of a behavior you have been trying to establish — an exercise routine, a study practice, a dietary change. Do you rely on abstract intentions ("I will exercise more") or on specific if-then plans ("If it is 7 AM on a weekday, then I will change into running shoes immediately after breakfast")? Research suggests the latter is dramatically more effective. Why might that be? What psychological work does the if-then structure actually perform?
