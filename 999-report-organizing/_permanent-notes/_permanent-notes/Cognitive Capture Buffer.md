# Cognitive Capture Buffer

> [!definition] Cognitive Capture Buffer
> In PKM design, a zero-friction, universally accessible input layer that records external cognitive content (ideas, references, quotes, observations) at the point of encounter with minimal processing requirements. The capture buffer decouples the timing of initial externalisation from the timing of full integration, allowing the practitioner to offload working memory contents immediately without the overhead of full processing. Functionally equivalent to the phonological loop's role in the multicomponent working memory model: a rapidly accessible temporary store that holds material pending deeper processing.
>
> **Boundary:** A capture buffer is not a permanent store. Material that enters the buffer without subsequent deeper processing contributes to an inbox pile — potentially overwhelming and certainly not an extended cognitive resource in the coupled sense required by the extended mind thesis.
>
> **See also:** [[personal-knowledge-management]], [[cognitive-offloading]], [[GTD]], [[obsidian-pkb-architecture]]
