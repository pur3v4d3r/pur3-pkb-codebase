## Additional Material

> [!person] Meredyth Daneman (active 1970s–2000s)
> Canadian cognitive psychologist, University of Toronto. Co-developed (with Patricia Carpenter) the reading span task as a measure of working memory capacity (1980), establishing the foundational empirical link between WMC and reading comprehension.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Daneman & Carpenter (1980) is the foundational study for Phase IV's account of working memory constraints on situation model construction.
