# Curse of Knowledge

> [!definition] Curse of Knowledge
> A cognitive bias in which possessing knowledge makes it systematically difficult to accurately model not having it, producing vocabulary miscalibration, skipped inferential steps, and expert-novice communication failures. A primary cause of poor communication from experts to non-expert audiences. Requires deliberate perspective-taking and vocabulary calibration to overcome.
