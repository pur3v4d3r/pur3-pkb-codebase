> [!evidence] Situation Model — Updated Through Section 7 (Final)
> **Key Entities:** All previous entities plus: Zimmerman's cyclical SRL model (three phases mapping to the monitoring-control loop); Pintrich's integrative framework (adding motivation and affect to the metacognitive core); Winne's COPES model (information-processing formulation of SRL); externalized metacognition (monitoring and controlling external knowledge systems); computational metacognition (formal implementations of the two-level architecture).
> **Causal Map:** Nelson-Narens architecture → provides monitoring-control mechanism → Zimmerman, Pintrich, Winne embed this mechanism in broader SRL models → each SRL model adds components (motivation, affect, context, phases) around the metacognitive core. Extension to PKM: external notes become object-level → monitoring operates on review cues → calibration problem persists → diagnostic cue provision improves monitoring. Computational modeling: formal implementations → can reproduce empirical patterns → reveal new architectural constraints.
> **Structural Overview:** The full trajectory is now visible: Problem (1) → History (2) → Architecture (3) → Monitoring (4) → Control (5) → Failure (6) → Integration (7). The report moves from the need for the framework through its formal specification through its empirical investigation through its failure modes through its downstream influence.
> **Evolution This Section:** The framework has been situated within the broader landscape of self-regulated learning theory, personal knowledge management, and computational cognitive science. The original synthesis characterizes it as the "metacognitive backbone" of SRL models.
> **Emerging Patterns:** The framework's influence radiates outward from metamemory to SRL to PKM to computational modeling, carrying its core commitments (cue-based monitoring, imperfect modeling, dominance asymmetry) into each new domain. The consistency of these commitments across domains constitutes evidence for their generality.
