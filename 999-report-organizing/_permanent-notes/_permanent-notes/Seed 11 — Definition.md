> [!flashcard] Seed 11 — Definition
> **Q:** What is situated learning (Lave & Wenger)?
> **A:** The theoretical claim that knowledge and learning are fundamentally inseparable from the context, practices, and communities in which they occur. Learning is not abstract cognitive acquisition but increasing participation in authentic practice — specifically, the progressive movement from peripheral to central participation in communities of practice.

> [!flashcard] Seed 11 — Definition
> **Q:** What is the fluency illusion, and why is elaborative interrogation structurally resistant to it?
> **A:** The fluency illusion is the metacognitive error of mistaking the subjective ease of re-processing familiar-seeming material for genuine comprehension or retention. Re-reading produces fluency without encoding depth, generating a false sense of understanding. EI is resistant to the fluency illusion because the generative demand — producing a causal answer — exposes the gap between fluency and genuine comprehension: a learner cannot feel fluent about a why-answer they cannot actually produce.
> **Source:** Phase III, V
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #fluency-illusion, #elaborative-interrogation, #metacognitive-monitoring
> *— [[elaborative-interrogation-foundational-report-2026-03-30_report]]*

> [!flashcard] Seed 11 — Definition
> **Q:** What is metacognitive scaffolding, and how does it differ from task-level scaffolding?
> **A:** Metacognitive scaffolding provides external support for regulatory processes (planning, monitoring, control) rather than for the target task content. A teacher asking "how do you know whether your current approach is working?" provides metacognitive scaffolding; a teacher giving hints about the solution provides task-level scaffolding. Metacognitive scaffolding is theoretically grounded in Vygotsky's ZPD: it enables regulatory performance within the learner's ZPD that could not be achieved independently, while modeling regulatory processes for eventual internalization.
> **Source:** Phase V
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #metacognitive-scaffolding, #scaffolding, #zone-of-proximal-development, #self-regulated-learning
> *— [[metacognitive-regulation-foundational-report-2026-03-31_report]]*
