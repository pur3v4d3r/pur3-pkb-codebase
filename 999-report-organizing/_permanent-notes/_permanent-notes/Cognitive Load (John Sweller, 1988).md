# Cognitive Load (John Sweller, 1988)

> [!definition] Cognitive Load (John Sweller, 1988)
> The total amount of mental effort being used in [[working-memory|working memory]] during information processing and learning. Cognitive load is determined by the interaction between the intrinsic complexity of the material, the quality of instructional design, and the learner's existing knowledge base (schemas).
>
> **Boundary:** Cognitive load refers specifically to demands on working memory, not to general mental fatigue, emotional distress, or motivational effort — though these factors may independently affect learning outcomes.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** This is the theory's central construct, from which all predictions and design principles derive.
>
> **See also:** [[cognitive-load-theory]], [[working-memory]], [[intrinsic-cognitive-load]], [[extraneous-cognitive-load]], [[germane-cognitive-load]]
