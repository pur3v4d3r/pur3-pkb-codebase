## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Report Scope and Purpose
> This report provides a comprehensive, applied analysis of [[Zimmerman's-Cyclical-Model-of-Self-Regulated-Learning]]'s two terminal phases — the [[forethought-phase]] and the [[self-reflection-phase]] — with the primary exemplar task being the reading and deep comprehension of complex academic texts (including the reports generated by Claude for this PKB). The analysis proceeds from theoretical architecture through metacognitive dimensions, into practical tool development (templates, prompts, scaffolding), with particular attention to the motivational-linguistic design of those tools, and culminates in a blueprint for personalised, PKB-integrated self-regulated learning across the iterative cycle. The goal is to understand not just what these phases *are*, but how to *build* them deliberately, how to make them *close* into a generative feedback loop, and how to do all of this in a way that preserves intrinsic motivation and self-efficacy.
>
> **This report is applied throughout.** Every theoretical concept is followed by an operational translation into your context.
> *— [[srl-forethought-selfreflection-applied-2026-03-25_report]]*
