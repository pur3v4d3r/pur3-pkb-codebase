> [!open-question] Testing Your Mechanisms
> Return to the Wason Selection Task described in Phase III. The abstract version (with letters and numbers) elicits correct responses from fewer than 10% of participants; the permission version (drinking-age rule) elicits correct responses from 70–90%. The logical structure is identical. Before reading on, try to articulate *why* familiar social content facilitates performance. Is it because schemas supply the modus tollens step automatically? Because the content makes the falsifying cases salient? Because motivation to detect rule violations is higher? The answer has significant pedagogical implications.
