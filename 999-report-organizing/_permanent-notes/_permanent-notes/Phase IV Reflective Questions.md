## Reflections

> [!reflection] Phase IV Reflective Questions
> 1. CET proposes a mediation chain in which external events → functional significance interpretation → PLOC and competence changes → intrinsic motivation. What are the points in this chain where individual differences could produce consistent deviations from the theory's average predictions?
> 2. The claim that ego involvement functions like an external controlling condition is theoretically bold — it asserts that internally generated pressures can undermine intrinsic motivation through the same mechanism as external reward. What evidence would fail to support this claim, and has that evidence been found?
> 3. If the interpersonal climate modulates how external events are parsed, what does this imply for the design of incentive systems in organizations? Could a reward-for-performance system be designed in a way that preserves intrinsic motivation?
> *— [[cognitive-evaluation-theory-foundational-report-2026-03-31_report]]*
