# Metacognitive Calibration** *(Nelson & Narens, 1990)*

> [!definition] Metacognitive Calibration** *(Nelson & Narens, 1990)*
> **Definition:** The degree of alignment between a learner's judgments of their own learning/comprehension and their actual learning/comprehension performance. Well-calibrated learners know what they know and what they don't. Poorly calibrated learners (typically overconfident, due to the fluency illusion) misallocate study time — spending it on already-understood material and insufficient time on genuine gaps.
> **Operational Indicators:** Calibration can be directly assessed by comparing pre-test confidence ratings with actual test performance. In PKB reading practice, the Cold Reconstruction protocol operationalizes this: comparing predicted comprehension with actual reconstruction performance.
> **Cross-References:** [[metacognitive-calibration]], [[metacognitive-accuracy]], [[judgment-of-learning]], [[fluency-illusion]]
