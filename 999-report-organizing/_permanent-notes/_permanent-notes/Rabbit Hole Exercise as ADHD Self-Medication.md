## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Rabbit Hole: Exercise as ADHD Self-Medication
> **What it is:** A growing literature examines physical activity as a complement to pharmacological ADHD management, showing that acute exercise produces temporary improvements in ADHD symptoms comparable in magnitude (though shorter in duration) to stimulant medication.
> **Why follow it:** Understanding this literature contextualizes walking-while-reading as a specific instance of a broader "exercise-as-cognitive-regulation" strategy that has particular potency for ADHD-spectrum individuals.
> **Time investment:** Moderate — several good review articles available.
> **Where to start:** Pontifex et al. (2013), "Exercise Improves Behavioral, Neurocognitive, and Scholastic Performance in Children with ADHD"; Hoza et al. (2015)
> **See also:** [[ADHD]], [[executive-function]], [[Dopamine]], [[self-regulation]]
