## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Transfer Domain 4: Interpersonal Communication and Collaborative Reasoning
> **Structural Principle:** Metacognitive regulation of one's own reasoning has interpersonal analogues in [[Epistemic-Humility-in-Communication|epistemic humility practices]] in dialogue and collaborative reasoning: recognizing the limits of one's own perspective, monitoring for self-serving reasoning biases, checking inferences before asserting them as facts, and explicitly marking the distinction between known and inferred in discourse.
>
> **Concrete Application:** High-functioning collaborative teams systematically implement practices that externalize the metacognitive monitoring functions that individuals fail to reliably perform internally: red team/blue team structures assign the monitoring role to designated adversarial thinkers; premortems require the metacognitive behavior of imagining failure before it occurs; structured disagreement protocols ([[Dialectical-Thinking|dialectical thinking]] practices) require explicit articulation of the opposing position before advocates may respond; [[Argument-Mapping|argument mapping]] externalizes inference structure in ways that make logical gaps and unsupported claims visible. In each case, the interpersonal structure implements a metacognitive monitoring function that individual cognition performs unreliably.
>
> **See also:** [[critical-thinking]], [[Argument-Mapping]], [[Dialectical-Thinking]], [[Collaborative-Intelligence]]
