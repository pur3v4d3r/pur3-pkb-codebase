# Universal Need (SDT Usage)

> [!definition] Universal Need (SDT Usage)
> In SDT, a need is "universal" if its frustration produces ill-being for all humans regardless of cultural context, developmental stage, or individual difference. This is distinct from the claim that its satisfaction takes the same form across cultures. The dialectical process supports the former claim (universal frustration effects) much more strongly than the latter (universal satisfaction forms). A more precise SDT formulation would state: "All humans are reliably vulnerable to motivational deterioration when their autonomy, competence, or relatedness needs are frustrated, though what satisfies these needs varies across cultural and individual contexts."
