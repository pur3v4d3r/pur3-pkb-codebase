## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[The-Situation-Model-in-Knowledge-Management]]
> The [[situation-model|situation model]] is the gold standard for reading comprehension, but it has underexplored implications for knowledge management design. Notes that support situation model construction — providing the inferential bridges that sparse propositions leave implicit, encoding causal and temporal relationships explicitly, connecting propositions to broader schemas — are more comprehension-supporting than those that merely record propositions. A design-oriented investigation of how PKB note-making practices can be optimized for situation model construction rather than mere propositional capture.
> *— [[silent-reading-fluency-speed-comprehension-focused-analysis-2026-03-21_report]]*
