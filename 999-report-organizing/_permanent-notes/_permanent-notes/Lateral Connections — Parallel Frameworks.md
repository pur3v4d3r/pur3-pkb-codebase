## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Lateral Connections — Parallel Frameworks
> - [[self-determination-theory]] — Parallel motivational framework; shares the internal/external locus dimension (internalization vs. external regulation); competence need relates to controllability and stability dimensions.
> - [[growth-mindset-theory]] — Downstream expression of Incremental Theory of Intelligence, which is Attribution Theory's account of the implicit schema generating effort-attribution tendencies.
> - [[expectancy-value-theory]] — Weiner's stability-expectancy link is a founding contribution to the Expectancy component of Expectancy-Value Theory.
> - [[achievement-goal-theory]] — Achievement goal orientations are both attribution antecedents and consequences; mastery vs. performance goal structure maps onto attribution profiles.
> - [[Control-Value-Theory]] — Pekrun's Control-Value Theory is a direct extension of Attribution Theory's attribution-emotion link into the domain of academic emotions including anxiety and enjoyment.
> *— [[attribution-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
