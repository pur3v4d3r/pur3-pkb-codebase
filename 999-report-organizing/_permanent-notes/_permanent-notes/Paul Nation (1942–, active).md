## Additional Material

> [!person] Paul Nation (1942–, active)
> **Core Contribution:** The most comprehensive empirical and theoretical treatment of vocabulary frequency, text coverage, and vocabulary learning load. Nation's BNC/COCA word frequency lists, his 95–98% coverage threshold research, and the Vocabulary Levels Test (a diagnostic for vocabulary size by frequency band) constitute the empirical infrastructure on which the reconstructed model is built.
>
> **Role in the Dialectical Arc:** Primarily *challenge and reconstruction* — Nation's frequency research provides both the most precise articulation of the threshold constraint (challenging the received view) and the frequency-indexed structure of the reconstructed model.
>
> **Relationship to Other Figures:** Nation was influenced by Thorndike's earlier frequency research and Michael West's General Service List; his work directly informs Schmitt's multiple-exposure research and Laufer's threshold studies.
>
> **Key Works:** *Learning Vocabulary in Another Language* (2001, 2022); *Teaching Vocabulary: Strategies and Techniques* (2008).
> *— [[vocabulary-building-reading-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-28_report]]*
