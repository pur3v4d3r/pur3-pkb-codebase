## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Transfer Domain 1: Teaching and Instructional Communication
> **Structural principle:** Teachers are professional communicators who routinely commit the Expert Blind Spot (assuming student mental models resemble their own), fail to build common ground before transmitting new knowledge, and trigger defensive communication through evaluative feedback. The entire arc of this report's claims translates directly:
>
> Effective teaching requires (1) diagnosing student mental models before presenting information (common ground audit); (2) structuring new content with appropriate signposting to manage extraneous cognitive load; (3) providing process-level feedback rather than dispositional praise/blame; (4) maintaining autonomy-supportive communication rather than controlling language; (5) developing classroom communication that is non-defensive and non-contemptuous about student errors.
>
> **Boundary condition:** Instructional communication differs from dyadic conversation in scale — a teacher cannot achieve the grounding loops that two conversational partners can. Advances in formative assessment (exit tickets, in-class questioning, think-pair-share) are partly compensatory strategies for the grounding deficit inherent in one-to-many communication.
>
