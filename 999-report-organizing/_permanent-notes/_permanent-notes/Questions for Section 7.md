> [!reflection] Questions for Section 7
> 1. Honestly assess your own comprehension monitoring during reading. Do you reliably detect comprehension failures in dense academic texts, or do you find that you often "remember" reading passages without being able to reconstruct their content under retrieval testing?
> 2. Which phase of Zimmerman's SRL cycle represents your current development zone — forethought, performance, or self-reflection? What specific behaviors would concretely improve your practice in that phase?
> 3. The social metacognition findings (reciprocal teaching, collaborative reading) suggest that solitary reading may not be the optimal modality for all learning objectives. When in your own intellectual practice might collaborative and social reading structures be more appropriate than private reading?
