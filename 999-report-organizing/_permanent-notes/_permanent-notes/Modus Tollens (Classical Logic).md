# Modus Tollens (Classical Logic)

> [!definition] Modus Tollens (Classical Logic)
> A valid deductive argument form: "If P then Q; not-Q; therefore not-P." It denies the consequent of a conditional to deny its antecedent, reasoning backward from a failed prediction to a refuted hypothesis. Modus tollens is the logical basis of scientific falsification and mathematical proof by contrapositive. It is psychologically more demanding than modus ponens and reliably elicits lower accuracy rates in laboratory studies, particularly in abstract presentations.
