## Additional Material

> [!person] Stephen Krashen (1941–, active)
> **Core Contribution:** The Input Hypothesis and its application to vocabulary through the *reading hypothesis*. Krashen's theoretical and advocacy work, combined with surveys of reading research, established extensive reading as the centrepiece of vocabulary acquisition pedagogy in English-language education from the 1980s forward.
>
> **Role in the Dialectical Arc:** Primary *thesis source* — Krashen articulates the strongest and most influential version of the reading-primary hypothesis, and his arguments provide the steel-manned position that the dialectical process engages.
>
> **Relationship to Other Figures:** Krashen built on Chomsky's language acquisition device framework and on naturalness assumptions from applied linguistics; Nation and Laufer represent the empirical pushback from frequency and threshold research.
>
> **Key Works:** *Principles and Practice in Second Language Acquisition* (1982); *The Power of Reading* (1993, 2004).
> *— [[vocabulary-building-reading-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-28_report]]*
