## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[self-determined-learning|Heutagogy]] and Metacognitive Reflection in Self-Directed Learning
> - **Description:** The synthesis section introduced "epistemic sovereignty" as a frame connecting metacognitive reflection to broader self-directed learning traditions. A dedicated report would examine how metacognitive reflection functions within fully self-directed learning (beyond the school and workplace contexts that dominate the literature), including: the developmental stages through which learners move from pedagogy to andragogy to heutagogy; how metacognitive reflection is the cognitive mechanism through which self-directed learners identify and direct their own learning; the role of reflection in PKM systems as institutionalized self-directed learning infrastructure; and the connections to lifelong learning theory and epistemological development models (Perry, Belenky, King & Kitchener's Reflective Judgment model).
> - **Connection to this report:** The Synthesis section introduced "epistemic sovereignty"; this report would develop it fully and contextualize it in self-direction literature.
