> [!flashcard] Seed 3 — Definition
> **Q:** What is the fluency illusion and what cognitive mechanism produces it?
> **A:** The misattribution of surface-reading fluency (decoding ease, lexical retrieval speed) to comprehension depth. Produced by the monitoring system's reliance on processing ease as a comprehension signal — but fluency carries information about surface-level processing, not situation-model construction. Cause: monitoring operates at the wrong level of text representation.
> **Source:** Phase II, Section 2.2
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #metacognition, #fluency-illusion, #monitoring-failure

> [!flashcard] Seed 3 — Definition
> **Q:** What is the Fluency Illusion, and why is it specifically dangerous for situation model construction?
>
> **A:** The Fluency Illusion is the metacognitive error in which ease of processing (fluency) is mistaken for depth of understanding. It is specifically dangerous for situation model construction because it produces a false comprehension signal that terminates comprehension effort before the situation model is built. A reader can fluently build a textbase, experience the ease of processing as understanding, and never engage the active inference generation that situation model construction requires.
>
> **Source:** Phase III, Evidence Area 2; Phase IV, Mechanism 2
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Type:** Definition
> **Tags:** #fluency-illusion #textbase-trap #metacognition #comprehension-monitoring
