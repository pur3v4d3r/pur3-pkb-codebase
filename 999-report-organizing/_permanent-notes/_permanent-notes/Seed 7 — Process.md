> [!flashcard] Seed 7 — Process
> **Q:** What does the evidence say about habit formation timelines, and what is wrong with the "21 days" claim?
> **A:** Lally et al. (2010) found formation timelines ranged from 18 to 254 days, with a median around 66 days. The 21-day claim originated from Maltz's plastic surgery patients and has no experimental basis. Formation time varies substantially with habit complexity and individual differences.
> **Source:** Phase IV
> **Difficulty:** Basic
> **Tags:** #habit-formation, #evidence, #misconceptions

> [!flashcard] Seed 7 — Process
> **Q:** What is the estimated probability of learning a new word from a single reading encounter, and how many encounters are typically required for full word knowledge?
> **A:** Single-encounter learning probability: approximately 5–15%. Full, robust word knowledge typically requires 8–20 spaced, varied encounters. The low single-encounter rate is offset by high reading volume over years.
> **Source:** Phase III, Theoretical Landscape
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
