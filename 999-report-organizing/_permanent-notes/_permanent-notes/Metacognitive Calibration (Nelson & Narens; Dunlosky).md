# Metacognitive Calibration (Nelson & Narens; Dunlosky)

> [!definition] Metacognitive Calibration (Nelson & Narens; Dunlosky)
> The degree of correspondence between a learner's metacognitive judgements (confidence in their own understanding) and their actual performance. Well-calibrated learners accurately distinguish what they know from what they do not; poorly calibrated learners overestimate comprehension, producing the illusion of understanding.
>
> **Boundary:** Calibration is domain-specific and context-sensitive. A learner may be well-calibrated in familiar domains and poorly calibrated in novel ones. Calibration accuracy can be improved through generation-based testing, delayed assessment, and structured self-questioning.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** The overconfidence effect makes calibration the central challenge of self-assessment in PKM; the report proposes capability-based goal criteria as a calibration-improving mechanism.
>
> **See also:** [[calibration]], [[metacognition]], [[metacognitive-monitoring]], [[nelson-narens-model]]
