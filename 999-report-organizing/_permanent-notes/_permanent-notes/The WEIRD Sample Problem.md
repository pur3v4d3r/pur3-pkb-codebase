## Practical Implications

> [!warning] The WEIRD Sample Problem
> Most acquisition rate studies have been conducted with school-aged L1 English readers or adult L2 English learners in controlled laboratory conditions. The degree to which findings generalise to adult self-directed readers engaging with domain-specific academic texts — the primary audience for this analysis — is an open empirical question. The vocabulary threshold and context quality effects likely operate across populations, but the precise quantitative thresholds and processing depth dynamics may differ for adults with extensive prior knowledge in specific domains.
> *— [[vocabulary-acquisition-reading-focused-analysis-2026-03-28_report]]*
