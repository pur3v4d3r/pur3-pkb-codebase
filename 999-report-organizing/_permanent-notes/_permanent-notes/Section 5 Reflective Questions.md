## Reflections

> [!reflection] Section 5 Reflective Questions
> 1. Engle and Kane's research suggests that working memory capacity differences are primarily differences in controlled attention, not storage capacity. What methodological approaches could distinguish these two possibilities, and what evidence currently favors the controlled attention account?
> 2. The central executive interfaces with long-term memory to activate relevant schemas. How does this interaction explain why experts can handle more complex information in a domain than novices — is it that their central executive is stronger, or that long-term memory reduces the demands on the executive?
> 3. If the central executive is the seat of voluntary cognitive control, what practices (metacognitive, behavioral, environmental) are most effective at amplifying its effective capacity or reducing unnecessary demands on it?
> *— [[baddeley-model-of-working-memory-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
