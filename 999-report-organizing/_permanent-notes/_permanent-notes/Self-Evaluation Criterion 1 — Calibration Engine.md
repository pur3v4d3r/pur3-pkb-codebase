## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Self-Evaluation: Criterion 1 — Calibration Engine
> **Goal:** Explain the Calibration Engine synthesis without the report.
>
> *Attempt:* The Forethought-Reflection cycle is a recursive system whose structural output is metacognitive calibration — an increasingly accurate self-model. Forethought sets a prediction (goal + comprehension criterion). Performance generates evidence. Self-Reflection evaluates prediction vs. evidence, generates attribution (ideally controllable), and produces an adaptive inference that updates both strategy and self-model. Over many well-executed cycles, the cumulative output is not just content knowledge but knowing *what you know, at what depth, with what confidence.* Calibration is not targeted explicitly — it emerges as a consequence of building the cycle well.
>
> **Criterion met?** Yes, with one gap: I'm still fuzzy on the precise mechanism by which the adaptive inference updates the self-model specifically (as opposed to just updating next-session strategy). The concepts may be the same thing, but I don't have clarity on this distinction yet.
