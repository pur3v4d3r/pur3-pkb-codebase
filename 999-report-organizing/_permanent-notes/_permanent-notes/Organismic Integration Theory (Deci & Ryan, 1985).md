# Organismic Integration Theory (Deci & Ryan, 1985)

> [!definition] Organismic Integration Theory (Deci & Ryan, 1985)
> SDT's sub-theory describing the process by which externally regulated behavior is progressively internalized and integrated into the self. The internalization continuum runs from external regulation through introjected regulation, identified regulation, and integrated regulation. OIT's empirical claims about the benefits of higher-quality internalization are well-supported; its teleological claim that internalization naturally tends toward integration is the dialectically challenged element. The reconstructed version: internalization can proceed toward integration under sustained need-supportive conditions, but this progression is contextually reversible and should be understood as a conditional achievement rather than a natural trajectory.
