## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Neural Bottleneck Claim
> The dorsolateral [[prefrontal-cortex]] (dlPFC) is the single locus of meaningful neural competition between walking and academic reading. All other neural systems involved in the two tasks are architecturally distinct. The dual-task cost of walking-while-reading is, at the neural level, a **dlPFC time-sharing problem** — and the magnitude of this cost depends on how much dlPFC resource walking actually requires, which depends on the automaticity of the locomotion.
> *— [[walking-academic-reading-working-memory-deep-dive-2026-04-13_report]]*
