## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] PAP Goals as Approach Motivation Under a Precarious Standard
> The contested status of PAP goals makes theoretical sense if we understand them as *approach motivation operating under a precarious evaluative standard*. The approach component — moving toward positive competence — confers genuine advantages. But the normative standard introduces fragility: it depends on the performance of others (uncontrollable), on evaluative judgments (external), and on zero-sum distributions of relative standing. MAP goals share the approach orientation but operate under a standard (self-referenced mastery) that is more robustly under the learner's control. The contrast illuminates why approach-avoidance is more fundamental than mastery-performance: it is the approach orientation, not the mastery standard, that drives the core adaptive benefits of MAP goals. PAP goals capture the approach orientation but apply it to a more fragile standard, producing context-dependent outcomes. The question is not whether to approach, but what to approach.
> *— [[achievement-goal-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-24_report]]*
