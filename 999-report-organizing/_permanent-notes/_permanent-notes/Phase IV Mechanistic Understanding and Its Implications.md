## Reflections

> [!reflection] Phase IV: Mechanistic Understanding and Its Implications
> 1. The mechanism chain — cued prior knowledge retrieval → elaborative integration → networked encoding — reveals that EI is not primarily a memory technique but a *knowledge integration* technique. What are the implications of this reframing for how EI should be taught to learners?
> 2. The interaction between EI and cognitive load suggests that environmental conditions significantly moderate EI's effectiveness. For your own reading practice, what are the primary sources of extraneous cognitive load that might compromise EI execution?
> 3. If EI's contribution to situation model construction is primarily in the causal dimension, which types of academic texts would benefit most from systematic EI deployment, and which types least? Consider the contrast between narrative texts, argumentative texts, and scientific expository texts.
> *— [[elaborative-interrogation-foundational-report-2026-03-30_report]]*
