## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Processing Depth Audit for a Study Session
> Before concluding study, ask:
> - [ ] Have I explained the material in my own words?
> - [ ] Have I connected this to at least 2 things I already knew?
> - [ ] Have I generated at least one concrete example?
> - [ ] Have I asked "why" about at least one major claim?
> - [ ] Have I related any content to personal experience?
> - [ ] If I were asked to explain this to someone else in 5 minutes, could I?
>
> If fewer than 4 boxes are checked, the study session likely involved primarily shallow processing and should be extended with elaborative activities.
> *— [[levels-of-processing-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-07_report]]*
