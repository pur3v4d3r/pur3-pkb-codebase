## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[metacognitive-calibration]] — Deep Exploration
> - **Description:** The accuracy of metacognitive judgments — the correspondence between expressed confidence and actual performance — is both the primary operational measure of metacognitive quality and a trainable skill with documented instructional interventions. A dedicated treatment would examine the measurement methodology (overconfidence, underconfidence, resolution, calibration curves), the cognitive mechanisms underlying miscalibration (fluency illusion, hard-easy effect, knowledge-in-pieces), and the full portfolio of calibration-improving interventions (test-restudying comparison, three-stage metacognitive challenge, delayed JOL protocols, externalizing confidence with numerical ranges).
> - **Connection to this report:** Section 5 treated the fluency illusion as the primary calibration distortion; a dedicated report would examine calibration more fully and systematically.
> - **Priority:** High
> - **Suggested report type:** Foundational Report
> - **Prerequisites:** [[metacognition]], [[nelson-narens-model]], [[judgment-of-learning]]
