## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[Expertise-in-Ill-Structured-Domains]] — Chunking Beyond Well-Defined Problems
> **Description:** Much of the chunking and expertise research has been conducted in well-structured domains (chess, physics, mathematics) where problems have clear solutions and performance can be objectively assessed. Expertise in ill-structured domains — teaching, psychotherapy, management, design, creative arts — may involve different chunking processes and different developmental trajectories.
>
> **Connection to this report:** Section VIII acknowledges domain-specificity as a limitation, and the chess paradigm's dominant role in the literature may have biased theoretical development. An examination of ill-structured domains would test the generality of the chunking framework.
>
> **Priority:** Medium
> **Suggested report type:** Annotated Critical Analysis — examining the evidence for and against chunking theory's applicability in ill-structured domains
> **Prerequisites:** [[expertise-development]], [[domain-knowledge]], [[Naturalistic-Decision-Making]]
