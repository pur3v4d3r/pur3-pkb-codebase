# Autonomy Need (SDT)

> [!definition] Autonomy Need (SDT)
> The need for [[autonomy-need|autonomy]] in SDT refers to the experience of volition — of acting from one's own choice and with a sense of psychological ownership over one's behavior. Critically, SDT's definition of autonomy is not synonymous with independence or individualism. A person can be deeply connected to others, fully responsive to external guidelines, and even engaged in activities that were externally initiated — and still experience high autonomy, provided that their engagement is governed by values they have genuinely internalized and endorse as their own. Autonomy is thus about the *locus of initiation and regulation* of behavior as subjectively experienced, not about the absence of social influence.
