## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 2 — Framework: Nelson-Narens
> **Q:** Describe the two levels and two information flows in the Nelson-Narens metacognitive monitoring-control framework.
> **A:** Object level: first-order cognitive processes (studying, reasoning, retrieving). Meta level: metacognitive processes that observe and control the object level. Upward flow (monitoring): the meta level receives information about the object level's state (via JOLs, FOK, EOL). Downward flow (control): the meta level sends commands that modify object-level processes (study more, stop, seek help).
> **Source:** Section 3
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
