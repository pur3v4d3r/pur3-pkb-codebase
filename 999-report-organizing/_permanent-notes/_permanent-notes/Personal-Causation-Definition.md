# Personal-Causation-Definition

> [!definition] Personal-Causation-Definition
> deCharms' term for the phenomenological experience of being the causal origin of one's own behavior, as opposed to experiencing oneself as an instrument controlled by external forces. The origin-pawn distinction inaugurated the concept of perceived locus of causality that CET would later formalize and operationalize.
