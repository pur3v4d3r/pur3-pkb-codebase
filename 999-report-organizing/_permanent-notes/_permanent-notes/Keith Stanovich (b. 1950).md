> [!person] Keith Stanovich (b. 1950)
> Canadian cognitive psychologist, most recently at the University of Toronto. One of the most influential researchers in reading science, Stanovich contributed foundational work on reading disability, the role of phonological awareness, the Matthew Effect in reading, and the psychology of rationality and cognitive biases.
>
> **Key Works (referenced in this report):** Stanovich, K.E. (1986). "Matthew Effects in Reading: Some Consequences of Individual Differences in the Acquisition of Literacy." *Reading Research Quarterly*, 21, 360–407.
>
> **Lineage:** Stanovich built on earlier work by Chall on reading development stages and on information-processing accounts of reading skill. His work was subsequently extended by Perfetti (verbal efficiency), Nation and Waring (coverage thresholds), and a generation of researchers on print exposure and vocabulary.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** The Matthew Effect is the empirical phenomenon that frames the entire analytical investigation — the observation that requires a mechanistic explanation, which the Cognitive Slack Model provides.

> [!person] Keith Stanovich (b. 1950)
> Cognitive psychologist and reading researcher at the University of Toronto. Originator of the Matthew Effect in reading (1986) and a major contributor to the empirical science of individual differences in reading development. Also known for his work on [[dual-process-theory]] as applied to human rationality.
>
> **Key Works:** Stanovich, K.E. (1986). Matthew effects in reading: Some consequences of individual differences in the acquisition of literacy. *Reading Research Quarterly*, 21, 360–407.
>
> **Lineage:** Extended the reading science tradition of Perfetti and Gough. His Matthew Effect work synthesised cognitive, developmental, and sociological perspectives. His later work with West and Cunningham on print exposure as a predictor of vocabulary knowledge provides indirect support for the spiral model.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** The Matthew Effect is the empirical pattern this report's spiral model explains mechanistically. Stanovich identified the trajectory; this report specifies the cognitive architecture that generates it.
> *— [[vocabulary-acquisition-reading-focused-analysis-2026-03-28_report]]*

> [!person] Keith Stanovich (b. 1950)
> Canadian cognitive psychologist and reading researcher; professor emeritus at the University of Toronto. Best known for his 1986 paper "Matthew Effects in Reading," which articulated the self-amplifying vocabulary-comprehension dynamic that has become central to reading science. Also known for his work on cognitive biases, rational thinking, and the distinction between fluid and crystallized intelligence.
>
> **Lineage:** Influenced by Perfetti's reading comprehension program; independently converged on similar conclusions about the centrality of vocabulary to comprehension.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Stanovich's Matthew Effect framework structures Phase III's Vocabulary-Comprehension Spiral section.
