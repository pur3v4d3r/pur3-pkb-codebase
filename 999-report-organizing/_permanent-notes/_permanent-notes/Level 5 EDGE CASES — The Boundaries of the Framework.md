## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Level 5: EDGE CASES — The Boundaries of the Framework
> **Zoom progression:** Level 4 showed how element interactivity produces the CLT instructional effects through a consistent dynamic. This level examines the *boundaries* — the situations where element interactivity's clean explanatory picture encounters complications, anomalies, or outright failures. These are not merely "exceptions" — they are the analytically richest territory in any theory, because they reveal where the theory's mechanisms are incomplete.
> **What you'll see at this level:** Six edge cases where element interactivity either fails to predict outcomes, requires supplementation by additional mechanisms, or produces contradictions that the theory has not fully resolved.
> **Specialist value:** A specialist who knows only where a theory works is half a specialist. Knowing where it *breaks* is what enables genuine theoretical progress.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-deep-dive-2026-04-12_report]]*
