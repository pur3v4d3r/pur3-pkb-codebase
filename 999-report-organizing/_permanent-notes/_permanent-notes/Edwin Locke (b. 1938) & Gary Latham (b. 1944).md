> [!person] Edwin Locke (b. 1938) & Gary Latham (b. 1944)
> American organisational psychologists who jointly developed Goal-Setting Theory through a research programme spanning four decades. Their 1990 monograph *A Theory of Goal Setting and Task Performance* and 2002 review constitute the canonical statement of the theory. Locke's contribution centred on the philosophical foundations (goal-directed action as a basic feature of human nature), while Latham contributed the applied and experimental dimensions. Their work is foundational for Section 1 of this report.
>
> **Key works:** Locke & Latham (1990, 2002, 2006).
> *— [[goal-setting-and-assessment-in-pkm-foundational-report-2026-04-15_report]]*

> [!person] Edwin Locke (b. 1938) & Gary Latham (b. 1944)
> **Primary Contribution**: Developed and validated [[goal-setting-theory]] across 400+ studies from the late 1960s to present. Established that specific, challenging goals consistently produce higher performance than vague or easy goals, mediated by direction, effort, persistence, and strategy activation.
> **Key Works**: *A Theory of Goal Setting and Task Performance* (1990); "Building a Practically Useful Theory of Goal Setting and Task Motivation" (2002, *American Psychologist*)
> **Intellectual Legacy**: Framework is the most replicated finding in industrial-organizational psychology; its integration with SRL research by Zimmerman, Pintrich, and Schunk produced the educational applications central to this report.
> *— [[goal-setting-and-self-regulated-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
