## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[Epistemic-Calibration-and-Bayesian-Updating|Epistemic Calibration and Bayesian Updating]]
> This expansion would provide a rigorous treatment of calibration — the alignment between confidence and accuracy — using the formal machinery of Bayesian probability theory. It would cover subjective probability, prior and posterior credences, likelihood ratios, and the normative ideal of the Bayesian agent who updates perfectly on evidence. Psychological research on calibration (Kahneman and Tversky's findings on overconfidence, availability, and representativeness heuristics) would ground the formal ideals in empirical reality. This node would directly operationalise the fallibilist commitment to proportioning belief to evidence, making it the most immediately actionable expansion from the current report.
> *— [[fallibilism-foundational-report-2026-03-06_report]]*
