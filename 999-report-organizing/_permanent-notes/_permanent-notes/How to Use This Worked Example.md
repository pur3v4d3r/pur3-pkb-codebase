## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] How to Use This Worked Example
> This note is not a summary of the target report. It is a **documented simulation of the reading process itself** — showing what a skilled, self-regulated reader *produces* while moving through a complex academic text. Each technique is labeled with its name and a brief explanation of why it is deployed at that moment. The content used in every demonstration is real material drawn directly from the target report.
>
> When reading your own PKB reports in future, open a blank note alongside the report and replicate this structure. The goal is not to fill every technique slot on every session — it is to develop judgment about which tools to pick up and when. This note gives you a model to imitate until that judgment is internalized.
> *— [[optimal-reading-session-worked-example-2026-03-25_report]]*
