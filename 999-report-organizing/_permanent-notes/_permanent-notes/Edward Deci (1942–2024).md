## Additional Material

> [!person] Edward Deci (1942–2024)
> Professor of Psychology at the University of Rochester. Founder, with Richard Ryan, of Self-Determination Theory. Conducted the first controlled experiments demonstrating the undermining effect of external rewards on intrinsic motivation (1971). His 1975 book *Intrinsic Motivation* provided the initial theoretical architecture; the 1985 co-authored volume with Ryan *Intrinsic Motivation and Self-Determination in Human Behavior* formalized SDT including CET's seven propositions. The 1999 meta-analysis with Koestner and Ryan remains the definitive quantitative synthesis of the CET reward literature.
>
> **Lineage:** Influenced by de Charms' (1968) Origins-Pawns distinction; White's (1959) effectance motivation; Heider's attribution theory. Influenced subsequent SDT researchers including Reeve, Vansteenkiste, Niemiec, and Grolnick.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Foundational — every major theoretical claim in this report traces to Deci's empirical and theoretical contributions.
