## Reflections

> [!reflection] Phase II: Consolidating the Conceptual Foundation
> 1. The accuracy constraint introduces a tension: EI is most powerful when prior knowledge is sufficient to generate accurate elaborations, yet learners who most need help with new content may have insufficient prior knowledge to do so. How would you operationalise a threshold for "sufficient prior knowledge" in a practical reading context?
> 2. The distinction between *generating* an elaboration and *receiving* a provided elaboration mirrors broader debates in learning science about effort and fluency. What does the attenuation of benefit when elaborations are provided imply about the mechanism — is it the *content* of the elaboration or the *act of production* that drives retention?
> 3. Consider your own subject-matter domains. In which of them would you have sufficient prior knowledge to execute EI reliably, and in which would you not? How should you adjust your study strategy at the boundary?
> *— [[elaborative-interrogation-foundational-report-2026-03-30_report]]*
