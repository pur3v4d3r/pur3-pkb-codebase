> [!cite] Kintsch, W. (1998). *Comprehension: A paradigm for cognition*. Cambridge University Press.
> The definitive exposition of the construction-integration model of reading comprehension, the dominant cognitive theory of how readers build text representations from local processing into global coherence. While not primarily metacognitive, the book supplies the comprehension-side architecture that metacognitive monitoring is monitoring.
