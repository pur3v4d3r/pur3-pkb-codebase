## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] SDLRS Validity Evidence and Its Limits
> Multiple validation studies have found that SDLRS scores correlate positively with academic achievement, professional performance, and willingness to engage in continuing education. However, the factor structure underlying the 58-item instrument has not been consistently replicated, leading some researchers to question whether it is measuring a unitary construct or a heterogeneous collection of loosely related attributes. Studies have also found mean-level differences across cultural groups that may reflect genuine variation in self-directedness readiness, or may reflect cultural differences in the meaning of the items, or measurement artefacts. This uncertainty limits the instrument's utility as a universal diagnostic tool.
> *— [[self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-03-11_report]]*
