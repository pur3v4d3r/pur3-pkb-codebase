## Additional Material

> [!diagram] Core Argument Structure of the LOP Framework
> ```
> PREMISE 1: Memory performance varies greatly 
>            even with identical rehearsal time
>            ↓
> PREMISE 2: Processing TYPE (not duration) correlates 
>            with retention differences
>            ↓
> CLAIM A: Memory trace durability is a function of 
>          processing depth
>          ↓ supported by
>          ├── Orienting task paradigm (Craik & Lockhart, 1972)
>          ├── Incidental vs. intentional learning parity
>          └── Maintenance rehearsal failure (Craik & Watkins, 1973)
>            ↓ refined by
> CLAIM B: Within depth, elaboration and distinctiveness 
>          matter additionally
>          ↓ supported by
>          └── Sentence frame elaboration (Craik & Tulving, 1975)
>            ↓ challenged by
> CLAIM C: Encoding-retrieval match may override depth
>          ↓ supported by
>          └── Phonological encoding + rhyme test reversal 
>              (Morris, Bransford & Franks, 1977)
>            ↓ current synthesis
> CONCLUSION: Depth = best general-purpose encoding heuristic;
>             TAP = condition under which depth is overridden;
>             Elaboration + distinctiveness = within-depth enhancers
> ```
> *— [[levels-of-processing-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-07_report]]*
