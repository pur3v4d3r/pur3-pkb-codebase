## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] What the Prior Knowledge Research Shows
> Studies consistently demonstrate that prior knowledge about a text's topic predicts situation model quality independently of, and often more strongly than, verbal ability, vocabulary, and reading fluency. Recht and Leslie (1988), in a landmark study, showed that low-verbal students with high baseball knowledge comprehended a baseball passage significantly better than high-verbal students with low baseball knowledge. The situation model — a coherent representation of *what is happening in the described world* — simply cannot be constructed by a reader who lacks the conceptual structures to interpret the events the text describes. This is not a motivational or strategic problem; it is a structural one. Prior knowledge is constitutive of situation model construction, not merely helpful for it.
> *— [[situation-model-construction-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-29_report]]*
