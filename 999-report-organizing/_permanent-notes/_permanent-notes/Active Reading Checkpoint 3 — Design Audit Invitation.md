## Additional Material

> [!active-reading] Active Reading Checkpoint 3 — Design Audit Invitation
> With these design principles in front of you, take five minutes to perform a rapid audit of your current PKM system:
>
> - **P1 (Simplicity):** What is the single most complexity-generating element of your system that produces the least retrieval value?
> - **P2 (Separation):** Do you have a reliable mechanism for deferring structural decisions until a dedicated time, or do you resolve them synchronously during capture?
> - **P3 (Retrieval paths):** For your last ten notes, how many retrieval paths does each have (folder position, tags, links)? Is one path sufficient?
> - **P4 (Mandatory connections):** Is linking to existing notes required by your workflow, or optional? What proportion of new notes have no outgoing links?
>
> Prioritise one improvement from each principle level before moving to the Appendix.
> *— [[pkm-and-cognitive-load-foundational-report-2026-04-05_report]]*
