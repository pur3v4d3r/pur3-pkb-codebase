# Autonomy Need (SDT; Deci & Ryan, 2000)

> [!definition] Autonomy Need (SDT; Deci & Ryan, 2000)
> The basic psychological need to be the perceived origin of one's own behavior — to act from volition rather than compulsion. Satisfaction of the autonomy need is a prerequisite for full intrinsic motivation and for the deepest forms of internalization of extrinsic regulation. In learning contexts, autonomy support from instructors involves providing choice, offering meaningful rationale for activities, acknowledging learner perspectives, and using informational rather than controlling language.
