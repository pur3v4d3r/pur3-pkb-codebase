## Open Threads

> [!open-question] Mapping the Landscape in Advance
> Five theoretical frameworks are about to be presented. Before you encounter them, consider: what would a *comprehensive* theory of SDL need to explain? It would need to account for where motivation comes from, how competence develops, what role the environment plays, how individual differences affect outcomes, and how SDL changes over a lifetime. As you read each framework, notice which of these questions it addresses well and which it leaves unanswered. The gaps in any single framework are as informative as its insights.
> *— [[self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-03-11_report]]*
