> [!flashcard] Seed 11 — Distinction
> **Q:** What is "habit stacking" and how does it reduce the friction of new habit formation?
> **A:** Habit stacking links a new desired behavior to an already-established habit as its cue: "After [current habit], I will [new habit]." The existing habit already fires reliably via the automatic cue-routine link, providing a dependable trigger for the new behavior without requiring the construction of a new cue-context association from scratch.
> **Source:** Phase V
> **Difficulty:** Basic

> [!flashcard] Seed 11 — Distinction
> **Q:** How does the domain-generality debate in metacognitive knowledge research resolve, and what are the implications for instruction?
> **A:** Evidence supports a layered model: declarative metacognitive knowledge (broad cognitive principles) is domain-general and transfers; conditional metacognitive knowledge is domain-specific and transfers poorly. Implication: generic metacognitive workshops improve declarative metacognitive knowledge but not conditional; metacognitive instruction must be domain-embedded for conditional knowledge development. The general principles do not substitute for domain-specific conditional knowledge but accelerate its acquisition by providing conceptual frameworks for organizing domain-specific experience.
> **Source:** Phase III.6
> **Difficulty:** Advanced
> **Tags:** #metacognitive-knowledge #domain-generality #domain-specificity #transfer #instruction

> [!flashcard] Seed 11 — Distinction
> **Q:** What is the Matthew Effect in reading, and how does it differ from ordinary developmental variation?
> **A:** The Matthew Effect is a self-amplifying inequality dynamic: early vocabulary advantages → easier reading → more reading → faster vocabulary growth → growing advantage. Unlike ordinary developmental variation (which might narrow over time), the Matthew Effect tends to *widen* gaps because the mechanisms producing advantage are also the mechanisms producing further advantage. It operates through the Vocabulary-Comprehension Spiral.
> **Source:** Phase III, Theoretical Landscape
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #matthew-effect, #stanovich, #vocabulary, #reading
