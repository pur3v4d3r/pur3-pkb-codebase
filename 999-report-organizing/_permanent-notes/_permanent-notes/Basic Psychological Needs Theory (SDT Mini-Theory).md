# Basic Psychological Needs Theory (SDT Mini-Theory)

> [!definition] Basic Psychological Needs Theory (SDT Mini-Theory)
> SDT's sub-theory specifying that autonomy, competence, and relatedness are the three organismic psychological needs whose satisfaction is necessary (not merely beneficial) for psychological health and integrated functioning. Distinguishes psychological needs from desires, preferences, or goals — needs are innate and universal, not learned or culturally constructed. The theory's claim that the three identified needs are *complete* (that there are no other basic psychological needs) is the weakest element; the claim that these three are genuinely necessary is substantially better supported.
