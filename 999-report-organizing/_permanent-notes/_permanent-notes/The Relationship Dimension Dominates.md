## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Relationship Dimension Dominates
> In human communication, the relationship dimension — who these people are to each other, what relational context they operate within — systematically shapes how content is received. The same request ("Could you close the door?") reads as polite, impatient, controlling, or intimate depending entirely on relational context. This is why technically impeccable communication can still fail utterly when the relational frame is misaligned.
> *— [[how-to-communicate-effectively-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
