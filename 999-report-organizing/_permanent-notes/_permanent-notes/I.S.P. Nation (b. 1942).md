## Additional Material

> [!person] I.S.P. Nation (b. 1942)
> New Zealand applied linguist; emeritus professor at Victoria University of Wellington. The most influential figure in the empirical vocabulary research program, Nation's contributions include the Word Knowledge Framework (the most-cited model of what "knowing a word" entails), the Vocabulary Levels Test (standardized diagnostic of vocabulary breadth by frequency band), and the development of vocabulary-size threshold estimates. His book *Learning Vocabulary in Another Language* (2001, revised 2013) is the standard reference work in the field.
>
> **Lineage:** Influenced by Thorndike's frequency studies and extended by Nation's collaborators including Rob Waring, Paul Meara, and Stuart Webb.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Nation's framework provides the definitional foundation for Phase II; his threshold research grounds the coverage analysis in Phase II and III.
> *— [[vocabulary-building-reading-foundational-report-2026-03-28_report]]*
