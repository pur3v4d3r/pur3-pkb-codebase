# [[simple-view-of-reading]] (Gough & Tunmer, 1986)

> [!definition] [[simple-view-of-reading]] (Gough & Tunmer, 1986)
> **Definition:** The theoretical model expressing reading comprehension as the product of decoding ability and linguistic comprehension ability (RC = D × LC), where both components are necessary and neither alone is sufficient. Boundary condition: the model correctly identifies decoding as a binding constraint for early and struggling readers but does not specify the cognitive operations required for situation model construction within linguistic comprehension.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** The strongest empirical support for the steel-manned received view; preserved in the reconstruction as correctly identifying decoding necessity while being supplemented by the situation model construction account.
>
> **Cross-References:** [[decoding]], [[verbal-efficiency-theory]], [[reading-fluency]], [[lexical-automaticity]]
