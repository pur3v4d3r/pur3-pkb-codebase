# Expository Advance Organizer

> [!definition] Expository Advance Organizer
> An advance organizer used when the new learning material is entirely unfamiliar to the learner — when there is essentially no relevant prior knowledge to activate. An expository organizer provides new, superordinate conceptual scaffolding that the learner did not already possess, giving them a framework within which the new material can be organized as it is encountered. Example: before teaching the concept of evolution to a student with no biology background, presenting a brief exposition of the idea that living things change over generations in response to environmental pressure provides the conceptual scaffolding into which specific mechanisms (mutation, selection, drift) can be fitted.
