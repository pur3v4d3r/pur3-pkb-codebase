> [!flashcard] Seed 13 — Application
> **Q:** How can the fresh-start effect be leveraged in habit formation?
> **A:** Temporal landmarks (start of week, month, year; birthdays; life transitions) create psychological separation from past behavioral patterns, reducing the weight of past failures and supporting the construction of new behavioral identities. Habit formation attempts are more likely to succeed when initiated at fresh-start moments, suggesting strategic timing of behavior change.
> **Source:** Phase IV
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #fresh-start-effect, #habit-formation, #identity, #behavior-change

> [!flashcard] Seed 13 — Application
> **Q:** What is the KWL strategy, and which metacognitive reading functions does each component serve?
> **A:** K (What do I *Know*?) = prior knowledge activation and establishing comprehension baseline; W (What do I *Want* to know?) = goal-setting and directing monitoring attention; L (What have I *Learned*?) = comprehension evaluation, gap detection, and situation model assessment. Collectively: the KWL sequence operationalizes the forethought-monitoring-self-reflection SRL cycle for a single reading session.
> **Source:** Phase V, Section 5.3
> **Difficulty:** Basic
> **Tags:** #KWL-strategy, #metacognition, #reading
> *— [[metacognition-and-reading-foundational-report-2026-03-31_report]]*

> [!flashcard] Seed 13 — Application
> **Q:** What distinguishes effective from ineffective metacognitive knowledge instruction?
> **A:** Effective metacognitive knowledge instruction: (1) is explicit — does not rely on implicit development alone; (2) is domain-embedded — develops conditional knowledge within domain-specific content; (3) targets the conditional dimension explicitly — addresses when and why, not just what and how; (4) is feedback-rich — structures retrieval-based feedback that makes calibration errors visible; (5) practices metacognitive knowledge in the contextual conditions where it should activate, not only in decontextualized instruction.
> **Source:** Phase V.1
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #metacognitive-knowledge #instruction #educational-application #conditional #feedback
> *— [[metacognitive-knowledge-foundational-report-2026-03-31_report]]*
