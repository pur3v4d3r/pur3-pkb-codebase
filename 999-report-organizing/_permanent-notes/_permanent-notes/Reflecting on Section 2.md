## Reflections

> [!reflection] Reflecting on Section 2
> 1. The SOI model treats selection, organization, and integration as three distinct processes. In practice, how cleanly do these processes separate? Can you think of examples where they occur simultaneously or where the order is reversed (integration before organization, for instance)?
> 2. Wittrock argues that the locus of learning causation is inside the learner's head, not in instructional materials. But instructional design clearly matters empirically — some presentations produce better learning than others. How should we understand the causal role of instructional design within the GLT framework?
> 3. The motivational dimension raises a difficult question: is a theory of generative *learning* (the cognitive processes) necessarily also a theory of generative *motivation* (the conditions under which learners engage in those processes)? Or should these be treated as separate theoretical levels?
> *— [[generative-learning-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
