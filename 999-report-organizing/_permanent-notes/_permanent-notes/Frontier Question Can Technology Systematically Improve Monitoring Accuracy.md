## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Frontier Question: Can Technology Systematically Improve Monitoring Accuracy?
> **The question:** Can adaptive learning technologies, biofeedback, or AI-mediated feedback systems improve human metacognitive calibration beyond what traditional educational interventions achieve?
> **Current best understanding:** Early results are promising but limited. Several technology-mediated approaches have shown calibration improvements, but the ecological validity, durability, and transfer of these improvements remain open questions.
> **What we don't know:** Whether technology-mediated calibration improvements transfer to untreated domains, how durable the improvements are without ongoing technology support, and whether improving calibration through external scaffolding eventually becomes internalized.
> **Active research directions:** Adaptive learning systems that provide item-level calibration feedback; confidence-weighted testing with automated feedback; [[metacognitive-calibration-training]] incorporated into intelligent tutoring systems.
> **Predicted resolution timeline:** 3-5 years for clear evidence on efficacy; 10+ years for implementation at scale.
> **What would change if resolved:** Personalized learning systems that systematically improve learner monitoring accuracy as a deliberate learning outcome, not just incidental to content instruction.
> *— [[metacognitive-monitoring-accuracy-calibration-deep-dive-2026-04-12_report]]*
