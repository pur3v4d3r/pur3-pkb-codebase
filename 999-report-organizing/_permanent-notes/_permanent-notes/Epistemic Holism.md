# Epistemic Holism

> [!definition] Epistemic Holism
> The thesis, associated primarily with W.V.O. Quine, that individual beliefs do not face empirical evidence in isolation but as part of an interconnected system. When experience conflicts with our expectations, we must revise our beliefs somewhere, but logic does not uniquely determine where. Holism supports fallibilism by showing that revision is always possible, and complicates Popperian falsificationism by showing that individual statements cannot be straightforwardly refuted without affecting surrounding assumptions.
