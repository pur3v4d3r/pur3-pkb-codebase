> [!open-question] Topics for Further Investigation
> > [!topic-idea] **[[metacognitive-feelings|Metacognitive Feelings and the Affective Dimension of Monitoring]]**
> > **Description:** A comprehensive examination of how affective signals — feeling of knowing, feeling of difficulty, feeling of confidence, tip-of-the-tongue states — function as monitoring cues within the Nelson–Narrens architecture. This investigation would trace the phenomenology of metacognitive feelings, their informational content, their neural substrates, and the conditions under which they provide accurate versus misleading monitoring information.
> > **Connection to this report:** Sections VI and VIII identify the neglect of affect as the Nelson–Narrens model's most significant limitation. Efklides's MASRL framework is mentioned but not given full treatment.
> > **Priority:** High
> > **Suggested report type:** Foundational Report
> > **Prerequisites:** [[metacognition]], [[nelson-narens-model]], [[efklides]]
>
> > [!topic-idea] **[[metacognitive-calibration-training|Metacognitive Calibration Training: Methods, Evidence, and Limitations]]**
> > **Description:** A practitioner-oriented examination of interventions designed to improve the accuracy of metacognitive monitoring — delayed JOL training, confidence calibration protocols, retrieval practice as a calibration tool, and the ecological validity of laboratory-based calibration findings.
> > **Connection to this report:** Section VI discusses calibration and the Calibration–Regulation Coupling Thesis, but the report does not provide a comprehensive review of calibration training interventions.
> > **Priority:** High
> > **Suggested report type:** Practitioner's Field Guide
> > **Prerequisites:** [[calibration]], [[judgment-of-learning]], [[self-regulated-learning]]
>
> > [!topic-idea] **[[predictive-processing|Predictive Processing and Metacognition: The Hierarchical Self-Modelling Architecture]]**
> > **Description:** An investigation of the structural parallels between the Nelson–Narrens monitoring–control architecture and predictive processing / active inference frameworks. This topic would examine whether metacognition is a domain-specific capacity or a specific instance of a domain-general hierarchical predictive principle.
> > **Connection to this report:** Section VIII and the Far Transfer section draw explicit parallels between the Nelson–Narrens architecture and predictive processing, but these parallels deserve systematic elaboration.
> > **Priority:** Critical
> > **Suggested report type:** Comparative Architecture
> > **Prerequisites:** [[predictive-processing]], [[Free-Energy-Principle]], [[bayesian-inference]], [[metacognition]]
>
> > [!topic-idea] **[[monitoring-regulation-decoupling|Monitoring–Regulation Decoupling: When Self-Knowledge Fails to Produce Self-Regulation]]**
> > **Description:** An examination of the conditions under which accurate monitoring fails to translate into adaptive control — the decoupling phenomenon. This investigation would trace motivational, affective, and contextual factors that disrupt the monitoring–control link, and would examine developmental, clinical, and educational dimensions of decoupling.
> > **Connection to this report:** Sections IV, V, and VI discuss monitoring–control coupling but identify decoupling as an underspecified phenomenon in the Nelson–Narrens framework.
> > **Priority:** High
> > **Suggested report type:** Dialectical Report
> > **Prerequisites:** [[monitoring-control-coupling]], [[self-regulated-learning]], [[self-efficacy]]
>
> > [!topic-idea] **[[Stephen-Fleming|The Neuroscience of Metacognition: From Nelson–Narrens to Neural Implementation]]**
> > **Description:** A comprehensive treatment of the neural substrates of metacognitive monitoring and control, focusing on Fleming's meta-d' programme, the role of the anterior prefrontal cortex, and the relationship between neural metacognition and phenomenal self-awareness.
> > **Connection to this report:** Section VIII introduces the neuroscientific programme but does not give it the extended treatment it warrants.
> > **Priority:** High
> > **Suggested report type:** Foundational Report
> > **Prerequisites:** [[prefrontal-cortex]], [[cognitive-neuroscience]], [[metacognitive-accuracy]]
