> [!person] Andy Clark (b. 1957) & David Chalmers (b. 1966) — Philosophy, Edinburgh/ANU
> **Core Contribution:** Extended Mind Thesis (1998): cognitive processes can extend beyond the brain when external resources satisfy functional equivalence and coupling conditions. Provided the philosophical scaffolding for treating the PKB as a constituent part of the practitioner's cognitive system, not merely a tool.
> **Key Work:** Clark, A., & Chalmers, D. (1998). "The extended mind." *Analysis, 58*(1), 7–19.
> **Relationship to others:** Clark extended his earlier work in being-in-the-world (1997); Chalmers brought consciousness philosophy; joint work influenced Hutchins' distributed cognition empirically.
