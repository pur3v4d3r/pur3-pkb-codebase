> **Q:** What are Dunlosky et al.'s (2013) utility ratings for EI relative to retrieval practice and re-reading?
> **A:** EI received "moderate utility" — above re-reading and highlighting (low utility), but below retrieval practice and distributed practice (high utility). The moderate rating reflects genuine but conditioned efficacy: EI is reliably effective when prior knowledge supports accurate elaboration generation, but not universally effective.

> [!flashcard] Seed 6 — Application
> **Q:** What is the Frame-Before-Fill Asymmetry, and what practical reading intervention does it most directly support?
>
> **A:** The Frame-Before-Fill Asymmetry states that situation model construction is directionally one-way: a structural frame can accept and organize details, but an accumulation of unframed details cannot generate a structural frame. This directly supports pre-reading scaffolding as a prerequisite (not merely a helpful addition) to content reading. Scanning structure, writing a prediction sentence, and formulating a purpose question before reading build the partial structural frame into which content-level propositions can attach. Without this frame, proposition accumulation produces a textbase but not a situation model.
>
> **Source:** Phase VI, Original Synthesis; Phase V, Practical Applications

> **Type:** Application

> [!flashcard] Seed 6 — Application
> **Q:** You are a self-directed learner who reads two hours per day in your domain but notices that your academic vocabulary grows slowly. Based on the depth-of-processing account, what is the most likely explanation and what practice change would the mechanisms predict to be effective?
>
> **A:** Most likely explanation: reading for comprehension suppresses elaborative word processing via goal-structure competition — the primary goal (comprehend the argument) is epistemically satisfied by shallow processing of novel terms, so deep encoding does not occur. Predicted intervention: implement the Elaborative Pause Protocol — complete the comprehension read first (to establish context), then return to acquisition targets for explicit elaboration: infer, contrast, situationalise, and verify. Supplementary direct instruction of high-value domain vocabulary would prime the positive spiral if vocabulary coverage is below threshold.
>
> **Source:** Phase IV, Phase V, Appendix G
> **Difficulty:** Advanced
> **Type:** Application
> **Tags:** #vocabulary, #self-regulated-learning, #depth-of-processing
