## Additional Material

> [!person] Patricia Alexander** — University of Maryland
> **Core contribution:** The Model of Domain Learning (MDL) — a developmental model of how domain knowledge and reading interest interact across three stages (acclimation, competence, proficiency) to shape academic reading comprehension. Alexander's research demonstrates that reading strategies interact differently with domain knowledge depending on which stage the reader occupies.
>
> **Key works:** Alexander (1997) *Mapping the multidimensional nature of domain learning*; Alexander, Kulikowich & Schulze (1994) *How subject-matter knowledge affects recall and interest*
>
> **Intellectual relationship:** Integrates schema theory, interest theory, and strategy research in a developmental frame; compatible with the CI model but adds a longitudinal, motivational dimension.
>
> **See also:** [[patricia-alexander]]
