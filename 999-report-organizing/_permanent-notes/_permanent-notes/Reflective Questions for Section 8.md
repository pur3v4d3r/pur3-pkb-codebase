## Reflections

> [!reflection] Reflective Questions for Section 8
> 1. If constructivism's epistemological anti-realism is philosophically untenable, can the instructional principles derived from constructivism still be valid? What would a constructivist methodology grounded in fallibilist realism rather than anti-realism look like?
> 2. How would you respond to the cultural critique that constructivism implicitly privileges dominant cultural frameworks of knowledge? Can constructivism be adapted to genuinely honor plural epistemologies, or does this require a more fundamental theoretical revision?
> 3. Given the integration of AI into learning environments, what new research questions does constructivist theory need to address? What empirical evidence would help determine when AI should scaffold and when it should "productively" not help?

> 2. The elaborative retrieval synthesis proposes that the strongest encoding occurs when retrieval itself forces elaboration. What study design would most cleanly test this hypothesis, and what results would disconfirm it?
> 3. Depth theory began as a challenger to stage models and has itself been challenged and refined. What is the current best "unified account" of encoding and retention that integrates depth, TAP, elaboration, and distinctiveness?
