## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Why the Three-Component Taxonomy Matters Even After Revision
> The germane load construct was criticized as circular and unfalsifiable (if adding a design feature increases performance, it must have increased germane load; if it decreases performance, it must have increased extraneous load instead). But the taxonomy's fundamental contribution — distinguishing load that arises from content difficulty from load that arises from design inefficiency — remains foundational. The practical implication is clear even without the germane construct: identify what is inherently complex about the content, design instruction to match that complexity, and eliminate all design-imposed overhead.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-foundational-report-2026-03-11_report]]*
