## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[Autonomy-Supportive Teaching: Evidence and Practice]]
> A comprehensive treatment of the autonomy-supportive instructional style as a trainable pedagogical competency, drawing on Reeve's intervention research, the Situations in School questionnaire, and the full body of experimental and observational classroom studies. Would cover the specific linguistic and interactional features distinguishing autonomy-supportive from controlling teaching, the institutional and dispositional factors that pull teachers toward controlling practices, and practical implementation frameworks. Priority: **High** — this is the most direct educational application of the SDT framework and would serve as an operational companion to this report.
