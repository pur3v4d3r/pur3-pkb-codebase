# Controlled Motivation (Deci & Ryan, 1985)

> [!definition] Controlled Motivation (Deci & Ryan, 1985)
> Motivation that originates from external pressure or internal compulsion rather than genuine endorsement. Encompasses external regulation (acting to obtain reward or avoid punishment) and introjected regulation (acting under pressure from an internalized but not accepted standard; ego-involvement). Associated with external or internal-compulsive perceived locus of causality. Not synonymous with extrinsic motivation in the everyday sense — one can be extrinsically motivated (acting for outcomes other than the activity itself) in an autonomous way if one genuinely values those outcomes.
