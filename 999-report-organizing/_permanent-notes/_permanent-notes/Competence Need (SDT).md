# Competence Need (SDT)

> [!definition] Competence Need (SDT)
> The [[competence-need]] in SDT refers to the experience of having an effect on one's environment — of being able to produce desired outcomes through skillful action. This concept derives from Robert White's (1963) notion of *effectance motivation*, the innate tendency to explore and act effectively on the world. In SDT, competence is not about actual skill level (one can be objectively highly skilled and feel incompetent, or be a novice and feel effective) but about the *subjective experience of growth and efficacy* during engagement.
