## Methodology & Sources

> [!methodology-and-sources] Far Transfer Grounding
> The following section applies [[transfer-of-learning|transfer of learning]] principles to extract structural insights from COT that generalize to domains outside motivational psychology. Following Halpern (2003), Perkins and Salomon (1992), and Barnett and Ceci (2002), far transfer involves identifying the abstract structural principles underlying a domain-specific phenomenon and applying those principles to novel contexts where the surface features are entirely different but the underlying logic applies. The value of far transfer is not metaphorical enrichment but genuine cross-domain insight: understanding how the same structural principle operates in different surface contexts deepens understanding of the principle itself.
> *— [[causality-orientations-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-01_report]]*
