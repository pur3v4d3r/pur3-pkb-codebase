## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Achievement Goals → Creative Performance Research
> Research on the conditions supporting creative performance shows a striking structural parallel to the MAP-PAV contrast. Conditions that activate promotion-focused self-regulation (positive affect, autonomy support, intrinsic motivation, low evaluative threat) enhance creative output and quality. Conditions that activate prevention-focused regulation (high-stakes evaluation, normative comparison, performance-contingent external control) suppress creativity, particularly in domains requiring conceptual risk-taking and tolerance for incomplete or ambiguous ideas. The mechanism is the same: approach regulation supports the broad attentional scope and error tolerance necessary for exploration; avoidance regulation narrows attention and prioritizes error avoidance. The transfer encoding: whenever creative quality is the target, evaluate the motivational climate using the PAV-detection heuristics developed in educational research. Diagnostic question: Are participants more afraid of generating bad ideas than motivated to generate good ones?
