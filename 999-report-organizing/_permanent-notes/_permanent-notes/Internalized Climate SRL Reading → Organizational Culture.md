## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Internalized Climate: SRL Reading → Organizational Culture
> Organizational psychologists studying psychological safety (Amy Edmondson) have found that the evaluative vocabulary used by leaders in feedback contexts is progressively internalized by team members and eventually shapes their spontaneous self-evaluative inner speech during independent work. A team whose leader consistently uses process-focused feedback vocabulary ("what did the approach miss" vs. "why did you fail") develops an inner vocabulary for failure processing that supports psychological safety and learning. The structural analogue to SRL is direct: the vocabulary resources your PKB reports provide — through their own inner-speech structure, their evaluative language, and their framing of difficulty — are shaping the internalized evaluative climate you bring to future reading.
> *— [[inner-speech-vocabulary-motivation-self-efficacy-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-25_report]]*
