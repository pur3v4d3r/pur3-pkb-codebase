## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Cultural Considerations in RMT Research
> The [[WEIRD-Sample-Bias]] — the overrepresentation of Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich, and Democratic samples in psychological research — applies to RMT's evidence base as it does to much motivational psychology. Most of the empirical work directly testing RMT propositions has used European and North American samples. [[cross-cultural-psychology]] perspectives raise legitimate questions about whether the specific behavioral correlates of autonomous relational motivation (e.g., explicit communication about relationship needs, individual self-disclosure practices) operate similarly in more collectivistic cultural contexts or in relationship structures governed by different social norms. The basic need framework within SDT maintains that the *psychological* needs themselves are universal; the manifestations and the cultural permissions for their expression are variable. This is an important but insufficiently resolved distinction in the current RMT literature.
> *— [[relationship-motivation-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-01_report]]*
