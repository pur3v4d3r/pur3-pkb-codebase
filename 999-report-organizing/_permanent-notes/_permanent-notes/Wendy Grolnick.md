## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Wendy Grolnick
> Developmental SDT researcher whose foundational work (with Ryan) on internalization in early childhood (the [[grolnick-ryan-1989|Grolnick & Ryan 1989]] paper and subsequent line) established the empirical foundation for OIT's developmental account and continues to anchor the developmental SDT literature.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-internalization-continuum-deep-dive-2026-04-21]]*

## Additional Material

> [!person] Wendy Grolnick
> Developmental researcher who extended CET to parent-child relationships and educational contexts, establishing the foundational empirical program on autonomy-supportive vs. controlling parenting and their differential effects on children's intrinsic motivation, school performance, and psychological adjustment. Her work with Ryan (1987, 1989) remains the most important early extension of CET to naturalistic educational contexts.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Applied translator of CET's predictions — her studies moved CET from laboratory environments to classrooms and families.
> *— [[cognitive-evaluation-theory-foundational-report-2026-03-31_report]]*
