## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Cognitive Bandwidth Liberation as the Unifying Mechanism
> The multiple mechanisms identified in Phase IV — phonological trace decay, schema automation, spreading activation dissipation, the compensation trap — can be unified under a single architectural principle: **reading fluency is a cognitive bandwidth liberation problem**. The human cognitive architecture has a fixed attentional bandwidth. Word recognition, when non-automatic, consumes bandwidth that should be available for meaning-making. The educational project of fluency development is not primarily about reading faster — it is about expanding the effective cognitive bandwidth available for comprehension by driving the resource cost of decoding toward zero. This framing unifies the [[cognitive-load-theory]] perspective (reducing extraneous load to free resources for germane processing) with the [[dual-process-theory]] perspective ([[type-1-processing|Type 1]] automatic recognition enabling [[type-2-processing|Type 2]] deliberate meaning-making) and the [[focal-subsidiary-attention-distinction]] (from words-as-focal to words-as-subsidiary). The common structure in all three is the same liberation of higher-order capacity through automatization of lower-order processes.
> *— [[reading-fluency-automaticity-focused-analysis-2026-03-20_report]]*
