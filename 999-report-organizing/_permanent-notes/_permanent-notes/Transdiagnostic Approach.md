# Transdiagnostic Approach

> [!definition] Transdiagnostic Approach
> A treatment framework that targets psychological processes shared across multiple diagnostic categories rather than developing separate, disorder-specific protocols for each DSM diagnosis. The transdiagnostic approach is motivated by (1) high rates of comorbidity in clinical practice that render single-disorder protocols unwieldy, (2) evidence for common mechanisms (such as emotion dysregulation, avoidance, and attentional biases) underlying ostensibly distinct disorders, and (3) the practical inefficiency of training clinicians in dozens of separate treatment manuals. Prominent transdiagnostic protocols include Barlow's Unified Protocol for Emotional Disorders and ACT's functionally unified framework.
