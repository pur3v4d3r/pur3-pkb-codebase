## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Seed 13 — Process
> **Q:** Why does comprehension monitoring fail in novice/immature readers, according to Brown's research?
> **A:** Novice readers typically monitor only against the lexical standard (do I know the words?), missing higher-order cohesion and consistency standards. This produces the paradox of feeling comprehension has occurred (because words were decoded) when in fact no coherent situation model has been constructed. Skill development involves expanding the standards against which monitoring operates, not merely the monitoring process itself.
> **Source:** Phase IV
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate

> [!flashcard] Seed 13 — Process
> **Q:** What are the four self-motivation belief structures in the Forethought Phase, and what regulatory function does each serve?
> **A:** (1) Self-efficacy — beliefs about capacity to execute strategies; determines challenge level attempted, persistence, and resilience. (2) Outcome expectations — beliefs about what consequences will follow from performance; determines whether the task feels worth attempting. (3) Intrinsic interest — genuine curiosity and task value; the most durable motivational fuel, least susceptible to setback. (4) Goal orientation — the implicit purpose framework (mastery vs. performance); determines how all subsequent monitoring and evaluation data is processed.
> **Source:** Phase II
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #srl, #motivation, #forethought, #self-efficacy
