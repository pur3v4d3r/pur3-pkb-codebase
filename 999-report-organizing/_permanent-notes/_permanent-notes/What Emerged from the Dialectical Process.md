## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] What Emerged from the Dialectical Process
> The insight that explanation is an *output* of understanding rather than its *criterion* could not have been reached by the Respondent alone, defending the original thesis. It required the pressure of the elenchus — the systematic exposure of the gaps between explanation and understanding — to force a reconceptualisation. This is the maieutic function of the Socratic method: not to supply an answer but to create the conditions under which the interlocutor can give birth to insight that was, in some sense, available all along but invisible while the original thesis remained unquestioned.
> *— [[what-does-it-mean-to-truley-understand-something-2026-03-10_report]]*
