## Additional Material

> [!protocol] Four-Area Self-Assessment Protocol (PKB Learning Sessions)
> **Context:** For use at the beginning and end of any significant PKB learning session. Takes 3–5 minutes and implements Pintrich's monitoring framework in miniature.
>
> **Steps:**
> 1. **Before:** Record current self-efficacy for the session's learning goals (1-10) and interest/value level (1-10). Identify potential environmental threats (distractions, resource gaps). Set cognitive, motivational, and behavioral goals explicitly.
> 2. **During:** At natural pause points, check: Am I understanding? Is my motivation holding? Is my effort consistent? Is my environment still supportive? Apply regulation where monitoring signals a problem.
> 3. **After:** Note what worked and what failed in each of the four areas. Attribute specifically (what caused the failures — strategy, environment, motivation, behavior?). Record one adjustment for the next session.
>
> **Success Criteria:** The protocol is working when your post-session attribution analysis identifies different causes across different areas — suggesting that area-specific monitoring is genuinely happening rather than undifferentiated "it went well/badly."
>
> **Common Pitfalls:** Conflating cognitive monitoring ("I understood this material") with motivational monitoring ("I wanted to keep going"). Attributing all failures to effort (a single-area attribution) rather than investigating which of the four areas was the actual locus of breakdown.
> *— [[pintrichs-framework-srl-focused-analysis-2026-03-23_report]]*
