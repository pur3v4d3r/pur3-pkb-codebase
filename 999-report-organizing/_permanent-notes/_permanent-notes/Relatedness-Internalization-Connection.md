# Relatedness-Internalization-Connection

> [!definition] Relatedness-Internalization-Connection
> Relatedness need satisfaction is often underemphasized in OIT applications relative to autonomy support, but the empirical literature consistently shows that genuine relational warmth and involvement are indispensable facilitators of high-quality internalization — the organismic integration tendency requires not just the space of autonomy but the warmth of connection to function optimally.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Relatedness-Internalization-Connection
> Relatedness need satisfaction is often underemphasized in OIT applications relative to autonomy support, but the empirical literature consistently shows that genuine relational warmth and involvement are indispensable facilitators of high-quality internalization — the organismic integration tendency requires not just the space of autonomy but the warmth of connection to function optimally.
