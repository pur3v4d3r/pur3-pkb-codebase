# Richard M. Ryan

> [!definition] Richard M. Ryan
> *Definition pending — derived from 3 source report(s).*

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Richard M. Ryan
> Co-founder of self-determination theory with Deci. Primary architect of organismic integration theory and the internalization continuum specifically. Co-author of the 2017 *Self-Determination Theory* synthesis volume that consolidated four decades of theoretical and empirical work. Continues active programmatic research and is the central living figure in the framework's contemporary development.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-internalization-continuum-deep-dive-2026-04-21]]*

## Additional Material

> [!person] Richard M. Ryan
> Professor at Australian Catholic University and co-founder of SDT. Ryan contributed particularly to the philosophical and phenomenological foundations of SDT, the theoretical elaboration of OIT's internalization framework, and the cross-cultural research program examining SDT's universality claims. Ryan and Connell (1989) established the empirical measurement framework for OIT through the SRQ assessment approach.
> *— [[organismic-integration-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-01_report]]*
