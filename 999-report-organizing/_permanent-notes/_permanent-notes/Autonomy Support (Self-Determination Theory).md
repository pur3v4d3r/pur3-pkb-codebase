# Autonomy Support (Self-Determination Theory)

> [!definition] Autonomy Support (Self-Determination Theory)
> A cluster of interpersonal and instructional behaviours that communicate respect for the learner's perspective, minimise external pressure and control, provide explanatory rationale for requirements, and foster self-initiation. Autonomy support is not permissiveness — it is fully compatible with structured expectations and evaluative standards, provided those standards are presented in an informational rather than controlling functional significance. Contrasted with controlling teaching styles.
