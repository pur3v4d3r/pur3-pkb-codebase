## Examples

> [!example] Herbig-Haro Objects: Jets Frozen in Time
> Among the most visually arresting evidence for protostellar outflows are [[Herbig-Haro-Object|Herbig-Haro objects]] — luminous knots and bow shocks created when the supersonic jets from a protostar slam into the surrounding interstellar medium. First catalogued by George Herbig and Guillermo Haro in the 1950s, these objects appear as small, glowing nebulae aligned in chains extending away from the hidden protostar. The spacing and proper motions of the knots record episodes of variable ejection, providing a time-lapse record of the accretion history of the protostar. Herbig-Haro objects dramatically illustrate the violent dynamism of star formation — a process that sculpts the surrounding medium even as it constructs the star.
> *— [[how-stars-are-created-in-the-universe-foundational-report-2026-04-17_report]]*
