# Automatic-Process-Unavoidability

> [!definition] Automatic-Process-Unavoidability
> The characteristic resistance of automatized operations to intentional suppression once triggering conditions are present - demonstrated through interference effects (Stroop), habit slip errors, and difficulty inhibiting overlearned responses even when inappropriate for current task demands.
