# Transient Hypofrontality Hypothesis (Dietrich, 2003; 2006)

> [!definition] Transient Hypofrontality Hypothesis (Dietrich, 2003; 2006)
> [**Transient-Hypofrontality**:: The hypothesis that during exercise, metabolic resources are preferentially allocated to motor and sensory cortical regions at the expense of the [[prefrontal-cortex]], producing a temporary reduction in prefrontal function. This "transient hypofrontality" is proposed as the mechanism for exercise-induced changes in consciousness (runner's high, flow), reduced self-awareness, and impaired executive function during exercise. Proposed by Arne Dietrich (2003).]
