# SDL as Educational Goal

> [!definition] SDL as Educational Goal
> The aspiration that learners develop the capacity and inclination to take increasing responsibility for their own learning throughout life. In this sense, SDL names what educators are working toward when they design autonomy-supportive environments, employ learning contracts, or engage in coaching rather than direct instruction. This usage is normative rather than descriptive — it says what education *should* produce, rather than what is happening in a given learning episode.
