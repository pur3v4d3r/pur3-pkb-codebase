# Calibration (Metacognitive Accuracy)

> [!definition] Calibration (Metacognitive Accuracy)
> Calibration is the correspondence between confidence judgments and actual performance accuracy — the degree to which a learner's expressed confidence in their answers, understanding, or performance matches the probability of those answers, understandings, or performances being correct. A well-calibrated learner who says "I am 80% confident" on a set of items is correct approximately 80% of the time on those items. Miscalibration is systematic: most untrained learners are overconfident (confidence exceeds accuracy), and overconfidence increases in domains where the learner has less competence.
>
> **Boundary:** Calibration is a metacognitive monitoring accuracy measure, not a performance measure. A highly accurate performer can be miscalibrated (overconfident about the items they got wrong); a low-accuracy performer can be well-calibrated (accurately knowing they are unlikely to be correct). The targets for improving calibration differ from the targets for improving performance.
>
