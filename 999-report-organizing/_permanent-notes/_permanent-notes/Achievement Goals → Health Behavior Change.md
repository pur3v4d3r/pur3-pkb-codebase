## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Achievement Goals → Health Behavior Change
> Regulatory mode primacy operates in health behavior research with remarkable structural similarity. Promotion-focused health goals (approach: I want to be healthy, fit, energized) predict sustained behavioral change and intrinsic regulation of health behaviors. Prevention-focused health goals (avoidance: I want to avoid being sick, unfit, overweight) predict short-term compliance but higher relapse rates and lower autonomous motivation. The structural parallel is exact: approach health goals support the exploratory, error-tolerant engagement with health practices that generates genuine internalization; avoidance health goals activate threat-monitoring that makes the health domain cognitively expensive and motivationally brittle. The self-protection paradox also reappears: people who approach health as a domain for protecting against feared negative states often avoid health-related feedback (medical checkups, weight monitoring, fitness tests) because that feedback might contain failure signals — precisely the feedback needed for adaptive health management.
> *— [[achievement-goal-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-24_report]]*
