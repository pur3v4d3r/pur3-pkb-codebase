## Additional Material

> [!person] Michelene Chi (b. 1950)
> Chi (Arizona State University, previously University of Pittsburgh) is the primary researcher behind the self-explanation effect. Her studies of expert-novice differences in physics problem solving revealed that spontaneous self-explanation — generating causal accounts of worked examples — is the primary mechanism distinguishing experts from novices during learning.
>
> **Key Works:** Chi, Bassok, Lewis, Reimann & Glaser (1989), "Self-Explanations: How Students Study and Use Examples in Learning to Solve Problems"; Chi et al. (1994).
>
> **Lineage:** Influenced by expertise research (Ericsson, Simon) and constructivist learning theory; her work grounded in think-aloud protocol analysis.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Chi's self-explanation research provides the primary empirical evidence for the Generation Constraint and its role in situation model construction.
> *— [[situation-model-construction-focused-analysis-2026-03-29_report]]*
