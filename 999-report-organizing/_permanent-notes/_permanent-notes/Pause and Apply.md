> [!evidence] Pause and Apply
> Consider a concrete learning experience from your own practice — perhaps a time when you felt confident in your mastery of material that you later discovered you had not actually understood. Using the Nelson-Narens architecture, trace the failure: at which point did the meta-level's model diverge from the object-level's actual state? What cues might have led the meta-level to construct an inaccurate model? What control decisions followed from that inaccurate model, and how did they compound the problem?
> *— [[nelson-narens-metacognitive-control-framework-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!evidence] Pause and Apply
> Before proceeding to the micro-analysis of regulatory styles, map your own motivational landscape. For three activities in your current life — one you pursue entirely willingly, one you pursue with some sense of obligation, and one you actively avoid — identify where on the internalization continuum each falls. What environmental or relational conditions shaped each? What would need to change for activities at lower autonomy levels to move toward identification or integration?
> *— [[organismic-integration-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-01_report]]*
