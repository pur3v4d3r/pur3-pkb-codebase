> [!cite] Craik, F.I.M., & Lockhart, R.S. (1972). Levels of processing: A framework for memory research. *Journal of Verbal Learning and Verbal Behavior*, 11(6), 671–684.
> The foundational paper for the depth-of-processing framework used in Phase IV. While not specifically a vocabulary study, its core claim — that elaborative semantic processing produces more durable memory representations than surface processing — is the theoretical foundation for the depth-frequency multiplier.

> **Historical Significance:** This paper was one of the most influential in cognitive psychology, initiating a research program on encoding conditions that transformed memory research and educational application.
