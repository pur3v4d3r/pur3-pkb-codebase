> [!diagram] Argument Map 2: Germane Processing Requires Effortful Engagement
> ```
> EVIDENCE: Levels-of-processing research (Craik & Lockhart, 1972)
>     → Deeper processing → stronger memory traces
>         |
> EVIDENCE: Retrieval practice advantage (Karpicke & Roediger, 2008)
>     → Active recall outperforms passive re-study
>         |
> EVIDENCE: Generation effect
>     → Self-generated answers better retained than provided answers
>         |
>         v
> PRINCIPLE: Cognitive effort during encoding,
>     not passive exposure, drives schema formation
>         |
>         v
> APPLICATION TO PKM:
>     Smooth, frictionless capture produces weak encoding
>     Generative note-making produces strong encoding
>         |
>         v
> TENSION: Frictionless capture is widely promoted as
>     PKM best practice (zero-friction capture philosophy)
>         |
>         v
> RESOLUTION: Distinguish capture friction from
>     synthesis friction — reduce the former,
>     preserve and increase the latter
>         |
>         v
> DESIGN PRESCRIPTION: Capture can be frictionless;
>     synthesis must involve active generation,
>     connection-making, and retrieval under partial cues
> ```
> *— [[pkm-and-cognitive-load-foundational-report-2026-04-05_report]]*
