## Additional Material

> [!person] Mark McDaniel (Contemporary)
> Professor at Washington University in St. Louis; co-developer (with Carol Donnelly) of the definitive mechanistic account of elaborative interrogation. McDaniel's research integrates cognitive experimental psychology with educational application, and his work on the generation effect, desirable difficulties, and EI provides the most theoretically rigorous account of when and why EI works. The 2013 Dunlosky et al. meta-analysis, in which McDaniel is a co-author, provides the most comprehensive comparative evaluation of learning strategies.
>
> **Lineage:** Trained in the experimental cognitive tradition; influenced by Fergus Craik's levels-of-processing framework and Robert Bjork's desirable difficulties research.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** McDaniel & Donnelly (1996) is the central primary source for the mechanism analysis in Phase IV.
> *— [[elaborative-interrogation-foundational-report-2026-03-30_report]]*
