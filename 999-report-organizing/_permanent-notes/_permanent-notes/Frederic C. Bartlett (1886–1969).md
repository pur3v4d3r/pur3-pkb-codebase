## Additional Material

> [!person] Frederic C. Bartlett (1886–1969)
> Bartlett (Cambridge University) developed schema theory in his landmark work *Remembering* (1932), demonstrating that memory is reconstructive rather than reproductive — that people remember schematized, interpreted versions of events, not verbatim records.
>
> **Key Works:** Bartlett (1932), *Remembering: A Study in Experimental and Social Psychology*.
>
> **Lineage:** Influenced by Gestalt psychology and James's functionalism; anticipated by Peirce and Dewey; extended by Rumelhart, Minsky (frames), and Schank & Abelson (scripts).
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Schema theory provides the theoretical foundation for the prior knowledge dimension of situation model construction — specifically why prior knowledge is not merely helpful but structurally necessary.
> *— [[situation-model-construction-focused-analysis-2026-03-29_report]]*
