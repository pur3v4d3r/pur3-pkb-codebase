## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Stability-Hopelessness Link Is the Strongest
> Across the attribution literature, the stability-expectancy-hopelessness pathway shows the most consistent empirical support. Studies using both experimental manipulations (feedback framed as indicating stable versus unstable ability differences) and correlational designs (measuring [[attribution-style]] across individuals) reliably find that stable attributions for negative outcomes predict reduced persistence, lower subsequent effort investment, and affective states consistent with helplessness. The locus and controllability emotion-assignments, while supported, are more susceptible to cultural and individual moderators.
> *— [[attribution-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-24_report]]*
