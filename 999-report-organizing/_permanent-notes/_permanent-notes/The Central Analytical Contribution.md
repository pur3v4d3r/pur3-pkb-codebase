## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Central Analytical Contribution
> This analysis establishes that the primary causal mechanism in the fluency-comprehension relationship is not merely speed or accuracy but **cognitive bandwidth liberation** — the freeing of working memory resources through automatization of lower-level processes. This framing is not merely terminologically new; it has implications for assessment (target automaticity, not accuracy), for instruction (target volume and schema automation, not accuracy or speed per se), for reading disability (automaticity failure is a distinct clinical profile from language comprehension failure), and for understanding why early fluency gaps compound (the Matthew Effect is cognitively guaranteed, not merely motivationally mediated).
> *— [[reading-fluency-automaticity-focused-analysis-2026-03-20_report]]*
