> [!reflection] Level 6 Reflection
> - If the germane load reconceptualization succeeds, what happens to existing instructional design literature that uses the tripartite model? Does it need to be rewritten, or can it be reinterpreted within the bipartite framework?
> - The measurement problem seems fundamental — element interactivity may be intrinsically resistant to precise measurement because it's expertise-relative. > - If adaptive instruction based on real-time element interactivity tracking becomes possible, what are the ethical implications of an instructional system that knows more about the learner's cognitive state than the learner does?
