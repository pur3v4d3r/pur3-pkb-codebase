# Elaboration (LOP Framework)

> [!definition] Elaboration (LOP Framework)
> The richness, complexity, and breadth of cognitive processing applied to an incoming stimulus at a given level of analysis. Elaboration refers to the number of associations activated, the contextual embedding of the stimulus, the number of inferential connections drawn, and the degree to which the stimulus is integrated with existing knowledge structures. Higher elaboration at the same depth level produces more durable and distinctive memory traces than lower elaboration.
>
> **Boundary:** Elaboration is distinct from depth: a stimulus can be processed at the semantic level with either sparse elaboration (simple category membership judgment) or rich elaboration (complex sentence frame evoking multiple contextual associations). Both are cases of deep processing; they differ in elaboration. This distinction becomes important in assessments of what kinds of study activities are most effective — merely processing semantically is not sufficient; semantic processing should also be elaborated.
>
