## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Monitoring-Control Loop
> Nelson and Narens (1990) formalized metacognition as a **bidirectional flow** between object-level (cognition) and meta-level (metacognition) processes. Information flows **upward** through monitoring (cognition informs metacognition about its current state), and control flows **downward** (metacognition modifies cognitive processes). However, recent neuroscience research (Boldt et al., 2022) reveals these processes are only **partially overlapping** in neural implementation—you can have accurate monitoring without effective control, or deploy control strategies without accurate monitoring to guide them. This dissociation explains why students often know they don't understand something (monitoring) but fail to take corrective action (control), or conversely, why they "study harder" (control) without accurately assessing what specifically needs attention (monitoring).
> *— [[cog-psy-critical-thinking-metacognitive-regulation_report]]*
