## Additional Material

> [!person] Richard M. Ryan (1953–)
> **[[richard-ryan]]** — Co-founder (with [[edward-deci]]) of [[self-determination-theory]] at the University of Rochester, later at Australian Catholic University. Ryan provided the metatheoretical framework within which GCT's goal content distinction became intelligible — the organismic-dialectical perspective that treats humans as growth-oriented organisms whose flourishing depends on basic need satisfaction. Ryan co-authored the foundational GCT papers with Kasser and, with Deci, integrated GCT into the broader SDT architecture as its fifth mini-theory.
> *— [[goal-contents-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-01_report]]*

> [!person] Richard M. Ryan (1953–)
> Professor at the Institute for Positive Psychology and Education at Australian Catholic University and Professor Emeritus at the University of Rochester. Co-developer of SDT, Ryan's contributions have been central to the theoretical development of the need structure, the internalization continuum (OIT), Goal Contents Theory, Relationship Motivation Theory, and the eudaimonic well-being framework. Ryan's integrative theoretical work has maintained SDT's coherence as the framework has expanded to encompass multiple mini-theories and application domains. **Key works**: *Deci & Ryan (1985), Ryan & Deci (2000, 2017)*.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-01 (1)]]*

> [!person] Richard M. Ryan (1953–)
> University of Rochester, later Australian Catholic University and Seoul National University. Co-founder of SDT. Ryan's contributions emphasized the philosophical and developmental dimensions — the articulation of the organismic metatheory, the development of OIT's internalization continuum, and the formalization of BPNT and RMT. His 1995 chapter on psychological needs and the facilitation of integrative processes remains one of the most cited theoretical statements in the SDT literature.
>
> **Relationship to other figures:** Equal partnership with Deci; influenced by Rogers' humanistic psychology and Piaget's organismic developmental theory; trained numerous SDT researchers including Vansteenkiste, Niemiec, and Weinstein.
>
> **Key works:** Ryan & Connell (1989), Ryan & Deci (2000, 2017).
> *— [[self-determination-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*
