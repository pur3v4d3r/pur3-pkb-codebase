## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Far Transfer Domain 2: Educational Design and Teaching
> **Structural Principle:** The combined framework of [[schema-theory]], [[cognitive-load-theory]], [[advance-organizers]], and [[formative-feedback]] constitutes the empirical backbone of contemporary evidence-informed instructional design. Teaching is a communicative act performed at scale, and the principles governing effective teacher communication map closely onto the effectiveness principles developed throughout this report.
> **Concrete Application:** An educator using feedback principles from the [[Hattie-&-Timperley-Feedback-Model]] will operate at process and self-regulation levels, design formative (rather than exclusively summative) feedback, and create the [[psychological-safety]] conditions under which student questions and errors become learning resources. An educator using the active listening principles of Section 3 will focus on understanding student mental models before intervening — a practice that research consistently shows more efficacious than content delivery alone.
> **Boundary Condition:** Educational contexts introduce power differentials and evaluation dynamics that substantially affect psychological safety and the quality of communication available in the classroom; these structural features require explicit management that goes beyond generic communication skill.
> **See also:** [[constructivist-learning-theory]], [[self-efficacy]]
> *— [[how-to-communicate-effectively-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
