# Autonomous Relationship Motivation

> [!definition] Autonomous Relationship Motivation
> The motivational quality in which a person maintains a close relationship because they genuinely value it, because participation expresses their identity, or because the relationship is intrinsically rewarding. Distinguished from high commitment, which can co-occur with controlled motivation. Autonomous motivation predicts resilience in the face of relational challenge, positive responses to partner behavior, and lower psychological cost for equivalent relational investment.

> [!evidence] Autonomous Relationship Motivation
> Motivation for engagement with a close relationship that is self-endorsed and experienced as emanating from the self — including intrinsic motivation (genuine love, care, and interest) and integrated regulation (valuing the relationship as consistent with one's deeply held values) and identified regulation (recognizing the importance of the relationship to one's goals). Autonomous relationship motivation is associated with genuine psychological endorsement of the relational engagement rather than compliance with internal or external pressure. *Contrasts with*: [[controlled-relationship-motivation]]. *See also*: [[intrinsic-motivation]], [[integrated-regulation]], [[autonomous-motivation]]
