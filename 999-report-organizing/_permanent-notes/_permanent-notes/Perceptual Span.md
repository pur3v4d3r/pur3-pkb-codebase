# Perceptual Span

> [!definition] Perceptual Span
> The region from which useful information is extracted during a single eye fixation during reading. Approximately 14–15 characters to the right and 3–4 characters to the left of the fixation point in alphabetic scripts. Sets the physical upper limit on information available per fixation and is not substantively modifiable through training.
