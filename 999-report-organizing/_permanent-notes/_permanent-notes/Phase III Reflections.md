## Reflections

> [!reflection] Phase III Reflections
> 1. The Nelson-Narens monitoring-control model specifies that control commands from the meta-level are only as good as the metacognitive knowledge that generates them. What does this imply for the design of environments intended to support metacognitive development? Specifically, what features of an environment would produce accurate monitoring and responsive control rather than monitoring-control decoupling?
> 2. Efklides's framework suggests that metacognitive feelings serve as the primary triggers for regulatory intervention. If this is correct, then learners whose metacognitive feelings are systematically miscalibrated (experiencing fluent comprehension when actual comprehension is poor) will fail to regulate even when they possess accurate declarative metacognitive knowledge. What does this imply for the design of metacognitive instruction?
> 3. The generality-specificity debate has been framed here as warranting a "layered model." But what does this model predict about the sequencing of metacognitive instruction? Should domain-general principles be taught before domain-specific conditional knowledge, or should the sequence be reversed?
> *— [[metacognitive-knowledge-foundational-report-2026-03-31_report]]*
