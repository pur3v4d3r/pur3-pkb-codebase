## Open Threads

> [!open-question] Theory to Practice Gap
> The gap between a well-supported theory and its effective translation into practice is a recurring theme in educational research. Before reading this section, consider: what would you expect to be lost or distorted when SDL theory — developed primarily through observational research on naturally occurring adult learning — is transplanted into formal institutional settings with their evaluative pressures, diverse readiness levels, and competing time demands? Holding this question makes the practical tensions in the applications literature more visible.
> *— [[self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-03-11_report]]*
