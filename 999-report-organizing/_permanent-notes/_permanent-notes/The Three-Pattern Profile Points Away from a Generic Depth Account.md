## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Three-Pattern Profile Points Away from a Generic Depth Account
> If elaborative interrogation worked simply by increasing "depth of processing" in a generic sense, we would expect: (a) relatively uniform effects across knowledge levels, (b) relatively similar effects for good and bad "why" questions, and (c) roughly comparable advantages for recall and comprehension. The observed pattern — knowledge-dependent, question-quality-sensitive, comprehension-dominant effects — is precisely what a mechanism specifically targeting situation model construction would predict. The evidence thus provides converging support for the architectural specificity argument developed in Phase IV.
> *— [[elaborative-interrogation-reading-focused-analysis-2026-03-30_report]]*
