> [!flashcard] Seed 9 — Distinction
> **Q:** What distinguishes effective annotation strategies from passive highlighting in terms of metacognitive engagement?
> **A:** Passive highlighting = topographic marking of verbatim text; associated with fluency illusion rather than comprehension monitoring. Effective annotation = content-transforming marks (questions, summaries, connections, evaluations) that require comprehension evaluation, inference generation, and prior-knowledge integration. Effective annotation externalizes the metacognitive regulatory dialogue; passive highlighting does not.
> **Source:** Phase V, Section 5.4
> **Difficulty:** Basic
> **Tags:** #annotation, #metacognition, #reading-strategies

> [!flashcard] Seed 9 — Distinction
> **Q:** Distinguish declarative, procedural, and conditional metacognitive knowledge.
> **A:** Declarative: propositional knowledge about cognitive processes and one's own cognitive characteristics (knowing that distributed practice outperforms massed practice). Procedural: knowledge of how to execute cognitive strategies (knowing how to implement retrieval practice). Conditional: knowledge of when and why to deploy particular strategies — the discriminative competence specifying appropriate context for each strategy. Conditional knowledge is the most neglected and most practically significant.
> **Source:** Phase II
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #metacognition, #conditional-knowledge, #metacognitive-knowledge

> [!flashcard] Seed 9 — Distinction
> **Q:** What is the difference between mastery grammar and performance grammar in self-reflection prompts? Give an example of each for the same evaluative moment.
> **A:** Performance grammar frames evaluation as judgment against a standard ("How well did I do?"). Mastery grammar frames evaluation as diagnosis of learning progress ("What do I understand better now than I did at the start, and where is the growing edge?"). Example: Performance — "I didn't do well enough on the synthesis goals." Mastery — "The synthesis goal revealed that I can identify connections but cannot yet reconstruct causal mechanisms from memory — that's the specific capability to develop."
> **Source:** Phase VI
> **Difficulty:** Basic
> **Tags:** #achievement-goal-theory, #mastery-grammar, #formative-assessment
