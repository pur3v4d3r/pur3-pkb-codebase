## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] The Asymmetric Ratchet → Organizational Learning
> In organizations, the equivalent of Zimmerman's SRL cycle is the strategy-execute-reflect cycle of organizational learning. [[single-loop-learning-vs.-double-loop-learning|Argyris and Schön's double-loop learning]] operates through the same mechanism as adaptive SRL inference: not merely correcting strategy errors (single-loop) but questioning the underlying assumptions that generated the strategy (double-loop, equivalent to Zimmerman's adaptive inference). The asymmetric ratchet is visible in organizational failure cascades: organizations in crisis progressively reduce their strategic experimentation (the equivalent of self-handicapping), which prevents the performance recovery that would break the cycle, while the failure history generates ability-equivalent attributions ("this industry is impossible," "this team cannot compete") that further deplete organizational self-efficacy. The structural analogue predicts that organizational interventions that target belief architecture (strategic confidence, attribution of failure to correctable strategy rather than inherent incapacity) will be more durable than interventions that target execution alone.
> *— [[zimmerman-srl-focused-analysis-2026-03-22_report]]*
