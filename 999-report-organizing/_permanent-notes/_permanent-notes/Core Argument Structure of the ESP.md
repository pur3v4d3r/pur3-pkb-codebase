> [!diagram] Core Argument Structure of the ESP
> ```
> OBSERVATION: Recognition failure of recallable words
> (Tulving & Thomson 1973 paradigm)
>          │
>          ▼
> ANOMALY: Trace strength models predict recognition ≥ recall
>          But: Recall with encoded cue > Recognition with strong associate
>          │
>          ▼
> EXPLANATORY PRINCIPLE: Encoding Specificity
>   "Retrieval requires encoding-retrieval match,
>    not just trace strength"
>          │
>     ┌────┴────┐
>     ▼         ▼
> MECHANISM:  EVIDENCE:
> Synergistic  Context-dependent memory (Godden & Baddeley)
> Ecphory     State-dependent memory (Bower et al.)
>             Hippocampal reinstatement (Ranganath et al.)
>     │
>     ▼
> THEORETICAL RELATIONSHIPS:
>   ↔ Transfer-Appropriate Processing (compatible, different level)
>   ↔ Levels of Processing Theory (orthogonal: richness vs. match)
>     │
>     ▼
> APPLICATIONS:
>   ⟹ Retrieval practice in test-format conditions
>   ⟹ PKB design: retrieval-anticipatory indexing
>   ⟹ Cognitive Interview technique
>   ⟹ Desirable difficulties (contextual variation)
>     │
>     ▼
> LIMITS:
>   ⚠ Cueing asymmetries unexplained
>   ⚠ Source monitoring failures
>   ⚠ Circularity risk in application
>   ⚠ Smaller for semantic/automatized knowledge
> ```
> *— [[encoding-specificity-principle-tulving-foundational-report-2026-04-07_report]]*
