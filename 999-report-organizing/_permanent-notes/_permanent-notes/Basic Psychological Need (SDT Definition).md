# Basic Psychological Need (SDT Definition)

> [!definition] Basic Psychological Need (SDT Definition)
> In SDT, a basic psychological need is a nutriment that is *essential* for psychological growth, integrity, and well-being. Specifically, a psychological need is considered "basic" in SDT if it meets three criteria: (1) its satisfaction is reliably associated with enhanced well-being and functioning across populations and settings; (2) its frustration is reliably associated with diminished well-being, impaired functioning, and psychological ill-health; and (3) it cannot be reduced to other needs or explained as a learned preference. The basic needs are thus *universal* psychological requirements, not culturally specific values or individually varying preferences.
