> [!protocol] Protocol 2: Post-Session Reflective Assessment
> **Purpose:** Evaluate learning quality using strategy-based attribution rather than output counting.
> **Steps:**
> 1. **Understanding comparison:** "What could I explain *before* this session that I can now explain better or differently?"
> 2. **Connection generation:** "Without looking at notes, what connections can I draw between what I learned today and what I already knew?"
> 3. **Strategy evaluation:** "Which of my approaches worked well? What would I do differently?"
> 4. **Calibration test:** "How confident am I that my understanding is genuine? What question would test whether I really understand vs. merely recognise?"
> 5. **Attribution check:** "If things didn't go as planned, was it because of my strategy (adjustable), the material's difficulty (normal), or my ability (almost certainly not the real answer)?"
> 6. **Forward planning:** "What should I focus on next — and what does today's session suggest about how to approach it?"
> *— [[goal-setting-and-assessment-language-in-pkm-foundational-report-2026-04-15_report]]*
