## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Principle 3 → Clinical Expertise Development
> Medical students who encounter clinical cases in rich, contextually elaborated environments — with access to the patient history, the physical examination findings, and the diagnostic reasoning of an expert — develop more transferable clinical pattern recognition than students who encounter decontextualised case summaries. The mechanism is the same as the vocabulary case: rich contextual elaboration at encoding produces representations that carry the relational structure needed to generalise to novel instances. This connects to [[transfer-appropriate-processing]] and the research on [[situated-learning]] showing that decontextualised learning produces knowledge that is application-inert in new contexts.
> *— [[vocabulary-acquisition-reading-focused-analysis-2026-03-28_report]]*
