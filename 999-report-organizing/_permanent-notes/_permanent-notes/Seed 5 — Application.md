> [!flashcard] Seed 5 — Application
> **Q:** How does the overconfidence effect threaten self-assessment in PKM, and what mechanisms can improve calibration?
> **A:** The overconfidence effect causes learners to systematically overestimate their understanding, especially after superficial exposure. Calibration-improving mechanisms include: generation-based testing (explaining without notes), application-based assessment (using concepts in novel situations), connection-based evaluation (linking to other concepts), and temporal spacing (reassessing after delay).
> **Source:** Section 5
> **Difficulty:** Advanced

> [!flashcard] Seed 5 — Application
> **Q:** Why is context stability important for habit formation, and what does this imply for behavior change strategy?
> **A:** Habits are encoded in association with specific contextual features — time, place, preceding events. The triggering cue is context-bound. If context changes (travel, new job), the habit trigger may not fire. This implies that environment design — creating stable, reliable cue conditions — is as important as motivation for habit maintenance.
> **Source:** Phase IV
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #habit-formation, #context-dependency, #environment-design
