# Mastery Goal Orientation** *(Ames, 1992; Dweck, 1986)*

> [!definition] Mastery Goal Orientation** *(Ames, 1992; Dweck, 1986)*
> **Definition:** A goal orientation focused on developing competence, understanding, and learning — where success is defined as improved understanding rather than relative performance. Associated with deeper processing, greater persistence, and more adaptive responses to failure than performance goal orientations.
> **Operational Indicators:** A learner with an active mastery orientation frames comprehension failures as information about process, seeks to understand *why* a concept is difficult, and experiences setback as a learning signal rather than a verdict about ability.
> **Cross-References:** [[mastery-goal-orientation]], [[achievement-goal-theory]], [[carol-dweck]], [[mastery-approach-goal]]
