> [!reflection] Section 3 Reflective Questions
> 1. The word-length effect shows that digit span varies across cultures because languages differ in how long it takes to pronounce their numerals. What does this imply about the relationship between cognitive architecture (universal) and cognitive performance (culturally variable)?
> 2. The irrelevant speech effect demonstrates that the phonological store captures incoming speech involuntarily, even when we intend to ignore it. What are the implications of this automatic capture for the design of learning environments and the limits of voluntary attentional control?
> 3. The phonological loop is implicated in foreign language acquisition as well as comprehension. Does this suggest that working memory serves both a temporary processing function and a long-term learning function? How might these two functions be related?
