# Knowledge-of-Cognition

> [!definition] Knowledge-of-Cognition
> The declarative dimension of metacognition — what a person knows about cognitive processes in general and their own cognition in particular, including knowledge of persons, tasks, and strategies (Flavell, 1979; Schraw & Dennison, 1994).
