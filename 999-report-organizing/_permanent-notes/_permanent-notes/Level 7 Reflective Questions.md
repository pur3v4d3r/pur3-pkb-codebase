## Reflections

> [!reflection] Level 7 Reflective Questions
> 1. Schema engineering in PKM would require knowing what schemas the learner needs to develop. How would you audit your own existing schemas in a domain — identify which are well-formed, which are miscalibrated, and which are missing?
> 2. If AI systems can function as "schema transmission media" — not just information transferors but facilitators of schema formation — what design principles would guide the interaction? What would it look like to interact with an AI in a schema-formation mode rather than an information-retrieval mode?
> 3. The integration synthesis claims that "the schema IS the organization of knowledge." What distinguishes someone who has the same information as an expert but lacks their schemas? What would you observe differently about their behavior, their errors, and their learning rate?
> *— [[schema-and-how-they-work-deep-dive-2026-04-08_report]]*
