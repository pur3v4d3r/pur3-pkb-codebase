## Practical Implications

> [!example] When to Deploy Each Technique
> | Technique | Deploy When | Output |
> |-----------|-------------|--------|
> | **Schema Activation** | Before every session — no exceptions | 3–5 sentence statement of prior model + expectation of challenge |
> | **Text Survey** | Before reading prose — every session | Header map, structural inference, surprises flagged |
> | **Pre-Question Generation** | After text survey | 3–5 live questions that become reading purposes |
> | **Goal Setting (Process + Outcome)** | After pre-questions | 2–3 outcome goals; 2–4 behavioral process goals |
> | **Marginal Annotation** | On central thesis claims, load-bearing distinctions, surprising reframings | Brief paraphrase + "why this matters" note |
> | **Elaborative Interrogation** | When a claim is counterintuitive or surprising | Question → generated answer → connection made |
> | **Self-Explanation** | After every new conceptual architecture or enumerable model | Written reconstruction with report closed; note gaps |
> | **Connection-Making** | When content maps onto existing PKB nodes | Wiki-link + 1–2 sentence structural description of the connection |
> | **Comprehension Monitoring** | Continuously — flag confusion explicitly rather than reading past it | Flagged question → scan for resolution → note if unresolved |
> | **Cold Reconstruction** | Immediately after closing the report — before any notes | 5-minute timed free-recall write |
> | **Comprehension Gap Analysis** | Immediately after Cold Reconstruction | List of specific gaps with grain-size assessment |
> | **Causal Attribution Analysis** | For each identified gap and process failure | Attribution (locus/stability/controllability) + specific action implication |
> | **PKB Integration** | Final 5–10 minutes of session | Updated nodes, new edges, Forethought modification for next session |
> *— [[optimal-reading-session-worked-example-2026-03-25_report]]*
