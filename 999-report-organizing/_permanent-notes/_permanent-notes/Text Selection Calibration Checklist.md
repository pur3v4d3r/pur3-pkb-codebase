## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Text Selection Calibration Checklist
> **Context:** Before committing to a text as a vocabulary development vehicle, use this checklist to assess whether it falls in the productive acquisition band.
>
> - [ ] Does the text contain 5–7 unknown or uncertain words per page? (If fewer: below the productive band for this reader; if more: above the coverage threshold)
> - [ ] Are the unknown words surrounded by enough semantic and syntactic context to support inference? (Spot-test: try inferring the meaning of 3 unknown words before reading; if all inferences are impossible, context quality is too low)
> - [ ] Is the topic sufficiently familiar that background knowledge can activate relevant schemata for inference support?
> - [ ] Can you read the text at a normal comprehension pace without losing the thread due to unknown words?
>
> **If ≥2 items unchecked:** The text is not in the productive acquisition band for this reader. Recalibrate difficulty or prepare by pre-teaching critical vocabulary.
> *— [[vocabulary-acquisition-reading-focused-analysis-2026-03-28_report]]*
