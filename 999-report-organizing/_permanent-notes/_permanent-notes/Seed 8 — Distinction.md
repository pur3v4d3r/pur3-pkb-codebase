## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Seed 8 — Distinction
> **Q:** Distinguish craving from routine in the habit loop, and explain why the distinction matters for habit disruption.
> **A:** The routine is the behavioral response itself; craving is the anticipatory motivational state triggered by the cue — the desire for the reward before any action. Disrupting the routine without addressing the craving leaves the motivational pull intact, enabling substitute routines. Effective habit change must target the craving-reward link, not only the behavioral routine.
> **Source:** Phase III
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
