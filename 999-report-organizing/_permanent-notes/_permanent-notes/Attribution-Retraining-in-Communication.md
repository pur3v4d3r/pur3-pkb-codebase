# Attribution-Retraining-in-Communication

> [!definition] Attribution-Retraining-in-Communication
> The practice of deliberately auditing causal explanations for communicative failures, challenging self-serving attributions by generating alternative explanations that locate causes in modifiable situations or behaviors rather than in fixed character traits of self or other.
