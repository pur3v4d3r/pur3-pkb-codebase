## Additional Material

> [!person] Carol Dweck (1946 – present)
> **Core Contribution:** Developed [[growth-mindset]] theory, demonstrating that implicit theories of intelligence (entity vs. incremental) shape goal orientation, resilience in the face of difficulty, and academic achievement.
>
> **Role in the Dialectical Arc:** Primarily challenge and reconstruction. Growth mindset interventions implicitly operate through vocabulary substitution, providing the clearest practical evidence for the constitutive account. The vocabulary of entity theory ("I'm not smart enough") vs. incremental theory ("I haven't built this yet") constitutes motivationally divergent self-conceptions.
>
> **Relationship to Other Figures:** Directly connected to Weiner (attribution dimensions) and Elliot (achievement goal orientation); influenced by Bandura.
>
> **Key Works:** *Self-Theories* (1999); *Mindset* (2006)
> *— [[inner-speech-vocabulary-motivation-self-efficacy-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-25_report]]*
