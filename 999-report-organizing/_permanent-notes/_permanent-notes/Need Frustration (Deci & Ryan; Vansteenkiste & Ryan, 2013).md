# Need Frustration (Deci & Ryan; Vansteenkiste & Ryan, 2013)

> [!definition] Need Frustration (Deci & Ryan; Vansteenkiste & Ryan, 2013)
> The active blocking or undermining of basic psychological need satisfaction, distinguished from need absence (the neutral state of neither satisfying nor frustrating a need). Need frustration produces reliable ill-being effects — anxiety, defensiveness, alienation, motivational rigidity — that need absence does not. The [[satisfaction-frustration-asymmetry]] is now understood as one of SDT's more robust empirical contributions. Preventing need frustration (designing environments that do not actively undermine autonomy, competence, and relatedness) is as important as promoting need satisfaction.
