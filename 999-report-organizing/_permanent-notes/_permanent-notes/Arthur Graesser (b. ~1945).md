## Additional Material

> [!person] Arthur Graesser (b. ~1945)
> American cognitive psychologist; Professor, University of Memphis. Co-developer of Constructionist Theory (with Singer & Trabasso, 1994) and prolific contributor to discourse processing and intelligent tutoring research. Principal author of *Handbook of Discourse Processes* (2003).
>
> **Lineage:** Trained in experimental cognitive psychology; influenced by Kintsch, van Dijk, and Schank's conceptual dependency theory.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Constructionist Theory provides the specification of which inference types are automatic versus strategic — the theoretical foundation for this report's prescriptive reading strategies.
> *— [[situation-models-text-comprehension-foundational-report-2026-03-29_report]]*
