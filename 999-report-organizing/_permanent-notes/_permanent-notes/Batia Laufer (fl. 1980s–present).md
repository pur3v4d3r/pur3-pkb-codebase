## Additional Material

> [!person] Batia Laufer (fl. 1980s–present)
> Applied linguist whose research established the vocabulary threshold hypothesis empirically and investigated the relationship between vocabulary size and reading comprehension in L2 contexts. Her studies of minimum coverage requirements for text comprehension are foundational to the field.
>
> **Key Works:** Laufer, B. & Sim, D.D. (1985). Measuring and explaining the reading threshold needed for English for academic purposes texts. *Foreign Language Annals*, 18, 405–411.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** The vocabulary threshold evidence in Phase III draws directly on Laufer's coverage ratio research.
> *— [[vocabulary-acquisition-reading-focused-analysis-2026-03-28_report]]*
