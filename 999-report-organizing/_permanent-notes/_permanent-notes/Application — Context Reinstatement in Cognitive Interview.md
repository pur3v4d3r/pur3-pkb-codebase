## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Application — Context Reinstatement in Cognitive Interview
> **Q:** How does the Cognitive Interview technique apply the Encoding Specificity Principle?
> **A:** The Cognitive Interview instructs eyewitnesses to mentally reinstate the context of the witnessed event — not just its content, but the sensory environment, their physical state, and emotional state at the time. This mental context reinstatement provides retrieval cues that match the encoded context of the event, facilitating more complete ecphoric output. Geiselman & Fisher's research showed this significantly improves witness recall quality.
