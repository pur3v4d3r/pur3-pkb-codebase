## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Athletic Training and Deliberate Practice
> **Structural principle:** The monitoring-control externalization framework applies to motor skill acquisition, where training logs and performance analytics serve the same metacognitive scaffolding function as PKB notes serve for knowledge acquisition.
>
> **Application:** Elite athletic training has independently converged on many of the same metacognitive externalization principles identified in this report. Training logs externalize monitoring (recording perceived effort, performance metrics, subjective states), video analysis makes execution visible for self-evaluation (analogous to reviewing one's own notes), and periodization plans externalize control (structuring training progression based on monitoring data). The metacognitive design paradox also applies: athletes who become overly dependent on wearable monitoring technology may lose the proprioceptive self-monitoring capacity that the technology was designed to support.
>
> **Boundary condition:** Motor skill metacognition operates on different timescales and involves different cognitive systems than declarative knowledge metacognition.
>
> **See also:** [[deliberate-practice]], [[self-regulated-learning]], [[embodied-cognition]]
