> [!methodology-and-sources] Methodological Transparency
> This report synthesizes research from several distinct intellectual traditions within [[cognitive-psychology|cognitive psychology]] and the learning sciences:
>
> **Traditions Synthesized:**
> - **Experimental cognitive psychology** (Miller, Baddeley, Cowan) — laboratory studies of memory capacity and working memory architecture
> - **Expert-novice studies** (de Groot, Chase & Simon, Chi) — comparative investigations of skilled and unskilled performance across domains
> - **Expertise development** (Ericsson, Gobet) — longitudinal and cross-sectional studies of skill acquisition and expert performance
> - **Cognitive load theory** (Sweller, van Merriënboer, Kalyuga, Paas) — experimental studies of instructional design effects on learning outcomes
> - **Naturalistic decision making** (Klein) — field studies of expert decision processes in real-world, high-stakes environments
> - **Cognitive neuroscience** — neuroimaging and neuroanatomical studies of expertise-related brain changes
