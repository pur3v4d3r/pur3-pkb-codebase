# Backward Design (Wiggins & McTighe)

> [!definition] Backward Design (Wiggins & McTighe)
> A curriculum design framework that begins with the identification of desired understandings and transfer goals, then determines acceptable evidence of those understandings, and only then plans learning experiences and instruction. For lifelong learning, backward design forces prioritisation of transferable competencies over content coverage.
>
> **Boundary:** Backward design requires clarity about desired transfer goals, which may be difficult to specify for learning aimed at developing open-ended self-direction.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** Programme-level architecture mechanism that ensures curriculum is designed for transfer and long-term capacity rather than immediate content mastery.
>
> **See also:** [[transfer-of-learning]], [[instructional-design]], [[far-transfer]]
