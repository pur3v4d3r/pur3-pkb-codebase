## Open Threads

> [!open-question] Strategic Forethought Inventory
> Before developing a formal Forethought protocol, it is worth auditing your current pre-reading behavior against the four dimensions of Task Analysis and four components of Motivational Beliefs described above. For your last complex PKB reading session: Did you set a goal? Was it specific or vague? Was it process-oriented or outcome-oriented? Was it mastery or performance framed? Did you select strategies in advance? Did you assess your self-efficacy for this particular text? Honest answers to these questions will tell you where your current Forethought practice has the most room to grow.
> *— [[srl-forethought-self-reflection-pkb-focused-analysis-2026-03-24_report]]*
