> [!section-summary] Level 2 Summary
> At the surface level, CLT appeared as three load types and a list of instructional effects. At the mechanism level, we now see that element interactivity is the single explanatory engine. The three load types reconceptualize as: intrinsic element interactivity (from content structure), extraneous element interactivity (from instructional format), and germane processing (the work of constructing schemas from intrinsic interactivity). The evolutionary cognitive architecture provides theoretical closure for why this mechanism must be central. The schema-interactivity feedback loop — where schema construction reduces element interactivity, enabling further schema construction — is the fundamental learning dynamic. The next level will zoom further to reveal the *substructure* of element interactivity: what constitutes an "element," how interactivity is formally defined, and where the concept's precision begins to strain.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-deep-dive-2026-04-12_report]]*

> [!section-summary] Level 2 Summary
> At surface level, intrinsic load was a given — a property of the material to be accepted. At THIS level we now see that intrinsic load is the cognitive consequence of [[Element-Interactivity]] — the number of mutually constraining elements that the learner must hold simultaneously in working memory in order to understand the material. The reformulation transforms intrinsic load from a category into a variable, and in doing so transforms the entire theoretical structure of CLT from a classification scheme into a mechanism. The next level will zoom further to ask what an *element* actually is and what makes two elements *interact*, because the answers turn out to be neither obvious nor settled.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-element-interactivity-deep-dive-2026-04-20]]*

> [!section-summary] Level 2 Summary
> At surface level, monitoring accuracy appeared as a confidence-performance gap. At the MECHANISM level, we now see that this gap arises because monitoring is not direct access to memory contents but **heuristic inference from proxy cues**. The [[nelson-narens-model|Nelson-Narens architecture]] establishes the two-level structure (meta-level monitoring object-level processing), and the cue-utilization framework (Koriat, 1997) explains how the monitoring signal is generated from intrinsic, extrinsic, and mnemonic cues. The cues carry genuine information (explaining moderate resolution) but are systematically imperfect in their alignment with the criterion (explaining systematic overconfidence). Monitoring is, at its deepest mechanistic level, a signal detection problem under noise.
>
> **The next level zooms further:** What are the specific individual cues, and what are their individual reliability profiles?
> *— [[metacognitive-monitoring-accuracy-calibration-deep-dive-2026-04-12_report]]*

> [!section-summary] Level 2 Summary
> At Level 1 we observed that surface compliance underdetermines regulatory architecture. Level 2 has opened the architecture: four extrinsic regulatory styles (external, introjected, identified, integrated) plus intrinsic motivation, ordered developmentally and distinguished categorically rather than quantitatively. External regulation is contingency-driven from outside; introjection has internalized the contingency without endorsing the regulation; identification has consciously endorsed the value the regulation serves; integration has organized the endorsed value with the rest of the self. The autonomous/controlled higher-order distinction is empirically powerful but theoretically downstream; specialists should keep both levels of analysis available. The mechanism of movement along the continuum has been flagged but not yet opened.
>
> Level 3 will reveal that the continuum is not self-driving — that movement from one regulatory style to the next requires specific conditions in the social and structural environment, and that these conditions are organized around the satisfaction or frustration of three basic psychological needs.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-internalization-continuum-deep-dive-2026-04-21]]*

> [!section-summary] Level 2 Summary
> At surface level, walking-while-reading appeared to be a generic multitasking problem. At mechanism level, we now see something more specific: it is a dual-task pair with **unusually low structural resource overlap** (different WM subsystems, different resource dimensions), whose residual interference is concentrated in **[[central-executive]] concurrence costs** that are **modulated by the automaticity of the walking component.** The mechanism-level prediction is: dual-task cost is small, non-zero, concentrated at high-CE-demand reading moments, and decreasing with walking-route habituation. The next level zooms into the specific neural subsystems and circuits that underlie this mechanism.
> *— [[walking-academic-reading-working-memory-deep-dive-2026-04-13_report]]*
