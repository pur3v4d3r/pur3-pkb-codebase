## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Connection: Episodic Buffer and Reading Comprehension
> **Q:** How does the episodic buffer explain the comprehension of complex narratives that exceed the capacity of the phonological loop or visuospatial sketchpad alone?
> **A:** Complex narrative comprehension requires integrating verbal content (phonological loop), physical scene descriptions (visuospatial sketchpad), semantic knowledge (long-term memory), and temporal/causal structure into a unified situation model. The episodic buffer provides the temporary multimodal integration workspace where this binding occurs — it can hold approximately 4 chunks of integrated multimodal information, where each "chunk" is a rich episodic representation combining verbal, visual, spatial, and semantic features. Without the episodic buffer, the model cannot explain how working memory maintains complex situation models during reading.
> *— [[baddeley-model-of-working-memory-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
