## Reflections

> [!reflection] Phase III Reflective Questions
> 1. The meta-analytic controversy between Deci et al. (1999) and Cameron and Pierce (1994) illustrates the theory-laden nature of even quantitative literature syntheses — the categorization scheme used to organize studies determines the conclusions drawn. What does this suggest about how you should interpret meta-analytic findings in motivational psychology?
> 2. If CET and achievement goal theory are complementary frameworks, why isn't there a single integrated theory? What does the persistence of separate theoretical traditions with overlapping claims suggest about the nature of theoretical progress in motivational psychology?
> 3. How does the distinction between CET (focused on how events affect intrinsic motivation) and OIT (focused on how extrinsic motivation becomes internalized) suggest that different aspects of motivation require different theoretical accounts?
