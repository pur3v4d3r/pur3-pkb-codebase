## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application Bridge: Clinical Intervention for Perfectionism
> Perfectionist psychopathology — which reliably predicts anxiety, depression, and reduced performance quality — maps onto the MAV and PAV profiles. Maladaptive perfectionism is not high standards per se but avoidance motivation applied to self-referenced standards. Acceptance and Commitment Therapy and Compassionate Mind Training work through mechanisms that can be understood as converting avoidance-mode regulation toward approach-mode — defusing the self-threatening character of failure signals and reestablishing the conditions for exploratory engagement with challenging material. The transfer from achievement goal theory: the therapeutic target in perfectionism is not the standard (reduce expectations) but the regulatory mode (reduce avoidance activation in response to failure signals). This explains why purely cognitive challenging of perfectionist beliefs is often insufficient — it addresses the content of the goals without changing the regulatory mode in which they are held.
> *— [[achievement-goal-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-24_report]]*
