## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] The Efficiency-Capability Tradeoff: CLT → Athletic Training
> Athletic training science has documented the efficiency-capability tradeoff precisely. Training to compete in a specific event (optimizing for near-term performance in familiar conditions) differs systematically from training to develop athletic capacity (optimizing for performance across a range of demands). Athletes who train exclusively with high specificity and low variability plateau earlier and are more fragile under novel competitive conditions. Periodization, variable training loads, and cross-training introduce productive difficulty that impairs immediate performance but builds the broad athletic base that supports long-term development. This is the exact structure of the CLT-desirable difficulties tension: specificity equals efficiency, variability equals robustness.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-22_report]]*
