# Self-Efficacy (Bandura, 1977; Zimmerman, 2000)

> [!definition] Self-Efficacy (Bandura, 1977; Zimmerman, 2000)
> Self-efficacy is a learner's judgment about their capability to execute specific behaviors required to produce a particular outcome. Critically distinct from global self-esteem or general confidence, self-efficacy is *domain-specific, task-specific, and prospective*. In Zimmerman's model, self-efficacy occupies the pivot point between cycles: it is updated by the Self-Reflection Phase's outputs and becomes the primary determinant of Forethought Phase quality in the next cycle.
