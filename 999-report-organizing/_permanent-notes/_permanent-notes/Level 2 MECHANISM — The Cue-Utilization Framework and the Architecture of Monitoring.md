## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Level 2: MECHANISM — The Cue-Utilization Framework and the Architecture of Monitoring
> **Zoom progression:** Level 1 established that humans are systematically miscalibrated in their metacognitive monitoring. This level reveals WHY — by zooming into the mechanism that generates monitoring signals. The critical insight at this level is that **monitoring is not direct introspective access to memory strength; it is heuristic inference from proxy cues.**
> **What you'll see at this level:** The [[nelson-narens-model|Nelson-Narens monitoring-control architecture]], the cue-utilization framework, and the fundamental reason why monitoring signals are inherently noisy.
> **Specialist value:** This level dismantles the intuitive model of metacognitive monitoring (that we "look inside" and see what we know) and replaces it with the actual mechanism (that we infer what we know from indirect signals). This shift is the conceptual foundation for everything that follows.
> *— [[metacognitive-monitoring-accuracy-calibration-deep-dive-2026-04-12_report]]*
