## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Internalization Teleology → Developmental Stage Theories
> OIT's assumption that development naturally tends toward integration has a close structural analogue in stage-developmental theories generally (Piaget, Kohlberg, Loevinger, Kegan). All of these theories describe qualitatively distinct developmental stages and claim that development under appropriate conditions tends toward the highest stage — with the highest stage reliably reflecting the theorist's own cultural values about what mature functioning looks like. The pattern is robust enough to warrant a general caution: any developmental theory that posits a natural endpoint should be examined for the degree to which that endpoint is descriptive (this is what tends to happen) vs. normative (this is what should happen) vs. culturally inflected (this is what looks like flourishing in the context where the theorist lives and works). SDT is not uniquely guilty here — but nor is it exempt.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-19_report]]*
