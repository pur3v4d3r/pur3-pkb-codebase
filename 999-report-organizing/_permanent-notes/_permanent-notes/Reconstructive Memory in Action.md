> [!open-question] Reconstructive Memory in Action
> Think of a specific autobiographical memory — a birthday, a journey, a conversation. As you recall it, are you confident you are remembering accurately, or do you notice fragments, gaps, or moments where you are reasoning from general knowledge rather than specific recall? The reconstructive character of memory means that every act of retrieval is also, to some degree, an act of creation. What are the implications of this for how you evaluate your own memories as a source of knowledge?
