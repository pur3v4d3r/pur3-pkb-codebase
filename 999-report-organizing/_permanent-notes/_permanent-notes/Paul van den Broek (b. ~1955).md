## Additional Material

> [!person] Paul van den Broek (b. ~1955)
> Dutch cognitive psychologist; Professor, Leiden University. Developer of the Landscape Model of reading (with Risden & Husebye-Hartmann, 1995) and the standards of coherence framework. His developmental work has extended situation model theory into reading development and individual differences contexts.
>
> **Lineage:** Influenced by Kintsch, Trabasso, and the causal inference research tradition.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** The Landscape Model provides the dynamic account of situation model activation and decay that underlies the foregrounding effect and the discussion of model updating.
> *— [[situation-models-text-comprehension-foundational-report-2026-03-29_report]]*
