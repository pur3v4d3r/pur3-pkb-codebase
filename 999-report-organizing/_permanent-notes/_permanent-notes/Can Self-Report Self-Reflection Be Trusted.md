## Open Threads

> [!open-question] Can Self-Report Self-Reflection Be Trusted?
> **The Question:** The Self-Reflection Phase depends on honest self-evaluation. But the same monitoring accuracy problems that afflict in-task comprehension monitoring also afflict post-task self-reflection. Can learners accurately assess the quality of their own learning through journaling and template completion?
>
> **What We Know:** Self-report measures of SRL are moderately predictive of academic performance but substantially affected by social desirability bias, demand characteristics, and the same fluency illusions that affect comprehension monitoring. The generative calibration tests in the Self-Reflection template partially address this by replacing self-assessment with performance tests.
>
> **What We Don't Know:** The extent to which written self-reflection exercises suffer from fluency-as-thoroughness illusions — the experience of having written a detailed reflection feeling like having conducted a genuine self-assessment even when the written account is largely superficial.
>
> **Research Direction:** Development of more objective, performance-based self-reflection metrics that supplement self-report. The generative tests in this report's template are a step in this direction.
>
