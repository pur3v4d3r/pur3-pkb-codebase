# MSLQ (Motivated Strategies for Learning Questionnaire)

> [!definition] MSLQ (Motivated Strategies for Learning Questionnaire)
> Pintrich's major assessment instrument for self-regulated learning. Measures both motivational components (value, expectancy, affect) and cognitive/metacognitive strategy use across multiple subscales. Used extensively in research on SDT-SRL integration studies. The motivational subscales partially operationalise SDT constructs; the cognitive subscales operationalise Zimmerman's and Pintrich's process components.
