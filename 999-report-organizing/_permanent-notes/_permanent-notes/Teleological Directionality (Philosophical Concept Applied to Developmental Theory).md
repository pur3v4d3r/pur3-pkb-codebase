# Teleological Directionality (Philosophical Concept Applied to Developmental Theory)

> [!definition] Teleological Directionality (Philosophical Concept Applied to Developmental Theory)
> The implicit or explicit assumption in a developmental theory that development has a natural endpoint toward which it tends under appropriate conditions. In OIT, the teleological directionality is the assumption that internalization naturally tends toward integration. Philosophically, teleological directionality in developmental theories should be examined for: (1) the degree to which the "natural endpoint" is empirically demonstrated vs. normatively prescribed; (2) whether the endpoint reflects universal human development or the theorist's cultural context; and (3) whether the directionality is genuinely unidirectional or contextually reversible.
