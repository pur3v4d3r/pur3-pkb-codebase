## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Distinction — ESP vs. Trace Strength
> **Q:** What finding by Tulving and Thomson (1973) most directly contradicted strength-based models of memory?
> **A:** "Recognition failure of recallable words" — items that participants failed to recognize on a standard recognition test (presented with a strong pre-experimental associate) were nevertheless successfully recalled when cued with the weaker but *encoded* context word. Strength models predict recognition ≥ recall performance; the ESP correctly predicts the opposite when test conditions fail to match encoding conditions.
