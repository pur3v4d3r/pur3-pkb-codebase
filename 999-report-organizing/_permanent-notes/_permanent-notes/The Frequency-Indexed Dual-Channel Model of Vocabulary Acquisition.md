## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] The Frequency-Indexed Dual-Channel Model of Vocabulary Acquisition
> The dialectical process produces an original synthesis that cannot be articulated from within the reading-primary hypothesis: vocabulary acquisition is most efficiently conceptualised as a *dual-channel* process, with the optimal channel determined by vocabulary frequency and knowledge dimension. **Channel A: Deliberate Instruction** handles high-frequency vocabulary (bands 1–3,000 word families) and mid-frequency academic vocabulary (bands 3,000–9,000), using frequency-ranked word lists, spaced retrieval practice, and morphological pattern instruction to build the vocabulary threshold that enables independent reading. **Channel B: Extensive Reading** handles consolidation of partially known vocabulary, collocational enrichment, register sensitivity development, and exposure to low-frequency vocabulary in contextually rich authentic text — but only once Channel A has established the threshold.
>
> The crucial innovation over the received view is that the two channels are *sequentially dependent* in the direction opposite to what the reading-primary hypothesis implies. The received view treats reading as primary and instruction as supplementary. The reconstructed model treats threshold instruction as the prerequisite that *unlocks* reading's acquisition potential — reading is the more important long-term mechanism but is conditionally dependent on prior deliberate learning. The relationship is not "instruction vs. reading" but "instruction → threshold → reading → consolidation → richer reading → further consolidation." This is a recursively generative architecture, but it cannot be entered from the reading end for learners who are below the threshold.
>
> An additional dimension is metacognitive: Channel B is not passive. Extensive reading builds vocabulary efficiently only when the reader maintains *active word-noticing* — a metacognitive disposition toward flagging unfamiliar words, attempting inference, and following up with deliberate study or consultation. This is not automatic reading; it is strategically monitored reading, and developing this disposition requires metacognitive instruction that the reading-primary hypothesis does not include in its prescriptions.
> *— [[vocabulary-building-reading-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-28_report]]*
