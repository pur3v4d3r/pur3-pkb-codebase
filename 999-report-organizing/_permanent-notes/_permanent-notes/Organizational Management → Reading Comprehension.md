## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Organizational Management → Reading Comprehension
> In organizational psychology, the equivalent phenomenon is the difference between knowing individual team members and understanding team dynamics. A manager who understands each team member individually — their skills, motivations, and challenges — may be completely blindsided by team-level phenomena (groupthink, dysfunctional communication patterns, informal power structures) that are not visible from individual-level analysis. Team dynamics are not automatically assembled from individual-member understanding. The transfer encoding: in any domain involving multiple interacting agents or components, the properties of the whole do not follow automatically from understanding the parts. Dedicated systems-level attention is required.
> *— [[situation-model-construction-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-29_report]]*
