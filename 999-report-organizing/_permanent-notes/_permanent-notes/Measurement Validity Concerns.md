## Practical Implications

> [!warning] Measurement Validity Concerns
> The SDLRS, despite its wide use, has significant unresolved psychometric issues. The factor structure claimed in Guglielmino's original work has not been consistently replicated in independent analyses. Some researchers have found two-factor solutions, some three-factor solutions, and some have argued that the 58 items form a relatively undifferentiated general factor. This matters because the instrument's utility for diagnosing specific areas of SDL readiness for targeted intervention depends on its sub-scale validity — if the sub-scales do not measure distinct constructs, intervention targeting based on sub-scale scores is not warranted. The instrument may be most defensible as a general screening tool rather than a diagnostic profile.
> *— [[self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-03-11_report]]*
