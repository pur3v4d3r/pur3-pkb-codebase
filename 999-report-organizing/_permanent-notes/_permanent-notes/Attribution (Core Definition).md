# Attribution (Core Definition)

> [!definition] Attribution (Core Definition)
> An **attribution** is a causal explanation assigned by an individual to account for an outcome, event, or behavior — their own or another's. Attributions are the cognitive answers to the question "Why did this happen?" They are not neutral registrations of fact but interpretive constructions that reflect prior beliefs, social context, emotional state, and information availability. The field of Attribution Theory studies the content, structure, antecedents, and consequences of these causal explanations.
