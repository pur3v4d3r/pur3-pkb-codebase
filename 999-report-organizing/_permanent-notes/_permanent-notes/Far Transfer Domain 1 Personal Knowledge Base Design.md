## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Far Transfer Domain 1: Personal Knowledge Base Design
> **Structural principle:** Effective academic reading comprehension requires rich, well-organised schemas to support inference generation and situation model construction. The same principles apply to personal knowledge base (PKB) design: the connection density, conceptual organisation, and bi-directional linking of a [[schema-theory-and-learning-foundational-report-2026-03-06|PKB built on schema theory]] directly determines how effectively the PKB supports future reading comprehension. Notes that are anchored in existing conceptual networks, that capture inferences rather than just surface text, and that articulate relational connections to prior knowledge mimick the situation model construction process in persistent externalised form.
>
> **Concrete application:** When capturing notes from an academic text, prioritise recording the situation model — the inferred relationships, causal structures, and comparative frameworks — rather than the textbase (summarised propositions). The PKB as a situation model externalisation engine.
>
> **Boundary:** This transfer works in both directions: better PKB structure supports better future comprehension (the schemas become more accessible), AND better reading comprehension skills improve PKB quality (deeper models produce richer notes).
>
