## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Level 4: Dynamics — How the Substructure Produces Observable Behavior
> **Zoom progression:** Levels 2-3 established that walking-while-reading has minimal structural resource overlap and a narrow prefrontal bottleneck. This level reveals something the static architectural analysis missed: **walking is not just a resource consumer — it is also an arousal modulator, a neurochemical intervention, and a dynamic system that changes the very cognitive landscape on which reading operates.** The walking reader is not merely doing two things at once; they are doing one thing (reading) inside a physiological state that is different from the seated baseline.
> **What you'll see at this level:** The inverted-U arousal curve and how walking positions the reader on it, [[transient-hypofrontality]] theory and its predictions for walking-while-reading, the acute neurochemical effects of light exercise, and the interaction between task difficulty and exercise intensity as dynamic moderators.
> **Specialist value:** This level introduces the dynamic analysis that transforms the question from "does walking cost or benefit reading?" to "under what parameter space does walking's arousal benefit exceed its dual-task cost?" — a much more productive question.
> *— [[walking-academic-reading-working-memory-deep-dive-2026-04-13_report]]*
