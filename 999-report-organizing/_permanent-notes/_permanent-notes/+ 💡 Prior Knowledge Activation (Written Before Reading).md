> [!evidence] + 💡 Prior Knowledge Activation (Written Before Reading)
> What I know about Zimmerman's SRL model: It's a three-phase cycle — Forethought precedes effort, Performance monitors effort during, and Self-Reflection evaluates effort after. I know the Forethought Phase involves goal-setting and strategy selection. I know the cycle is supposed to be self-improving, but I'm unclear on the *mechanism* of that improvement. I've heard about Attribution Theory (Weiner) in passing — something about how people explain their successes and failures — but I couldn't define locus, stability, and controllability off the top of my head. Achievement Goal Theory: mastery goals vs. performance goals, from Dweck and Ames. I know mastery orientation is better for learning. SDT: intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivation, three needs (autonomy, competence, relatedness). That's roughly my current map.
