## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Protocol 1: The Comprehension Check
> **When:** Every 15-20 minutes during walking-reading
> **How:**
> 1. Pause reading (continue walking or stop — doesn't matter)
> 2. Close eyes or look away from text
> 3. Attempt to explain the last 2-3 key points in your own words
> 4. Notice: Can you produce the gist, or only surface fragments?
>
> **If gist production fails:** You've hit the fluency-comprehension dissociation. Transition to sitting for that material.
> **If gist production succeeds:** Continue walking-reading for this difficulty level.
>
> **Purpose:** Calibrates the fluency-comprehension dissociation (Edge Case 4) for your specific case.
> *— [[walking-academic-reading-working-memory-deep-dive-2026-04-13_report]]*
