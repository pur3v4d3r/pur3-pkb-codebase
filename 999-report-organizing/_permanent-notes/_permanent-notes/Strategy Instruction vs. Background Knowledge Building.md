## Open Threads

> [!tension-identified] Strategy Instruction vs. Background Knowledge Building
> **Position A:** Situation model construction failure is primarily a strategy problem. Readers fail to build situation models because they lack the metacognitive awareness and strategic habits to orient toward causal tracking, active generation, and frame-before-fill construction. Strategy instruction can reliably improve situation model quality across domains and difficulty levels.
>
> **Position B:** Situation model construction failure is primarily a prior knowledge problem. Without sufficient domain knowledge to bridge implicit causal gaps, no reading strategy can produce a coherent situation model. Strategy instruction is valuable but secondary to domain knowledge building.
>
> **Current Evidence State:** Both positions have substantial empirical support. The evidence suggests the relationship is interactive: strategies help most when prior knowledge is moderate; when prior knowledge is very low, strategies cannot compensate; when prior knowledge is very high, strategies are less necessary.
>
> **This Report's Stance:** Strategy and knowledge are complementary, but this report emphasizes strategies because they are more directly actionable for a reader who is actively developing reading skills. The practical recommendation is to pursue both simultaneously.
> *— [[situation-model-construction-focused-analysis-2026-03-29_report]]*
