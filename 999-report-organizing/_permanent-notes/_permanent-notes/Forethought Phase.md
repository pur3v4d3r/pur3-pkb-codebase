# Forethought Phase

> [!definition] Forethought Phase
> The first temporal phase in Pintrich's SRL cycle. Encompasses all regulatory activity prior to task engagement: goal adoption and planning, activation of self-efficacy beliefs, assessment of task value, activation of goal orientations, and appraisal of contextual conditions. Argued in this analysis to constitute the construction of the *regulatory architecture* that governs subsequent monitoring and control.

> [!evidence] Forethought-Phase
> The preparatory, goal-setting, and strategic planning phase of Zimmerman's SRL cycle that precedes task engagement. It encompasses two main categories of processes: Task Analysis (goal-setting and strategic planning) and Motivational Beliefs (self-efficacy, outcome expectation, goal orientation, and intrinsic interest). The quality of the Forethought Phase is the single strongest predictor of self-regulatory success.
