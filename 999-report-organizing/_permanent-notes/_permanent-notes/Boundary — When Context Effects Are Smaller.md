## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Boundary — When Context Effects Are Smaller
> **Q:** Under what conditions are encoding specificity / context-dependent memory effects reduced or eliminated?
> **A:** Context-dependent effects are attenuated when: (a) participants use deliberate mental context reinstatement (Smith, 1979, 1986); (b) material is tested via recognition rather than recall (recognition provides its own cue); (c) material is deeply semantically elaborated (creating more context-flexible traces); (d) material is highly automatized or proceduralized (procedural memory is less context-bound than episodic). These boundary conditions refine rather than refute the ESP — they specify when context is a primary determinant vs. when other factors dominate.
> **Source:** Sections 4.5, 8.1
