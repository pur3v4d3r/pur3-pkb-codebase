## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Inference Question Paradigm
> Kintsch and van Dijk (1978) and subsequent researchers showed that readers who were asked "inference questions" (questions requiring integration across text segments or combination with prior knowledge) performed dramatically worse than those asked "textbase questions" (questions answerable from explicit text statements) — but only when readers had read without strategic purpose. Readers given explicit instructions to construct an understanding of "how the described system works" showed dramatically improved inference question performance without losing textbase performance. This finding was replicated by Graesser, Singer, and Trabasso (1994) using longer, more complex texts.
