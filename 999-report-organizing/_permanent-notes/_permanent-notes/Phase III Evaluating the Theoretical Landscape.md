## Reflections

> [!reflection] Phase III: Evaluating the Theoretical Landscape
> 1. Levels of processing theory has been critiqued for circularity (deep processing is defined by memory durability, which makes the prediction unfalsifiable without independent measurement of processing depth). How does the EI research program partially address this circularity — and where does it remain vulnerable to it?
> 2. Generative learning theory and schema theory converge in predicting that prior knowledge moderates EI effectiveness. But they explain this moderation differently. What distinct predictions would each theory make about a learner who has abundant but inaccurate prior knowledge?
> 3. The generation effect contribution to EI suggests that *producing* an answer matters beyond the content of the answer. Are there circumstances in which a learner should deliberately choose a less accurate why-answer that they generated over a correct answer they merely read? What would LOP theory predict?
> *— [[elaborative-interrogation-foundational-report-2026-03-30_report]]*
