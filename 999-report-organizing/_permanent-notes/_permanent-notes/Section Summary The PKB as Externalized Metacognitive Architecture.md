> [!section-summary] Section Summary: The PKB as Externalized Metacognitive Architecture
> *The central thesis of this report is that a PKB, under appropriate conditions, functions as an externalized metacognitive architecture — not merely storing knowledge but externalizing the meta-level processes of monitoring, regulating, and evaluating cognition. The Metacognitive Architecture Mapping Framework demonstrates that each component of internal metacognition (knowledge of cognition, monitoring, regulation, metacognitive experience) has a corresponding structural externalization in PKB features. The inspectability advantage created by this externalization provides qualitatively new metacognitive capabilities beyond what internal cognition can achieve, including simultaneous inspection of knowledge states and longitudinal tracking of conceptual development.*
> *— [[pkb-as-externalized-metacognition-foundational-report-2026-04-12_report]]*
