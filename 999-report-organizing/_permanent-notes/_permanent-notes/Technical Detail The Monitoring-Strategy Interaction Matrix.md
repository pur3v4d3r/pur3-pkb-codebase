## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Technical Detail: The Monitoring-Strategy Interaction Matrix
> The interaction between monitoring accuracy and strategy selection can be mapped as a 2×2:
>
> | | **Accurate Monitoring** | **Inaccurate (Overconfident) Monitoring** |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Good Strategies Available** | Optimal: Right items get right strategies | Degraded: Right strategies applied to wrong items or termination too early |
> | **Poor Strategies Available** | Suboptimal: Right items get wrong strategies, but at least effort is directed appropriately | Worst case: Wrong strategies applied to wrong items, with premature termination |
>
> The best outcomes require BOTH accurate monitoring AND access to effective strategies. But the interaction is asymmetric: accurate monitoring without good strategies is recoverable (the learner at least knows what they don't know), while good strategies without accurate monitoring are largely wasted (the strategies are applied to the wrong targets or abandoned too soon).
>
> **Precision:** This matrix is schematic. In practice, the interaction is continuous rather than categorical.
> **Dependencies:** Full Level 3 understanding of cue reliability and this level's understanding of the discrepancy reduction model.
> *— [[metacognitive-monitoring-accuracy-calibration-deep-dive-2026-04-12_report]]*
