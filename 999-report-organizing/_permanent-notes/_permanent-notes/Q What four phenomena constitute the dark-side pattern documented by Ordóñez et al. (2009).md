> [!flashcard] Q:** What four phenomena constitute the dark-side pattern documented by Ordóñez et al. (2009)?
> **A:** (1) Narrowed attention beyond useful boundaries, (2) distorted risk preference under threshold rewards, (3) eroded cooperation under individual targets, (4) ethical compromise under unattainable difficulty.
