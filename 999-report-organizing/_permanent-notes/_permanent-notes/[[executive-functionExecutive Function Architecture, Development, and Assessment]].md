## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[executive-function|Executive Function: Architecture, Development, and Assessment]]
> The fractionation of executive function into updating, shifting, and inhibition (Miyake & Friedman) is one of the most consequential findings in modern cognitive psychology, yet this report treats it only as a component of working memory. A dedicated treatment would address the developmental trajectory of executive functions, their role in self-regulation beyond working memory contexts, the debate over the unity/diversity framework, and practical assessment methods.
>
