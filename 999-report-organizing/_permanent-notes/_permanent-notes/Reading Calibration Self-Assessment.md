## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Reading Calibration Self-Assessment
> Use this checklist when beginning a new book or sustained reading project to assess whether the text is in your Zone of Productive Difficulty.
>
> - [ ] In the first 5 pages, did I encounter 3–8 unfamiliar words (but not more)?
> - [ ] For most unfamiliar words, could I generate a plausible contextual inference without stopping to look them up?
> - [ ] Was comprehension of the passage as a whole reasonably sustained despite encountering unknown words?
> - [ ] Did I feel engaged and appropriately challenged — not frustrated and not bored?
>
> **If more than 2 items fail:** The text is likely too lexically demanding for optimal incidental acquisition. Consider building breadth in this domain before reading this text extensively.
>
> **If all 4 items pass easily with no unfamiliar words at all:** The text is likely too easy for vocabulary growth. Consider a more challenging text in the same domain or switching to a more demanding genre.
> *— [[vocabulary-building-reading-foundational-report-2026-03-28_report]]*
