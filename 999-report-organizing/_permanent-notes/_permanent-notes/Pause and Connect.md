> [!active-reading] Pause and Connect
> Before continuing, take a moment to consider how the Nelson-Narens monitoring-control model maps onto your own PKB practice. When you write a note, you are engaging in object-level cognition. When you re-read that note and evaluate whether it captures your understanding, you are engaging in meta-level monitoring. When you revise the note based on that evaluation, you are executing a meta-level control signal. This loop — write, read, evaluate, revise — is the externalized monitoring-control cycle in action. Hold this mapping in mind as we move to the theoretical framework that justifies treating these externalized processes as *genuinely cognitive*.
