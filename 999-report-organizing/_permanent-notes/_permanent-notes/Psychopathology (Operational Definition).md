# Psychopathology (Operational Definition)

> [!definition] Psychopathology (Operational Definition)
> The scientific study of mental disorder as a phenomenon occurring at multiple levels of analysis: behavioral, cognitive, emotional, neurobiological, developmental, and social. Contemporary psychopathology research increasingly employs transdiagnostic and dimensional frameworks (as in the Hierarchical Taxonomy of Psychopathology, HiTOP, and the RDoC initiative) rather than the categorical structure of the DSM, based on evidence that psychological disorders form a continuous spectrum rather than discrete categories, share genetic and neurobiological substrates across traditional diagnostic boundaries, and are better understood as extreme variants of normal psychological dimensions than as qualitatively distinct diseases.
