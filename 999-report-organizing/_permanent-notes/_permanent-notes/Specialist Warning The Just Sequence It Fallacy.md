## Practical Implications

> [!warning] Specialist Warning: The "Just Sequence It" Fallacy
> A common response to irreducibly high element interactivity is: "Just sequence the instruction — teach isolated elements first, then integrate." The isolated-interacting elements effect was designed for exactly this. However, some material cannot be meaningfully decomposed. In such cases, the isolated phase teaches elements that are *unintelligible* in isolation — the elements draw their meaning from their interactions, and learning them individually produces inert knowledge fragments rather than meaningful building blocks. The specialist must distinguish between material where isolation preserves meaning (the effect works) and material where isolation destroys meaning (the effect creates a different learning problem).
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-deep-dive-2026-04-12_report]]*
