# Reading Comprehension (Multi-Level Definition)

> [!definition] Reading Comprehension (Multi-Level Definition)
> Reading comprehension is the cognitive process of constructing meaning from text through the simultaneous activation of word identification, syntactic parsing, propositional encoding, inference generation, and situation model construction — all coordinated by metacognitive monitoring and regulation processes. In academic contexts, comprehension is adequate only when a situation model (a rich, integrated mental model of what the text is about) is constructed, not merely when a textbase representation (a propositional semantic representation of what the text says) is available.
>
> **Boundary:** Fluent word recognition and syntactic parsing are necessary but not sufficient for comprehension in the academic sense. Comprehension failure can occur despite fully accurate decoding when background knowledge, inference capacity, text structure knowledge, or metacognitive regulation is inadequate.
>
