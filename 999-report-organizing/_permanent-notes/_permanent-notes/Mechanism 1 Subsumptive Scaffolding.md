## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Mechanism 1: Subsumptive Scaffolding
> By providing a higher-order conceptual structure before instruction, the organizer gives working memory a framework into which incoming information can be slotted. Rather than holding every piece of new information as a separate entity, the learner can relate each piece to the organizer's structure — effectively chunking the new material. This reduces effective working memory load because chunks (conceptual slots) consume less capacity than the individual items they contain. The organizer transforms potentially overwhelming novelty into tractable elaboration of an existing structure.
> *— [[advance-organizers-prior-knowledge-foundational-report-2026-03-11_report]]*
