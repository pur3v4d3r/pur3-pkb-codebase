# Learning Projects (Tough, 1971)

> [!definition] Learning Projects (Tough, 1971)
> A concept from Allen Tough's empirical research designating any deliberate effort lasting at least seven hours to gain and retain specific knowledge or skills. Research using structured interviews found that average adults undertake roughly five to ten learning projects per year, approximately seventy percent of which are self-planned rather than institutionally directed. Learning projects data established the empirical foundation for the claim that SDL is not merely an educational ideal but a description of how the majority of adult learning actually occurs.
