> [!evidence] [[Metacognitive-Development-Across-Expertise-Levels|Metacognitive Development Across Expertise Levels: From Novice to Expert Self-Regulation]]
> **Description:** A developmental and expertise-oriented analysis of how metacognitive capacity changes as learners progress from novice to expert within a domain. Examines how the need for external scaffolding shifts, what metacognitive functions are most resistant to internalisation, and how expert self-regulators differ qualitatively (not just quantitatively) from novice self-regulators.
> **Connection to this report:** The Developmental Arc Model (Section 7.4) proposes four stages but does not deeply examine the empirical literature on metacognitive development across expertise levels.
