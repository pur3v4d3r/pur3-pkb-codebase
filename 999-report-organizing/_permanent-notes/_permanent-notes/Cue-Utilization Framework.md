# Cue-Utilization Framework

> [!definition] Cue-Utilization Framework
> <span style='color: #FFC700;'>The cue-utilization framework</span> (Koriat, 1997) holds that [[metacognitive-judgments|metacognitive judgments]] are based on heuristic cues drawn from three sources: intrinsic properties of the material, extrinsic properties of the study conditions, and mnemonic properties of the learner's own processing experience. The framework explains both why monitoring is sometimes accurate (the cues often do correlate with actual learning) and why it systematically fails (the correlation is imperfect and the cues are subject to specific misalignment conditions).
>
> [**Cue-Utilization-Framework**:: The dominant explanatory model for metacognitive monitoring, holding that judgments are based on heuristic inference from intrinsic, extrinsic, and mnemonic cues rather than direct access to memory trace strength (Koriat, 1997).]
