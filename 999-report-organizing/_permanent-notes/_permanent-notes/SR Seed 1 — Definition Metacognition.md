## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 1 — Definition: Metacognition
> **Q:** What is Flavell's definition of metacognition, and what two broad categories does it comprise?
> **A:** Metacognition is cognition about cognition — knowledge and processes that take one's own cognitive states and products as their objects. It comprises (a) metacognitive knowledge (stored declarative knowledge about person, task, and strategy factors affecting cognition) and (b) metacognitive experiences (real-time monitoring signals arising during cognitive engagement).
> **Source:** Section 2; Flavell (1979)
> **Difficulty:** Basic
