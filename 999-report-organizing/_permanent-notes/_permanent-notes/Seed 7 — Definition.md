> [!flashcard] Seed 7 — Definition
> **Q:** What is Flavell's distinction between metacognitive knowledge and metacognitive experiences?
> **A:** Metacognitive knowledge is stable, explicitly accessible propositional knowledge about cognitive processes, tasks, and strategies. Metacognitive experiences are phenomenological states that arise during cognitive engagement and carry monitoring information — feelings of difficulty, confidence, familiarity, and satisfaction that signal the quality of ongoing processing in real time.

> [!flashcard] Seed 7 — Definition
> **Q:** What is a generative calibration test, and why is it superior to a recognition-based comprehension check?
> **A:** A generative calibration test requires the learner to produce something the text did not provide — a novel example, a mechanism reconstruction from memory, a cross-domain connection — rather than recognising what the text said. It is superior because genuine comprehension (not fluency) is required to generate novel production; a recognition check can be passed through fluency alone.
> **Source:** Phase V
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
