## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Listening Is the Foundation of Being "Good to Talk To"
> The experience that another person is genuinely engaging with what you say — thinking with you rather than at you — is one of the most reliably positive relational experiences humans have. It is also vanishingly rare. Most conversations are exchanges of overlapping monologues loosely coordinated by social convention. The communicator who develops genuinely active listening skills becomes someone others consistently describe as unusually intelligent, empathetic, and "easy to talk to" — whether or not their verbal contributions are especially brilliant.
> *— [[how-to-communicate-effectively-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
