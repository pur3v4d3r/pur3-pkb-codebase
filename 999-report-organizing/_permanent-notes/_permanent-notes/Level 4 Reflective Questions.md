> [!reflection] Level 4 Reflective Questions
> 1. The distinction between accretion and restructuring maps onto what educational researchers call "superficial learning" vs. "deep learning." Given the dynamics described, what instructional conditions would tend to produce each? What is the student experience of each from the inside?
> 2. Schema automation makes application effortless but tacit. Design a teaching intervention that would help an automated expert recover (partially) conscious access to automated sub-schemas for teaching purposes.
