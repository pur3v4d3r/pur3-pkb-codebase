## Open Threads

> [!tension-identified] Constructivist Assessment vs. Standardized Accountability
> **The Tension:** Constructivist theory implies that valid assessment requires probing the structure of understanding — not merely the accuracy of recall. But performance assessments and portfolios that can do this are expensive, variable in evaluator judgment, and difficult to standardize for accountability purposes.
>
> **Position A — Constructivist Validity is Non-Negotiable:** Assessment that fails to probe the structure of understanding systematically misrepresents learning. The institutional inconvenience of valid assessment is not a principled objection to validity.
>
> **Position B — Accountability Requirements are Legitimate:** Standardized assessment, despite its limitations, provides the only feasible large-scale information about educational outcomes. The challenge is to improve its alignment with constructivist validity, not to abandon reliability and comparability.
>
