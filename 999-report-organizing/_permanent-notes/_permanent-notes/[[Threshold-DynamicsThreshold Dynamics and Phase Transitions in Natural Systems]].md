> [!evidence] [[Threshold-Dynamics|Threshold Dynamics and Phase Transitions in Natural Systems]]
> **Description:** A systematic investigation of threshold-mediated transitions — the general phenomenon whereby systems undergo qualitative changes when a critical parameter exceeds a boundary value. Drawing on star formation (Jeans instability, nuclear ignition), physics (phase transitions), biology (developmental thresholds), and cognitive science (insight, skill automaticity). This would formalise the Threshold Cascade Model introduced in Section 5.
