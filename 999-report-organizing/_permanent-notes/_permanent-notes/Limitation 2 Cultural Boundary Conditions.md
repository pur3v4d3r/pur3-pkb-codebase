## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Limitation 2: Cultural Boundary Conditions
> While cross-cultural replications are substantial, they are not unlimited. Research suggests that in contexts of genuine economic scarcity, financial security goals have a somewhat different functional relationship to well-being than GCT's standard predictions would suggest — they may be more functionally related to genuine safety needs than to the comparative-social-status dynamics GCT associates with extrinsic goal content in economically secure samples. The theory is most confidently applied in contexts where basic material needs are met, and its predictions should be qualified when applied to populations experiencing genuine economic deprivation.
> *— [[goal-contents-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-13_report]]*
