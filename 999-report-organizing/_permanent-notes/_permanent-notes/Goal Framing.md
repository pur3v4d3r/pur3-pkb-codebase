# Goal Framing

> [!definition] Goal Framing
> The language and rationale used to describe the *purpose* of a goal or task, independent of the task's objective content. Intrinsic goal framing emphasizes personal growth, interest, understanding, or relational value; extrinsic goal framing emphasizes grades, external evaluation, career advancement, or social comparison. Experimental manipulation of goal framing has been shown to causally affect learning depth, motivation quality, and transfer performance (Vansteenkiste et al., 2004, 2007).
