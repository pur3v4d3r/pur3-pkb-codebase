## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Measurement Problem Remains Unsolved
> Despite decades of SRL research, there is no widely adopted, validated measure of SRL that directly assesses the quality of the regulatory process rather than self-reported strategy frequency. Process measures (think-aloud protocols, eye-tracking, digital trace data from learning management systems) provide superior construct validity but are expensive, intrusive, and context-specific. This measurement gap means that the interventional evidence is largely based on outcome measures (achievement) rather than process measures (did the intervention actually change the regulatory cycle?), leaving the specific mechanisms of change uncertain even in successful interventions.
> *— [[self-regulated-learning-focused-analysis-2026-03-06_report]]*
