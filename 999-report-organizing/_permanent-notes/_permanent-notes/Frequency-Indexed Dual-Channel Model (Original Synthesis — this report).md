# Frequency-Indexed Dual-Channel Model (Original Synthesis — this report)

> [!definition] Frequency-Indexed Dual-Channel Model (Original Synthesis — this report)
> **Definition:** A reconstructed model of vocabulary acquisition that assigns different acquisition strategies to different vocabulary frequency bands and knowledge dimensions. *Channel A (Deliberate Instruction)* handles high-to-mid-frequency vocabulary (approximately the 1–9,000 word family range) using spaced retrieval practice, frequency-ranked word lists, and morphological instruction to establish the vocabulary threshold. *Channel B (Extensive Reading with Active Noticing)* handles consolidation, collocational enrichment, and exposure to contextually embedded low-frequency vocabulary — but only after Channel A has established the threshold. The two channels are sequentially dependent, with Channel A as the prerequisite for Channel B's efficiency.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** The original contribution of this dialectical re-examination — a reconstructed model that preserves what is correct in the reading-primary hypothesis while specifying the conditions under which it holds and the complementary mechanism required when those conditions are not met.
>
