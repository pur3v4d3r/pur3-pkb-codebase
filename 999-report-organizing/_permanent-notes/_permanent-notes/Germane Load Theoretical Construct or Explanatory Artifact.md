## Open Threads

> [!tension-identified] Germane Load: Theoretical Construct or Explanatory Artifact?
> **Position A (Retained Category):** Germane cognitive load — the load generated by effortful cognitive processing that contributes to schema construction — is a theoretically necessary category that explains why instructional techniques promoting active processing (elaboration, self-explanation, variability of practice) enhance learning despite increasing total cognitive load.
>
> **Position B (Eliminated Category):** Sweller's (2010) revision collapsed germane load into a reallocation of intrinsic load. The category was redundant because the processes attributed to germane load are simply the processes of dealing with intrinsic load effectively. The two-factor model (intrinsic + extraneous) is more parsimonious.
>
> **Current evidence:** The field remains divided. The three-factor model has greater explanatory utility for instructional designers (it distinguishes "good" from "bad" cognitive demands), but the two-factor model is more theoretically parsimonious and avoids the circularity of defining germane load by its outcomes.
>
> **This report's stance:** The report uses germane load as a heuristic category while acknowledging its contested status, consistent with van Merriënboer's continued use of the tripartite framework.
> *— [[four-component-instructional-design-model-4cid-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*
