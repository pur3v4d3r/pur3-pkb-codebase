## Additional Material

> [!protocol] Protocol 1: The Pre-Sleep Review Protocol
> *Based on:* Sleep-and-memory consolidation research; synaptic consolidation window
>
> **Purpose:** Maximize consolidation of material learned earlier in the day during NREM slow-wave sleep reactivation.
>
> **Steps:**
> 1. Within 30-60 minutes before sleep, briefly review the key ideas from the day's learning without re-reading in full (avoid over-stimulation).
> 2. Use *retrieval-based review*: close notes and write/say the key ideas from memory; only then check what was missed.
> 3. Create a brief one-sentence "consolidation cue" for each major concept — a hook that will serve as the morning retrieval cue.
> 4. On waking, before checking phone or beginning other tasks, attempt to retrieve the consolidation cues from the previous evening.
> 5. Note what was retained vs. lost — this provides a rough estimate of consolidation success and identifies items needing immediate review.
>
> **Contraindications:** Avoid highly arousing emotional material immediately pre-sleep; emotional activation delays sleep onset and may interfere with slow-wave sleep.
> *— [[memory-systems-working-memory-and-long-term-memory-foundational-report-2026-04-07_report]]*
