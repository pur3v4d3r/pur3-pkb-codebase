## Examples

> [!example] The Template as Regulatory Scaffolding
> Consider a PKM template for processing research papers that includes the following prompts: "What is the central claim?", "What evidence supports it?", "How does this relate to [existing note X]?", "Where is the argument weakest?", "What would I need to know to evaluate this claim myself?" Each prompt is a *regulatory instruction* — a control directive from a meta-level (the template designer, who was either the practitioner's past self or a community member) to the object-level (the practitioner's current cognitive processing). The template instantiates [[metacognitive-regulation|metacognitive regulation]] in an external structure, ensuring that regulatory processes are executed even when the practitioner's in-the-moment metacognitive capacity is depleted by the demands of processing complex material. This is a prime example of what [[cognitive-load-theory|cognitive load theory]] would describe as reducing extraneous load: the template handles the regulatory overhead, freeing cognitive resources for the germane processing of content.
> *— [[metacognition-and-pkm-foundational-report-2026-04-12_report]]*
