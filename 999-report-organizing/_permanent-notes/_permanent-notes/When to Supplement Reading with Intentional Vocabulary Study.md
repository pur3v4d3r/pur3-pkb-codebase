## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] When to Supplement Reading with Intentional Vocabulary Study
> **Context:** To decide when reading-based acquisition alone is sufficient and when deliberate vocabulary study should be added.
>
> ```
> Q1: Is the vocabulary domain general (everyday, literary, journalistic)?
>   YES → Q2: Do you read ≥30 min/day in material at 98-99% coverage?
>           YES → Reading alone is likely sufficient for continued growth
>           NO  → Increase reading volume and/or improve material selection
>   NO  (domain-specific: academic, technical, professional) →
>     Q3: Is the vocabulary high-frequency within your target domain?
>       YES → Q4: Are you reading extensively in that domain (≥2 hrs/week)?
>               YES → Supplement with morphological study of key roots
>               NO  → Increase domain-specific reading first
>       NO  (low-frequency or highly specialized) →
>         → Intentional study required — reading alone will not provide
>           sufficient exposure frequency for these terms.
>           Recommendation: Direct study + contextual reading in domain texts.
> ```
> *— [[reading-vocabulary-focused-analysis-2026-03-28_report]]*
