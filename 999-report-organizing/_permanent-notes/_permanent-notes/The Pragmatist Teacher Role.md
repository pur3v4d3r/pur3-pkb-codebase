# The Pragmatist Teacher Role

> [!definition] The Pragmatist Teacher Role
> In pragmatist pedagogy, the teacher's primary role is the design and management of problem environments — the selection of genuine difficulties that are optimally challenging for the learner's current level, the provision of resources and scaffolding that support inquiry without eliminating it, and the stimulation of reflective self-awareness through productive questioning. This contrasts with the transmission model, in which the teacher's primary role is efficient presentation of organized content. The distinction maps directly onto the difference between [[formative-assessment|formative]] and summative assessment orientations.
