## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Directionality Remains Partially Contested
> While the bidirectional relationship between metacognition and motivation is broadly accepted, the causal primacy question — which variable should be targeted first in interventions — remains genuinely open. Some researchers argue that motivational orientation is the more foundational variable, and that metacognitive skill development follows naturally from intrinsic motivation. Others argue that metacognitive skill is the more teachable variable, and that its development produces motivational benefits as a secondary consequence. The empirical evidence does not definitively resolve this question, and pragmatic answers likely depend on the age, prior learning history, and specific context of the learner.
> *— [[metacognition-motivation-foundational-report-2026-03-05_report]]*
