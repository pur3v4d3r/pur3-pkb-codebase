> [!cite] McNamara, D. S., & Kintsch, W. (1996). Learning from texts: Effects of prior knowledge and text coherence. *Discourse Processes, 22*(3), 247–288.
> Counterintuitive finding: high-knowledge readers learn more from low-coherence texts (they must generate more inferences, producing deeper processing); low-knowledge readers learn more from high-coherence texts (explicit coherence compensates for absent prior knowledge). Demonstrates the interaction between domain knowledge and text coherence in situation model construction.
