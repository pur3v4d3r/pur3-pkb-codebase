## Open Threads

> [!open-question] Surface Your Current Post-Reading Practice
> What do you do after finishing a complex academic report in your PKB? If you are honest: do you close the file and move on? Do you write a brief summary note? Do you compare your understanding to your goals? Do you ask yourself why certain sections were harder? Do you consider what you will do differently next time? Most readers — even sophisticated ones with established PKB systems — engage in something like "passive processing" after reading: a vague sense of completion, perhaps some note-filing, and then forward movement to the next task. The Self-Reflection Phase, properly executed, is something fundamentally different — and the difference compounds over time in ways that can be measured.
> *— [[srl-forethought-self-reflection-pkb-focused-analysis-2026-03-24_report]]*
