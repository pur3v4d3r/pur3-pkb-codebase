# Decoding

> [!definition] Decoding
> The translation of printed symbols (graphemes) into their phonological equivalents (phonemes) and from there to word-level representations. Decoding is the primary focus of early reading instruction and is the foundation on which fluency and comprehension are built. Decoding accuracy (getting the correct phonological representation) is distinguished from decoding automaticity (getting it without consuming working memory resources).
