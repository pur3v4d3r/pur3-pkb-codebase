# Personal Learning Environment — PLE

> [!definition] **Personal Learning Environment — PLE**
> A self-curated digital ecosystem of tools, platforms, communities, and resources that a learner constructs and maintains to support their ongoing learning across domains and over time. Unlike institutional learning management systems, PLEs are owned and controlled by the learner and persist beyond any single course or program.
> 
> **Boundary:** A PLE is not simply a collection of bookmarks or tools; it implies deliberate curation and ongoing management of a learning infrastructure aligned with the learner's goals and needs.
> 
> **Report-Specific Significance:** Identified as the primary contemporary infrastructure for self-directed learning in digital environments, with PKM systems (Obsidian, Roam, etc.) representing the most systematic form.
>
