## Additional Material

> [!person] Teun van Dijk (b. 1943)
> Dutch discourse analyst; Professor Emeritus, Universitat Pompeu Fabra (Barcelona). With Walter Kintsch, developed the tripartite text representation framework in *Strategies in Discourse Comprehension* (1983), introducing the situation model concept. Van Dijk's primary contribution was the discourse-analytical architecture: macrostructure, superstructure, and the situational dimension of comprehension.
>
> **Lineage:** Influenced by Chomskyan linguistics and European structuralism; influenced later work in critical discourse analysis and sociolinguistic approaches to reading.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** Co-originator of the three-level representation architecture that is this report's foundational theoretical commitment.
> *— [[situation-models-text-comprehension-foundational-report-2026-03-29_report]]*
