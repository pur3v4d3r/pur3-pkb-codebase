## Methodology & Sources

> [!cite] Key Empirical Studies and Reviews
> Dweck, C. S. (1986). Motivational processes affecting learning. *American Psychologist, 41*(10), 1040–1048.
> *Early synthesis of Dweck's research program connecting goals to response patterns.*
>
> Ames, C., & Archer, J. (1988). Achievement goals in the classroom: Students' learning strategies and motivation processes. *Journal of Educational Psychology, 80*(3), 260–267.
> *Classic empirical study demonstrating the classroom behavioral correlates of mastery vs. performance goal adoption.*
>
> Harackiewicz, J. M., Barron, K. E., Pintrich, P. R., Elliot, A. J., & Thrash, T. M. (2002). Revision of achievement goal theory: Necessary and illuminating. *Journal of Educational Psychology, 94*(3), 638–645.
> *Key paper in the debate over the rehabilitation of performance-approach goals.*
>
> Midgley, C., et al. (2000). *Manual for the Patterns of Adaptive Learning Scales*. University of Michigan.
> *Standard instrument for measuring perceived classroom goal structures and individual goal adoption; extensively used in educational intervention research.*
>
> Huang, C. (2012). Discriminant and criterion-related validity of achievement goals in predicting academic achievement: A meta-analysis. *Journal of Educational Psychology, 104*(1), 48–73.
> *Comprehensive meta-analysis confirming the differential predictive validity of the 2×2 goal cells.*
>
> Elliot, A. J., Murayama, K., & Pekrun, R. (2011). A 3×2 achievement goal model. *Journal of Educational Psychology, 103*(3), 632–648.
> *Extension to the 3×2 framework distinguishing task, self, and other referencing.*
>
> Linnenbrink-Garcia, L., Tyson, D. F., & Patall, E. A. (2008). When are achievement goal orientations beneficial for academic achievement? Timing, individual differences, and achievement-goal contextual interactions. *International Review of Social Psychology, 21*(1–2), 19–70.
> *Sophisticated analysis of moderators and context-dependence in achievement-goal-outcome associations.*
