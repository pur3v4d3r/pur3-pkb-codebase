# Constructivism (Educational)

> [!definition] Constructivism (Educational)
> The family of educational theories deriving from the epistemological claim that knowledge is actively constructed by learners rather than passively received. Educational constructivism encompasses diverse positions united by this core claim — ranging from Piagetian cognitive constructivism to Vygotskian social constructivism to radical constructivism to situated learning theory — that differ in their emphasis on the individual, social, or context-embedded dimensions of construction.
>
> **Boundary:** Educational constructivism is a theory of learning, not a theory of teaching. It describes how learning occurs; it does not straightforwardly prescribe how instruction should proceed. The inference from constructivist epistemology to minimally guided instruction is an additional theoretical step that requires and has received independent empirical scrutiny.
>
