> [!protocol] SDL Self-Assessment Protocol
> **Purpose:** Evaluate your current self-directed learning capacity across the three pillars of the Metacognitive Sovereignty Framework.
>
> **Step 1 — Metacognitive Sovereignty Assessment:**
> - Select a recent self-directed learning project.
> - Rate (1-5): How accurately did you assess your starting knowledge? How effectively did you monitor your comprehension during learning? How appropriately did you adjust strategies when something wasn't working?
> - Identify: What was your biggest metacognitive blind spot?
>
> **Step 2 — Motivational Alignment Assessment:**
> - For the same project: Did you learn because you wanted to (intrinsic) or because you had to (extrinsic)?
> - Rate (1-5): How confident were you in your ability to learn this material? How persistent were you when encountering difficulty?
> - Identify: What sustained your motivation? What threatened it?
>
> **Step 3 — Contextual Navigation Assessment:**
> - Rate (1-5): How effectively did you identify and evaluate learning resources? How well did you manage your learning environment (time, space, tools)?
> - Identify: What contextual factors supported your learning? What hindered it?
>
> **Step 4 — Integration:**
> - Which pillar is your strongest? Which needs the most development?
> - Design one specific action to strengthen your weakest pillar.
> *— [[self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*
