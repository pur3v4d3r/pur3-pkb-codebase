## Additional Material

> [!person] Rolf Zwaan (b. ~1963)
> Dutch cognitive scientist; Professor, Erasmus University Rotterdam. Developed the Event Indexing Model (with Langston & Graesser, 1995; with Radvansky, 1998) and the embodied simulation account of language comprehension. His situation change paradigm provided the primary empirical methodology for testing the five-dimension account.
>
> **Lineage:** Trained in the Kintsch tradition; influenced by Perky's mental imagery research and Barsalou's perceptual symbol systems theory.
>
> **Relationship to Report:** The Event Indexing Model provides the structural account of what situation models contain, and is the theoretical basis for the DIME annotation protocol.
> *— [[situation-models-text-comprehension-foundational-report-2026-03-29_report]]*
