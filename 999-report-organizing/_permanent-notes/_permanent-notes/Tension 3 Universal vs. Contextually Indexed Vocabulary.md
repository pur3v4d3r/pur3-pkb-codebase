## Open Threads

> [!tension-identified] Tension 3: Universal vs. Contextually Indexed Vocabulary
> **Position A:** Growth-oriented attributional vocabulary ("not yet," "effort + strategy") represents an objectively superior motivational architecture that is universally applicable.
>
> **Position B:** What counts as constructive inner speech vocabulary is culturally and contextually indexed. The specific formulations of growth mindset and attribution retraining research carry cultural presuppositions that may not translate across contexts.
>
> **Current Evidence State:** The intervention evidence base is predominantly WEIRD-sample. Cross-cultural studies show some variation in the effectiveness of specific formulations, though the broad directional claim (controllable-cause attribution supports motivation better than uncontrollable-cause in modifiable situations) appears robust.
>
> **Why This Matters:** Determines how the vocabulary development program should be customized for individual learners and contexts.
>
> **This Report's Resolution:** The reconstruction preserves the core directional claim while requiring cultural and contextual calibration in specific vocabulary choices. The accuracy condition — vocabulary should reflect genuine causal structure, not just impose optimistic framing — is especially important.
> *— [[inner-speech-vocabulary-motivation-self-efficacy-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-25_report]]*
