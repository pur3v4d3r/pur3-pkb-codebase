## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The RDoC Challenge to Categorical Diagnosis
> The Research Domain Criteria (RDoC) framework, developed by NIMH, proposes a radical alternative: rather than defining disorder categories by behavioral symptom clusters (as the DSM does), organize research around biobehavioral dimensions — such as threat responding, reward learning, and cognitive control — that cut across traditional diagnostic categories and can be studied at multiple levels (genetic, neural, behavioral, self-report). RDoC does not replace DSM for clinical purposes, but it represents a growing scientific consensus that the DSM's diagnostic categories do not correspond to discrete biological entities and may not be the most fruitful units for etiological research.
> *— [[clinical-psychology-foundational-report-2026-03-19_report]]*
