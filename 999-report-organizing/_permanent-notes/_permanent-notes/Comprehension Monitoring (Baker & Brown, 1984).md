# Comprehension Monitoring (Baker & Brown, 1984)

> [!definition] Comprehension Monitoring (Baker & Brown, 1984)
> The metacognitive process of continuously evaluating the adequacy of one's current text representation against multiple standards (lexical, semantic, propositional, situational) and detecting when that representation has become inadequate to the reading goal. Comprehension monitoring is the evaluative half of the metacognitive reading loop; fix-up strategy deployment is the regulatory half.
>
> **Boundary:** Comprehension monitoring is distinct from comprehension itself; a reader may monitor accurately (detect failures) while the underlying comprehension deficit remains. It is also distinct from reading fluency; fluent reading can occur with minimal comprehension monitoring.
>
> **Common Misconception:** Many readers and teachers conflate comprehension monitoring with "checking understanding" at the end of reading. In fact, authentic comprehension monitoring is a continuous, within-text process, not a post-hoc evaluation.
>
> **Research Status:** Among the best-established empirical findings in reading psychology: deficits in comprehension monitoring reliably distinguish skilled from unskilled comprehenders, independent of decoding ability or vocabulary size.
>
> **See also:** [[comprehension-monitoring]], [[metacognitive-monitoring]], [[fix-up-strategy]], [[fluency-illusion]], [[monitoring-control-coupling]]

> [!evidence] Comprehension Monitoring (Baker & Brown, 1984)
> The metacognitive process by which a reader continuously evaluates the state of their developing comprehension during reading and detects breakdowns when the developing representation fails to cohere with the incoming text. Comprehension monitoring is a specific application of the broader metacognitive monitoring function, specialized for the particular cognitive activity of reading and operating against criteria collectively known as the standard of coherence.
>
> **Boundary:** Comprehension monitoring is not the same as comprehension itself, nor is it identical with reading comprehension assessment after the fact; it is the *online* process by which the reader maintains awareness of comprehension state during reading, distinct from both the underlying construction of meaning and the post-hoc evaluation of what was understood.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** Comprehension monitoring is the specific metacognitive process around which the architecture of skilled metacognitive reading is organized. Failures of monitoring are the most extensively documented metacognitive reading failures.
>
> **See also:** [[comprehension-monitoring]], [[Linda-Baker]], [[ann-l.-brown]], [[reading-comprehension-strategies]]
> *— [[metacognition-and-reading-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> [!evidence] Comprehension Monitoring (Baker & Brown, 1984)
> The process of evaluating one's own text comprehension during reading — detecting failures of understanding, inconsistencies between text and background knowledge, and gaps in situational coherence. Comprehension monitoring is a specific application of [[metacognitive-monitoring]] to the reading domain. It is distinguished from comprehension *testing* (external assessment of comprehension) by its real-time, self-directed character.
