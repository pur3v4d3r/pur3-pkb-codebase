## Practical Implications

> [!warning] The Hidden Self-Protection Trap
> Zimmerman notes that one of the most common failure modes in Self-Judgment is the deployment of **self-protective attributions** — attributions that protect self-esteem in the short term at the cost of regulatory information in the longer term. Reading a complex theoretical text and finding it confusing is more psychologically comfortable if attributed to the text's density than to gaps in one's own schema for the domain. But comfort purchased at the price of accurate attribution is a bad trade. The SRL system needs accurate diagnostic information to improve. Self-protection corrupts that information.
>
