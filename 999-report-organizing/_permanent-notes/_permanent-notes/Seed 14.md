## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Seed 14
> **Q:** What is the "Early Implementation Dip" and how should a PKB learner respond to it?
> **A:** The Early Implementation Dip is the initial performance degradation that occurs when learners begin implementing explicit self-regulatory protocols — because the cognitive overhead of the new practices competes with resources available for content processing. It is normal, temporary, and expected. The correct response is to persist with process-goal evaluation standards (counting success by protocol execution, not comprehension outcome), scaffold the protocol by implementing one zone at a time, and explicitly expect the dip rather than interpreting it as evidence that the protocol is wrong.
