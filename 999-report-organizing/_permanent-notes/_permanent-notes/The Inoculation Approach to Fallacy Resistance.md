## Practical Implications

> [!warning] The Inoculation Approach to Fallacy Resistance
> Research on [[inoculation-theory]] by William McGuire and, more recently, Sander van der Linden has shown that *inoculation* — presenting weakened versions of misleading arguments alongside explicit refutations — is significantly more effective at producing fallacy resistance than merely providing accurate information. The communicator who wants to help their audience think more clearly benefits from explicitly naming and unpacking the misleading moves in circulation, not just making positive arguments for accurate conclusions. This is particularly relevant in the digital information environment (Section 8) where fallacious arguments circulate at enormous scale.
> *— [[how-to-communicate-effectively-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
