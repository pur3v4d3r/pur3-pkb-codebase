# Defensive Inference (Zimmerman, 2000)

> [!definition] Defensive Inference (Zimmerman, 2000)
> A Self-Reaction that reduces the psychological threat of failure by disengaging from the performance domain. Common forms: self-handicapping, procrastination, task devaluation. Psychologically functional in the short term; learning-destructive over cycles.
