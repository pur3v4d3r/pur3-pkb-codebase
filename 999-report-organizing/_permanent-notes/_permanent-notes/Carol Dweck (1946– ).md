## Additional Material

> [!person] Carol Dweck (1946– )
> **Core Contribution:** Independently developed the mastery-performance distinction (originally framed as learning vs. performance goals) from a social-cognitive perspective focused on implicit theories of intelligence. Identified the helpless vs. mastery-oriented response patterns as the behavioral signatures of fixed vs. growth mindset.
> **Relationship to Other Figures:** Developed parallel to but largely independently of Nicholls; converged with Elliot in recognizing the approach-avoidance distinction as critical to understanding achievement motivation.
> **Key Works:** *Self-theories: Their role in motivation, personality, and development* (1999); "Motivational processes affecting learning" (*American Psychologist*, 1986).
> **Relevance to This Analysis:** Dweck's framework provides the developmental and cognitive pathway through which implicit theories of intelligence generate characteristic goal orientations, grounding the approach-avoidance analysis in a broader theory of the self.
> *— [[achievement-goal-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-24_report]]*

> [!person] Carol Dweck (1946– )
> **Core Contribution:** Developed the Implicit Theories of Intelligence framework (Growth Mindset vs. Fixed Mindset), demonstrating that the implicit theory activated by praise vocabulary determines motivational and performance consequences — with ability praise producing fixed mindset fragility and process/effort praise producing growth mindset resilience.
>
> **Relationship to Other Figures:** Directly connects to Weiner's attribution dimensions (ability praise = stable-internal-uncontrollable); influenced and was influenced by Bandura's efficacy work; her work on praise vocabulary is the most direct antecedent to this report's emphasis on inner speech vocabulary.
>
> **Key Works:** *Self-Theories* (1999); *Mindset: The New Psychology of Success* (2006)
>
> **Relevance:** Growth Mindset Theory provides the evidence that vocabulary (specifically the vocabulary of self-assessment) shapes underlying motivational architecture, not just surface mood.
> *— [[inner-speech-attribution-self-efficacy-focused-analysis-2026-03-25_report]]*
