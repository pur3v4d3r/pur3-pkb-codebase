## Additional Material

> [!person] Tim Kasser (1966–)
> **[[tim-kasser]]** — American psychologist at Knox College whose doctoral work with [[richard-ryan]] at the University of Rochester produced the foundational GCT publications (1993, 1996). Kasser developed the [[aspiration-index]], conducted the seminal studies linking materialistic value orientation to diminished well-being, and extended GCT into ecological behavior, advertising effects, and socioeconomic influences on aspiration formation. His book *The High Price of Materialism* (2002) translated GCT findings for popular audiences.
> *— [[goal-contents-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-01_report]]*

> [!person] Tim Kasser (1966–)
> Knox College. Developed Goal Contents Theory with Ryan, creating the Aspiration Index and generating the research program demonstrating that the relative priority of extrinsic (wealth, fame, image) over intrinsic (growth, relationships, community) goals predicts diminished well-being.
>
> **Relationship to other figures:** Student of Ryan; extended SDT into materialist values research; connected GCT to environmental sustainability.
>
> **Key works:** Kasser & Ryan (1993, 1996), Kasser (2002) *The High Price of Materialism*.
