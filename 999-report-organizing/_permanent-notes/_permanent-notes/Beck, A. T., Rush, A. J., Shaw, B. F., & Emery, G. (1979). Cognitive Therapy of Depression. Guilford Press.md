## Methodology & Sources

> [!cite] Beck, A. T., Rush, A. J., Shaw, B. F., & Emery, G. (1979). *Cognitive Therapy of Depression*. Guilford Press.
> The foundational text establishing cognitive therapy's theoretical basis and clinical method for depression. Presents the cognitive model (cognitive triad, cognitive distortions, schemas) and the major therapeutic techniques (Socratic questioning, behavioral experiments, activity scheduling). Remains essential reading for understanding the intellectual architecture that subsequent CBT developments have elaborated, modified, and applied to other disorders. Supports Phase III and Phase IV discussions of CBT theory and mechanism.
> *— [[clinical-psychology-foundational-report-2026-03-19_report]]*
