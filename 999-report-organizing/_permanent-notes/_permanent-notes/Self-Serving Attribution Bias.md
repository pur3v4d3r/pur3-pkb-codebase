# Self-Serving Attribution Bias

> [!definition] Self-Serving Attribution Bias
> The **self-serving attribution bias** is the motivationally driven tendency to attribute success to internal, personal factors (I am skilled, I worked hard, I had a good plan) and failure to external, situational factors (the task was unfair, I had bad luck, circumstances conspired against me). This asymmetry in blame assignment vs. credit-taking serves self-enhancing and self-protective functions: it maximizes pride from success while minimizing shame from failure. The bias is most pronounced when both self-concept and social presentation are at stake, and it can be reduced or even reversed in certain cultural contexts and by specific accountability conditions.
