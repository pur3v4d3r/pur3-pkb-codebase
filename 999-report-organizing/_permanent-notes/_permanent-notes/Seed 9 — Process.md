## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Seed 9 — Process
> **Q:** What is the self-reflection phase vocabulary audit, and why is conducting it after each reading session motivationally important?
> **A:** The vocabulary audit reviews: (1) dominant attribution type (ability vs. strategy); (2) regulatory register (controlled vs. autonomous); (3) comprehension criterion applied; (4) efficacy accuracy (evidence-based vs. emotion-contaminated). It is important because the self-reflection phase's attributions feed directly into the forethought phase of the next learning episode — maladaptive attributions made during self-reflection become the prior motivational conditions for future reading.
> **Source:** Phase V
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate

> [!flashcard] Seed 9 — Process
> **Q:** Describe the Failure Cascade in situation model construction.
>
> **A:** Working memory load from dense text reduces available resources for inference generation (Mechanism 1). Automatic textbase building proceeds fluently, producing a false comprehension signal (Mechanism 2, Textbase Trap). The false signal terminates active monitoring and inference generation before the causal spine is established (Mechanism 3). Without active inference generation, the CATSM dimensions remain incoherent (Mechanism 4). The reader progresses through the text building a rich textbase and a deficient situation model, confident throughout.
>
> **Source:** Phase IV, Dynamic Interaction section

> **Type:** Process
