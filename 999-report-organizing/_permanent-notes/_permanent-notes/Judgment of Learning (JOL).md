# Judgment of Learning (JOL)

> [!definition] Judgment of Learning (JOL)
> A metacognitive judgment made by a learner about how well they have encoded material — "I understand this," "I would recall this on a test," "I am confident about this claim." JOLs are systematically inflated by passive study strategies that produce fluency without genuine understanding. Generative strategies tend to produce more accurate JOLs because they require actual retrieval and production, revealing comprehension failures that re-reading masks. Accurate JOLs are essential for effective self-regulated learning. See also: [[metacognition]], [[metacognitive-regulation]], [[self-regulated-learning]]

> [!evidence] Judgment of Learning (JOL)
> A metacognitive judgment made during or after study assessing how well particular material has been learned and how likely it is to be remembered later. JOLs are the most extensively studied metacognitive monitoring judgment. Delayed JOLs (made after a brief delay from study) are more accurate than immediate JOLs because they require actual retrieval rather than relying on current processing fluency as a proxy for memory.

> [!evidence] Judgment of Learning (JOL)
> A JOL is a metacognitive monitoring assessment in which a learner judges the probability that they will recall an item or concept in a future memory test. JOLs are measured on scales from "definitely will not remember" to "definitely will remember" (or equivalently, 0-100%). JOL accuracy (the correlation between JOLs and actual test performance) is a canonical measure of metacognitive monitoring quality and a core dependent variable in the Nelson-Narens experimental tradition.
>
> **Boundary:** JOLs are a specific type of monitoring judgment over memory items; the construct does not automatically generalize to comprehension judgments (which may tap different monitoring mechanisms than recall predictions). Delayed JOLs (made after a delay since study) are more accurate than immediate JOLs due to a variety of processing differences.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** JOLs are the experimental handle by which the Nelson-Narens framework was operationalized and tested, making them the most thoroughly studied metacognitive monitoring construct.
>
