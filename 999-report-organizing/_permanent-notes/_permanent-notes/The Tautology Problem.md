## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Tautology Problem
> The most persistent methodological critique of schema theory is that it risks being tautological: a schema is inferred from the evidence (successful comprehension, systematic recall errors, context-dependent memory) rather than independently measured, making it difficult to specify in advance what schemas a learner has and to falsify the theory. The response in research practice has been to operationalize schemas through specific experimental manipulations (e.g., assigning participants to different interpretive contexts before reading), but this workaround limits the theory's predictive generativity outside controlled settings.
> *— [[schema-theory-and-learning-foundational-report-2026-03-06_report]]*
