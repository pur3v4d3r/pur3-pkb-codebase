> [!flashcard] Seed 01
> **Q:** What is the vocabulary threshold (text coverage percentage) required for comfortable, acquisition-enabling independent reading in English according to Hu and Nation (2000)?
> **A:** Approximately 98% — meaning the reader must already know roughly 98 in 100 running words. This corresponds to approximately 8,000–9,000 word families for typical fiction and more for academic text.
