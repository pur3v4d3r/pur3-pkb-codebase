## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Original: Calibration–Regulation Coupling Thesis
> **Q:** What does the Calibration–Regulation Coupling Thesis propose about the relationship between monitoring accuracy types and control decisions?
> **A:** The thesis proposes that calibration (absolute accuracy) maps primarily onto threshold control decisions (e.g., when to stop studying an item), while resolution (relative accuracy) maps primarily onto allocation control decisions (e.g., which items to prioritise for further study). Different types of monitoring training are therefore needed for different types of control problems.
