## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Seed 09
> **Q:** What is the difference between receptive and productive vocabulary knowledge, and why does reading primarily build the former?
> **A:** Receptive vocabulary comprises words a reader can recognise and approximately understand in context; productive vocabulary comprises words a speaker/writer can accurately deploy in their own output. Productive vocabulary is typically 50–70% of receptive vocabulary in size and substantially shallower in depth. Reading builds receptive knowledge because the cognitive task is comprehension (processing incoming words for meaning), not production (generating words from memory). Productive knowledge requires active use, generation, and retrieval practice — cognitive operations that reading rarely demands.
