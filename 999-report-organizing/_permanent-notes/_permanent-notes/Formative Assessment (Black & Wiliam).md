# Formative Assessment (Black & Wiliam)

> [!definition] Formative Assessment (Black & Wiliam)
> Assessment designed to inform and improve ongoing learning rather than to certify or rank attainment. Characterised by three functions: making learning goals and success criteria transparent, providing evidence of current standing relative to those criteria, and indicating actions to close the gap between current and desired states.
>
> **Boundary:** Formative assessment is defined by its *function* (informing learning), not its *form* (quizzes, rubrics, etc.). Any assessment mechanism that generates actionable feedback for the learner is formative, regardless of format.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** Formative assessment principles translate directly into PKM monitoring-layer design — self-assessment prompts, capability-based criteria, and strategy suggestions.
>
> **See also:** [[formative-assessment]], [[feedback-design]], [[informational-feedback]], [[scaffolding]]
