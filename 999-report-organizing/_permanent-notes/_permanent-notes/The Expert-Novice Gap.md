## Open Threads

> [!open-question] The Expert-Novice Gap
> The research on expert-novice differences in scientific reasoning consistently shows that experts generate *fewer* hypotheses, not more — but higher-quality ones that are more precisely aligned with the experimental data. What does this suggest about what develops when someone becomes more skilled at abductive reasoning? Is it more practice at generating hypotheses, more knowledge to constrain which hypotheses are plausible, or something else?
