## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Frontier Question: Is Predictive Processing a Replacement for Schema Theory or a Restatement?
> **The question:** The PP framework appears to subsume schema theory into a broader computational framework. Is this a genuine theoretical advance (explaining schema-theory predictions from more fundamental principles) or primarily a vocabulary substitution (renaming schemas "priors" without adding explanatory power)?
>
> **Position A (PP as genuine unification):** PP provides a computational explanation for *why* schemas have the structure they do (they are Bayesian priors over generative models), *why* they resist accommodation (precision-attenuation of prediction errors), and *why* automatic schema activation occurs (hierarchical predictive inference). The PP framework also generates novel predictions not in original schema theory, such as specific predictions about how precision affects schema updating.
>
> **Position B (PP as descriptive restatement):** PP remains sufficiently flexible (via precision-weighting and hierarchical structure) to accommodate almost any finding post-hoc. The claim that schemas ARE Bayesian priors is not falsifiable in the standard tests — it predicts all the same qualitative results as schema theory. The PP framework's greater generativity is at the level of neural implementation, not psychological prediction.
>
> **What the debate hinges on:** Whether PP makes *novel, risky predictions* that schema theory does not, and whether those predictions are confirmed. The precision-weighting account of accommodation resistance is a candidate — it predicts that pharmacological manipulation of neuromodulators that affect precision weighting (e.g., acetylcholine, dopamine) should selectively affect schema updating. Some supporting evidence exists.
>
> **Current state:** Converging, but slowly. The PP framework is increasingly adopted as a descriptive vocabulary in schema research, but its computational superiority over traditional schema accounts has not been definitively demonstrated.
> *— [[schema-and-how-they-work-deep-dive-2026-04-08_report]]*
