## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Rabbit Hole: Body Schemas and the Embodied Cognition Connection
> **What it is:** The claim that propositional cognitive schemas are grounded in sensorimotor (body) schemas — that our knowledge of "grasping" is organized around the sensorimotor schema of grasping, not just the propositional description of grasping. This connects schema theory to embodied cognition ([[active-inference]]) and simulation theories of understanding.
> **Why follow it:** If propositional schemas are grounded in motor schemas, then schema theory has implications for how abstract knowledge is represented (as simulated sensorimotor activity), and for how instructional design should engage multiple modalities.
> **Time investment:** Significant — requires reading Merleau-Ponty, Gallese's mirror neuron accounts, and the embodied cognition literature
> **Where to start:** Gallese (2011) "Neuroscience and Phenomenology"; Lakoff & Johnson (1999) "Philosophy in the Flesh"
> **See also:** [[active-inference]], [[predictive-processing]]
> *— [[schema-and-how-they-work-deep-dive-2026-04-08_report]]*
