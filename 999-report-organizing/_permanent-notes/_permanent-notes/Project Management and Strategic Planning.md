## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Project Management and Strategic Planning
> Project management methodologies — Agile sprints, waterfall phases, OKR frameworks — are metacognitive scaffolding systems for organisational cognition. Sprint planning scaffolds forethought (what will be accomplished, how, by whom). Daily standups scaffold performance monitoring (what progress has been made, what blockers exist). Sprint retrospectives scaffold self-reflection (what went well, what needs improvement, what will change). The parallel to PKB study scaffolding is exact. And the same developmental challenge applies: teams that rely permanently on rigid process scaffolds without ever internalising the underlying regulatory principles may become process-dependent rather than genuinely adaptive.
>
> **Structural principle:** Cyclical planning-monitoring-reflection structures improve performance in any domain where work unfolds over time with uncertainty.
