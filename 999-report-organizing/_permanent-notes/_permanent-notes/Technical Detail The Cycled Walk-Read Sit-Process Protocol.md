## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Technical Detail: The Cycled Walk-Read / Sit-Process Protocol
> **Theoretical basis:** The protocol exploits three features of the walking-reading dynamic:
> 1. Walking provides an arousal benefit during the walk (catecholamine elevation: Level 4)
> 2. Walking imposes a small CE dual-task cost that becomes meaningful during high-difficulty passages (Level 2-3)
> 3. The post-exercise catecholamine window provides the arousal benefit WITHOUT the dual-task cost for 15-60 minutes after walking stops (Level 4, temporal dynamics)
>
> **Phase 1 — Walk-Read (20-30 minutes):**
> - Walk at a comfortable self-paced speed on a habituated indoor route
> - Read material during walking, prioritizing:
>   - First passes of new material (survey/skim quality — getting the gist)
>   - Material at or below current comprehension difficulty ceiling
>   - Material where the goal is exposure and familiarity rather than deep integrated understanding
> - **Goal:** Build arousal, achieve first-pass familiarity with material, identify passages that need careful re-reading
> - **Metacognitive strategy:** When you notice difficulty increasing significantly (struggling to hold the thread, re-reading sentences), this is the cue to end Phase 1
>
> **Phase 2 — Sit-Process (20-40 minutes):**
> - Sit at the [[personal-knowledge-base|PKB]] desk within 5 minutes of walking cessation (to remain within the post-exercise catecholamine window)
> - Re-read the material with [[active-note-making]]:
>   - Extract key claims and concepts into atomic notes or literature notes
>   - Engage in elaborative processing: explain concepts in own words, make connections to existing knowledge
>   - Process the high-difficulty passages that were identified during Phase 1 as exceeding the walking-compatible difficulty ceiling
> - **Goal:** Deep comprehension, [[schema-construction]], and externalization into the PKB
> - **Advantage:** Full [[central-executive]] capacity (no dual-task cost) + elevated arousal (post-exercise window) = optimal conditions for [[deep-processing]]
>
> **Phase 3 — Repeat or Conclude:**
> - If more material remains and the reader is becoming restless or under-aroused from sitting: return to Phase 1
> - If the reader is maintaining good seated concentration: continue Phase 2
> - If fatigue is accumulating: conclude the session
>
> **Estimated cycle time:** 40-70 minutes per walk-sit cycle
> **Expected benefit over pure walking-reading:** Captures arousal benefit for ALL material (including high-difficulty passages) while eliminating dual-task cost during the deepest processing
> **Expected benefit over pure sitting-reading:** Eliminates under-arousal and restlessness costs, provides regular movement breaks, creates natural punctuation in the reading session
> *— [[walking-academic-reading-working-memory-deep-dive-2026-04-13_report]]*
