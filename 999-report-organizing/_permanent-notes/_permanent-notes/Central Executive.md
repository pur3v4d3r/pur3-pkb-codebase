# Central Executive

> [!definition] Central Executive
> **Central Executive**: The attentional-control component of working memory, a limited-capacity system responsible for directing attention, coordinating slave systems, switching between tasks, inhibiting irrelevant information, and interfacing with long-term memory. Theoretically based on Norman and Shallice's (1986) Supervisory Attentional System. Core functions: focusing attention, dividing attention, shifting attention, long-term memory activation. Neuroanatomically: dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (DLPFC) and anterior cingulate cortex. Different from but closely related to the broader construct of [[executive-function]]. See: [[central-executive]], [[Cognitive-Control]].

> *— [[baddeley-working-memory-model-foundational-report-2026-03-31_report]]*

> [!evidence] Central Executive
> The attention-controlling component of the working memory system. Unlike the phonological loop and visuospatial sketchpad, the central executive is not a storage buffer but an attentional controller that supervises and coordinates the slave systems, switches between tasks, directs attention toward relevant information, suppresses irrelevant information, and integrates information from different sources. Baddeley has characterised it as a limited-capacity attentional system analogous to [[Norman-and-Shallice|Norman and Shallice]]'s Supervisory Attentional System (SAS). The central executive is the most theoretically underdetermined component of the working memory model — a acknowledged problem sometimes called the "homunculus problem."
> *— [[memory-systems-working-memory-long-term-memory-foundational-report-2026-03-11_report]]*
