## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Nuance on Growth Mindset Interventions
> The initial excitement around growth mindset interventions has been tempered by more careful examination of effect sizes and generalizability. A 2019 large-scale replication by Yeager and colleagues found meaningful but modest effects of brief growth mindset interventions, concentrated particularly in lower-achieving students and supportive contexts. Critics like Sisk et al. (2018) have noted that effect sizes from meta-analyses are smaller than originally hoped. The relationship between mindset and metacognitive engagement is likely real but mediated by context, prior academic history, and the degree to which the broader learning environment supports the mindset claim (i.e., a growth mindset is more actionable when combined with genuine opportunities to develop through effort).
> *— [[metacognition-motivation-foundational-report-2026-03-05_report]]*
