## Additional Material

> [!debate] The Orthogonality Question: Are the Three Orientations Truly Independent?
> The GCOS operationalizes the three orientations as independent, continuous dimensions — a person can be high on all three, low on all three, or any combination. Yet there are theoretical reasons to expect some degree of inverse relationship: strong autonomy orientation might logically suppress control or impersonal orientation. The empirical evidence shows modest negative correlations between autonomy and impersonal orientations, and more complex relationships between autonomy and control orientations (which are sometimes weakly positive, weakly negative, or uncorrelated depending on the sample). The question of whether the orientations are truly independent dimensions or whether they share some common variance structure remains unresolved.
> *— [[causality-orientations-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-01_report]]*
