## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Nagy, Herman & Anderson (1985): The Acquisition Rate Estimate
> The researchers had students read passages at their grade level and then tested knowledge of words that had appeared in those passages. Their estimate: approximately 5% of unfamiliar words encountered in reading are retained after a single reading. Subsequent meta-analyses by Swanborn and de Glopper (1999) confirmed this range, finding a mean incidental acquisition rate of approximately 15% per encounter — but this figure applies to words that were reasonably inferable from context. For words that were not contextually inferrable, the acquisition rate dropped to essentially zero.
> *— [[reading-vocabulary-focused-analysis-2026-03-28_report]]*
