## Open Threads

> [!open-question] The Cross-Cultural Stability of the Emotion-Attribution Correspondences
> The evidence reviewed in this analysis is predominantly from Western, predominantly individualistic cultural contexts. The question of whether the specific emotion-attribution mappings (particularly the locus-pride/shame correspondence) hold cross-culturally remains genuinely open. Some cross-cultural evidence suggests that the stability dimension may be more cross-culturally robust than the locus dimension, but systematic comparative data on the full emotion-mediation chain across cultural contexts is limited. This matters for the generalizability of attribution retraining interventions in non-Western educational contexts.
> *— [[attribution-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-24_report]]*
