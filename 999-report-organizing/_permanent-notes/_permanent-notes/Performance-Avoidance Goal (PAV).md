# Performance-Avoidance Goal (PAV)

> [!definition] Performance-Avoidance Goal (PAV)
> **Definition:** Striving to avoid demonstrating inferior competence — to avoid being the worst in the class, to avoid public failure, to avoid being judged as incompetent. The standard is normative, but the motivational orientation is avoidant.
> **Report-Specific Significance:** PAV goals are the most uniformly maladaptive goal type. Unlike PAP goals, their effects across outcomes, populations, and contexts are remarkably consistent in direction.
