# Volitional Congruence (Reconstructed Concept)

> [!definition] Volitional Congruence (Reconstructed Concept)
> An alternative formulation of SDT's autonomy concept that emerges from this dialectical process. Rather than framing autonomy as self-authorship or independence, volitional congruence understands autonomy as *acting in ways that align with one's own values and relational commitments*. This formulation preserves the predictive content of SDT's autonomy need while making it coherent with collectivist cultural contexts where choosing to conform to one's group — when that choice is experienced as genuinely one's own — constitutes authentic autonomy rather than controlled compliance.
