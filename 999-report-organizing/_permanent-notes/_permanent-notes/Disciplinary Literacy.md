# Disciplinary Literacy

> [!definition] Disciplinary Literacy
> Disciplinary literacy refers to the discipline-specific reading competencies required to comprehend and critically evaluate texts within a particular academic field — including knowledge of disciplinary text genres (research articles, theoretical reviews, case studies), disciplinary vocabulary, disciplinary epistemological norms (what counts as evidence, how claims are warranted), and disciplinary rhetorical conventions (how arguments are structured, what objections must be addressed). Disciplinary literacy is distinct from general academic reading ability and must be developed separately for each major field of academic engagement.
>
