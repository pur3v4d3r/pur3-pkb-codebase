> Brown's foundational chapter introduces the knowledge-regulation distinction that has proven as influential as Flavell's taxonomy. Particularly valuable for its treatment of reading comprehension monitoring and its review of developmental studies showing metacognitive deficits in young readers. A long chapter but one of the most important primary sources in the metacognitive knowledge literature.
