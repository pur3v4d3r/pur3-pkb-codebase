## Reflections

> [!reflection] Phase VI Reflections
> 1. This report proposes the Knowledge-Activation Gap as a novel construct to explain why accurate declarative metacognitive knowledge consistently fails to translate into spontaneous regulatory use. Does this construct add explanatory value beyond existing accounts, or does it simply rename a pattern that Brown's knowledge-regulation distinction already captures? What would be needed to establish the Knowledge-Activation Gap as a theoretically distinct phenomenon rather than a restatement of the knowledge-regulation dissociation?
> 2. The synthesis argues that conditional metacognitive knowledge is the most educationally consequential dimension yet the most neglected in practice. What would a genuine conditional metacognitive knowledge curriculum look like in a domain you know well? How would you assess whether students had developed genuine conditional knowledge as opposed to declarative knowledge of conditional knowledge?
> 3. Considering the PKB connection map above, metacognitive knowledge is described as occupying an "integrating position" in the learning science cluster. What are the risks of a PKB architecture organized around a central integrating node? What knowledge relationships might be distorted or invisible within a metacognitive-knowledge-centric organizing framework?
