## Additional Material

> [!person] Norbert Schmitt (active)
> **Core Contribution:** The most systematic research programme on how many encounters with a word are needed for reliable vocabulary acquisition, and on the relationship between vocabulary size, reading comprehension, and academic performance. Schmitt's work consolidates and extends Nation's frequency framework with an emphasis on learning trajectories.
>
> **Role in the Dialectical Arc:** *Empirical challenge* — Schmitt's multiple-exposure research (10–20+ encounters for productive knowledge) provides the most precise quantitative challenge to the acquisition rate assumptions of the received view.
>
> **Key Works:** *Vocabulary in Language Teaching* (2000); *Researching Vocabulary* (2010).
> *— [[vocabulary-building-reading-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-28_report]]*
