## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Motivational-Regulatory Asymmetry → Organizational Learning Architecture
> In organizational learning and knowledge management contexts, the equivalent asymmetry appears between *psychological safety* (the motivational/affective condition that enables knowledge sharing and error acknowledgment) and *knowledge management systems* (the cognitive/structural tools for capturing, organizing, and retrieving organizational knowledge). Amy Edmondson's research on psychological safety demonstrates that cognitive tools for knowledge management consistently underperform in teams with low psychological safety, even when the tools are excellent. But teams with high psychological safety can produce significant collective learning with minimal formal knowledge management infrastructure. The asymmetry holds: motivational architecture (psychological safety) is the more foundational domain. The transfer prediction: organizational knowledge management interventions that invest in tool quality without investing in the motivational climate that governs tool use will systematically underperform — precisely the pattern seen in many enterprise knowledge management failures.
> *— [[pintrichs-srl-focused-analysis-2026-03-22_report]]*
