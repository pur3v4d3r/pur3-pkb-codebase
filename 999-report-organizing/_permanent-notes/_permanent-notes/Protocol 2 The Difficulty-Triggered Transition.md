# Protocol 2: The Difficulty-Triggered Transition

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Protocol 2: The Difficulty-Triggered Transition
> **When:** During any walking-reading session
> **Trigger:** You notice yourself re-reading a sentence or paragraph, or you realize you cannot hold the thread of the current argument while walking
> **Action:**
> 1. Mark the passage (bookmark, highlight, mental note)
> 2. Continue walking-reading past the difficult section
> 3. On next transition to sitting: return to the marked passage first
> 4. Process it with full CE capacity + [[active-note-making]]
>
> **Rationale:** The difficulty trigger is more adaptive than fixed timing because difficulty varies within and across materials.
> *— [[walking-academic-reading-working-memory-deep-dive-2026-04-13_report]]*
