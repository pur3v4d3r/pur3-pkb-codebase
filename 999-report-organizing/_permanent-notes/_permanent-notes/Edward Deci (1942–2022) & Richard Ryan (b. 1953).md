> [!person] Edward Deci (1942–2022) & Richard Ryan (b. 1953)
> American psychologists who co-developed Self-Determination Theory across four decades of collaborative research at the University of Rochester. Deci's early experimental work on the undermining effects of external rewards on intrinsic motivation (1971) launched the research programme; Ryan contributed the theoretical architecture, particularly the organismic integration framework. Their work provides the motivational foundation for Sections 2, 5, and 6.
>
> **Key works:** Deci (1971), Deci & Ryan (1985, 2000), Ryan & Deci (2017).
> *— [[goal-setting-and-assessment-in-pkm-foundational-report-2026-04-15_report]]*

> [!person] Edward Deci (1942–2022) & Richard Ryan (b. 1953)
> Co-founders of [[self-determination-theory]]. Developed the taxonomy of motivational regulation (intrinsic, identified, introjected, external), the basic psychological needs framework ([[autonomy-need|autonomy]], [[competence-need|competence]], [[relatedness-need|relatedness]]), and six mini-theories including cognitive evaluation theory, organismic integration theory, and goal contents theory. Key works: *Intrinsic Motivation and Self-Determination in Human Behavior* (1985), *Self-Determination and Intrinsic Motivation in Human Behavior* (2000). Relationship: their autonomy framework provides the central qualifying condition for Locke and Latham's specificity mechanism.
> *— [[goal-setting-and-assessment-language-in-pkm-foundational-report-2026-04-15_report]]*
