## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Attribution Analysis
> **Where I was strong:** The mechanism sections (Phase IV) were well-comprehended. The elaborative interrogation technique was highly effective here — it forced me to generate causal explanations for the mechanisms rather than just accepting the assertions, which produced much more durable understanding than I typically get.
>
> **Where I fell short:** The count error (three vs. four design challenges) and the residual fuzziness on adaptive inference vs. self-model update. 
>
> **Controllable attribution:** The count error reflects a goal-setting failure in the Forethought Phase — "three design challenges" was an estimate, not a verified criterion. The fix is to confirm specific numbers from the table of contents or headers before setting the comprehension criterion. The mechanism fuzziness reflects that I read Phase IV quickly after the calibration check, wanting to move on. Strategic adjustment: after a calibration check flagging a gap, *resolve it before proceeding* rather than flagging and continuing.
>
> **Not an ability issue.** The comprehension of the core argument was solid. The failures were strategic and structural, not capability-based.
