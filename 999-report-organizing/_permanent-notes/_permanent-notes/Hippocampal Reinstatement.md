# Hippocampal Reinstatement

> [!definition] Hippocampal Reinstatement
> The phenomenon, documented via fMRI, whereby the pattern of neural activity during successful memory retrieval recapitulates the pattern present during the original encoding episode. The degree of reinstatement correlates with the quality and richness of recollective experience. Provides neural-level support for the ESP's cue-engram resonance model.
>
> **Boundary:** Reinstatement is a correlate of successful retrieval, not its full explanation. The mechanism by which reinstatement is achieved (hippocampal pattern completion) is an active area of investigation. The relationship between reinstatement strength and subjective confidence is not always monotonic.
>
> **See also:** [[hippocampus]], [[encoding-specificity-principle]], [[episodic-memory]]
