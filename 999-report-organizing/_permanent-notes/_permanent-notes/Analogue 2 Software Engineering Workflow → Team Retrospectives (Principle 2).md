## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Analogue 2: Software Engineering Workflow → Team Retrospectives (Principle 2)
> The monitoring phase's function of making implicit processes explicit maps to the retrospective practice in agile software development. Teams that conduct structured retrospectives are essentially implementing Principle 2: surfacing implicit team norms, implicit communication failures, and implicit technical debt so they can be deliberately addressed. Teams that do not retrospect remain dependent on whatever regulatory capacity has been automated, which is generally insufficient for adapting to novel challenges. The structural isomorphism is: sprint retrospective :: explicit monitoring phase; implicit team dysfunction :: automatic (unreflective) learning episode. The principle predicts that the quality of retrospectives matters more than their frequency — a brief but honest externalization of what is actually happening generates more regulatory leverage than a lengthy but defensive one.
> *— [[pintrichs-framework-srl-focused-analysis-2026-03-23_report]]*
