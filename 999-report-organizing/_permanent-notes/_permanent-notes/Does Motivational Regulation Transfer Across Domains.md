## Open Threads

> [!open-question] Does Motivational Regulation Transfer Across Domains?
> Is the capacity to regulate motivation a general, transferable skill — or a set of domain-specific competencies? If general, then motivational regulation training in one context should generalize to others. If domain-specific, then the MSLQ's subscales and Wolters' strategy taxonomy describe families of similar but domain-specific behaviors. The answer has significant implications for how motivational regulation should be taught. Current evidence is insufficient to resolve this question definitively.
> *— [[pintrichs-framework-srl-focused-analysis-2026-03-23_report]]*
