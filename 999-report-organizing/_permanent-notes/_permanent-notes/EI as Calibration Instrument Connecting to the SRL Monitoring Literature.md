## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] EI as Calibration Instrument: Connecting to the SRL Monitoring Literature
> The metacognitive monitoring function of EI connects to the broader problem of monitoring accuracy in [[self-regulated-learning-srl|self-regulated learning]] studied extensively by Winne, Hadwin, and colleagues (see [[Winne-&-Hadwin's-Metacognitive-Processing-Model]]). Poor monitoring accuracy — the inability to distinguish what you understand from what you merely recognize — is a primary driver of ineffective self-study. EI functions as a monitoring *instrument*, not just a learning strategy: it provides diagnostic feedback that re-reading and re-exposure do not. A PKB practitioner who regularly applies EI is not just learning the material more deeply; they are also building metacognitive data about which nodes in their knowledge graph are genuinely understood at the situation model level versus merely recognized at the textbase level.
> *— [[elaborative-interrogation-reading-focused-analysis-2026-03-30_report]]*
