> [!evidence] [[Algorithmic-Metacognition-—-When-Spaced-Repetition-Systems-Do-Metacognitive-Work|Algorithmic Metacognition: When Spaced Repetition Systems Do Metacognitive Work]]
> **Description:** An analysis of how algorithmically driven learning systems (Anki, SuperMemo, adaptive learning platforms) perform metacognitive functions — scheduling reviews, adjusting difficulty, estimating knowledge state — and the implications of delegating these functions to algorithms. Explores whether algorithmic metacognition develops or atrophies the human practitioner's metacognitive capacity.
> **Connection to this report:** Section 7.3 introduces the metacognitive design paradox (scaffolding dependency vs. capacity development). Algorithmic metacognition is the most extreme case of this paradox, where the algorithm performs monitoring and control functions that the human would otherwise need to develop.
