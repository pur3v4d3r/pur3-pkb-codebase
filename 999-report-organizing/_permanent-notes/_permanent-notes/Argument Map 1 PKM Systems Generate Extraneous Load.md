## Additional Material

> [!diagram] Argument Map 1: PKM Systems Generate Extraneous Load
> ```
> PREMISE 1: PKM systems require ongoing maintenance
>     (tagging, filing, reviewing, updating hierarchies)
>         |
>         v
> PREMISE 2: Maintenance activities generate cognitive
>     load independent of content complexity
>         |
>         v
> PREMISE 3: Working memory capacity is shared between
>     maintenance and comprehension/synthesis tasks
>         |
>         v
> INTERMEDIATE CONCLUSION: Maintenance activities
>     consume working memory that would otherwise be
>     available for synthesis and connection-making
>         |
>         v
> PREMISE 4: Working memory resources allocated to
>     maintenance cannot simultaneously support
>     elaborative encoding or connection generation
>         |
>         v
> CONCLUSION: PKM systems create extraneous load
>     that directly reduces the cognitive bandwidth
>     available for the generative work they exist to support
>
> COUNTERCLAIM: PKM systems can reduce load by
>     offloading memory to external storage
> RESPONSE: This is true for accessed material,
>     but only when access costs < reconstruction costs;
>     maintenance costs are ALWAYS extraneous and
>     cannot be offset by offloading benefits
> ```
> *— [[pkm-and-cognitive-load-foundational-report-2026-04-05_report]]*
