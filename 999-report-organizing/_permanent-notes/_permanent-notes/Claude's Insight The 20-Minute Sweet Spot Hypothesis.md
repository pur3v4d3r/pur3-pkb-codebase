## Reflections

> [!claude-insight] Claude's Insight: The 20-Minute Sweet Spot Hypothesis
> Synthesizing the temporal dynamics with the task-difficulty interaction generates a non-obvious prediction: the optimal walking-while-reading session is probably **about 20-30 minutes long**, after which the reader should transition to seated reading to benefit from the post-exercise catecholamine window. Sessions longer than this face diminishing arousal returns with constant dual-task costs — the balance tilts against walking. The post-exercise window means the reader can have the arousal benefit *and* the full CE capacity, but only for the window after walking stops.
>
> This is a testable protocol recommendation: Walk 20-30 minutes while reading moderate-difficulty material → Sit down at [[personal-knowledge-base|PKB]] desk for high-difficulty passages and [[active-note-making]] during the post-exercise enhancement window → Repeat cycle. This would be predicted to outperform both pure walking-reading (which pays dual-task costs throughout) and pure sitting-reading (which pays under-arousal costs throughout).
> *— [[walking-academic-reading-working-memory-deep-dive-2026-04-13_report]]*
