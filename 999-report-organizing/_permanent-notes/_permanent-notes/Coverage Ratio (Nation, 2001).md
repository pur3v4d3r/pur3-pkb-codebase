# Coverage Ratio (Nation, 2001)

> [!definition] Coverage Ratio (Nation, 2001)
> The proportion of running word tokens in a text that fall within the reader's known vocabulary. A coverage ratio of 98% means the reader knows 98 out of every 100 word tokens encountered. The vocabulary threshold hypothesis holds that fluent, unassisted comprehension requires a coverage ratio approaching 95–98%.
>
> **Operational Indicator:** Approximately 5–7 unknown words per typical page of prose in a given reader constitutes a rough indicator of the productive acquisition band (95–97% coverage).
>
> **See also:** [[simple-view-of-reading]], [[cognitive-load-theory]], [[schema-theory]]
