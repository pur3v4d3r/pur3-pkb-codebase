# Internalisation

> [!definition] Internalisation
> The process by which externally regulated behaviours are gradually taken in and re-regulated by the self — moving along the self-determination continuum from external regulation toward identified regulation and ultimately integrated regulation. While CET specifically addresses intrinsic motivation (the far end of the intrinsic pole), Organismic Integration Theory (another SDT sub-theory) addresses internalisation of extrinsic motivation. The two processes are related: creating conditions that support autonomy and competence facilitates both intrinsic motivation (when activities are inherently interesting) and internalisation (when activities are not inherently interesting but have personal or social value).
