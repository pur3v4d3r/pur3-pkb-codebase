## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Distinction: Strategic vs. Habitual Automaticity
> **Q:** How does strategic automaticity differ from the automaticity of consolidated habits?
> **A:** Strategic automaticity is plan-dependent (produced by deliberate planning rather than repetition), revocable (decommissioned when the plan is deliberately revoked), and supported by different neural systems (prefrontal-mediated rather than basal-ganglia-mediated). Habit automaticity is repetition-driven, more difficult to revoke, and procedurally encoded.
