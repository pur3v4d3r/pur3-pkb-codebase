## Additional Material

> [!person] Andrew Elliot (1964– )
> **Core Contribution:** Extended the mastery-performance dichotomy by adding the approach-avoidance dimension, producing the 2×2 achievement goal framework (with McGregor, 2001). Developed the hierarchical model of approach-avoidance motivation connecting personality traits (BIS/BAS) to achievement goal adoption.
> **Relationship to Other Figures:** Built directly on Nicholls; developed the 2×2 in dialogue with Harackiewicz (with whom he had a productive theoretical controversy) and Dweck (whose implicit theories model provides a dispositional pathway to goal adoption).
> **Key Works:** "A hierarchical model of approach and avoidance achievement motivation" (*Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 1997); "A 2×2 achievement goal framework" (with McGregor, *Journal of Educational Psychology*, 2001).
> **Relevance to This Analysis:** Elliot is the primary architect of the analytical framework examined in this report. His hierarchical model grounds the approach-avoidance distinction in personality systems (BAS/BIS), providing mechanistic depth that purely descriptive frameworks lack.
> *— [[achievement-goal-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-24_report]]*
