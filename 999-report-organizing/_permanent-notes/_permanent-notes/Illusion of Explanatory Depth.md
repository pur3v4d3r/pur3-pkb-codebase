# Illusion of Explanatory Depth

> [!definition] Illusion of Explanatory Depth
> A cognitive phenomenon documented by Rozenblit and Keil (2002): people systematically overestimate their understanding of causal/mechanical systems before attempting to explain them. When asked to explain in detail, the overestimation collapses. The illusion arises because familiarity with the *function* of a system (what it does) is mistaken for understanding of its *mechanism* (how and why it does it). The phenomenon demonstrates that the *feeling* of understanding is an unreliable guide to its presence.
