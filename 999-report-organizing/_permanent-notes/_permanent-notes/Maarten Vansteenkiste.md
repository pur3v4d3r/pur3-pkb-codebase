# Maarten Vansteenkiste

> [!definition] Maarten Vansteenkiste
> *Definition pending — derived from 5 source report(s).*

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Maarten Vansteenkiste
> Belgian SDT researcher whose programmatic work has been central to the development of need-frustration measurement, the empirical articulation of the dark-side of motivation, and the educational applications of SDT. Co-author of the [[chen-2015|Chen et al. 2015]] cross-cultural study that has been the most influential single contribution to the universality literature.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-internalization-continuum-deep-dive-2026-04-21]]*

## Additional Material

> [!person] Maarten Vansteenkiste
> Contemporary SDT researcher who has advanced the understanding of need frustration (versus mere absence of satisfaction) and conducted extensive cross-cultural investigations of SDT constructs. His work has implications for COT's cross-cultural generalizability and for understanding the developmental pathways through which need-thwarting environments produce control and impersonal orientations.
> *— [[causality-orientations-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-01_report]]*

> [!person] Maarten Vansteenkiste
> Professor at Ghent University, Belgium, one of the leading contemporary contributors to SDT research. Vansteenkiste's work has focused on the need satisfaction-frustration asymmetry, cross-cultural SDT research, SDT in parenting and family contexts, and the theoretical refinement of the regulatory continuum. His collaborative work has substantially advanced BPNT and the distinction between need satisfaction and need frustration as partially independent constructs.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-01 (1)]]*
