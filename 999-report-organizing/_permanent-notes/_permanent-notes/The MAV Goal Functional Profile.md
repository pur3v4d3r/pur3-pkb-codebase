## Open Threads

> [!open-question] The MAV Goal Functional Profile
> Mastery-avoidance goals remain the least studied cell in the 2×2. The open question is whether MAV goals constitute a genuinely distinct motivational category with characteristic adaptive functions (perfectionist motivation for sustained performance maintenance) or whether they are a variant of anxiety-driven motivation that happens to target a self-referenced standard. What longitudinal evidence exists suggests that MAV goals are associated with elevated anxiety and reduced intrinsic interest even when they sustain performance level — but the mechanisms are incompletely specified and the population in which they are most functional (expert performers concerned with skill maintenance) has not been adequately studied.
> *— [[achievement-goal-theory-focused-analysis-2026-03-24_report]]*
