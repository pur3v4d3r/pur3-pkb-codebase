# Actively Open-Minded Thinking (Baron, 1995)

> [!definition] Actively Open-Minded Thinking (Baron, 1995)
> A dispositional orientation toward thinking characterized by active seeking of evidence against one's current beliefs, willingness to revise positions in response to evidence, and resistance to premature closure. Activated by the Private Generative Space condition of silent reading; suppressed by social performance pressure and identity threat.
