# Performance-Approach Goal (PAP)

> [!definition] Performance-Approach Goal (PAP)
> **Definition:** Striving to demonstrate superior competence relative to others — to outperform peers, to be the best in the class, to secure the highest grade. The standard is normative; success means standing above. This is the approach pole of the performance dimension.
> **Report-Specific Significance:** PAP goals are the theoretically contested cell. They predict higher academic performance on some measures but are associated with maladaptive patterns on others (shallow processing, lower intrinsic interest, reduced deep learning). The controversy about PAP goals is the analytic epicenter of this report.
