## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] The Cognitive Priority Principle — A Unifying Framework
> The central original contribution of this report can be stated as the **Cognitive Priority Principle**: an effective PKM system is one that allocates cognitive resources in proportion to their cognitive value — investing most heavily in the phases and activities that produce the most durable knowledge structures, and minimising overhead in phases that can be made routine. By this principle, synthesis and connection generation should receive the majority of the deliberate cognitive investment a PKM practitioner makes; capture, filing, and maintenance should be made as automatic and low-overhead as possible. The system succeeds not by being comprehensive or elaborate but by consistently creating the conditions for the practitioner's mind to do its best intellectual work.
>
> **Epistemic Status:** Well-motivated synthesis from Cognitive Load Theory, Working Memory research, and the specific PKM analyses in this report. The principle is consistent with available evidence but is a novel integration rather than a direct empirical finding.
