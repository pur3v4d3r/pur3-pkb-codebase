# Forethought Phase** *(Zimmerman, 2000)*

> [!definition] Forethought Phase** *(Zimmerman, 2000)*
> **Definition:** The first phase of the SRL cycle, occurring before learning effort, comprising Task Analysis (goal-setting and strategic planning) and Motivational Beliefs (self-efficacy, outcome expectations, task value, goal orientation). NOT equivalent to "planning" in the colloquial sense — it is a dual-subsystem process that prepares both cognitive direction and motivational fuel for the Performance Phase.
> **Dialectical Note:** The Forethought Phase is widely misunderstood as primarily cognitive (just planning). Its motivational subsystem is equally important and less frequently implemented, yet it filters the goal-setting and strategy selection of the cognitive subsystem in largely invisible ways.
> **Cross-References:** [[forethought-phase]], [[goal-setting-theory]], [[self-efficacy]], [[achievement-goal-theory]]
