# Autonomy-Structure Dialectic

> [!definition] Autonomy-Structure Dialectic
> The productive tension between the provision of external structure (which enables learning by reducing cognitive load and providing direction) and the cultivation of learner autonomy (which requires the progressive withdrawal of that same structure). The dialectic is resolved not by choosing one pole over the other but by recognising structure and autonomy as developmentally sequenced rather than opposed.
>
> **Boundary:** The resolution through developmental sequencing depends on the assumption that learners are capable of progressively internalising external regulation — an assumption that may not hold for all learners in all contexts.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** The foundational paradox of this report, identified in Section 1 and resolved through the Scaffolding-Sovereignty Progression in Section 4.
>
> **See also:** [[self-determination-theory]], [[scaffolding]], [[cognitive-load-theory]]
