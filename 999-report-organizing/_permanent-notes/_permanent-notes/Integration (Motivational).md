# Integration (Motivational)

> [!definition] Integration (Motivational)
> The deepest and most fully autonomous form of extrinsic motivation regulation, in which the importance and value of an activity or relationship is assimilated to the self's core values and identity rather than merely identified as instrumentally important. Integrated relational motivation differs from identified regulation in degree of self-endorsement: identified regulation involves valuing the relationship as important; integrated regulation involves the relationship being part of who one is. *Location on regulatory continuum*: Between identified regulation and intrinsic motivation. *See also*: [[integrated-regulation]], [[organismic-integration-theory]], [[self-concordance]]
