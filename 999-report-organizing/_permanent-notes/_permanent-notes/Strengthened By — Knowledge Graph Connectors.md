## Cross-Domain Connections

> [!cross-domain-connection] Strengthened By — Knowledge Graph Connectors
> - [[learned-helplessness]] — Attribution Theory provides the cognitive mechanism for the reformulated learned helplessness model; the two constructs are theoretically inseparable.
> - [[locus-of-control]] — Rotter's Locus of Control is the generalized trait-level expectancy construct that Attribution Theory's specific attribution research complements; internal LOC at trait level corresponds to effort-attribution pattern at state level.
> - [[academic-emotions]] — The attribution-emotion mapping is the foundational theoretical basis for academic emotions research; Attribution Theory predicts which emotions follow from which causal attributions.
> - [[implicit-theory-of-intelligence]] — The Entity/Incremental theory distinction is Attribution Theory's account of the deep schema from which specific attributional tendencies are generated; the two frameworks are reciprocally illuminating.
> - [[self-efficacy]] — Bandura's Self-Efficacy is the prospective, action-focused analog of Attribution Theory's retrospective controllability-stability analysis; high self-efficacy corresponds to controllable-unstable attribution pattern.
> - [[emotional-regulation]] — Attribution shapes emotion, and emotion regulation shapes subsequent attribution; the bidirectional relationship makes Attribution Theory a key framework within the emotional regulation literature.
> *— [[attribution-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-03_report]]*
