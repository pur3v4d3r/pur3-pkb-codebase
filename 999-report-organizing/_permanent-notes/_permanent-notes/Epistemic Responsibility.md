# Epistemic Responsibility

> [!definition] Epistemic Responsibility
> The ethical and cognitive obligation of communicators to hold themselves to argument quality standards, distinguish legitimate influence from manipulation, acknowledge uncertainty accurately, and take responsibility for the epistemic effects of their communication on listeners. See [[critical-thinking]], [[epistemic-vigilance]], [[epistemic-humility]].
