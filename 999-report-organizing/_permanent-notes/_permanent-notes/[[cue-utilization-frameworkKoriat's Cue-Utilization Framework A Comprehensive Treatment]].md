## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[cue-utilization-framework|Koriat's Cue-Utilization Framework: A Comprehensive Treatment]]
> **Description:** A dedicated analysis of Asher Koriat's cue-utilization framework, including the full taxonomy of intrinsic, extrinsic, and mnemonic cues; the accessibility model of FOK judgments; the distinction between experience-based and information-based judgments; and the framework's implications for understanding when and why metacognitive judgments succeed or fail.
> **Connection to this report:** Sections 4 and 6 rely heavily on the cue-utilization framework as the mechanistic complement to the Nelson-Narens architecture, but the framework's full richness — including its treatment of cue validity, cue weighting, and the role of learning conditions in shaping cue availability — could not be fully elaborated within the scope of this report.
