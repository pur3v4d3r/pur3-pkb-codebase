# Self-Directed Learning Readiness Scale — SDLRS

> [!definition] **Self-Directed Learning Readiness Scale — SDLRS**
> The most widely used instrument for measuring self-directed learning readiness, assessing dimensions including openness to learning opportunities, self-concept as an effective learner, initiative and independence in learning, informed acceptance of responsibility for learning, love of learning, creativity, positive orientation to the future, and ability to use basic study and problem-solving skills.
> 
> **Boundary:** Critiqued for conflating self-direction with love of learning, exhibiting inconsistent factor structure across populations, and measuring attitudes rather than actual SDL behavior. Measures readiness, not behavior.
> 
> **Report-Specific Significance:** Discussed in the critique section as an illustration of measurement challenges facing the field.
>
