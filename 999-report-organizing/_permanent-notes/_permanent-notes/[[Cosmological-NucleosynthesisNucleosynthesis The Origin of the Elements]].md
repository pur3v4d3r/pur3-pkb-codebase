## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[Cosmological-Nucleosynthesis|Nucleosynthesis: The Origin of the Elements]]
> **Description:** The processes by which all chemical elements are created — from Big Bang nucleosynthesis (hydrogen, helium, lithium) through stellar nucleosynthesis (carbon through iron) to explosive nucleosynthesis in supernovae and neutron star mergers (elements heavier than iron). This topic addresses the fundamental question of where the matter that forms stars (and planets, and life) comes from.
>
> **Connection to this report:** This report discusses the raw materials of star formation (primarily hydrogen and helium) but does not trace their ultimate origin. The metallicity-dependent physics discussed in Section 6 (Population III stars, IMF variation) depends directly on nucleosynthetic history.
>

>
