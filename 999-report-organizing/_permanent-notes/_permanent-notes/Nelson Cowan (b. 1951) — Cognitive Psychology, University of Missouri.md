## Additional Material

> [!person] Nelson Cowan (b. 1951) — Cognitive Psychology, University of Missouri
> **Core Contribution:** Embedded-processes model of working memory that downwardly revised capacity estimates from Miller's 7±2 to 4±1 chunks (2001). Clarified that the "chunks" whose activation counts are episodic, context-dependent units of meaning, not fixed-size storage slots.
> **Key Work:** Cowan, N. (2001). "The magical number 4 in short-term memory: A reconsideration of mental storage capacity." *Behavioral and Brain Sciences, 24*, 87–114.
> **Relationship to others:** Engaged critically with Miller (1956); refined and constrained Baddeley-Hitch model.
> *— [[pkm-working-memory-cognitive-offloading-foundational-report-2026-04-05_report]]*
