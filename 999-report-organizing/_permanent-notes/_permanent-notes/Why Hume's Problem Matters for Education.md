## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Why Hume's Problem Matters for Education
> Hume's problem has a direct pedagogical resonance: students who reason inductively are always making a bet that their generalizations will hold. Understanding this — that inductive conclusions are always provisional, subject to revision by new evidence — is itself a crucial metacognitive and epistemological insight. Teaching students that "this is what we know so far" rather than "this is certainly true" is not a pedagogical hedge but an accurate representation of how inductive knowledge actually works.
> *— [[inductive-reasoning-foundational-report-2026-03-06_report]]*
