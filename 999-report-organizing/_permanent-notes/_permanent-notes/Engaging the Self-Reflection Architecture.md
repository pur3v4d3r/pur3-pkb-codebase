## Reflections

> [!reflection] Engaging the Self-Reflection Architecture
> 1. What is your characteristic affective response to finishing a complex academic reading that you feel was only partially successful? Does that affect typically mobilise you toward strategy revision or toward avoidance of the next difficult reading? What does the pattern suggest about your operative goal orientation?
> 2. When you reflect on the last time you genuinely revised a *strategy* in response to recognising it wasn't working — not just "I'll work harder next time" but "I'll use a different approach" — what made that strategic revision possible? What conditions supported it?
> 3. The distinction between adaptive and defensive self-reactions is often invisible to the learner making them — defensive reactions are experienced as reasonable responses, not as avoidance. What signals might you watch for that indicate you're reacting defensively rather than adaptively?
> *— [[srl-forethought-selfreflection-pkb-report-2026-03-24_report]]*
