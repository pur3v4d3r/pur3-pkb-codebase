## Open Threads

> [!tension-identified] The Operational Independence Problem
> Pintrich's framework implies that the four areas are conceptually distinct regulated domains. Yet the evidence consistently shows high correlations among the subscales — high-SRL learners score higher on all four areas simultaneously. The correlation structure of the MSLQ does not cleanly reproduce the four-area architecture. This creates an interpretive dilemma: are the areas genuinely distinct with correlated empirical manifestations, or are they post-hoc decompositions of a single underlying SRL capacity? Confirmatory factor analyses have produced inconsistent results across different samples.
> *— [[pintrichs-framework-srl-focused-analysis-2026-03-23_report]]*
