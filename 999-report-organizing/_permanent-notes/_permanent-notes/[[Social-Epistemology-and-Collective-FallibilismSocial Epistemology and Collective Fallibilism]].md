## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] [[Social-Epistemology-and-Collective-Fallibilism|Social Epistemology and Collective Fallibilism]]
> Examines how fallibilism scales to communities and institutions. Key topics would include: the epistemology of testimony (when and why we should trust what others tell us), the philosophical significance of peer disagreement (should disagreement with an epistemic peer cause you to reduce your confidence?), the social conditions for collective inquiry, the epistemology of expert consensus, and the pathologies of collective inquiry (groupthink, information cascades, motivated cognition at the institutional level). This report would situate individual fallibilism within the social structures that either enable or obstruct epistemic self-correction.
> *— [[fallibilism-foundational-report-2026-03-06_report]]*
