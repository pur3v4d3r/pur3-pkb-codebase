## Additional Material

> [!person] Keith Stanovich (1950–, active)
> **Core Contribution:** The Matthew Effect in reading development — the empirical demonstration that vocabulary-reading relationships are self-reinforcing and produce divergent trajectories for high and low-vocabulary learners. Also pioneering work on print exposure and its correlations with vocabulary, knowledge, and cognitive ability.
>
> **Role in the Dialectical Arc:** *Critical lens challenge* — Stanovich's Matthew Effect analysis reveals the structural inequity in the reading-primary prescription and provides the empirical basis for the critical lens challenge.
>
> **Relationship to Other Figures:** Stanovich worked within the cognitive psychology of reading tradition (Perfetti's verbal efficiency, LaBerge and Samuels' automaticity theory) while connecting it to developmental and educational outcomes; his work informs Biemiller's vocabulary gap research.
>
> **Key Works:** "Matthew Effects in Reading" (1986, *Reading Research Quarterly*); *Progress in Understanding Reading* (2000).
> *— [[vocabulary-building-reading-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-28_report]]*
