# Surveillance

> [!definition] Surveillance
> The awareness of being observed or monitored, even in the absence of explicit evaluative events. CET research (Lepper & Greene, 1975) shows that surveillance carries a controlling functional significance on its own: being watched introduces an external audience, makes the evaluative relationship salient, and tends to shift PLOC external. This finding has significant implications for digital learning environments in which learner behaviour is continuously logged and monitored, even if that monitoring is not connected to explicit rewards or consequences.
