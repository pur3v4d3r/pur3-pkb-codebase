## Additional Material

> [!protocol] Protocol 3: The Post-Reading Attributional Reflection Template
> **Context:** Use in the self-reflection phase after a reading session. Based on Weiner's attributional dimensions and the reconstructed account that attributional vocabulary in the self-reflection phase constitutes the efficacy-building or efficacy-depleting event that will carry forward to the next reading session.
>
> **Template:**
>
> *For comprehension successes:*
> "I understood [specific content] because [controllable internal cause: strategy deployed / effort applied / background knowledge activated]. This shows that [transferable efficacy belief: when I use this approach with similar material, I can build understanding]."
>
> *For comprehension gaps:*
> "I didn't yet understand [specific content]. This is because [controllable-unstable cause: I haven't yet built / I need more exposure to / I need a better strategy for]. My next step is [specific controllable action]."
>
> *Regulatory language check:*
> Is the reflection vocabulary integrated ("I'm building toward") or introjected ("I have to have gotten this right")? If introjected, reformulate.
>
> **Success Criteria:** Successes are attributed to controllable internal causes with a transferable efficacy belief; gaps are attributed to controllable-unstable causes with a specific next step; no uncontrollable-stable attribution formulations.
>
> **Common Pitfalls:** Attributing successes to luck or text difficulty (external, unstable — doesn't build efficacy); attributing gaps to ability (internal, stable, low-controllability — undermines efficacy and motivation).
> *— [[inner-speech-vocabulary-motivation-self-efficacy-dialectical-re-examination-2026-03-25_report]]*
