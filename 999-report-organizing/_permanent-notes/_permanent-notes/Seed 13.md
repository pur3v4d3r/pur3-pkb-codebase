## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Seed 13
> **Q:** What does it mean for the SRL cycle to function as a "self-improving algorithm," and what determines whether it does?
> **A:** The SRL cycle is self-improving when each cycle generates better inputs for the next — calibrated self-efficacy, richer prior knowledge, refined strategic knowledge, more accurate goal standards, sustained adaptive motivation. This is determined primarily by the quality of the Self-Reflection Phase: when it produces accurate self-evaluation, controllable attributions, and adaptive inferences, each cycle's output enriches the next cycle's input. When Self-Reflection is skipped or produces defensive inferences, cycles remain episodically isolated and do not compound.
