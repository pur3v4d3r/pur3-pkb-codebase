# Evergreen Notes

> [!definition] **Evergreen Notes**
> Evergreen Notes are atomic, concept-oriented notes that are continually refined over years rather than written once and archived, designed to compound in value as the network around them grows. This practice falls under [[Zettelkasten]], where it is a specialized form of interconnected note-taking that emphasizes ongoing refinement and linking.

> [!attention] **Boundary**
> This concept excludes static notes that are not revisited or linked. Evergreen Notes focus on ongoing refinement and interconnectedness rather than a one-time write-and-archive approach.

## Core Explanation

Evergreen Notes are characterized by their atomic nature, meaning each note focuses on a single concept or idea, making them modular and reusable. This atomic structure allows for easy integration into a broader network of knowledge, where notes can be linked to related concepts, forming a web of interconnected ideas that grow in value over time.

The continual refinement aspect is crucial; Evergreen Notes are not static but evolve as the writer revisits and updates them based on new insights or feedback. This process ensures that the information remains relevant and accurate, making it more valuable for future reference. The act of refining notes is a first-class activity in this practice, distinct from the one-time write-and-archive approach.

Theoretical roots of Evergreen Notes can be traced back to Zettelkasten, where the concept of interconnectedness is central. In Zettelkasten, each note serves as a node in a network, and the strength of the system lies in its ability to connect disparate ideas. Evergreen Notes take this further by emphasizing the dynamic nature of these connections through continuous refinement.

Empirically, Evergreen Notes have been shown to enhance long-term retention and understanding. By revisiting and updating notes over time, learners can reinforce their knowledge and adapt it to new contexts, making the information more accessible and applicable in various scenarios.

## Mechanism

The mechanism of Evergreen Notes involves a continuous cycle of review and update. When a note is created, it is initially focused on capturing a specific concept or idea. Over time, as the writer gains new insights or encounters related information, they revisit the note to add, modify, or expand upon its content. This process ensures that each note remains relevant and useful, contributing to the overall network of knowledge.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] **Application 1 — Instructional design**
> In instructional design, Evergreen Notes can be used to create a dynamic curriculum that evolves with new research or feedback. Teachers can update their notes based on student performance and new educational theories, ensuring the material remains relevant and effective.

> [!example] **Application 2 — Personal knowledge management**
> For individuals managing personal knowledge, Evergreen Notes provide a structured way to build a comprehensive network of interconnected ideas. Regular updates ensure that the information stays current and accessible, making it easier to recall and apply in various contexts.

> [!example] **Application 3 — Collaborative projects**
> In collaborative projects, Evergreen Notes can facilitate shared understanding by allowing team members to contribute and refine notes over time. This ensures that all participants are working with up-to-date information, reducing the risk of outdated or conflicting knowledge.

## Key Distinctions

> [!key-distinction] **Evergreen Notes vs Static Notes**
> Evergreen Notes differ from static notes in that they are continually refined and linked to form a network. Static notes, on the other hand, are written once and archived without further updates. The key difference lies in their dynamic nature and interconnectedness, which enhances long-term value.

## Key Figures

- **Andy Matuschak** — Andy Matuschak is credited with originating the concept of Evergreen Notes. He formulated this practice as a way to create permanent, interconnected notes that evolve over time, emphasizing the importance of continual refinement and linking.

By continually refining notes, learners can ensure that their knowledge remains relevant and accessible. This practice aligns with the principles of Zettelkasten and progressive summarization, enhancing long-term retention and understanding.
