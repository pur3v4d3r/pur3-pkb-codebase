# Fleeting Notes

> [!definition] **Fleeting Notes**
> Fleeting Notes are short, low-friction captures of in-the-moment thoughts or observations that serve as raw material for further processing into more permanent notes within a short window. They fall under the Zettelkasten workflow and are deliberately discardable, serving only as temporary storage before being transformed into Literature or Permanent notes.

> [!attention] **Boundary**
> This concept excludes long-term storage and durable notes; it focuses on the initial capture phase before refinement.

## Core Explanation

Fleeting Notes play a crucial role in the initial capture phase of the Zettelkasten workflow. Their primary purpose is to quickly jot down ideas, observations, and quotes without the need for immediate refinement. This low-friction nature allows writers to focus on generating content rather than perfecting it immediately.

In practice, Fleeting Notes are created spontaneously as they arise, often in response to a specific moment or stimulus. These notes are then reviewed and processed within a short window, typically during regular review sessions. The goal is to transform these raw materials into more structured and durable forms of knowledge, such as Literature or Permanent notes.

Theoretical roots of Fleeting Notes can be traced back to cognitive psychology, particularly the concept of intrinsic vs. extraneous load. By focusing on capturing fleeting thoughts without overthinking, writers reduce extraneous cognitive load, allowing for a more efficient flow of ideas and better retention of information.

Historically, Fleeting Notes have been part of various note-taking systems, including Zettelkasten. John Sweller's work in 1988 on cognitive load theory provides a foundational understanding of how these notes can be optimized to enhance learning and knowledge management.

## Mechanism

The process of creating Fleeting Notes is straightforward: they are quickly written down using low-friction tools such as pens, digital note-taking apps, or even voice memos. These notes are then reviewed during regular intervals, typically within a few days to ensure that the initial spark of inspiration is captured before it fades.

During review, Fleeting Notes are evaluated for their potential to be developed into more permanent forms. Those deemed valuable are further refined and linked to other notes in the Zettelkasten network, while those considered less useful are discarded.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] **Application 1 — Instructional design**
> In instructional design, Fleeting Notes can be used by educators to capture student feedback or observations during class. By quickly jotting down these insights and processing them into more structured notes, instructors can better tailor their teaching methods and improve the learning experience.

> [!example] **Application 2 — Personal knowledge management**
> For individuals managing personal knowledge, Fleeting Notes provide a way to capture fleeting thoughts or ideas without the pressure of immediate perfection. This allows for a richer accumulation of raw material that can later be refined into more meaningful insights and connections.

## Key Distinctions

> [!key-distinction] **Intrinsic vs Extraneous Load**
> Fleeting Notes are designed to reduce extraneous cognitive load by capturing thoughts quickly without overthinking. In contrast, Evergreen Notes aim for intrinsic value and permanence, focusing on timeless knowledge rather than temporary insights.

## Synthesis

Understanding Fleeting Notes is crucial for effective note-taking and knowledge management because they serve as a foundational layer that enables more structured and durable forms of knowledge. By capturing fleeting thoughts quickly, writers can maintain a steady flow of ideas and insights, which are then refined into more permanent notes. This process not only enhances the quality of captured information but also improves overall cognitive efficiency.

Fleeting Notes integrate seamlessly with other concepts in the Zettelkasten workflow, such as Evergreen and Literature Notes, by providing raw material that can be developed further. Their role in learning science is significant, as they help reduce cognitive load and facilitate better knowledge retention.
