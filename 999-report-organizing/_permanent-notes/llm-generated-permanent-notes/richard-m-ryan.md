# Richard-M.-Ryan

> [!definition] Richard-M.-Ryan

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Richard M. Ryan co-developed SDT with Edward L. Deci, emphasizing the importance of intrinsic motivation and the satisfaction of basic psychological needs for personal growth and engagement.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, SDT is applied across various fields including education, health, and organizational psychology to enhance motivation and well-being by fostering autonomy-supportive environments that meet individuals' need for competence, relatedness, and autonomy.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Ryan's work distinguishes between autonomous and controlled forms of motivation, highlighting the significance of intrinsic versus extrinsic factors in driving behavior.
