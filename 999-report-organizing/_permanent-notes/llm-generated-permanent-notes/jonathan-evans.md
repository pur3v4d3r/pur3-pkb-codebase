# jonathan-evans

> [!definition] jonathan-evans
> - **Key-Term**: [[jonathan-evans]]
> - **Definition**: Jonathan Evans is a researcher known for his work on cognitive biases and decision-making, particularly the concept of attribute substitution as proposed by Daniel Kahneman and Shane Frederick in their 2002 paper 'Representativeness Revisited: Attribute Substitution in Intuitive Judgment'.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Attribute substitution refers to a cognitive bias where people rely on an easier question than the one actually asked, often leading to systematic errors in judgment.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this occurs when individuals use readily available information or heuristics to answer complex questions, which can result in inaccurate conclusions.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> This phenomenon is closely related to the concept of cognitive miserliness, where people conserve mental effort by relying on simple strategies rather than more accurate but cognitively demanding processes.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> Understanding attribute substitution helps in designing better decision-making tools and interventions that can mitigate biases.

> [!example] Application
> It also informs the development of training programs aimed at improving critical thinking skills and reducing cognitive errors.
