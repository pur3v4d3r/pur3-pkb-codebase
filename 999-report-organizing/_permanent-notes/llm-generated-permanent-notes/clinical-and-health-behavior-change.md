# Clinical and Health Behavior Change

> [!definition] Clinical and Health Behavior Change
> Clinical and Health Behavior Change is a framework that integrates Goal Setting Theory with motivational interviewing to enhance patient commitment and achieve durable changes in health behaviors such as diabetes self-management and exercise adherence.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Clinical and Health Behavior Change
> Behavior change in clinical settings — diabetes self-management, exercise adherence, dietary modification — applies Goal Setting Theory's prescriptions with the explicit augmentation of [[goal-intention]] specifications and the careful attention to autonomous commitment that [[motivational-interviewing]] develops. The combination of all three frameworks produces substantially larger and more durable behavior change effects than any single framework, and the joint application has become the standard in evidence-based behavior-change practice. **Boundary:** The translation-window diagnostic is especially important in clinical applications, because outcomes that matter to clinicians (HbA1c levels, weight loss, medication adherence) are not always faithful proxies for the underlying values that motivate the patient.
> *— [[goal-setting-theory-locke-latham-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*
