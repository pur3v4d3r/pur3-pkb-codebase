# Educational Goal Setting

> [!definition] Educational Goal Setting
> Educational Goal Setting involves setting learning goals rather than performance goals for complex tasks, integrating self-regulated-learning frameworks and mastery-goal orientation to support autonomous commitment, metacognitive processing, and ethical practices in educational settings.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Educational Goal Setting
> Educational applications of Goal Setting Theory are most productive when integrated with [[self-regulated-learning]] frameworks and [[mastery-goal-orientation]] research, because the educational context combines the conditions — extended timescales, complex tasks, evolving strategy, learner well-being concerns — under which the classical theory most strains. The integrated approach uses learning goals rather than performance goals on tasks of substantial strategic complexity, supports autonomous commitment through choice and meaningful framing, scaffolds metacognitive processing of performance feedback, and explicitly attends to the translation window through multi-dimensional assessment. **Boundary:** Direct importation of organizational goal-setting practices into educational settings without these adaptations reliably produces the dark-side pathologies — narrowed curricular attention, distorted assessment behaviors, eroded cooperation, and ethical compromise — that the dark-side literature documents.
> *— [[goal-setting-theory-locke-latham-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*
