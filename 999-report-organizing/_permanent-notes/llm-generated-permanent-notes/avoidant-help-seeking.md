# avoidant-help-seeking

> [!definition] avoidant-help-seeking
> - **Key-Term**: [[avoidant-help-seeking]]
> - **Definition**: Avoidant-help-seeking refers to the tendency of individuals to avoid seeking help from others, even when they need it, due to concerns about appearing incompetent or a desire for independence.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Avoidant-help-seeking is rooted in self-perception and social anxiety. Individuals who exhibit this behavior often fear being perceived as weak or incompetent if they ask for assistance, leading them to rely on their own resources even when external support could be beneficial.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, avoidant-help-seeking can manifest in various settings such as academic, professional, and personal life. For instance, a student might struggle with a complex problem but refrain from seeking help from peers or instructors out of fear of appearing unintelligent.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> This behavior is closely related to self-efficacy beliefs and social anxiety. Individuals with lower self-efficacy may be more prone to avoidant-help-seeking because they doubt their ability to handle challenges independently.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In academic settings, avoidant-help-seeking can lead to poorer performance as students miss out on valuable resources that could enhance their learning.

> [!example] Application
> Professionally, it can hinder career development by preventing individuals from acquiring necessary skills and knowledge through mentorship or training programs.
