# Metacognitive Calibration Self-Assessment

> [!definition] Metacognitive Calibration Self-Assessment
> Metacognitive Calibration Self-Assessment is a reflective practice conducted after study sessions to evaluate the accuracy of one's monitoring strategies, focusing on aspects such as retrieval practice, delayed self-assessment, and allocation of study time.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Metacognitive Calibration Self-Assessment
> Use this checklist after a study session to evaluate your monitoring accuracy:
> - [ ] Did I use retrieval practice (not just rereading or reviewing)?
> - [ ] Did I delay my self-assessment rather than judging immediately after study?
> - [ ] Did I generate answers rather than merely recognizing them?
> - [ ] Did I encounter any material that surprised me (suggesting my prior monitoring was inaccurate)?
> - [ ] Can I explain the key concepts without reference to the source material?
> - [ ] Did I allocate more study time to difficult material and less to already-mastered material?
> - [ ] Did I resist the temptation to stop studying just because the material "felt familiar"?
> *— [[nelson-narens-metacognitive-control-framework-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*
