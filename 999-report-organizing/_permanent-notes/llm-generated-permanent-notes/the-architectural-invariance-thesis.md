# The Architectural Invariance Thesis

> [!definition] The Architectural Invariance Thesis
> The Architectural Invariance Thesis posits that working memory's capacity for processing novel elements is fixed and unchangeable through training or motivation.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Architectural Invariance Thesis
> The central claim grounding [[Cognitive Load Theory (CLT)]] is that working memory's capacity for processing novel elements is architecturally fixed and cannot be expanded through training, motivation, or instructional technique. Effective instruction therefore cannot aim to "push through" this bottleneck but must instead work *around* it — primarily by facilitating the construction and automation of schemas in [[long-term-memory]] that allow complex information to be treated as single elements in working memory.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*
