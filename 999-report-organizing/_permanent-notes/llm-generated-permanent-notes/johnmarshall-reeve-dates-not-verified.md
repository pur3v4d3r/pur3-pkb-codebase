# Johnmarshall Reeve (dates not verified)

> [!definition] Johnmarshall Reeve (dates not verified)
> Johnmarshall Reeve was a researcher in motivational psychology who applied self-determination theory to educational practice, demonstrating that autonomy-supportive instructional behaviors can be taught to teachers, leading to improved student motivation and engagement.

## Additional Material

> [!person] Johnmarshall Reeve (dates not verified)
> Korea University (formerly University of Iowa). Leading researcher on autonomy-supportive teaching. Demonstrated that autonomy-supportive instructional behaviors can be taught to teachers, and that trained teachers' students show improved motivation and engagement.
>
> **Relationship to other figures:** Applied Deci and Ryan's framework to educational practice; empirically validated the teachability of autonomy support.
>
> **Key works:** Reeve (2006), Reeve & Jang (2006).
