# Metacognitive Development Across Expertise Levels: From Novice to Expert Self-Regulation

> [!definition] Metacognitive Development Across Expertise Levels: From Novice to Expert Self-Regulation
> Metacognitive Development Across Expertise Levels: From Novice to Expert Self-Regulation refers to the progression in metacognitive skills as individuals move from novice to expert status within a specific domain, characterized by changes in self-regulatory strategies and reduced reliance on external scaffolding.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Metacognitive Development Across Expertise Levels: From Novice to Expert Self-Regulation
> **Description:** A developmental and expertise-oriented analysis of how metacognitive capacity changes as learners progress from novice to expert within a domain. Examines how the need for external scaffolding shifts, what metacognitive functions are most resistant to internalisation, and how expert self-regulators differ qualitatively (not just quantitatively) from novice self-regulators.
> **Connection to this report:** The Developmental Arc Model (Section 7.4) proposes four stages but does not deeply examine the empirical literature on metacognitive development across expertise levels.
