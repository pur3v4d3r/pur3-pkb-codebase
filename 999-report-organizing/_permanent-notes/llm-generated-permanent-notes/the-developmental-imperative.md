# The Developmental Imperative

> [!definition] The Developmental Imperative
> The Developmental Imperative refers to the design principle in metacognitive scaffolding that emphasizes creating supports which are temporary and aimed at fostering independent learning, rather than becoming permanent substitutes for developing internal cognitive capacities.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Developmental Imperative
> Metacognitive scaffolding achieves its purpose only when it is designed with its own obsolescence in view. A scaffold that permanently supports a function has not scaffolded development; it has substituted for it. The design challenge is therefore not merely to build effective scaffolds but to build scaffolds that systematically work toward their own removal — that train the internal capacity they temporarily replace.
