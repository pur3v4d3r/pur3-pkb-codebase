# The Metacognition-Motivation Interface: How Self-Assessment Shapes Engagement

> [!definition] The Metacognition-Motivation Interface: How Self-Assessment Shapes Engagement
> The Metacognition-Motivation Interface refers to the dynamic interaction between self-assessment processes and motivational states, influencing learning behaviors such as self-efficacy, goal commitment, and persistence.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Metacognition-Motivation Interface: How Self-Assessment Shapes Engagement
> **Description:** An investigation of the bidirectional relationship between metacognitive monitoring and motivational states — how monitoring outcomes (overconfidence, underconfidence, accurate calibration) influence self-efficacy, goal commitment, and persistence, and how motivational factors (achievement goals, self-efficacy beliefs, interest) bias the monitoring process itself.
> **Connection to this report:** Section 7 briefly notes Pintrich's integration of motivational and metacognitive components within SRL, but the specific mechanisms by which metacognition and motivation interact — and the consequences of their interaction for learning behavior — warrant dedicated examination.
