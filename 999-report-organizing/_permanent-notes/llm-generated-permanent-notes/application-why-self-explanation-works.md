# Application: Why Self-Explanation Works

> [!definition] Application: Why Self-Explanation Works
> Self-explanation is a cognitive strategy that involves explaining one's thoughts and inferences while engaging with material, which enhances comprehension and transfer by making implicit knowledge explicit and filling gaps in understanding.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Application: Why Self-Explanation Works
> **Q:** Why is self-explanation consistently more effective than rereading for comprehension and transfer?
> **A:** Self-explanation forces the reader to make implicit inferences explicit, exposes gaps in the developing representation that monitoring alone would not flag, and requires articulation that surfaces the reader's actual rather than assumed understanding. It substitutes a *direct test* of representation for the *indirect inference* that ordinary monitoring relies upon, making it a regulatory action and a calibration check at the same time.
