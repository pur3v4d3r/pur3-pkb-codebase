# Tim Kasser (1966–)

> [!definition] Tim Kasser (1966–)
> Tim Kasser is a motivational psychologist known for developing Goal Contents Theory with Ryan, which explores the impact of prioritizing extrinsic versus intrinsic goals on well-being.

## Additional Material

> [!person] Tim Kasser (1966–)
> Knox College. Developed Goal Contents Theory with Ryan, creating the Aspiration Index and generating the research program demonstrating that the relative priority of extrinsic (wealth, fame, image) over intrinsic (growth, relationships, community) goals predicts diminished well-being.
>
> **Relationship to other figures:** Student of Ryan; extended SDT into materialist values research; connected GCT to environmental sustainability.
>
> **Key works:** Kasser & Ryan (1993, 1996), Kasser (2002) *The High Price of Materialism*.
