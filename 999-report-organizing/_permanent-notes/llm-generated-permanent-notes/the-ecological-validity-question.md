# The Ecological Validity Question

> [!definition] The Ecological Validity Question
> The Ecological Validity Question refers to the uncertainty about whether findings from controlled cognitive-scientific studies can be applied to real-world personal knowledge management practices.

## Open Threads

> [!open-question] The Ecological Validity Question
> The cognitive-scientific evidence cited in this report was overwhelmingly generated in laboratory or classroom settings with experimental paradigms designed for internal validity. The extent to which these findings transfer to naturalistic PKM practice — which is self-paced, self-directed, ecologically variable, and lacking experimental control — remains an open empirical question. Longitudinal studies of PKM practitioners using cognitively aligned systems would provide the most direct evidence, but such studies have not yet been conducted at scale.
> *— [[pkb-pkm-planning-and-cognitive-science-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*
