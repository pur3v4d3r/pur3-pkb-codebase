## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Adult-learning is grounded in theories such as experiential learning and transformative learning, which emphasize the role of experience and reflection in the learning process. It often involves setting personal goals and applying new knowledge to real-world situations.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, adult learners may engage in self-directed study, participate in workshops or seminars, or pursue formal education programs tailored to their needs and interests. The focus is on relevance, applicability, and motivation specific to the learner's life stage and context.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the importance of prior knowledge and experience, the role of intrinsic motivation, and the need for supportive learning environments that accommodate diverse adult learners.
