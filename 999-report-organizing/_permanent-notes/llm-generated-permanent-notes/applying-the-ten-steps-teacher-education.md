# Applying the Ten Steps: Teacher Education

> [!definition] Applying the Ten Steps: Teacher Education
> Applying the Ten Steps in Teacher Education involves a structured approach to designing teacher preparation programs, covering lesson planning, real-time adaptation, cognitive strategies, classroom management, and procedural routines.

## Examples

> [!example] Applying the Ten Steps: Teacher Education
> Designing a teacher preparation program for secondary science instruction using the Ten Steps:
>
> - **Step 1:** Task classes progress from lesson planning for familiar content → delivering planned lessons to small groups → adapting lessons in real-time for diverse learners → managing complete units including assessment, differentiation, and parent communication.
> - **Steps 4-6:** Supportive information includes mental models of student cognition ([[cognitive-constructivism|cognitive constructivism]], misconception research) and cognitive strategies for pedagogical reasoning (anticipating student difficulties, designing formative checks).
> - **Steps 7-9:** Procedural information covers classroom management routines, equipment setup procedures, standardized assessment administration protocols.
> - **Step 10:** Part-task practice targets questioning technique (rapid formulation of Socratic questions), wait-time management, and use of classroom technology tools.
> *— [[four-component-instructional-design-model-4cid-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*
