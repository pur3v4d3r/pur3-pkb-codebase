# reflective-practice

> [!definition] reflective-practice
> - **Key-Term**: [[reflective-practice]]
> - **Definition**: Reflective practice is a process of engaging in systematic reflection and analysis of one's own teaching practices, learning experiences, or professional activities to improve performance and enhance understanding.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Reflective practice involves critically examining one’s actions, decisions, and outcomes within educational settings. It encourages practitioners to consider the 'why' behind their choices and how these can be improved for better results.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> This process typically includes steps such as observing an event or experience, analyzing what happened, considering alternative approaches, and planning future actions based on this reflection. Educators often use journals, discussions, or structured reflective frameworks to facilitate this practice.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Reflective practice is closely linked with theories of adult learning (andragogy) and has roots in the works of educators like Malcolm Knowles.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In education, reflective practice can lead to more effective teaching strategies and improved student outcomes by fostering a deeper understanding of pedagogical methods.

> [!example] Application
> For professionals in various fields, it enhances problem-solving skills and adaptability, leading to better performance and innovation.
