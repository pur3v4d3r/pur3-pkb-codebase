# Translation Window Diagnostic Checklist

> [!definition] Translation Window Diagnostic Checklist
> A structured list of questions to evaluate the appropriateness of a goal-setting intervention based on current conditions and motivational fit.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Translation Window Diagnostic Checklist
> For any proposed goal-setting intervention:
> - [ ] Do the activities required by the goal coincide with the activities the underlying value requires?
> - [ ] Is honest pursuit feasible given current ability and accessible strategy?
> - [ ] Does the measurement capture all dimensions of performance that matter?
> - [ ] Do failure consequences preserve capacity to pursue future goals constructively?
> - [ ] Have you considered whether a learning goal would be more appropriate than a performance goal?
> - [ ] Is the commitment autonomously regulated rather than externally pressured?
>
> **Decision rule:** If any answer is no, either re-engineer the conditions before setting the goal or set a different kind of goal whose mechanisms are appropriate to current conditions.
> *— [[goal-setting-theory-locke-latham-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*
