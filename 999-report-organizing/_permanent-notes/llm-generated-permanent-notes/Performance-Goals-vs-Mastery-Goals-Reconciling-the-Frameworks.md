# Performance-Goals-vs-Mastery-Goals-Reconciling-the-Frameworks

> [!definition] Performance-Goals-vs-Mastery-Goals-Reconciling-the-Frameworks
> - **Key-Term**: [[Performance-Goals-vs-Mastery-Goals-Reconciling-the-Frameworks]]
> - **Definition**: The concept of reconciling performance goals and mastery goals refers to the integration of two types of goal orientations—focusing on outcomes versus focusing on learning—in educational and psychological contexts, aiming to enhance motivation and performance across various domains such as athletics, music practice, and academic achievement.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Performance goals are oriented towards demonstrating ability or achieving a specific outcome, often leading to anxiety and avoidance of challenges. Mastery goals focus on improving skills and understanding, fostering persistence and intrinsic motivation.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> Reconciling these frameworks involves strategies that encourage individuals to adopt both types of goals simultaneously, balancing the pursuit of success with the drive for personal growth and learning.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the importance of context in determining which goal orientation is more beneficial, as well as the role of commitment and autonomy in achieving a balanced approach.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In educational settings, reconciling performance and mastery goals can lead to improved student engagement and academic achievement by promoting both intrinsic motivation and a growth mindset.

> [!example] Application
> In athletic training, integrating these goal orientations helps athletes develop resilience and continuous improvement rather than solely focusing on winning or losing.
