# Legal Interpretation as Metacognitive Reading

> [!definition] Legal Interpretation as Metacognitive Reading
> Legal Interpretation as Metacognitive Reading involves the explicit articulation of interpretive standards, structured externalization of reasoning, and institutional checks to ensure rigorous analysis and validation of legal texts.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Legal Interpretation as Metacognitive Reading
> The transferable principle is that any practice involving authoritative interpretation of authoritative texts will benefit from explicit articulation of interpretive standards (the canons), structured externalization of reasoning (the written opinion), and institutional checks that bring multiple readers to bear (the appellate process). The boundary condition is that these structures must operate at the level the architecture requires; institutions that have the structures but use them as performances rather than as genuine checks gain little from possession alone.
