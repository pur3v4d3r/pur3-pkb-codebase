# strategic-planning

> [!definition] strategic-planning
> - **Key-Term**: [[strategic-planning]]
> - **Definition**: Strategic planning is the process of defining an organization's long-term goals and determining actions to achieve them, involving analysis of internal resources and external environments, and setting priorities for achieving those goals over time.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Strategic planning involves a comprehensive approach to setting organizational objectives and developing strategies to meet these objectives. It typically includes environmental scanning, SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) analysis, and scenario planning to anticipate future challenges and opportunities.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> The process often begins with defining the organization's mission and vision, followed by setting specific, measurable goals and objectives. These are then broken down into actionable plans that align resources and activities across different departments or functions within the organization.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the iterative nature of strategic planning, which requires continuous monitoring and adjustment based on feedback and changing conditions.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In practice, effective strategic planning can lead to better resource allocation, improved decision-making, and enhanced organizational performance by aligning activities with long-term goals.

> [!example] Application
> It also helps in managing risks and uncertainties by preparing the organization for potential challenges and opportunities.
