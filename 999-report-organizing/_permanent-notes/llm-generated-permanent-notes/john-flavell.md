## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Flavell's research focuses on how individuals monitor their own thinking processes (metacognition) and adjust them accordingly. He introduced the concept of 'metacognitive knowledge' which includes awareness of one’s own cognitive processes, strategies for problem-solving, and beliefs about learning.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, Flavell’s theories have been applied in educational settings to enhance teaching methods that promote self-regulated learning, such as encouraging students to engage in self-explanation and reflection on their thought processes.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between procedural and declarative metacognitive knowledge, where procedural knowledge involves knowing how to do something, while declarative knowledge involves knowing what one knows.

## Practical Implications
