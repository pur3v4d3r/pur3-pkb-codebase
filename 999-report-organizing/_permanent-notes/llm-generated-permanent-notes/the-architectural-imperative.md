# The Architectural Imperative

> [!definition] The Architectural Imperative
> The Architectural Imperative refers to the requirement for a specific hierarchical organization of cognitive processes with defined information channels between levels, essential for grounding metacognitive phenomena theoretically.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Architectural Imperative
> The central claim motivating Nelson and Narens' framework is that metacognition requires not merely the existence of self-directed cognitive processes but a specific architectural arrangement in which those processes are organized across distinct levels, with formally specifiable channels of information flow between them. Without this architectural commitment, metacognitive phenomena remain descriptive curiosities — interesting but theoretically ungrounded.
> *— [[nelson-narens-metacognitive-control-framework-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*
