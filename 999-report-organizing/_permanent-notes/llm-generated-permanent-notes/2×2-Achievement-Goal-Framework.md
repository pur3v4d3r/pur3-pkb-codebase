# 2×2-Achievement-Goal-Framework

> [!definition] 2×2-Achievement-Goal-Framework
> - **Key-Term**: [[2×2-Achievement-Goal-Framework]]
> - **Definition**: The 2×2 Achievement Goal Framework is a theoretical model that categorizes achievement goals into two dimensions: performance-approach (aiming to outperform others) and performance-avoidance (fearing failure), as well as mastery-approach (seeking personal improvement) and mastery-avoidance (fearing incompetence).
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> This framework is foundational in understanding how individuals set and pursue goals, particularly in educational and athletic contexts. It helps explain why different goal orientations can lead to varying outcomes such as motivation, persistence, and performance.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, the framework is applied by educators and coaches to tailor interventions that promote adaptive goal-setting strategies, thereby enhancing learning or performance. For instance, it can guide teachers on how to encourage students towards mastery goals over performance goals to foster a growth mindset.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between approach (aiming for) and avoidance (fearing) outcomes, which significantly influence psychological states like anxiety and self-efficacy.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In education, this framework can help teachers design curricula that promote mastery goals to enhance student engagement and learning.

> [!example] Application
> For athletes, it aids in developing training programs that focus on performance improvement rather than just winning, which can lead to better long-term athletic development.
