## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The core mechanism of expertise development involves engaging in structured and purposeful activities that challenge one's current level of performance, receiving feedback on performance, and making adjustments to improve. This process is iterative and requires sustained effort and dedication.

> In practice, expertise development can be applied through various methods such as deliberate practice, coaching, and the use of AI-assisted learning environments. These methods help learners identify their weaknesses, correct them, and build upon their strengths in a systematic way.
