## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> This concept involves a blend of textualism, which emphasizes the plain meaning of statutory language, and purposivism, which focuses on the underlying intent or purpose behind the law. The approach aims to create coherent legal interpretations by harmonizing these two perspectives.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> Judges using this method first consider the text's literal meaning but then adjust their interpretation based on the broader context and the statute’s intended effect, ensuring that the final interpretation is both faithful to the text and aligned with its purpose.
