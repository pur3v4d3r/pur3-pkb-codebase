> Paragraph 1: Foundational context and core mechanism. The self-determination-theory-internalization-continuum-deep-dive-2026-04-21 is a key aspect of SDT, which posits that human motivation can be categorized into three basic psychological needs—autonomy, competence, and relatedness. Internalization refers to the process by which these needs are met in such a way that individuals' motivations become more self-generated and integrated with their personal values.
