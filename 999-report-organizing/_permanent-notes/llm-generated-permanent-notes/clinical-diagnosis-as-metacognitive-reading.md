# Clinical Diagnosis as Metacognitive Reading

> [!definition] Clinical Diagnosis as Metacognitive Reading
> Clinical Diagnosis as Metacognitive Reading refers to applying metacognitive strategies, such as explicitly articulating alternative hypotheses, during the diagnostic process to mitigate early-closure errors under time pressure and uncertainty.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Clinical Diagnosis as Metacognitive Reading
> The transferable principle is that complex evidence integration tasks operating under time pressure and uncertainty will be vulnerable to early-closure failures of the same architectural form as comprehension monitoring failures, and will benefit from the same kind of intervention: explicit articulation of the alternatives the felt-right hypothesis must be checked against. The boundary condition is that articulating alternatives only helps when the practitioner has the knowledge to recognize what the alternatives are; without that knowledge, the architectural insight cannot do its work.
