# Recurrent Constituent Skill (van Merriënboer, 1997)

> [!definition] Recurrent Constituent Skill (van Merriënboer, 1997)
> A constituent skill component of a complex cognitive skill whose execution remains consistent across problem situations. Recurrent skills are performed the same way regardless of context. They are governed by automated rules and procedures, and their performance improves through rule automation (repeated practice under consistent conditions) rather than through conceptual elaboration.
>
> **Boundary:** Recurrence does not imply simplicity. A recurrent skill may be a complex procedure with many steps — its defining characteristic is consistency across contexts, not low cognitive demand.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** Paired with the non-recurrent category, this distinction generates the model's dual-mechanism instructional logic.
>
> **See also:** [[Schema Automation]], [[automaticity]], [[deliberate-practice]]
