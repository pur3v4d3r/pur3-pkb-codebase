> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The core mechanism of Carole Ames' theory involves understanding different types of achievement goals (e.g., mastery, performance-approach, performance-avoidance) that influence learners' motivation and behavior.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
