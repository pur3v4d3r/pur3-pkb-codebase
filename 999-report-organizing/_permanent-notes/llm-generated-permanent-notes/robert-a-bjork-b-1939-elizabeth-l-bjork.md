# Robert A. Bjork (b. 1939) & Elizabeth L. Bjork

> [!definition] Robert A. Bjork (b. 1939) & Elizabeth L. Bjork
> Robert A. Bjork and Elizabeth L. Bjork are psychologists known for developing the desirable difficulties framework and the new theory of disuse, which distinguishes between storage strength and retrieval strength.

## Additional Material

> [!person] Robert A. Bjork (b. 1939) & Elizabeth L. Bjork
> UCLA. Developers of the [[desirable-difficulties|desirable difficulties]] framework and the new theory of disuse (storage strength vs. retrieval strength). Their work grounds the retrieval analysis in Section 4 and the productive friction concept in PKM design.
>
> **Key work in this report:** Bjork, R. A., & Bjork, E. L. (2011). Making things hard on yourself, but in a good way.
