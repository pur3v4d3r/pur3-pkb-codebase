# motivated-strategies-for-learning-questionnaire

> [!definition] motivated-strategies-for-learning-questionnaire
> - **Key-Term**: [[motivated-strategies-for-learning-questionnaire]]
> - **Definition**: A self-report questionnaire designed to assess students' motivation and the strategies they use for learning, often used in educational psychology research to understand how different motivational factors influence study behaviors and academic performance.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The motivated-strategies-for-learning-questionnaire (MSLQ) is a tool that helps researchers and educators understand the interplay between students' motivation and their learning strategies. It typically includes scales for assessing various aspects of motivation, such as self-efficacy, goal orientation, and value beliefs, alongside measures of specific learning strategies like elaboration, organization, and rehearsal.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> Participants are asked to rate their agreement with a series of statements that reflect different motivational factors and learning strategies. The questionnaire is often administered at the beginning and end of an academic term to track changes in motivation and strategy use over time.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> The MSLQ has several sub-scales that capture different dimensions of motivation and learning, allowing for a nuanced understanding of how these factors interact. For example, it includes scales for mastery goal orientation (focusing on personal improvement) versus performance goal orientation (focusing on outperforming others).

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> Educators can use the MSLQ to identify students who may need additional support in developing effective learning strategies and enhancing their motivation.

> [!example] Application
> Researchers can utilize the MSLQ to explore how different motivational factors influence academic performance and persistence, which can inform interventions aimed at improving student outcomes.
