# Personal-Knowledge-Management-—-PKM

> [!definition] Personal-Knowledge-Management-—-PKM
> - **Key-Term**: [[Personal-Knowledge-Management-—-PKM]]
> - **Definition**: Personal-Knowledge-Management (PKM) is the process of organizing, storing, and retrieving information to enhance personal learning and productivity. It involves strategies for managing one's own knowledge resources effectively, enabling individuals to build expertise and make informed decisions based on their accumulated knowledge.
> - **Domain**: learning-science

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Personal-Knowledge-Management (PKM) is a critical component of lifelong learning that helps individuals organize and leverage their knowledge in various domains. It involves the use of tools and techniques such as note-taking, categorization, and retrieval strategies to enhance personal understanding and application of information.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, PKM works by allowing individuals to create structured systems for storing and accessing information, which can be tailored to specific needs or contexts. This process often includes the use of digital tools like knowledge bases, wikis, and note-taking applications, as well as traditional methods such as index cards and binders.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances in PKM include the distinction between explicit and tacit knowledge, where explicit knowledge is codified and can be easily shared, while tacit knowledge is more personal and context-dependent. Theoretical roots of PKM are found in cognitive psychology, information science, and educational theory.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> PKM can significantly enhance an individual's ability to learn from experience by providing a structured way to record and review past learning.

> [!example] Application
> It also supports the development of expertise through deliberate practice and reflection, which are crucial for building deep understanding in specific domains.
