# information-processing-model-of-self-regulated-learning

> [!definition] information-processing-model-of-self-regulated-learning
> - **Key-Term**: [[information-processing-model-of-self-regulated-learning]]
> - **Definition**: The information-processing model of self-regulated learning describes how learners process and manage their cognitive, metacognitive, and motivational resources to achieve educational goals through active monitoring and regulation of their own learning processes.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> This model posits that self-regulated learning involves a cycle where students engage in goal-setting, plan strategies for achieving these goals, execute the planned actions, monitor their progress, and adjust their approach as needed. It emphasizes the role of metacognition—thinking about one's own thinking—in guiding this process.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> The model operates through several key components: activation of prior knowledge to facilitate understanding new information; setting clear learning objectives; selecting appropriate strategies for acquiring or applying knowledge; monitoring one’s progress and adjusting strategies accordingly; and evaluating the effectiveness of these strategies. These processes are interrelated, with each step informing and influencing the others.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the role of metacognitive calibration—adjusting beliefs about one's own abilities—and adaptive help-seeking as a regulatory skill. The model also acknowledges that learners may face challenges in accurately assessing their own knowledge and skills.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> Instructors can design learning activities that promote self-regulation by providing opportunities for students to set goals, plan strategies, monitor progress, and reflect on the effectiveness of these strategies.

> [!example] Application
> Educational interventions aimed at improving metacognitive skills, such as calibration training, can enhance learners' ability to accurately assess their own knowledge and adjust their study methods accordingly.
