# motivation

> [!definition] motivation
> - **Key-Term**: [[motivation]]
> - **Definition**: Motivation is the psychological process that initiates, guides, and sustains goal-directed behaviors.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Motivation arises from internal desires, needs, or external incentives that drive individuals to engage in specific actions towards achieving certain goals. It involves both conscious and unconscious processes that influence behavior and can be influenced by factors such as emotions, beliefs, and environmental conditions.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, motivation is often activated through various strategies like setting clear objectives, providing feedback, offering rewards, or fostering a supportive environment. For instance, in educational settings, teachers might use gamification to enhance students' intrinsic motivation for learning new material.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between intrinsic and extrinsic motivation, where intrinsic motivation stems from personal interest or enjoyment, while extrinsic motivation is driven by external factors such as rewards or recognition.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In educational settings, understanding students' motivations can help educators design more effective learning experiences that align with their interests and needs.

> [!example] Application
> In clinical practice, therapists might use motivational interviewing to engage clients in the therapeutic process by addressing ambivalence and enhancing commitment to change.
