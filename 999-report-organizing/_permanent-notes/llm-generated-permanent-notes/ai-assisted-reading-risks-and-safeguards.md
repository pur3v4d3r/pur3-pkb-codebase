# AI-Assisted-Reading-Risks-and-Safeguards

> [!definition] AI-Assisted-Reading-Risks-and-Safeguards
> AI-Assisted-Reading-Risks-and-Safeguards refers to the analysis of how AI tools impact metacognitive reading processes, identifying scenarios where AI substitutes for internal cognitive functions potentially weakening them, versus situations where it enhances these functions.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] AI-Assisted-Reading-Risks-and-Safeguards
> *Description:* A focused analysis of how AI tools that summarize, explain, and answer questions about texts interact with the metacognitive reading architecture — both the conditions under which AI assistance substitutes for internal metacognition (often degrading it) and the conditions under which it supplements (often strengthening it).

> *Priority:* Critical
