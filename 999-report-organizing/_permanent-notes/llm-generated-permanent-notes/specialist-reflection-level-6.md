> Specialist Reflection — Level 6 involves deep consideration of how self-determination theory (SDT) applies to one's work environment, particularly focusing on frontier questions that could significantly impact the work, comparing SDT's formative conditions with current work settings, and evaluating expert debates that could alter one’s understanding of the framework.

## Reflections

> [!reflection] Specialist Reflection — Level 6
