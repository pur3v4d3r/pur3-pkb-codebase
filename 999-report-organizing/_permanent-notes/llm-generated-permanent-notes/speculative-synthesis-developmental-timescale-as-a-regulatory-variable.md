# Speculative Synthesis: Developmental Timescale as a Regulatory Variable

> [!definition] Speculative Synthesis: Developmental Timescale as a Regulatory Variable
> Speculative Synthesis: Developmental Timescale as a Regulatory Variable refers to the idea that the duration of development during which a regulatory style was formed could become an important factor in understanding and predicting well-being within the OIT framework.

## Reflections

> [!claude-insight] Speculative Synthesis: Developmental Timescale as a Regulatory Variable
> The temporal compression thesis suggests that the developmental timescale at which a regulation was constructed may itself become a variable in the OIT mechanism — a variable that historically did not need to be specified because the timescale was approximately constant across the populations studied, but that may need to enter the framework explicitly as the range of available developmental timescales widens. A future OIT may need to characterize not only the regulatory style (external, introjected, identified, integrated) but also the developmental timescale at which the style was constructed (extended, typical, compressed, ultra-compressed), with predictions for stability and well-being conditioned on the conjunction of style and timescale.
