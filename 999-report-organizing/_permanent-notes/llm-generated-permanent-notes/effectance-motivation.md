## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Effectance-motivation is a key component of self-determination theory (SDT), emphasizing the importance of individuals' desire to understand and influence their surroundings. It is closely related to the need for competence, which drives people to seek out challenges and master new skills.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, effectance-motivation manifests as an intrinsic motivation to engage with tasks that offer opportunities for learning and growth. This can be observed in students who are eager to solve problems or conduct experiments, driven by a desire to enhance their understanding of the subject matter.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between effectance-motivation and other forms of autonomy, such as relatedness and competence. Effectance specifically pertains to the drive for mastery and exploration, while relatedness focuses on social connections and feelings of belonging.
