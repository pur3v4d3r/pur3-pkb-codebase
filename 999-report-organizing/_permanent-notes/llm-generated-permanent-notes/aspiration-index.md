# aspiration-index

> [!definition] aspiration-index
> - **Key-Term**: [[aspiration-index]]
> - **Definition**: The aspiration-index is a measure of an individual's level of autonomous motivation, reflecting their personal goals and aspirations that are driven by internal factors rather than external pressures or rewards.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The concept of the aspiration-index originates from Self-Determination Theory (SDT), which posits that individuals have innate psychological needs for autonomy, competence, and relatedness. The aspiration-index specifically focuses on the degree to which an individual's goals are self-determined and aligned with their personal values.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, the aspiration-index is often assessed through questionnaires or interviews that evaluate the extent to which a person’s aspirations are internally motivated and congruent with their intrinsic interests and values. This measure can be used in various contexts, such as education, work environments, and health behaviors, to understand and enhance individual motivation.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between autonomous and controlled motivations, where autonomous motivations are more likely to lead to sustained engagement and well-being.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
