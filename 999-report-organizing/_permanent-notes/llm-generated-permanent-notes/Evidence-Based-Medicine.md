# Evidence-Based-Medicine

> [!definition] Evidence-Based-Medicine
> - **Key-Term**: [[Evidence-Based-Medicine]]
> - **Definition**: Evidence-Based-Medicine is an approach to medical practice that emphasizes the use of current best evidence from systematic research, combined with clinical expertise and patient values and preferences, in making decisions about the care of individual patients.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Evidence-Based-Medicine seeks to integrate the best available scientific evidence with clinical expertise and patient values. It involves a rigorous process of identifying, critically appraising, and applying research findings to clinical decision-making.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> Practitioners use systematic reviews, meta-analyses, randomized controlled trials, and other forms of high-quality evidence to inform their decisions. They also consider the context in which care is provided, including patient preferences, local resources, and ethical considerations.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the importance of continuous learning and updating knowledge based on new evidence, as well as the challenge of translating research findings into everyday clinical practice.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> Improves patient outcomes by ensuring that treatment decisions are based on the most current and reliable scientific evidence.

> [!example] Application
> Enhances efficiency in healthcare delivery by reducing unnecessary or ineffective treatments and promoting cost-effective practices.
