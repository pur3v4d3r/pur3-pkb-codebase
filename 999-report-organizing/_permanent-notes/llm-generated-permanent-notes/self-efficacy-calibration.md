# self-efficacy-calibration

> [!definition] self-efficacy-calibration
> - **Key-Term**: [[self-efficacy-calibration]]
> - **Definition**: Self-efficacy-calibration refers to the process by which individuals adjust their confidence levels based on their performance and feedback, ensuring that their self-assessments accurately reflect their actual abilities or knowledge.
> - **Domain**: cognitive-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Self-efficacy-calibration is a fundamental aspect of metacognition, involving the continuous adjustment of one's belief in their ability to perform tasks successfully. This process is crucial for effective learning and decision-making as it helps individuals maintain accurate self-assessments that guide their actions.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> It works through a feedback loop where individuals evaluate their performance after attempting a task or receiving information about their success, then adjust their confidence levels accordingly. For example, if an individual performs poorly on a test but receives positive feedback from a teacher, they may recalibrate their belief in their abilities to perform better next time.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the role of external validation and the potential for biases such as overconfidence or underconfidence. Theoretical roots can be traced back to theories of self-efficacy by Albert Bandura, who emphasized the importance of personal beliefs in influencing behavior.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In educational settings, accurate self-efficacy-calibration can lead to more effective study strategies and better academic performance as students adjust their efforts based on their perceived abilities.

> [!example] Application
> In clinical decision-making, proper calibration helps healthcare professionals make informed choices by aligning their confidence levels with the actual risks and benefits of different treatment options.
