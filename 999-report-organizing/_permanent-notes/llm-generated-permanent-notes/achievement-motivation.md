# achievement-motivation

> [!definition] achievement-motivation
> - **Key-Term**: [[achievement-motivation]]
> - **Definition**: Achievement motivation refers to the drive and desire to achieve success, set challenging goals, and persistently work towards them despite obstacles. It is a fundamental aspect of human behavior that influences academic and athletic performance, as well as personal development in various domains.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Achievement motivation is rooted in the psychological need for competence and self-efficacy, which are central to understanding individual differences in goal-directed behaviors. Individuals with high achievement motivation tend to set ambitious goals, seek out challenging tasks, and maintain a strong commitment to their objectives.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> This drive can be influenced by various factors such as personal values, past experiences, and environmental support or pressure. In educational settings, students with high achievement motivation are more likely to engage in deep learning strategies, persist through difficulties, and achieve better academic outcomes.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> There are different sub-variants of achievement motivation, including the distinction between mastery orientation (focusing on improving skills) and performance orientation (focusing on outperforming others). These orientations can have varying impacts on learning and well-being.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In education, fostering a growth mindset and providing opportunities for students to set and achieve challenging goals can enhance their achievement motivation. Teachers can create supportive environments that encourage risk-taking and resilience.

> [!example] Application
> In sports, coaches can use goal-setting techniques and provide constructive feedback to boost athletes' performance orientation and help them develop the necessary skills.
