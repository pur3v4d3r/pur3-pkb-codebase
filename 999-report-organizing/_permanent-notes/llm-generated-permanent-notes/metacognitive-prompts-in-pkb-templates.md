# Metacognitive Prompts in PKB Templates

> [!definition] Metacognitive Prompts in PKB Templates
> Metacognitive Prompts in PKB Templates are structured questions embedded within Personal Knowledge Base note templates that guide users to monitor their understanding and cognitive processes actively, fostering metacognitive skills during knowledge acquisition.

## Examples

> [!example] Metacognitive Prompts in PKB Templates
> A PKB note template that includes fields like "What do I already know about this topic?", "What connections does this make to existing permanent notes?", "What remains unclear after writing this note?", and "What would I need to do to deepen this understanding?" embeds metacognitive monitoring directly into the note-making workflow. These prompts do not merely gather information; they force the practitioner to engage the monitoring function of metacognition at precisely the moment when monitoring is most productive — during the active processing of new material. Over time, as the practitioner internalizes these metacognitive habits, the external prompts can be faded (consistent with CLT's expertise reversal principle), replaced by internalized metacognitive routines that operate automatically during knowledge processing. The template, in this analysis, is not a formatting convenience but a metacognitive training device.
