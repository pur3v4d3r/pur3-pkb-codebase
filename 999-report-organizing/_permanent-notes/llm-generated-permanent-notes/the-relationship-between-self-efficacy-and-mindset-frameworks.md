# The Relationship Between Self-Efficacy and Mindset Frameworks

> [!definition] The Relationship Between Self-Efficacy and Mindset Frameworks
> The relationship between self-efficacy and mindset frameworks refers to the ongoing debate in social-cognitive psychology about whether these theories describe overlapping or distinct cognitive variables.
