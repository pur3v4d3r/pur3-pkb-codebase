# autonomous-motivation-vs.-controlled-motivation

> [!definition] autonomous-motivation-vs.-controlled-motivation
> - **Key-Term**: [[autonomous-motivation-vs.-controlled-motivation]]
> - **Definition**: Autonomous motivation refers to engaging in activities for the inherent enjoyment and personal satisfaction, while controlled motivation involves performing actions due to external pressures or rewards.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Autonomous motivation is characterized by a deep interest and engagement with an activity because it aligns with one's values and interests. This form of motivation is associated with higher levels of well-being and performance. In contrast, controlled motivation arises from external factors such as fear of punishment or desire for rewards, leading to less intrinsic satisfaction.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, autonomous motivation can be fostered through environments that support self-determination theory (SDT), which emphasizes the importance of autonomy, competence, and relatedness. Controlled motivation is often seen in settings where these needs are not met, such as highly regulated educational or work environments.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between amotivation (lack of motivation) and different types of controlled motivation, such as introjected regulation (where individuals engage in activities to avoid negative self-evaluations). Theoretical roots can be traced back to Self-Determination Theory (SDT), which posits that autonomy is a fundamental human need.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In education, fostering autonomous motivation through student-centered learning and choice can lead to better academic outcomes and higher levels of engagement.

> [!example] Application
> In workplace settings, promoting intrinsic rewards and opportunities for personal growth can enhance employee satisfaction and productivity.
