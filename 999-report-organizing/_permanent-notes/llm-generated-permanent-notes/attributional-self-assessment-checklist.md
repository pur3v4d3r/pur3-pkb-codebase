# Attributional Self-Assessment Checklist

> [!definition] Attributional Self-Assessment Checklist
> Attributional Self-Assessment Checklist is a tool for evaluating one's attributional tendencies after success or failure by assessing locus (internal vs. external), stability (temporary vs. enduring), and controllability (modifiable vs. fixed) of causes, along with emotional responses and adaptiveness of attributions.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Attributional Self-Assessment Checklist
> **Purpose:** Self-diagnostic tool for identifying one's own attributional tendencies.
>
> After an important success or failure, answer these questions:
>
> **Dimensional Classification:**
> - [ ] What cause did I assign? (Name it specifically)
> - [ ] Is this cause internal to me or external? → Locus
> - [ ] Will this cause be present next time? → Stability
> - [ ] Could I have changed this cause if I had chosen to? → Controllability
>
> **Emotional Check:**
> - [ ] Do I feel pride or shame? → Locus is operating
> - [ ] Do I feel hopeful or hopeless? → Stability is operating
> - [ ] Do I feel guilt or resentment? → Controllability is operating
>
> **Adaptiveness Assessment:**
> - [ ] If I failed: Am I attributing to stable, uncontrollable causes? (Maladaptive risk)
> - [ ] If I failed: Can I identify an unstable, controllable cause? (Adaptive reframe)
> - [ ] If I succeeded: Am I attributing to internal, stable causes? (Builds efficacy)
> - [ ] Am I making the fundamental attribution error about others?
>
