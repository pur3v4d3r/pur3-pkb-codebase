# Attributional-Processes-in-Intergroup-Relations

> [!definition] Attributional-Processes-in-Intergroup-Relations
> Attributional processes in intergroup relations refer to how individuals attribute causes for group behaviors or outcomes, influencing perceptions of stereotypes, prejudice, and social inequality.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Attributional-Processes-in-Intergroup-Relations
> **Description:** How attribution theory operates at the group level: the ultimate attribution error (Pettigrew, 1979), attributional biases in stereotyping and prejudice, the role of causal attribution in sustaining or challenging social inequality, and the intersection of attribution with social identity theory and system justification theory.
>
> **Connection to this report:** Section 6 touches on cultural variation in attribution, and the controllability-moral-inference bridge (Section 4) operates powerfully in attributions for poverty and social disadvantage. A dedicated report would develop the sociopolitical dimensions of attribution that this individually-focused report necessarily abbreviates.
