# SR Seed 10 — Distinction

> [!definition] SR Seed 10 — Distinction
> SR Seed 10 — Distinction refers to the ability of a well-calibrated learner to accurately match their confidence in knowledge with actual performance, distinguishing them from merely accurate learners who may perform well but lack this self-assessment calibration.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 10 — Distinction
> **Q:** What distinguishes a well-calibrated learner from a merely accurate learner?
> **A:** A well-calibrated learner's confidence accurately matches performance — they know what they know AND what they don't. A merely accurate learner performs well but may not accurately predict their own performance. Calibration is a property of the monitoring system, not the knowledge system.
> **Source:** Section 6.2, Appendix A.1
