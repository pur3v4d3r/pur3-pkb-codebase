# Central claim of this level

> [!definition] Central claim of this level
> Central claim of this level refers to the relationship between element interactivity and working-memory load, which organizes the theoretical edifice of cognitive-load theory beyond the three-load taxonomy.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Central claim of this level
> The three-load taxonomy is the *visible surface* of CLT, but it is not where the theoretical action is. The practitioner who stops at the taxonomy has acquired a useful tool but has missed the construct that has come to organize the entire theoretical edifice in its mature form. The construct is [[Technical Detail: The relationship between element interactivity and working-memory load]], and the work of subsequent levels is to make that construct visible, then trace its mechanism, then examine its boundary conditions, then engage the questions it leaves open.
