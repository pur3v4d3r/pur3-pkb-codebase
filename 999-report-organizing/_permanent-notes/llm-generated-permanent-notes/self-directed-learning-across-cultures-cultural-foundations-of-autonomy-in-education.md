## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Self-Directed Learning Across Cultures: Cultural Foundations of Autonomy in Education
> **Description:** An examination of how cultural values, epistemologies, and social structures shape the meaning and practice of self-directed learning across diverse cultural contexts — including collectivist cultures where autonomy may be constituted relationally rather than individually, and where the Western framing of self-direction as individual sovereignty may be inappropriate or counterproductive.
> **Connection to this report:** Section 8's discussion of the [[WEIRD-Sample-Bias|WEIRD sampling bias]] and cultural boundedness of self-direction concepts identified this as the most significant limitation of the current framework.
