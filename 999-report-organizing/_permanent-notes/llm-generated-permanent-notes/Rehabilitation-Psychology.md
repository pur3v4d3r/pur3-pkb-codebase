> [!definition] Rehabilitation-Psychology
> - **Key-Term**: [[Rehabilitation-Psychology]]

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Rehabilitation-Psychology integrates principles from psychology to address the mental health needs of patients during their rehabilitation process. It aims to enhance overall well-being by managing stress, anxiety, depression, and other psychological issues that can impede recovery.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> Practitioners in Rehabilitation-Psychology use various therapeutic techniques such as cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT), mindfulness, and motivational interviewing to help patients cope with their conditions and improve their quality of life. They also work closely with medical professionals to ensure a holistic approach to care.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the importance of individualized treatment plans that consider each patient's unique psychological profile and the role of family and social support systems in recovery.
