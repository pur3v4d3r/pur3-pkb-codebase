# Distinction: Locus of Causality vs. Locus of Control

> [!definition] Distinction: Locus of Causality vs. Locus of Control
> Locus of causality is a specific attribution categorizing causes as internal or external in a given situation, while locus of control refers to a general dispositional belief about whether outcomes are due to personal actions or external factors.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Distinction: Locus of Causality vs. Locus of Control
> **Q:** How does locus of causality (Weiner) differ from locus of control (Rotter)?
> **A:** Locus of causality is a situation-specific classification of a particular cause as internal or external. Locus of control is a generalised dispositional belief about whether outcomes in general are determined by one's own actions or by external forces. The first is a property of a single attribution; the second is a personality trait.
