# Definition: Causal Attribution

> [!definition] Definition: Causal Attribution
> Causal attribution is the cognitive process of assigning specific causes to events or outcomes along dimensions such as locus (internal vs. external), stability (permanent vs. temporary), and controllability (controllable vs. uncontrollable).

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Definition: Causal Attribution
> **Q:** What is a causal attribution in Weiner's framework?
> **A:** The cognitive process of assigning a specific cause to an event or outcome, classifiable along three dimensions (locus, stability, controllability), whose dimensional coordinates determine its emotional and motivational consequences.
