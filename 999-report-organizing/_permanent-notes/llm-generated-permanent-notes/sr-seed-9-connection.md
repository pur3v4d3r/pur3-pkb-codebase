# SR Seed 9 — Connection

> [!definition] SR Seed 9 — Connection
> SR Seed 9 — Connection refers to the external schema supports in Personal Knowledge Management systems that maintain inter-element relationships through wiki-links, ensuring that knowledge remains interconnected even when decomposed into atomic notes.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 9 — Connection
> **Q:** How can Personal Knowledge Management systems be analyzed through CLT's lens?
> **A:** PKM systems function as external schema supports that extend working memory through cognitive offloading. Atomic notes reduce element interactivity by decomposing complex ideas. Wiki-links maintain inter-element relationships externally. Maps of Content serve as advance organizers managing intrinsic load. The expertise reversal effect applies: organizational structures helpful for novices may become extraneous for experts who have internalized the domain structure.
> **Source:** Far Transfer Domain 4
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #clt #pkm #far-transfer #cognitive-offloading
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 9 — Connection
> **Q:** How does the Motivational Ecology Model complement the Scaffolding-Sovereignty Progression?
> **A:** The Scaffolding-Sovereignty Progression addresses the structural dimension (how much and what kind of support to provide), while the Motivational Ecology Model addresses the sustainability dimension (how engagement is maintained across the long arc of lifelong learning). The ecological model posits that sustainable motivation requires diversity, redundancy, development, social embeddedness, and metacognitive awareness.
> **Source:** Section 8 (original synthesis)
> **Difficulty:** Advanced
> **Tags:** #motivation #ecology #original-synthesis
> *— [[curriculum-design-for-lifelong-learning-and-self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 9 — Connection
> **Q:** What is the Cognitive Offloading Paradox, and how does the Developmental Arc Model resolve it?
> **A:** The paradox: offloading metacognitive processes to PKB scaffolds reduces cognitive load (benefit) but may prevent internalisation of those processes (cost). The Developmental Arc resolves it by treating scaffolding as a developmental tool — full scaffolding initially, progressive fading as competence develops, eventual independent performance.
> **Source:** Sections 3.3, 7.4
> **Difficulty:** Advanced
> **Tags:** #cognitive-offloading #scaffold-paradox #developmental-arc

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 9 — Application
> **Q:** What is an implementation intention, and how can it support PKM habit formation?
> **A:** An implementation intention is a specific "if-then" plan linking a situational cue to a behavior (e.g., "If I finish my morning coffee, then I open Obsidian and complete my review queue"). It delegates action initiation from deliberation to environmental detection, increasing follow-through probability.

> **Q:** What three conditions does SDT identify as necessary for effective internalization?
> **A:** (1) Autonomy support — providing choice and rationale rather than coercion, (2) Competence scaffolding — ensuring the individual has the skills to perform the behavior, (3) Relational context — a warm, caring relationship with the socializing agent.

> [!section-summary] Section 9 Summary
> SDT faces substantive criticisms regarding the universality of autonomy (addressed by distinguishing autonomy-as-endorsement from autonomy-as-independence), the replicability and mechanism of the undermining effect, measurement limitations inherent in self-report-dominant research, and potential explanatory circularity. Its relationship to competing theories (AGT, flow theory) involves ongoing integration efforts. The dynamic equilibrium reconceptualization — shifting from static need satisfaction to fluctuating organism-environment transactions — offers a promising direction that may address multiple criticisms simultaneously.
