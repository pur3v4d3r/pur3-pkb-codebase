# Autonomy-Supportive Structure

> [!definition] Autonomy-Supportive Structure
> An Autonomy-Supportive Structure in education is a curriculum design that combines clear expectations with meaningful choices within well-defined boundaries, fostering both competence and autonomy to maintain intrinsic motivation.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Autonomy-Supportive Structure
> The most effective curriculum designs for cultivating self-directed learning are those that combine high structure with high autonomy support — providing clear expectations, explicit rationale, and meaningful choice within well-defined boundaries. This combination satisfies the learner's need for competence (through structure) and autonomy (through choice and rationale) simultaneously, producing conditions under which [[Intrinsic Motivation|intrinsic motivation]] is maintained even as external demands increase. The mechanism is internalisation: well-structured, autonomy-supportive environments accelerate the process by which external educational structures are transformed into internal self-regulatory capacities.
