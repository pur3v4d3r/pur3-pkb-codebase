# self-determined-learning

> [!definition] self-determined-learning
> - **Key-Term**: [[self-determined-learning]]
> - **Definition**: Self-determined learning is an educational approach where learners take control of their own learning process, setting goals and making choices about what to learn, how to learn it, and when to learn it, based on intrinsic motivation and personal interests.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Self-determined learning is grounded in the belief that individuals are capable of directing their own educational journey. It emphasizes autonomy, competence, and relatedness as key psychological needs that must be met for effective learning. This approach fosters a sense of ownership over one's education, leading to higher engagement and deeper understanding.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, self-determined learning involves providing learners with the necessary resources, support, and feedback to make informed decisions about their educational path. Teachers or facilitators act as guides rather than authoritative figures, helping students navigate their learning journey while ensuring they have the autonomy to pursue their interests and goals.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between intrinsic and extrinsic motivation, where self-determined learning primarily focuses on fostering intrinsic motivation by aligning learning activities with personal values and interests. Sub-variants of this approach may include different levels of structure or guidance provided to learners.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> Instructors can create more engaging and effective learning environments by allowing students to have a say in what they learn, how they learn it, and when they learn it.

> [!example] Application
> Self-determined learning can lead to increased student satisfaction and motivation, as learners feel more invested in their educational outcomes.
