# performance-approach-goal

> [!definition] performance-approach-goal
> - **Key-Term**: [[performance-approach-goal]]
> - **Definition**: A performance-approach goal is a type of achievement goal where an individual focuses on improving their performance and surpassing personal standards, rather than outperforming others or avoiding failure.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Performance-approach goals are central to the Achievement Goal Theory (AGT), which distinguishes between different types of goals that learners set for themselves. These goals influence motivation, engagement, and performance in various domains such as education and sports.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> Individuals with a performance-approach orientation tend to set challenging but attainable personal standards and strive to improve their skills and abilities over time. This approach can lead to higher intrinsic motivation and better learning outcomes compared to other goal orientations like performance-avoidance or mastery goals.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between focusing on self-improvement versus outperforming others, as well as the potential for these goals to be maladaptive if they become too competitive or pressure-inducing.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In educational settings, fostering a performance-approach goal orientation can enhance students' engagement and academic achievement by encouraging them to set personal improvement targets.

> [!example] Application
> In sports training, coaches can use performance-approach goals to help athletes focus on skill development rather than just winning or losing, which may lead to better long-term performance.
