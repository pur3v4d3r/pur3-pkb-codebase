> Andragogy, as developed by Malcolm Knowles in the mid-20th century, emphasizes that adults learn differently from children. It focuses on self-directed learning, relevance to real-life situations, and the importance of experience in the learning process.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Paragraph 2: How it works or is applied in practice.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 4
> Knowles proposed several assumptions about adult learners, including their readiness to learn, orientation towards learning, and life experiences that serve as a foundation for new knowledge.
