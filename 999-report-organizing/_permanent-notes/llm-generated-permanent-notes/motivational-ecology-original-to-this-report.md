# Motivational Ecology (Original to This Report)

> [!definition] Motivational Ecology (Original to This Report)
> The interconnected system of motivational conditions — including intrinsic interest, identity commitment, social belonging, practical relevance, and competence satisfaction — that together create a self-sustaining motivational system characterised by diversity, redundancy, development, social embeddedness, and metacognitive awareness.
>
> **Boundary:** The ecological metaphor suggests dynamics (compensatory mechanisms, resilience through diversity) that have not been empirically tested in learning motivation contexts.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** The second original contribution of this report, complementing the Scaffolding-Sovereignty Progression by addressing motivational sustainability.
>
> **See also:** [[self-determination-theory]], [[Interest-Theory]], [[Intrinsic Motivation]], [[Legitimate-Peripheral-Participation]]
