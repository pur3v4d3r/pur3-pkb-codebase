> **Identified Limitations:**
> 1. The report focuses primarily on the Nelson-Narens framework as applied to metamemory and its extensions. Metacognitive monitoring and control in non-memory domains (e.g., problem-solving, decision-making, motor skill acquisition) receive only passing treatment.
> 2. Neuroimaging and neuroscientific evidence for the two-level architecture is not systematically reviewed.
> 3. Developmental research on how the monitoring-control loop matures is mentioned but not elaborated.
> 4. The computational modeling section is brief and does not examine specific implementations in depth.
> 5. The cultural and contextual factors that may influence metacognitive monitoring (cultural variation in self-assessment norms, educational context effects) are not addressed.
>
> **Recommendations for Future Revision:**
> 1. Add a dedicated section on neuroimaging evidence for meta-level/object-level dissociability.
> 2. Expand the computational modeling discussion with specific ACT-R and neural network implementations.
> 3. Include developmental trajectory research on metacognitive monitoring accuracy across ages.
> 4. Add cross-cultural considerations for metacognitive calibration norms.
> 5. Consider a companion Practitioner's Field Guide focused exclusively on calibration training interventions.
