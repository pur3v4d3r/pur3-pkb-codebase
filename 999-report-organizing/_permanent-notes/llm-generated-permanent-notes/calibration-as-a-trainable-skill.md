# Calibration as a Trainable Skill

> [!definition] Calibration as a Trainable Skill
> Calibration as a trainable skill refers to the ability to improve judgment accuracy through structured practice, including regular predictions about one's performance, objective assessment, and analysis of discrepancies.

## Reflections

> [!claude-insight] Calibration as a Trainable Skill
> [[metacognitive-calibration-training|Metacognitive calibration training]] research demonstrates that calibration accuracy improves with practice and feedback. The critical requirements are: (a) regular predictions about one's own performance, (b) subsequent objective assessment of actual performance, and (c) explicit comparison of prediction to outcome with analysis of the discrepancy. A PKB workflow that includes all three components — prediction in forethought, assessment after study, comparison in reflection — constitutes an informal but effective calibration training protocol. The key insight is that [[judgment-of-learning|judgment of learning]] accuracy is not a fixed trait; it is a skill that develops through structured practice. PKB scaffolds provide the structure.
