# performance-goal-orientation

> [!definition] performance-goal-orientation
> - **Key-Term**: [[performance-goal-orientation]]
> - **Definition**: Performance-goal orientation refers to an individual's focus on demonstrating their abilities and outperforming others, often leading to competitive behaviors in academic or athletic settings.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Performance-goal orientation is a key concept within achievement goal theory, which posits that individuals have different orientations towards goals related to performance (e.g., winning) versus mastery (e.g., learning and improving).

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> Individuals with a strong performance-goal orientation tend to set high standards for themselves and others, often leading to competitive behaviors aimed at surpassing peers or benchmarks.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> This orientation can be further divided into two subtypes: ego-involvement, where the focus is on relative standing compared to others, and task-involvement, which emphasizes personal improvement.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In educational settings, students with a performance-goal orientation may experience higher levels of stress and anxiety due to their competitive mindset.

> [!example] Application
> In sports, athletes with this orientation might prioritize winning over the development of skills or teamwork.
