## Additional Material

> [!section-summary] Section 2 Summary
> Weiner's three-dimensional taxonomy — locus, stability, and controllability — transforms the infinite variety of specific causal attributions into a systematic architecture with predictable emotional and motivational consequences. The locus dimension determines self-referent affects (pride, shame); the stability dimension determines future expectancy (hope, hopelessness); the controllability dimension determines social-moral affects (anger, pity, guilt, gratitude). This taxonomy integrates with earlier models (Kelley's covariation model, Jones and Davis's correspondent inference theory) by providing the *output* architecture that those models lack — specifying not just how attributions are formed, but what they *do* once formed.
> *— [[attribution-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 2 Summary
> The chunking account of expertise emerged from the convergence of three lines of work: de Groot's discovery that grandmasters do not search more deeply than masters but begin search from a better starting point; Chase and Simon's demonstration that this advantage results from the encoding of meaningful positions into larger memory units; and Miller's theoretical framework that distinguished the *number* of items in immediate memory (which is fixed) from the *information content* of each item (which can vary). Together these established that expertise resides in the contents of long-term memory rather than in any general cognitive enhancement, and that those contents act on incoming material by changing what counts as a single unit.
> *— [[chunking-and-expertise-development-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 2 Summary
> - The tripartite taxonomy distinguishes intrinsic load (inherent complexity), extraneous load (design-imposed demand), and germane load (schema-building effort) as three sources of demand on working memory.
> - Element interactivity — the degree to which information elements must be processed simultaneously — is the mechanism governing intrinsic load and CLT's most distinctive theoretical contribution.
> - The practical design imperative is to minimize extraneous load so that available working memory capacity can be directed toward schema construction, particularly when intrinsic load is high.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 2 Summary
> The intellectual trajectory from Dewey through Lindeman, Knowles, Tough, Candy, and Hase/Kenyon reveals a progressive deepening of the insight that curriculum must develop the learner's capacity for self-direction, not merely transmit content. Each generation extended the logic further: Dewey established experience-based education, Knowles operationalised it for adults through andragogy and learning contracts, Tough documented the prevalence of self-directed learning outside institutions, Candy distinguished personal self-direction from instructional self-direction, and heutagogy pushed toward full learner sovereignty. The common thread is the conviction that curriculum structure should serve as a launching architecture, not a permanent scaffolding.
> *— [[curriculum-design-for-lifelong-learning-and-self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 2 Summary
> - The 4C/ID model rests on three cognitive foundations: cognitive load theory, schema theory, and the dual-mechanism distinction between schema construction and schema automation.
> - Element interactivity explains why complex learning resists decomposition — the interactions between elements are essential to the meaning.
> - Cognitive load management requires minimizing extraneous load, managing intrinsic load through sequencing, and optimizing schema construction and automation.
> - The dual-mechanism thesis maps directly onto the model's four-component architecture: learning tasks and supportive information serve schema construction; procedural information and part-task practice serve schema automation.
> *— [[four-component-instructional-design-model-4cid-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 2 Summary
> - The Rubicon Model parses goal pursuit into four phases (deliberation, preplanning, action, evaluation) each governed by a distinct mind-set, with implementation intentions situated at the preplanning-to-action transition.
> - Bratman's planning theory contributed the philosophical insight that plans confer cognitive economy by exempting committed action from continual re-deliberation.
> - The if-then format that defines implementation intentions is what allows the situational cue to subsequently trigger the planned behavior without requiring fresh deliberative effort.
> - Goal intentions ("I want to do X") and implementation intentions ("When situation S, then behavior B") are categorically different cognitive structures despite their surface similarity.

> [!section-summary] Section 2 Summary
> The monitoring–control loop, formalized by Nelson and Narens, structures all metacognitive reading. An object level performs comprehension work; a meta level monitors and regulates it. Monitoring generates information (felt difficulty, sense of coherence, judgments of learning); control uses that information to modify object-level processing (rereading, slowing, looking up). The loop is logically distinguishable into separable failure modes — monitoring-side failures (poor sensitivity, poor calibration) and control-side failures (absent, inappropriate, or insufficient regulatory response) — and operates as a sampling rather than continuous system, which makes it most prone to silence in conditions of high fluency. The loop is embedded in a resource ecology (working memory, attention, prior knowledge, metacognitive knowledge) whose state determines how well the loop can function on any given occasion.
> *— [[metacognition-and-reading-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 2 Summary
> The Nelson-Narens framework emerged from the convergence of three intellectual streams: Flavell's taxonomic organization of metacognitive phenomena (which provided the vocabulary), the metamemory experimental tradition initiated by Hart and extended by Brown (which provided the empirical phenomena demanding explanation), and Nelson's own methodological innovations in measuring monitoring accuracy (which provided the tools for studying the meta-level's model). The transition from taxonomy to architecture — from listing components to specifying computational relationships between levels — represents the framework's defining theoretical contribution.
> *— [[nelson-narens-metacognitive-control-framework-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 2 Summary
> The framework's architecture combines four temporally ordered phases (forethought, monitoring, control, reaction) with four parallel areas (cognition, motivation, behavior, context), producing a sixteen-cell matrix in which each cell describes a distinct regulatory activity that can succeed or fail independently. The phases are logically ordered rather than strictly sequential, interleaving and overlapping as the episode unfolds; the areas are analytical distinctions whose interactions provide the framework's deepest insights. The matrix functions as a diagnostic instrument that directs intervention to the specific cell where regulation has failed, rather than treating self-regulation as an undifferentiated whole — but it must be used as a map of possibilities, not as a procedural checklist that would generate counterproductive metacognitive overhead.
> *— [[pintrich-self-regulation-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 2 Summary
> Three foundational frameworks provide the theoretical infrastructure for understanding PKB metacognitive scaffolding. Flavell's taxonomy identifies what the learner must know *about* cognition — person, task, and strategy knowledge — each of which maps to a scaffoldable function. The Nelson-Narens monitoring-control architecture reveals that effective self-regulation depends not on monitoring or control alone but on their tight coupling — a principle that becomes a generative design criterion for PKB templates. Zimmerman's cyclical model organises self-regulatory processes into three temporal phases (forethought, performance, self-reflection) connected by feedback loops, providing the structural grammar for all subsequent analysis of phase-specific scaffolds. Together, these frameworks establish that metacognitive scaffolding is not ad hoc tool use but principled architectural design grounded in well-established cognitive theory.

> [!section-summary] Section 2 Summary
> SDT's three basic psychological needs — autonomy (experienced volition), competence (experienced effectiveness), and relatedness (experienced connection) — function as universal nutriments for psychological growth. These are not preferences but requirements: their satisfaction enables the organismic integration tendency to operate, while their frustration produces predictable patterns of motivational dysfunction. Critically, need satisfaction and need frustration represent partially independent dimensions rather than opposite poles of a single continuum. The three needs form an interconnected ecological system in which the quality of motivation depends on the systemic pattern of satisfaction and frustration across all three.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!section-summary] Section 2 Summary
> The cognitive engine of self-directed learning is metacognitive self-regulation — the recursive monitoring-control loop through which learners assess their own understanding and adjust their strategies accordingly. This process follows the forethought-performance-reflection cycle but operates at significantly greater cognitive complexity than classroom self-regulation because the self-directed learner must also define the task, select resources, and generate evaluation criteria. Metacognitive calibration — the accuracy of self-assessment — is the structural vulnerability of SDL, because miscalibration can persist indefinitely without external correction.
> *— [[self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*
