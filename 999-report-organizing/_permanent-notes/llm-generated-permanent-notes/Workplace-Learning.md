## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Workplace-Learning is a form of experiential learning that occurs in real-world work environments. It involves both formal and informal processes where employees can apply theoretical knowledge to practical situations, leading to skill development and personal growth.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> This type of learning often includes mentoring, coaching, on-the-job training, and self-directed exploration of tasks and challenges encountered at work. The core mechanism is the transfer of tacit knowledge from experienced colleagues or through direct engagement with workplace tasks.
