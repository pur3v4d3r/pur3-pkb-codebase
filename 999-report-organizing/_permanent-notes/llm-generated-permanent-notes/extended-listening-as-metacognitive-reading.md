# Extended Listening as Metacognitive Reading

> [!definition] Extended Listening as Metacognitive Reading
> Extended Listening as Metacognitive Reading refers to the application of metacognitive strategies in listening comprehension that are adapted from reading skills but require modification due to medium-specific constraints.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Extended Listening as Metacognitive Reading
> The transferable principle is that the metacognitive architecture for skilled comprehension is medium-general at the architectural level even when the regulatory repertoire must be modified for the medium's constraints. The boundary condition is that the modifications matter substantially for what skilled practice looks like in the medium, and direct transfer of reading strategies to listening (or vice versa) without medium-appropriate modification will leave the architecture under-implemented.
