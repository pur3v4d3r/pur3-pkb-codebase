## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Knowledge of cognition is foundational in cognitive psychology as it pertains to metacognition, the ability to monitor and control one’s own cognitive activities. It involves recognizing when one understands a concept or task and being able to apply strategies effectively.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, knowledge of cognition can be applied through techniques such as self-explanation, where individuals explain their thought processes aloud or in writing to enhance understanding and retention. This is particularly useful in educational settings and AI-assisted reading systems that aim to improve comprehension and learning efficiency.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between procedural and declarative knowledge within metacognition. Procedural knowledge involves knowing how to perform tasks, while declarative knowledge includes knowing facts and information about one's cognitive processes.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
