# The Grain-Size Distinction Between SDL and SRL

> [!definition] The Grain-Size Distinction Between SDL and SRL
> The difference in the level of granularity at which self-directed learning and self-regulated learning operate.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Grain-Size Distinction Between SDL and SRL
> Self-regulated learning operates at the grain size of the learning task — how learners manage their cognitive, motivational, and behavioral processes within a defined learning episode. Self-directed learning operates at the grain size of the learning project — how learners manage the entire trajectory from need identification through goal formulation, resource selection, strategy design, and outcome evaluation. SRL is a necessary component of SDL but not a sufficient one; a learner may be an excellent self-regulator within structured tasks while lacking the capacity to initiate, design, and sustain an entire learning trajectory independently.
