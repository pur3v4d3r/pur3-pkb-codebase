# cyclical-model-of-self-regulated-learning

> [!definition] cyclical-model-of-self-regulated-learning
> - **Key-Term**: [[cyclical-model-of-self-regulated-learning]]
> - **Definition**: The cyclical model of self-regulated learning is a framework that describes the iterative process through which learners set goals, plan strategies, monitor their progress, and adjust their actions to achieve those goals effectively.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> This model emphasizes the dynamic nature of self-regulation in learning, where learners continuously evaluate their performance against their goals and adapt their approaches accordingly. It consists of several phases including forethought (planning), performance (execution), and self-reflection (evaluation).

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this involves students setting clear objectives, selecting appropriate study methods, implementing these strategies while monitoring their effectiveness, and reflecting on the outcomes to refine future learning plans.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the role of metacognition in guiding learners' decisions and the importance of feedback loops for continuous improvement.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> Educators can use this model to design instructional strategies that promote self-regulated learning by encouraging students to set specific, measurable goals and providing tools for reflection.

> [!example] Application
> Parents and tutors can apply these principles in home or tutoring settings to help learners develop effective study habits.
