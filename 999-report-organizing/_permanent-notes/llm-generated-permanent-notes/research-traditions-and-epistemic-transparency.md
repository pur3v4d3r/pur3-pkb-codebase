## Methodology & Sources

> [!methodology-and-sources] Research Traditions and Epistemic Transparency
> This report synthesises five primary intellectual traditions: (1) Heider's naive psychology and its extension into formal attribution theory by Jones, Kelley, and Weiner; (2) Weiner's dimensional-motivational framework, which constitutes the report's theoretical core; (3) the learned helplessness and explanatory style tradition (Abramson, Seligman, Teasdale); (4) implicit theories research (Dweck, Molden); and (5) self-determination theory's concept of perceived locus of causality (Deci, Ryan).
