# Procedural Information (van Merriënboer & Kirschner, 2018)

> [!definition] Procedural Information (van Merriënboer & Kirschner, 2018)
> Step-by-step instructions, rules, and procedures that specify exactly how to perform the recurrent (consistent, routine) aspects of a complex skill. Presented precisely when needed during task execution (just-in-time), faded as the learner automates the procedure through repeated practice.
>
> **Boundary:** Procedural information does not refer to all step-by-step content. It specifically addresses recurrent constituent skills — those that should be performed the same way regardless of context. Its defining temporal characteristic is just-in-time delivery, contrasting with the before-task-class timing of supportive information.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** Serves the schema automation mechanism, connecting to [[act-r-theory|ACT-R]] knowledge compilation and the broader automaticity literature.
>
> **See also:** [[Schema Automation]], [[automaticity]], [[guidance-fading-principle]]
