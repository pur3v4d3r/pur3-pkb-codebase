# SR Seed 7 — Process

> [!definition] SR Seed 7 — Process
> SR Seed 7 — Process refers to a curriculum design approach that addresses three dimensions of transfer—structural, motivational, and evaluative—to foster fully self-directed learners.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 7 — Process
> **Q:** What three dimensions of transfer must curriculum address to produce fully self-directed learners?
> **A:** (1) Structural transfer — from external scaffolding to internal self-regulation; (2) Motivational transfer — from external motivation to self-sustaining motivational ecology; (3) Evaluative transfer — from external assessment to accurate self-evaluation. Curriculum that addresses only one dimension produces an incomplete form of self-direction.
