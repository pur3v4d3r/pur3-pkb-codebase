# SR Seed 8 — Definition

> [!definition] SR Seed 8 — Definition
> SR Seed 8 refers to a specific aspect within Cognitive Evaluation Theory (CET) that assesses the psychological meaning of an external event based on its relative emphasis on pressure toward outcomes versus competence feedback, influencing intrinsic motivation.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 8 — Definition
> **Q:** What is the "functional significance" of an external event in CET?
> **A:** The psychological meaning of an event as determined by the relative salience of its controlling aspect (pressure toward outcomes) versus its informational aspect (competence feedback). Functional significance determines whether the event enhances or undermines intrinsic motivation.
