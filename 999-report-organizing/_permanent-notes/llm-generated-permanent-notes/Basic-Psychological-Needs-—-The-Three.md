# Basic-Psychological-Needs-—-The-Three

> [!definition] Basic-Psychological-Needs-—-The-Three
> - **Key-Term**: [[Basic-Psychological-Needs-—-The-Three]]
> - **Definition**: The Basic Psychological Needs refer to three fundamental requirements for human psychological well-being: autonomy, competence, and relatedness. These needs are essential for personal growth and motivation according to Self-Determination Theory (SDT).
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Autonomy involves the need to experience oneself as self-determined in one's actions, free from undue external control or pressure.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> Competence refers to the desire to be effective and skilled in what one does, feeling a sense of mastery over one’s environment.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Relatedness is the need for positive social connections and meaningful relationships with others.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In education, fostering these needs can lead to higher student engagement and better academic performance.

> [!example] Application
> In workplace settings, addressing these needs can enhance job satisfaction and productivity among employees.
