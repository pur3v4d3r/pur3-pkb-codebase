## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Regulating is a fundamental mechanism in self-regulated learning where individuals monitor their own understanding, adjust their strategies accordingly, and adapt their behaviors to achieve better academic or personal goals. This process involves setting goals, monitoring progress, and making necessary adjustments.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, regulating can be seen through various activities such as activating prior knowledge before engaging with new material, seeking help when needed, and calibrating one's metacognitive skills to accurately assess their own understanding and performance.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
