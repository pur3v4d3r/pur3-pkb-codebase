# The Retrieval Architecture Imperative

> [!definition] The Retrieval Architecture Imperative
> The Retrieval Architecture Imperative refers to the necessity for Personal Knowledge Management systems to include mechanisms such as spaced repetition or self-testing to ensure that learned information is retained over time.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Retrieval Architecture Imperative
> A PKM system that does not incorporate deliberate retrieval practice into its workflow architecture — through spaced repetition, self-testing, interleaved review, or equivalent mechanisms — is, regardless of the quality of its encoding and organizational features, systematically undermining the very learning it purports to support. The knowledge remains in the system; it fades from the learner.
> *— [[pkb-pkm-planning-and-cognitive-science-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*
