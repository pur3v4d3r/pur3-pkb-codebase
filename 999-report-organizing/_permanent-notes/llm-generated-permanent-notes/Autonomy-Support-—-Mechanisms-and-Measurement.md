## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Autonomy-support is a core concept within Self-Determination Theory (SDT), emphasizing the importance of creating an environment that respects and facilitates students' or employees' autonomy. This involves providing choices, encouraging self-regulation, and fostering a sense of competence and relatedness.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, autonomy-supportive teachers or managers provide clear goals but allow for individual choice in how to achieve them, offer reasons and explanations for tasks, and create a supportive climate that values personal growth and development.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between autonomy-support and control, where autonomy-support focuses on fostering self-determination rather than imposing external controls.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In education, autonomy-supportive teaching methods can lead to higher levels of intrinsic motivation and better academic performance among students.

> [!example] Application
> In organizational settings, autonomy-supportive leadership practices can enhance employee engagement and job satisfaction.
