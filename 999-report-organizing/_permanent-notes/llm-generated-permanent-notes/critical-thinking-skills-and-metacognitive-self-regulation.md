## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> This concept involves both the application of critical thinking skills—such as analysis, evaluation, and inference—to understand and solve problems—and metacognitive self-regulation—the ability to reflect on and control one’s own thought processes. Together, they enable individuals to effectively navigate complex situations.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this means that when faced with a problem or decision, an individual uses critical thinking skills to analyze the situation and generate potential solutions, while simultaneously using metacognitive self-regulation to monitor their thought process for biases, assumptions, and errors, and adjust as necessary. This dual approach enhances learning and performance.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between procedural knowledge (knowing how to do something) and declarative knowledge (understanding why a procedure works), which are both crucial components of effective critical thinking.
