# The Atomistic Fallacy

> [!definition] The Atomistic Fallacy
> The Atomistic Fallacy is the misconception that mastering individual sub-skills will automatically lead to proficiency in their integrated application, often leading to poor performance in real-world tasks.

## Practical Implications

> [!warning] The Atomistic Fallacy
> The most common error in designing training for complex professional competencies is the assumption that mastery of individual sub-skills guarantees mastery of their coordination. This assumption — seductive because it simplifies design — produces learners who perform well on component-skill assessments but poorly on authentic whole-task challenges. The 4C/ID model was developed specifically to counteract this tendency.
