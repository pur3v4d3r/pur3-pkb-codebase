## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Strategic reading involves a set of techniques and cognitive processes aimed at improving the efficiency and effectiveness of reading. It includes activities such as setting goals, predicting content, questioning, summarizing, and self-explanation during the reading process.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> These strategies are applied to actively engage with the text, making connections between new information and existing knowledge, which enhances understanding and retention. For example, activating prior knowledge helps readers make meaningful connections that deepen comprehension.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the importance of metacognitive awareness in monitoring one's own understanding and adjusting reading strategies accordingly. Sub-variants might include specific techniques like SQ3R (Survey, Question, Read, Recite, Review) or reciprocal teaching.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In education, strategic reading can lead to improved academic performance by enhancing comprehension and retention of material.
