# Connection: Attribution as the Interpretive Layer of Motivation

> [!definition] Connection: Attribution as the Interpretive Layer of Motivation
> Attribution functions as the interpretive layer by converting raw experiences into motivationally significant causal narratives, integrating concepts like self-efficacy, implicit theories, and perceived locus of causality within social-cognitive and motivational frameworks.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Connection: Attribution as the Interpretive Layer of Motivation
> **Q:** In what sense does attribution function as the "interpretive layer" connecting other motivational theories?
> **A:** Self-efficacy depends on attributional interpretation of mastery experiences (effort vs. ability attribution for success). Implicit theories constrain the stability dimension of attributions. SDT's perceived locus of causality is the attribution dimension itself. Attribution provides the cognitive mechanism through which raw experience is converted into motivationally significant causal narratives — the common plumbing through which different motivational constructs flow.
