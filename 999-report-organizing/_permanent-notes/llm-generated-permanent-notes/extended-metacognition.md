> [!definition] extended-metacognition
> - **Key-Term**: [[extended-metacognition]]
> - **Definition**: Extended metacognition refers to the process of continuously monitoring and adjusting one's own cognitive processes over an extended period, often involving self-reflection and strategic planning to improve learning outcomes or performance in various domains.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Extended metacognition involves a dynamic interplay between awareness of one’s current cognitive state and deliberate strategies for enhancing that state. It encompasses the ability to set goals, plan actions, monitor progress, and adjust methods as needed over time.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, extended metacognition can be seen in various settings such as academic learning, athletic training, or professional development, where individuals use self-assessment tools and feedback mechanisms to refine their approaches and achieve better results.
