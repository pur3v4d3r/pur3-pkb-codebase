## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Epistemic-virtues are essential for achieving reliable knowledge. They include traits such as open-mindedness, honesty, intellectual courage, and intellectual humility. These virtues help individuals avoid cognitive biases and make well-reasoned judgments.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, epistemic-virtues guide how we approach learning and inquiry. For instance, in educational settings, fostering these virtues can lead to more effective teaching and learning strategies that promote genuine understanding rather than mere rote memorization.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between intellectual virtues like wisdom and practical virtues like prudence. Intellectual virtues pertain specifically to reasoning and knowledge acquisition, while practical virtues relate to how one applies this knowledge in real-world situations.
