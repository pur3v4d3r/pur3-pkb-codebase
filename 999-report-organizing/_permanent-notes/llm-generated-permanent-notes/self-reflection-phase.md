> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> In the context of educational theory, particularly andragogy as proposed by Malcolm Knowles, the self-reflection-phase serves as a pivotal moment for learners to internalize new information and connect it with their existing knowledge. This phase encourages metacognition, enabling individuals to think about their thinking processes.
