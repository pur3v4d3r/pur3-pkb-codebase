# Prior Knowledge Bridge — Connecting to Your Existing Understanding

> [!definition] Prior Knowledge Bridge — Connecting to Your Existing Understanding
> A Prior Knowledge Bridge is a curriculum design approach that leverages and integrates an individual's existing understanding to foster self-directed learning, focusing on developing autonomy, competence, and relatedness within structured educational experiences.

## Additional Material

> [!schema-activation] Prior Knowledge Bridge — Connecting to Your Existing Understanding
> If one has already engaged with the foundational treatment of [[self-directed-learning|self-directed learning]] as a concept — the interplay between personal autonomy, readiness, and the institutional structures within which learning unfolds — then the present report builds directly upon that foundation by asking a more specific and operationally demanding question: *how should curricula be designed so that the capacity for self-direction is not merely assumed but actively developed?* The reader who has traced the mechanisms of [[self-regulated-learning|self-regulated learning]] through [[zimmerman-srl-model|Zimmerman's cyclical model]] will recognise how curriculum structure can function as the external architecture within which those internal regulatory cycles learn to operate independently. Similarly, the motivational landscape mapped by [[self-determination-theory|self-determination theory]] — the interplay of autonomy, competence, and relatedness — reappears here as a set of design constraints that curriculum must satisfy if it hopes to sustain voluntary learning across a lifetime. The guiding question that organises this report is therefore not whether self-directed lifelong learning is desirable — that question has been settled — but rather *what specific mechanisms of curriculum design transform a structured educational experience into a launching architecture for autonomous intellectual life?*
