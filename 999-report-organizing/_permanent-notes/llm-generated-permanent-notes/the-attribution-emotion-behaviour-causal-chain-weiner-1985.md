# The Attribution-Emotion-Behaviour Causal Chain (Weiner, 1985)

> [!definition] The Attribution-Emotion-Behaviour Causal Chain (Weiner, 1985)
> The Attribution-Emotion-Behaviour Causal Chain (Weiner, 1985) is a model describing how outcomes lead to emotional responses, which in turn influence causal attributions and motivate subsequent behaviors.

## Additional Material

> [!diagram] The Attribution-Emotion-Behaviour Causal Chain (Weiner, 1985)
> ```
> ┌─────────────┐
> │   OUTCOME   │ ──→ Outcome-Dependent Affect
> │ (Success or │      (happiness / sadness)
> │  Failure)   │
> └──────┬──────┘
>        │
>        ▼
> ┌─────────────────────────────┐
> │      CAUSAL SEARCH          │
> │  (Why did this happen?)     │
> │  Triggered by: unexpectedness,│
> │  importance, failure         │
> └──────────┬──────────────────┘
>            │
>            ▼
> ┌─────────────────────────────┐
> │   CAUSAL ATTRIBUTION        │
> │   (e.g., "I didn't try      │
> │    hard enough")             │
> └──────────┬──────────────────┘
>            │
>    ┌───────┼───────┐
>    ▼       ▼       ▼
> ┌──────┐┌──────┐┌──────────────┐
> │LOCUS ││STAB- ││CONTROLL-     │
> │      ││ILITY ││ABILITY       │
> │Int/  ││Stable/││Control/     │
> │Ext   ││Unstab ││Uncontrol    │
> └──┬───┘└──┬───┘└──────┬───────┘
>    │       │           │
>    ▼       ▼           ▼
> ┌──────┐┌──────┐┌──────────────┐
> │Self- ││Expect-││Moral-Social  │
> │Eval  ││ancy  ││Affects       │
> │Affect││Change ││              │
> │      ││      ││              │
> │Pride ││Hope/ ││Guilt/Shame   │
> │Shame ││Hope- ││Anger/Pity    │
> │      ││less- ││Gratitude     │
> │      ││ness  ││              │
> └──┬───┘└──┬───┘└──────┬───────┘
>    │       │           │
>    └───────┼───────────┘
>            │
>            ▼
> ┌─────────────────────────────┐
> │    MOTIVATED BEHAVIOUR      │
> │  (Approach / Avoidance /    │
> │   Persistence / Withdrawal) │
> └─────────────────────────────┘
> ```
