## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The COPES-model posits that individuals engage in a cyclical process of cognitive operations (C), operationalizing plans (O), evaluating outcomes (E), planning further actions (P), seeking external help when necessary (H), and stopping or continuing based on the situation (S).

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> This model highlights how students can actively monitor their understanding, seek appropriate assistance, and adjust their strategies to enhance learning efficiency.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the importance of metacognitive calibration, where individuals assess their own knowledge gaps and seek help accordingly.
