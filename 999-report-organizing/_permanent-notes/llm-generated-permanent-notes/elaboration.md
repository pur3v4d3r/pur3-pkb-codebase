# elaboration

> [!definition] elaboration
> - **Key-Term**: [[elaboration]]
> - **Definition**: Elaboration is the process of adding detail, examples, or explanations to an idea or concept to enhance understanding and retention.
> - **Domain**: learning-science

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Elaboration plays a crucial role in learning by connecting new information to existing knowledge. This strategy helps learners create more meaningful associations between concepts, making it easier to recall and apply the information later.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, elaboration can be applied through various methods such as storytelling, analogies, or providing real-world examples. For instance, when teaching a scientific concept like photosynthesis, an instructor might explain how plants convert sunlight into energy using chlorophyll, linking this process to everyday experiences with plants and sunlight.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the importance of personal relevance; elaborations that are closely tied to learners' existing knowledge or interests tend to be more effective. Additionally, there are different types of elaboration strategies, such as self-explanation (explaining one's own actions) and cross-modal elaboration (using multiple sensory channels).

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
