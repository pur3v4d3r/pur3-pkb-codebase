# The Calibration-Adaptation Engine

> [!definition] The Calibration-Adaptation Engine
> The Calibration-Adaptation Engine is a scaffolded feedback system that enhances metacognitive accuracy through iterative cycles of prediction-outcome comparison, self-assessment, and adaptive planning.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] The Calibration-Adaptation Engine
> The integration of prediction-outcome comparison, multidimensional self-assessment rubrics, and forward-looking adaptive planning prompts constitutes what this report terms the **Calibration-Adaptation Engine** — a scaffolded feedback system that progressively trains the learner's metacognitive accuracy while simultaneously generating actionable regulatory adjustments. The engine operates across cycles: each iteration produces calibration data that refines future predictions AND adaptive plans that improve future strategies. The compounding effect of these dual improvement streams — better predictions AND better strategies — means that a well-scaffolded PKB workflow becomes progressively more effective over time, not through additional content but through regulatory refinement. This is the fundamental mechanism by which metacognitive scaffolding transforms study from a series of isolated events into a genuine learning system.
