# mastery-orientation-vs-performance-orientation

> [!definition] mastery-orientation-vs-performance-orientation
> - **Key-Term**: [[mastery-orientation-vs-performance-orientation]]
> - **Definition**: Mastery orientation and performance orientation are two distinct motivational orientations that influence how students approach learning tasks, with mastery orientation focusing on the acquisition of knowledge and skills for their own sake, while performance orientation focuses on demonstrating competence relative to others.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Mastery orientation is characterized by a focus on personal improvement and understanding, where learners are motivated by the desire to master content and develop their abilities. In contrast, performance orientation is driven by the goal of outperforming others or achieving high grades, often leading to strategies that prioritize demonstrating knowledge over deep learning.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, mastery-oriented students tend to engage in deeper processing strategies such as elaboration and self-explanation, whereas performance-oriented students may rely more on surface-level strategies like rote memorization. These orientations can significantly impact academic achievement and long-term learning outcomes.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the role of feedback and assessment in reinforcing these orientations, with mastery orientation being more positively influenced by formative assessments that focus on improvement, while performance orientation is often reinforced by summative assessments that emphasize comparison to others.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> Instructors can promote a mastery orientation by designing assessments that encourage reflection and self-assessment rather than competition.

> [!example] Application
> Educational interventions aimed at shifting students from a performance orientation to a mastery orientation can lead to improved learning outcomes and reduced anxiety.

> [!example] Application
> A cautionary note is that while mastery orientation is generally beneficial, it may not always be the most effective approach in all contexts, such as when immediate feedback on relative performance is necessary.
