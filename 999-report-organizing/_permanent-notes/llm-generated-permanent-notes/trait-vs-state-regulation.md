# Trait vs State Regulation

> [!definition] Trait vs State Regulation
> Trait vs State Regulation refers to the distinction between a person's typical regulatory style (trait) across a behavioral domain and the regulatory style exhibited in a specific instance of behavior (state), which are correlated but considered distinct concepts.

## Open Threads

> [!tension-identified] Trait vs State Regulation
> The four-style typology can be measured as a trait (a person's typical regulatory style across a behavioral domain) or as a state (the regulatory style of a particular instance of behavior). The two are correlated but distinct, and predictions made at one level may not transfer cleanly to the other. The intensive-longitudinal frontier (Frontier Question 4) is partially about resolving this tension.
