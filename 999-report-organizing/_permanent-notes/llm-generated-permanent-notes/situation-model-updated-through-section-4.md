# Situation Model — Updated Through Section 4

> [!definition] Situation Model — Updated Through Section 4
> Situation Model — Updated Through Section 4 refers to a cognitive framework that elaborates on how emotions are classified based on three dimensions (locus, stability, controllability) after an outcome, with these classifications influencing behavior through expectancy changes and attribution-dependent emotions.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Situation Model — Updated Through Section 4
> **Key Entities:** All previous entities, plus: outcome-dependent vs attribution-dependent emotions; the three dimension-emotion mappings (locus → pride/shame, stability → hope/hopelessness, controllability → anger/pity/guilt/gratitude); the guilt-shame asymmetry; the temporal architecture of post-outcome affect.
> **Causal Map:** Triggering conditions → causal search → causal explanation → classified on three dimensions → [stability → expectancy change] AND [all three dimensions → attribution-dependent emotions] → expectancy + emotion jointly determine behaviour.
> **Structural Overview:** The core mechanism is now fully elaborated across four sections: triggering (1), classification (2), expectancy consequences (3), and emotional consequences (4). The remaining sections address distortions in the system (5), developmental and cultural variation (6), intervention (7), and integration with the broader motivational ecosystem (8).
> **Evolution This Section:** The model gains its emotional architecture. Emotion is not a byproduct but a mediating mechanism — the affective energy that translates dimensional classification into behavioural tendency. The temporal layering of post-outcome affect is an original synthesis.
> **Emerging Patterns:** The controllability dimension emerges as the bridge between causal explanation and moral judgment — a pattern with broad implications for social policy, clinical intervention, and interpersonal evaluation. The guilt-shame asymmetry is a design feature with direct intervention implications.
> **Open Threads:** The core mechanism is elegant, but is it accurate? Do people actually follow these dimensional rules, or are there systematic distortions? Section 5 examines the biases.
