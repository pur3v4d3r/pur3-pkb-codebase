# adaptive-learning

> [!definition] adaptive-learning
> - **Key-Term**: [[adaptive-learning]]
> - **Definition**: Adaptive learning is an educational approach that uses technology to tailor the learning experience to individual student needs, adjusting content and pace based on performance data in real-time.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Adaptive learning leverages algorithms and machine learning techniques to analyze a learner's performance and adapt the instructional materials accordingly. This ensures that each student receives personalized support and challenges, optimizing their learning path.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, adaptive learning systems can adjust the difficulty of questions, provide targeted feedback, or recommend additional resources based on the learner’s responses and progress. These systems often use data from previous interactions to predict what content will be most beneficial next.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the balance between customization and standardization, as well as the ethical considerations around data privacy and student autonomy.
