# Iyengar

> [!definition] Iyengar
> - **Key-Term**: [[Iyengar]]
> - **Definition**: Iyengar is a specific form of yoga that emphasizes precision and detail in the performance of asanas (postures) and pranayama (breathing exercises).

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Iyengar yoga was developed by B.K.S. Iyengar, who focused on the alignment and anatomical correctness of poses, often using props such as blocks, straps, and blankets to assist students in achieving proper form.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> The practice involves a detailed sequence of postures that are held for extended periods, allowing practitioners to focus on their breath and physical alignment. This method is particularly beneficial for those seeking a more structured and precise approach to yoga.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the use of props to aid in correct posture and alignment, as well as a strong emphasis on the therapeutic benefits of yoga.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
