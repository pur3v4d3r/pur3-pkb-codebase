# Basic Psychological Need (in SDT)

> [!definition] Basic Psychological Need (in SDT)
> A psychological condition whose satisfaction is necessary for ongoing psychological growth, integrity, and well-being, and whose frustration produces predictable patterns of motivational degradation, ill-being, and defensive functioning. Basic needs in SDT are conceptualized as **nutriments** — like vitamins, they are required for healthy functioning, their absence produces measurable deficits, and these deficits cannot be compensated for by surplus of the others. This is what distinguishes basic needs in SDT from drives (which seek tension reduction) and from preferences (which vary across persons and contexts).
