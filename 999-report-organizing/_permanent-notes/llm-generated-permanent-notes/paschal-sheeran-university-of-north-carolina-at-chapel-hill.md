# Paschal Sheeran (University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill)

> [!definition] Paschal Sheeran (University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill)
> Paschal Sheeran is a British-American social psychologist known for her influential 2006 meta-analysis on implementation intentions with Peter Gollwitzer, which established this construct as an evidence-based intervention in motivation science and volitional psychology.

## Additional Material

> [!person] Paschal Sheeran (University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill)
> The British-American social psychologist whose 2006 meta-analysis with Gollwitzer (94 studies, d=0.65) provided the most influential single empirical synthesis of implementation-intention effects, establishing the construct as an evidence-based intervention rather than a promising hypothesis. Sheeran's subsequent work has extended the meta-analytic record and explored boundary conditions across health-behavior domains.
