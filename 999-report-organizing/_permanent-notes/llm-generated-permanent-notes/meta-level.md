# meta-level

> [!definition] meta-level
> - **Key-Term**: [[meta-level]]
> - **Definition**: Meta-level refers to the level of awareness and reflection on one's own cognitive processes, often used in contexts such as metacognition, self-monitoring, and artificial intelligence systems where an entity is aware of its own thought processes and can reflect upon them.
> - **Domain**: other

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The meta-level concept is foundational in understanding how entities (such as humans or AI) are capable of reflecting on their cognitive processes. At the core, it involves awareness of one's own mental states and the ability to monitor and control these processes.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this can be seen in various applications such as self-regulated learning where students reflect on their understanding and adjust their study strategies accordingly, or in AI systems that monitor their performance and adapt their algorithms based on feedback.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between explicit and implicit meta-level awareness, with some theories emphasizing the importance of verbalizing thoughts for effective metacognition.
