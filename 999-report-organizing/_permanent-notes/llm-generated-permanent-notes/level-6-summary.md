# Level 6 Summary

> [!definition] Level 6 Summary
> Level 6 Summary represents a comprehensive review engaging with the current research frontier through five live questions and four expert debates, focusing on empirically mature applications and theoretical reorganization of a cognitive or psychological theory.

## Additional Material

> [!section-summary] Level 6 Summary
> At the previous level, the standard account was qualified by edge cases. At THIS level, the *current research frontier* has been engaged through five live questions and four expert debates, revealing CLT as a theory empirically mature in its applications and theoretically reorganizing in its foundations. The element-interactivity construct sits at the center of the reorganization, and the measurement, neural-correlates, and architectural-integration questions are the most consequential open problems. The next level will go beyond the current evidence into informed speculation about where the construct may go under pressure from predictive-processing accounts of cognition and from AI-mediated learning environments.
> *— [[cognitive-load-theory-element-interactivity-deep-dive-2026-04-20]]*

> [!section-summary] Level 6 Summary
> Five active frontier questions: neural correlates of regulatory style ([[Kenji Murayama|Murayama]] line), cross-cultural variability in need weighting and satisfying contents, internalization in algorithmically mediated environments, intra-person dynamics revealed by intensive longitudinal designs, and AI-mediated need support. Two expert debates: the completeness of the three-need list (with beneficence as the most credible candidate addition), and the separate-measurement of need frustration vs satisfaction. The frontier has a shape — most active questions concern the framework's extension into conditions outside its formative environment.
>
> Level 7 will move beyond the frontier into informed speculation about what internalization may mean in algorithmically mediated futures whose contours are not yet visible.
> *— [[self-determination-theory-internalization-continuum-deep-dive-2026-04-21]]*
