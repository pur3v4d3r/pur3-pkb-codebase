# SR Seed 4 — Connection

> [!definition] SR Seed 4 — Connection
> SR Seed 4 — Connection refers to the phenomenon where external rewards diminish intrinsic motivation by altering the perceived source of causality from internal to external factors.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 4 — Connection
> **Q:** How does Cognitive Evaluation Theory explain the undermining effect of external rewards?
> **A:** External rewards shift the perceived locus of causality from internal to external, reducing the experience of autonomy. When behavior that was previously felt as self-initiated becomes experienced as externally driven, intrinsic motivation declines.
