## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Fix-up strategies are integral to the process of making sense of complex or conflicting information. They involve actively engaging with the material by identifying inconsistencies and then adjusting one's mental model to incorporate new data in a coherent manner.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, fix-up strategies can be applied through various methods such as rereading sections of text, questioning assumptions, or seeking additional information from other sources.
