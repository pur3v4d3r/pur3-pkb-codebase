# Surface Markers vs Substrate Architecture

> [!definition] Surface Markers vs Substrate Architecture
> Surface markers refer to observable characteristics or behaviors indicative of a regulatory style, whereas substrate architecture denotes the underlying mechanisms or structures that may differ from these surface-level indicators, especially in complex configurations like controlled-internalization.

## Open Threads

> [!tension-identified] Surface Markers vs Substrate Architecture
> Self-report measurement instruments tap surface markers of regulatory style; the underlying regulatory architecture may differ from what the surface markers suggest, particularly in the controlled-internalization configuration of Edge Case 1. The tension is methodological as much as theoretical: developing measurement that probes substrate rather than surface is one of the field's most active methodological frontiers.
