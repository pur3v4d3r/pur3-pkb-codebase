# Self-Evaluation

> [!definition] Self-Evaluation
> Self-evaluation refers to an individual's assessment of their own abilities, behaviors, or characteristics, often in the context of personal growth and motivation.

> **Identified Limitations:**
> 1. The report relies on SDT's own empirical tradition for much of its evidence — a more critical treatment would draw more extensively on independent replications and critiques from outside the SDT research community.
> 2. Measurement instruments (Self-Regulation Questionnaire, Basic Psychological Need Satisfaction and Frustration Scale, Aspirations Index) receive mention but not detailed psychometric analysis.
> 3. The relationship between SDT and neuroscience research on motivation is touched on only briefly — the neural substrates of need satisfaction and autonomous motivation deserve more treatment.
> 4. Some important applied domains (sport psychology, military/first responder motivation, environmental behavior) receive less attention than education, health, and work.
