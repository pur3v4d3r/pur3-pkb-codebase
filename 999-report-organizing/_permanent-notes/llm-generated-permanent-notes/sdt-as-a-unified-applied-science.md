# SDT as a Unified Applied Science

> [!definition] SDT as a Unified Applied Science
> SDT as a Unified Applied Science refers to the application of Self-Determination Theory across various domains such as education, healthcare, work, sport, parenting, and digital environments, leveraging a consistent mechanism of need satisfaction that influences motivation quality and outcomes.

## Reflections

> [!claude-insight] SDT as a Unified Applied Science
> What the cross-domain evidence reveals is not merely that SDT "works" in multiple contexts but that it works through the *same mechanism* in every context. The mediational model — social environment → need satisfaction → motivation quality → outcomes — replicates with remarkable consistency across education, healthcare, work, sport, parenting, and digital environments. This consistency is not trivially expected. Many psychological theories that perform well in one domain fail to generalize because their mechanisms are domain-specific. SDT's mechanism — need satisfaction as the mediating process — is domain-general precisely because the needs themselves are. The practical implication is that a designer, teacher, manager, clinician, or parent who understands SDT's principles possesses a transferable framework that applies wherever human motivation is relevant.
