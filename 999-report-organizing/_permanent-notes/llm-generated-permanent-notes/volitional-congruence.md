# volitional-congruence

> [!definition] volitional-congruence
> - **Key-Term**: [[volitional-congruence]]
> - **Definition**: Volitional-congruence refers to the alignment between an individual's volition, or their autonomous and controlled commitments, and their actions or behaviors in pursuit of goals or objectives.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Volitional-congruence is a concept rooted in self-determination theory (SDT), which posits that individuals are motivated by both intrinsic and extrinsic factors. When these motivations align with an individual's values, interests, and goals, volitional-congruence is said to be present.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, volitional-congruence can be observed in various domains such as athletic performance, musical practice, health behavior change, and educational goal-setting. For instance, a musician who practices regularly because they enjoy the process (intrinsic motivation) rather than external rewards demonstrates high volitional-congruence.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between autonomous and controlled commitments. Autonomous commitments are aligned with personal values and interests, while controlled commitments may be driven by external pressures or rewards.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In athletic practice, athletes who experience volitional-congruence tend to perform better and maintain long-term commitment to their training regimens.

> [!example] Application
> In health behavior change, individuals with high volitional-congruence are more likely to adhere to healthy habits and sustain lifestyle changes over time.
