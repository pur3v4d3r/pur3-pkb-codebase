# Definition: Standard of Coherence

> [!definition] Definition: Standard of Coherence
> The standard of coherence is a criterion used to evaluate the comprehensibility of text, encompassing levels such as lexical meaning, sentence parsing, individual assertions, local discourse structure, and consistency with world knowledge.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Definition: Standard of Coherence
> **Q:** What is the standard of coherence in reading, and what are the levels Baker identified?
> **A:** The standard of coherence is the criterion against which a reader evaluates the coherence of their developing comprehension. Baker identified at least five levels: lexical (word meaning), syntactic (sentence parsing), propositional (assertions making sense individually), structural (local discourse coherence), and external (consistency with world knowledge and reading purpose).
