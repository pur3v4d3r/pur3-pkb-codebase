# Distinction: SDL vs. SRL

> [!definition] Distinction: SDL vs. SRL
> SDL operates at the grain size of the learning project, including tasks such as identifying what to learn, selecting resources, and constructing evaluation criteria, while SRL focuses on within-task regulation.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Distinction: SDL vs. SRL
> **Q:** What is the fundamental grain-size distinction between self-directed learning and self-regulated learning?
> **A:** SRL operates at the grain size of the learning task (within-task regulation), while SDL operates at the grain size of the learning project (including curricular self-design — identifying what to learn, selecting resources, constructing evaluation criteria).
