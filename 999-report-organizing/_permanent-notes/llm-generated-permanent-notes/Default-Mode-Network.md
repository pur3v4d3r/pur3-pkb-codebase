# Default-Mode-Network

> [!definition] Default-Mode-Network
> - **Key-Term**: [[Default-Mode-Network]]
> - **Definition**: The Default-Mode-Network is a set of brain regions that are active when the mind wanders and is not focused on the outside world, often associated with self-referential thinking and memory retrieval.
> - **Domain**: systems-thinking
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The Default-Mode-Network (DMN) is a network of brain regions that become more active during tasks requiring introspection or when an individual's attention is not directed toward the external environment. It includes areas such as the medial prefrontal cortex, posterior cingulate cortex, and angular gyrus.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> This network plays a crucial role in various cognitive processes, including autobiographical memory retrieval, self-reflection, and mind-wandering. When individuals are engaged in tasks that require focused attention, the DMN's activity typically decreases as other networks, such as those involved in perception and action, become more active.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the network's involvement in both positive and negative emotional states, its variability across different individuals, and its potential role in various neurological disorders.
