## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> In the context of self-regulation and goal-directed behavior, the forethought-phase is a critical component that precedes the actual execution phase. During this phase, individuals engage in cognitive processes such as setting specific objectives, devising step-by-step plans, and predicting possible obstacles.
