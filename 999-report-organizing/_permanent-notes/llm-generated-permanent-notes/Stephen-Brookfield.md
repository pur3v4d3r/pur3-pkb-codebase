## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Brookfield's theory emphasizes the importance of reflective practice among educators to foster deeper understanding and more effective teaching methods. He argues that teachers should engage in continuous self-reflection to improve their instructional strategies and better meet the needs of adult learners.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, Brookfield advocates for creating a learning environment where adults can critically examine their own beliefs and practices through structured reflection activities. This approach helps educators become more aware of their biases and assumptions, leading to more inclusive and effective teaching.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Brookfield's work is rooted in the belief that critical reflection is essential for personal and professional growth. He distinguishes between surface-level reflection and deeper, transformative reflection that can lead to significant changes in one's practice.
