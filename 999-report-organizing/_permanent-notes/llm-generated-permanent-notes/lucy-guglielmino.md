## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The core mechanism of Lucy Guglielmino involves aligning educational strategies with the principles of andragogy as proposed by Malcolm Knowles, emphasizing self-directed learning in adults. It integrates these principles with formative assessment practices to support learners' autonomy while providing necessary structure through scaffolding.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this framework is applied by educators who design assessments that not only evaluate but also facilitate learning progression. Scaffolding techniques are used to gradually reduce support as learners become more competent, fostering a sense of self-efficacy and independence in adult learners.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the balance between autonomy and structure, where too much autonomy can lead to learner frustration, while excessive structure may stifle creativity and initiative. The framework also emphasizes the importance of ongoing assessment that is both formative (supporting learning) and summative (evaluating outcomes).
