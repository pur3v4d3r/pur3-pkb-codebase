# Rabbit Hole: The 4C/ID model and complex learning

> [!definition] Rabbit Hole: The 4C/ID model and complex learning
> Rabbit Hole: The 4C/ID model is a curriculum-level instructional design approach that addresses element-interactivity management in complex learning, providing a framework for designing effective learning experiences based on cognitive load theory principles.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Rabbit Hole: The 4C/ID model and complex learning
> Van Merriënboer's Four-Component Instructional Design model (4C/ID) is the most worked-out attempt to apply CLT principles to the design of complex-learning curricula. Why follow it: 4C/ID treats element-interactivity management as a curriculum-level design problem rather than a lesson-level design problem, and the resulting framework illuminates aspects of CLT that lesson-level applications obscure. Time investment: a focused week. Where to start: van Merriënboer & Kirschner's *Ten Steps to Complex Learning*. See also: [[the-worked-example-effect]], [[guidance-fading-principle]].
