# Specialist Reflection — Level 2

> [!definition] Specialist Reflection — Level 2
> Specialist Reflection — Level 2 involves critically analyzing specific behaviors through the lens of self-determination theory's four regulatory styles, recognizing the reversibility of the autonomy-control continuum, and identifying instances where failure to distinguish these styles has impacted findings or recommendations.

## Reflections

> [!reflection] Specialist Reflection — Level 2
> *Before proceeding to Level 3:*
> - For a behavior you study or care about clinically: can you write down what each of the four regulatory styles would look like for that specific behavior in concrete terms, the way the dissertation example was made concrete?
> - The autonomous/controlled distinction is empirically powerful — but where, in your work, has the loss of the four-style resolution cost you something? Can you identify a specific finding or recommendation that would be different if the underlying styles were distinguished?
> - The continuum is reversible. What conditions in the contexts you study have you observed pushing behavior backward along the continuum, and what would the OIT framework predict about those conditions' effects?
