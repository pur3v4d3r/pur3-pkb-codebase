# Process: The Stability-Expectancy Mechanism

> [!definition] Process: The Stability-Expectancy Mechanism
> Process: The Stability-Expectancy Mechanism refers to how the perceived stability or instability of a cause influences changes in expectations about future outcomes.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Process: The Stability-Expectancy Mechanism
> **Q:** How does the stability dimension determine expectancy change after an outcome?
> **A:** Stable causes produce the expectation that the same outcome will recur in the future, maintaining the current expectancy level. Unstable causes break the causal continuity between past and future, allowing expectancy to shift — opening the possibility of different outcomes.
