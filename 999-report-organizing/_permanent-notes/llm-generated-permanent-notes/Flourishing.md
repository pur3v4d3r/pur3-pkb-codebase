# Flourishing

> [!definition] Flourishing
> - **Key-Term**: [[Flourishing]]
> - **Definition**: Flourishing refers to the state of thriving and experiencing well-being, characterized by positive emotions, engagement, meaning, and accomplishment in life.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Flourishing is rooted in the Self-Determination Theory (SDT) proposed by Edward Deci and Richard Ryan. It encompasses the fulfillment of basic psychological needs for autonomy, competence, and relatedness, leading to a sense of well-being and personal growth.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, flourishing involves engaging in activities that are meaningful and enjoyable, setting and achieving goals, and maintaining strong social connections. Individuals who flourish tend to report higher levels of life satisfaction and lower rates of mental health issues.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> There are different sub-variants of flourishing, such as psychological flourishing (focusing on internal states) versus environmental flourishing (focusing on external conditions). The universality vs. cultural specificity debate also highlights the extent to which flourishing is a universal human experience or varies across cultures.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In education, fostering environments that support autonomy, competence, and relatedness can lead to increased student engagement and academic success.

> [!example] Application
> In organizational settings, promoting conditions for employee flourishing can enhance job satisfaction, productivity, and retention.

> [!example] Application
> In therapy, interventions aimed at addressing unmet psychological needs can help individuals achieve a state of flourishing.
