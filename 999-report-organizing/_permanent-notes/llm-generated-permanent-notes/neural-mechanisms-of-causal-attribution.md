# Neural Mechanisms of Causal Attribution

> [!definition] Neural Mechanisms of Causal Attribution
> The neurological processes underlying the ability to attribute causes to events.

## Open Threads

> [!open-question] Neural Mechanisms of Causal Attribution
> The cognitive neuroscience of attribution is still relatively undeveloped. Preliminary evidence implicates the medial prefrontal cortex (self-referential processing for internal attributions), the temporoparietal junction (perspective-taking for actor-observer differences), and the anterior cingulate cortex (error detection that may trigger the causal search). A neuroscientifically grounded account of the three-dimensional classification process does not yet exist.
> *— [[attribution-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*
