# Community Development and Civic Education

> [!definition] Community Development and Civic Education
> Community Development and Civic Education involves designing programs that foster sustained civic engagement by cultivating interconnected motivational sources such as identity, social belonging, practical relevance, and competence, aiming to create self-sustaining communities.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Community Development and Civic Education
> Community development programmes that aim to build sustained civic engagement face the motivational ecology challenge in its most acute form: how to design programmes that cultivate self-sustaining civic participation beyond the initial period of facilitated community action. The parallel to lifelong learning curriculum design is structural: community development that remains dependent on external facilitation has not produced self-directing communities, just as curriculum that remains dependent on external instruction has not produced self-directing learners. The motivational ecology model suggests that sustainable civic engagement requires the cultivation of multiple, interconnected motivational sources — identity (as a community member), social belonging (through civic networks), practical relevance (visible impact on community conditions), and competence (the capacity to navigate civic processes effectively) — that together create a self-sustaining motivational system.
>
> **Boundary condition:** Civic engagement operates within political and economic power structures that may actively resist the development of community self-direction.
