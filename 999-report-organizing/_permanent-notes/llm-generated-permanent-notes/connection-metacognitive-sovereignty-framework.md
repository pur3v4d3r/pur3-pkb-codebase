# Connection: Metacognitive Sovereignty Framework

> [!definition] Connection: Metacognitive Sovereignty Framework
> Metacognitive Sovereignty Framework is a three-pillar model in adult education emphasizing metacognitive sovereignty (cognitive capacity for monitoring, evaluation, and regulation), motivational alignment (convergence of autonomy, self-efficacy, and internalized valuation), and contextual navigation (ability to manage environmental, social, and resource dimensions).

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Connection: Metacognitive Sovereignty Framework
> **Q:** What are the three pillars of the Metacognitive Sovereignty Framework proposed in this report, and why is each necessary?
> **A:** (1) Metacognitive sovereignty (cognitive capacity for monitoring, evaluation, regulation). (2) Motivational alignment (convergence of autonomy, self-efficacy, and internalized valuation). (3) Contextual navigation (ability to manage environmental, social, and resource dimensions). Weakness in any pillar constrains the others: skill without motivation → purposeless; motivation without skill → poorly calibrated; both without context → unable to execute.
