# Application: Attribution Retraining's Two-Stage Architecture

> [!definition] Application: Attribution Retraining's Two-Stage Architecture
> Application: Attribution Retraining's Two-Stage Architecture consists of Induction and Consolidation stages, where Induction introduces adaptive attribution patterns and Consolidation fosters internalized self-correction through practice and vocabulary expansion.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Application: Attribution Retraining's Two-Stage Architecture
> **Q:** What are the two stages of effective attribution retraining, and why are both necessary?
> **A:** Stage 1 (Induction) provides normative information and models to introduce adaptive attribution patterns. Stage 2 (Consolidation) builds capacity for spontaneous self-correction through practice, self-monitoring, and vocabulary expansion. Stage 1 alone produces fragile change; Stage 2 produces internalised, self-sustaining attributional habits.
