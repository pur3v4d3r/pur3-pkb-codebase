# organismic-integration-theory

> [!definition] organismic-integration-theory
> - **Key-Term**: [[organismic-integration-theory]]
> - **Definition**: Organismic-integration-theory is a psychological framework that emphasizes the integration of an individual's needs, goals, and experiences to promote personal growth and well-being.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> This theory posits that individuals strive for coherence in their lives by integrating various aspects such as biological drives, cognitive processes, and social interactions. The core mechanism involves aligning one’s actions with internal values and external opportunities to achieve a state of harmony or 'flow'.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this means that people seek to make decisions and engage in activities that are congruent with their self-concept and life goals, thereby enhancing their psychological well-being.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the idea that integration is not static but dynamic, evolving as individuals encounter new experiences and challenges. Sub-variants of this theory often focus on different aspects such as cognitive development or social relationships.
