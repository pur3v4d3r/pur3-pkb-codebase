# CLT Compliance Review for Multimedia Materials

> [!definition] CLT Compliance Review for Multimedia Materials
> CLT Compliance Review for Multimedia Materials involves ensuring that multimedia content adheres to cognitive load theory principles by integrating text and images spatially, removing redundant information, segmenting complex content, providing pre-training and worked examples, fading support appropriately, utilizing both auditory and visual channels effectively, evaluating decorative elements, and sequencing content from simple to complex.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] CLT Compliance Review for Multimedia Materials
> - [ ] Text and images are spatially integrated (not separated with cross-referencing required)
> - [ ] Redundant text removed from narrated animations
> - [ ] Complex content segmented into manageable portions
> - [ ] Pre-training for key concepts provided before complex instruction
> - [ ] Worked examples provided before problem-solving practice
> - [ ] Support faded as learner competence develops
> - [ ] Auditory and visual channels both utilized for complementary information
> - [ ] Decorative elements evaluated for potential distraction
> - [ ] Content sequenced from simple to complex (progressive element interactivity)
