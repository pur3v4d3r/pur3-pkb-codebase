## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Declarative knowledge encompasses both episodic (personal experiences) and semantic (general facts) memory. It involves storing and retrieving explicit information about the world, which can be consciously accessed and articulated.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, declarative knowledge is crucial for learning new information, recalling historical dates, or explaining scientific principles. It forms the basis of our ability to communicate and share knowledge with others.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> There are two main types: episodic (personal experiences) and semantic (general facts). Episodic memory involves remembering specific events, while semantic memory pertains to general world knowledge.

## Practical Implications
