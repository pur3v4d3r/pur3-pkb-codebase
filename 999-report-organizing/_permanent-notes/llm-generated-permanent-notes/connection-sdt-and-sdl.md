# Connection: SDT and SDL

> [!definition] Connection: SDT and SDL
> Connection: SDT and SDL refers to how Self-Determination Theory's needs for autonomy, competence, and relatedness support self-directed learning by providing motivational foundations such as volition, confidence, and social connection.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Connection: SDT and SDL
> **Q:** How do the three basic needs identified by Self-Determination Theory (autonomy, competence, relatedness) support self-directed learning?
> **A:** Autonomy provides the volitional foundation (learning from choice, not coercion). Competence (via self-efficacy) provides the confidence foundation (belief in ability to succeed). Relatedness provides the social foundation (connection to learning communities). Together they enable the motivational alignment that sustains SDL.
