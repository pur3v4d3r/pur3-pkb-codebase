# The Measurement Problem

> [!definition] The Measurement Problem
> The Measurement Problem in self-directed learning refers to the challenge of quantifying a process-based, context-dependent, and domain-specific construct when existing instruments often measure attitudes rather than behavior and treat self-direction as a stable trait.

## Open Threads

> [!open-question] The Measurement Problem
> How should self-directed learning be measured when the construct is process-based, context-dependent, and domain-specific? The dominant instrument (SDLRS) has been critiqued for measuring attitudes rather than behavior and for treating self-direction as a stable trait. No successor instrument has achieved widespread adoption.
