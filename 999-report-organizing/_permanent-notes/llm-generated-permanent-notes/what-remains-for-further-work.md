# What Remains for Further Work

> [!definition] What Remains for Further Work
> What Remains for Further Work refers to unresolved questions and areas requiring additional empirical and theoretical investigation in cognitive science, particularly concerning the application and integration of metacognitive architectures in different contexts.

## Reflections

> [!claude-insight] What Remains for Further Work
> The frontier questions this report has touched but not resolved — how the architecture reconfigures in disciplinary reading, how it operates in digital environments, how AI assistance integrates with or substitutes for it, how PKB practices extend it — are the questions that will most repay sustained empirical and theoretical attention in the coming years. The architecture as developed here provides a stable framework within which those questions can be precisely formulated; what it does not provide is the answers, which await the research that will come.
> *— [[metacognition-and-reading-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*
