# Learning Contract (Knowles)

> [!definition] Learning Contract (Knowles)
> A negotiated agreement between learner and facilitator that specifies learning objectives, strategies, resources, evidence of accomplishment, and evaluation criteria, all co-determined by the learner. The [[learning-contracts|learning contract]] functions as a curriculum mechanism for developing self-regulatory capacity through the process of negotiation and self-directed planning.
>
> **Boundary:** Learning contracts are most effective during the Negotiated Autonomy phase of the Scaffolding-Sovereignty Progression. Deployed before the learner has sufficient metacognitive capacity, they produce superficial or derivative goals.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** Among the first curriculum tools explicitly designed to transfer ownership of the learning process from institution to individual.
>
> **See also:** [[learning-contracts]], [[malcolm-knowles]], [[andragogy]], [[zimmerman-srl-model]]
