# Three Needs vs More Needs

> [!definition] Three Needs vs More Needs
> Three Needs vs More Needs refers to the debate between the traditional self-determination theory framework, which posits three fundamental psychological needs (autonomy, competence, and relatedness), and its extension by including additional needs like beneficence as potentially necessary for comprehensive human motivation.

## Open Threads

> [!tension-identified] Three Needs vs More Needs
> The mainstream three-need position is parsimonious and empirically supported but is contested by candidate additions, particularly beneficence. Resolution depends on whether candidate needs do specific developmental work in the mechanism that the existing three do not already cover, a question that the empirical literature is actively investigating.
