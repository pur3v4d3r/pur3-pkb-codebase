# Passive Storage vs. Active Cognitive Engagement in PKB Design

> [!definition] Passive Storage vs. Active Cognitive Engagement in PKB Design
> Passive Storage vs. Active Cognitive Engagement in PKB Design refers to the trade-off between efficiently capturing and storing information without cognitive processing versus deeply integrating and elaborating on knowledge during its capture for better learning outcomes.

## Open Threads

> [!tension-identified] Passive Storage vs. Active Cognitive Engagement in PKB Design
> **The tension:** The practical demands of PKB operation — capturing information quickly, maintaining organizational consistency, processing large volumes of material — pull toward efficiency and automation. The cognitive science evidence reviewed throughout this report pulls toward effortful, time-consuming, deliberate processing that resists automation.
>
> **Position A (Efficiency):** PKB value scales with the volume and accessibility of captured knowledge. Rapid capture, automated categorization, and frictionless retrieval maximize the system's utility as an information resource.
>
> **Position B (Cognitive Engagement):** PKB value scales with the depth of processing applied during knowledge integration. Every step that reduces cognitive effort during processing risks reducing the elaborative encoding, retrieval practice, and schema construction that produce genuine understanding.
>
> **Current state of evidence:** The evidence strongly favors cognitive engagement for learning outcomes, but acknowledges that not all captured information requires deep processing. The resolution lies in distinguishing between capture (where efficiency is appropriate) and integration (where cognitive engagement is essential).
>
> **Why it matters:** This tension defines the most fundamental design choice in PKB architecture — the trade-off between system throughput and learning depth.
>
> **This report's stance:** The cognitive offloading resolution proposed in Section 7: offload system management functions, maintain cognitive engagement with knowledge content.
