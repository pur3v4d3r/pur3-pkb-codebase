# Perkins-and-Salomon

> [!definition] Perkins-and-Salomon

> - **Definition**: The Perkins and Salomon framework refers to the collaborative learning approach where students work together, often using technology, to enhance their understanding of complex material through shared problem-solving and knowledge construction.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> In this framework, students engage in joint activities that require them to articulate their thoughts, challenge each other's ideas, and build upon collective insights. This process helps activate prior knowledge and deepen comprehension.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> The core mechanism involves the use of scaffolding techniques where more knowledgeable peers or teachers provide support until learners can work independently. This approach is particularly effective for complex tasks such as reading and problem-solving.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the importance of social interaction, the role of technology in facilitating collaboration, and the need for structured activities to ensure productive learning.
