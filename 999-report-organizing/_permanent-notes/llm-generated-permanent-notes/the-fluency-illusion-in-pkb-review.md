# The Fluency Illusion in PKB Review

> [!definition] The Fluency Illusion in PKB Review
> The Fluency Illusion in PKB Review refers to the misconception that passive re-reading of previously created notes generates genuine understanding, leading learners to overestimate their knowledge and discouraging further effortful study.

## Practical Implications

> [!warning] The Fluency Illusion in PKB Review
> The common PKB review practice of browsing through previously created notes — scanning content, recognizing key terms, experiencing a sense of familiarity — constitutes precisely the kind of [[the-fluency-trap|fluency-generating activity]] that produces the illusion of knowledge without the substance. [[the-fluency-illusion|Fluency illusion research]] demonstrates that such recognition-based review leads learners to systematically overestimate their understanding, creating a dangerous gap between perceived and actual competence. PKB review systems that rely on passive re-reading are not merely inefficient; they are actively misleading, producing false confidence that discourages the effortful retrieval activities that would produce genuine learning.
