# organizational-culture

> [!definition] organizational-culture
> - **Key-Term**: [[organizational-culture]]
> - **Definition**: Organizational culture refers to the shared values, beliefs, behaviors, and practices that characterize an organization and influence the way its members think and act.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Organizational culture is shaped by a variety of factors including leadership styles, company history, industry norms, and employee interactions. It serves as a guiding force for employees, influencing their attitudes, behaviors, and overall commitment to the organization's goals.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, organizational culture can be assessed through various methods such as surveys, interviews, and observations. Leaders play a crucial role in shaping and maintaining this culture by setting examples and reinforcing desired behaviors.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between strong and weak cultures, where strong cultures are more cohesive and aligned with the organization's mission, while weak cultures may lack clear direction or shared values.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> A positive organizational culture can enhance employee satisfaction, productivity, and retention.

> [!example] Application
> Organizational culture also impacts decision-making processes and innovation within an organization.
