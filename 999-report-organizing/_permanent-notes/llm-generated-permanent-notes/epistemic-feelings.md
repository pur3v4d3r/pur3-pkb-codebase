> [!definition] epistemic-feelings
> - **Key-Term**: [[epistemic-feelings]]
> - **Definition**: Epistemic feelings are subjective experiences that reflect one's confidence, certainty, or intuition about the truth of a belief or the correctness of a decision in an epistemic context.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Epistemic feelings play a crucial role in metacognition and decision-making processes. They help individuals gauge their own knowledge and understanding, influencing when to seek further information or rely on existing beliefs.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> These feelings can be influenced by various factors such as prior knowledge, experience, and the complexity of the task at hand. For instance, in problem-solving tasks, a strong feeling of certainty might prompt an individual to confidently assert a solution without further verification.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between confidence and accuracy; individuals may feel very confident about their beliefs even when they are incorrect.
