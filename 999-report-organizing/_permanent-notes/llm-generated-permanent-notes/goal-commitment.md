## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Goal-commitment is a psychological construct that integrates elements of motivation and perseverance. It involves not only setting a clear objective but also maintaining the drive and effort necessary to achieve it despite challenges or setbacks.

> In practice, goal-commitment can be observed in various domains such as athletic training, musical performance, academic pursuits, and health behavior changes.
