# Gabriele Oettingen (NYU / University of Hamburg)

> [!definition] Gabriele Oettingen (NYU / University of Hamburg)
> Gabriele Oettingen is a researcher at New York University and the University of Hamburg known for developing mental contrasting and co-developing the MCII compound intervention in motivation science and volitional psychology.

## Additional Material

> [!person] Gabriele Oettingen (NYU / University of Hamburg)
> The developer of mental contrasting and the principal collaborator on the MCII compound intervention. Oettingen's research program on positive future thinking, expectancy-dependent commitment, and the failure modes of unstructured fantasizing complements Gollwitzer's action-control program and produces the most evidence-supported applied form of implementation-intention practice.
