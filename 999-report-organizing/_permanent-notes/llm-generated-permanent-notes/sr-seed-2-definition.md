# SR Seed 2 — Definition

> [!definition] SR Seed 2 — Definition
> SR Seed 2 refers to a foundational element in curriculum design that emphasizes self-directed learning frameworks and metacognitive calibration for enhancing learner autonomy and performance alignment.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 2 — Definition
> **Q:** What distinguishes andragogy from heutagogy?
> **A:** Andragogy (Knowles) concerns self-directed learning where the learner controls *how* they learn within an institutionally defined framework. Heutagogy (Hase & Kenyon) extends this to self-determined learning where the learner also determines *what* they learn and *by what criteria* learning is evaluated.
> **Source:** Sections 2, 4
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate
> **Tags:** #andragogy #heutagogy #SDL
> *— [[curriculum-design-for-lifelong-learning-and-self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 2 — Definition
> **Q:** What is metacognitive calibration?
> **A:** The degree of alignment between a learner's subjective confidence in their knowledge and their actual, objectively measured performance. Well-calibrated learners accurately predict their performance; poorly calibrated learners are systematically overconfident or underconfident.
> **Source:** Section 6.2, Appendix A.1
> **Difficulty:** Basic
