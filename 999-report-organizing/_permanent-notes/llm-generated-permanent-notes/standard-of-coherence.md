# standard-of-coherence

> [!definition] standard-of-coherence
> - **Key-Term**: [[standard-of-coherence]]
> - **Definition**: Standard-of-coherence refers to the degree to which a set of beliefs, theories, or pieces of evidence are logically consistent and well-integrated within a given framework or system of knowledge.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The standard-of-coherence is a measure used in various fields such as cognitive psychology, philosophy, and epistemology to evaluate the consistency and integration of different beliefs or theories. It assesses how well these elements fit together without contradictions.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, coherence can be evaluated through logical analysis, empirical testing, and critical reasoning. For instance, in AI-assisted reading, it helps ensure that the information presented is logically consistent with existing knowledge.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the balance between coherence and simplicity; overly complex theories might be more coherent but less practical or understandable.
