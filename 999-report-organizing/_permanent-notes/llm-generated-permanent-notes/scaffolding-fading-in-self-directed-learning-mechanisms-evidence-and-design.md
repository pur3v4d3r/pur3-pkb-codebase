# Scaffolding Fading in Self-Directed Learning: Mechanisms, Evidence, and Design

> [!definition] Scaffolding Fading in Self-Directed Learning: Mechanisms, Evidence, and Design
> Scaffolding Fading in Self-Directed Learning refers to the gradual reduction of instructional support in self-directed learning environments, guided by mechanisms for detecting learner readiness, with a focus on empirical evidence for optimal fading schedules and design patterns for scaffold libraries.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Scaffolding Fading in Self-Directed Learning: Mechanisms, Evidence, and Design
> **Description:** A comprehensive review of the scaffolded fading literature with specific attention to self-directed (non-instructor-mediated) contexts. Examines detection mechanisms for fading readiness, empirical evidence for optimal fading schedules, and design patterns for scaffold libraries with graded support levels.
