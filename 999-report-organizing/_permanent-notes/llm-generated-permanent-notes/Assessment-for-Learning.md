# Assessment-for-Learning

> [!definition] Assessment-for-Learning
> - **Key-Term**: [[Assessment-for-Learning]]
> - **Definition**: Assessment-for-Learning is an educational practice where assessment is used as a tool for ongoing feedback and reflection, enabling students to understand their learning progress and improve their performance.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Assessment-for-Learning (AfL) is rooted in the belief that formative assessments should be integrated into teaching and learning processes. It involves teachers providing regular feedback to students about their understanding of the subject matter, helping them identify strengths and weaknesses, and guiding them towards improvement.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, AfL can take various forms such as self-assessment, peer assessment, and teacher-led discussions. These methods are designed to be ongoing and supportive rather than evaluative, fostering a growth mindset among students. Teachers use this information to adjust their teaching strategies and provide targeted support where needed.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the emphasis on student engagement and ownership of learning. AfL encourages students to take an active role in their education by setting personal goals, reflecting on their progress, and making adjustments based on feedback.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> Teachers can use AfL to tailor instruction to meet individual student needs more effectively.

> [!example] Application
> Students develop better self-regulation skills as they learn to monitor and manage their own learning processes.
