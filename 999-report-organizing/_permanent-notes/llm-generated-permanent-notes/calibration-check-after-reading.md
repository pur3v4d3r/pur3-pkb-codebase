# Calibration Check After Reading

> [!definition] Calibration Check After Reading
> A Calibration Check After Reading involves evaluating one's comprehension through specific tasks such as summarizing arguments, identifying evidence and objections, and connecting new material to existing knowledge, to identify gaps that may not have been revealed by initial reading alone.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Calibration Check After Reading
> Use this checklist when you have finished a reading session and want to assess whether your sense of comprehension reflects actual comprehension.
> - [ ] Can you summarize the central argument in 2–3 sentences without consulting the text?
> - [ ] Can you identify the strongest evidence the author offers and the strongest objection to it?
> - [ ] Can you explain why the author's chosen approach is preferable to a salient alternative?
> - [ ] Can you state how the material connects to two other things you already understood?
> - [ ] Can you formulate two questions about the material whose answers are not in the text?
> - [ ] If you were teaching this material to someone, what would you do first, and why?
> - [ ] What part of your sense of comprehension would not survive the explanation test?
>
> Items you cannot complete identify gaps that monitoring alone did not flag. Return to the text for those sections.
