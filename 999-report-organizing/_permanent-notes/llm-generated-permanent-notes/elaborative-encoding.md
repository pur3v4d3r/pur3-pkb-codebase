# elaborative-encoding

> [!definition] elaborative-encoding
> - **Key-Term**: [[elaborative-encoding]]
> - **Definition**: Elaborative encoding is the process of enriching and organizing information by adding meaningful associations, such as examples, explanations, and relationships to existing knowledge, thereby enhancing memory retention and understanding.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Elaborative encoding involves connecting new information with what one already knows. This technique helps in creating a richer mental representation of the material being learned.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, elaborative encoding can be applied through methods such as summarizing, questioning, and relating new information to personal experiences or existing knowledge structures.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the importance of depth over breadth; more detailed connections lead to better retention. Sub-variants might include semantic mapping and mnemonic devices.
