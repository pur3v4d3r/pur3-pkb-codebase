# Metacognitive Monitoring in Self-Directed Learning

> [!definition] Metacognitive Monitoring in Self-Directed Learning
> Metacognitive monitoring refers to the learner's ongoing assessment of their own cognitive states — their current level of understanding, the accuracy of their knowledge, the effectiveness of their current learning strategy, and the distance between their present comprehension and their learning goal. In self-directed learning contexts, metacognitive monitoring replaces the feedback loop that formal assessment typically provides, requiring the learner to generate internal signals of comprehension or confusion that would otherwise come from instructor evaluation.
>
> **Boundary:** Metacognitive monitoring is not the same as simple self-awareness. It is a specific cognitive process that generates judgments of learning, feelings of knowing, and confidence calibrations that the learner then uses to make regulatory decisions.
>
