> [!definition] Zimmerman
> - **Key-Term**: [[Zimmerman]]

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Zimmerman's theory posits that effective learners engage in a cyclical process of planning, performing, and evaluating their work. This involves setting goals, monitoring progress, and adjusting strategies as needed.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this means students are encouraged to take an active role in their learning by identifying their strengths and weaknesses, seeking out resources, and reflecting on their performance. Teachers facilitate this by providing feedback and fostering a supportive environment.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between autonomous and controlled commitment, where autonomous commitment involves intrinsic motivation and self-regulation, while controlled commitment relies more on external rewards.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
