# Explanatory Style / Attributional Style (Abramson, Seligman, & Teasdale, 1978)

> [!definition] Explanatory Style / Attributional Style (Abramson, Seligman, & Teasdale, 1978)
> A habitual pattern of generating causal attributions along the dimensions of internality, stability, and globality. Pessimistic explanatory style (internal-stable-global for failure) is associated with depression and learned helplessness; optimistic explanatory style (external-unstable-specific for failure) is associated with resilience and persistence.
>
> **Boundary:** Explanatory style is a trait-like tendency, not a state. It describes the habitual direction of attributional processing, not a specific attribution for a specific event.
>
> **See also:** [[Explanatory-Style]], [[Attribution (Heider, 1958)]], [[learned-helplessness]]
