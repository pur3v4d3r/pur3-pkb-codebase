# Scaffolding-Fading-in-Self-Directed-Learning

> [!definition] Scaffolding-Fading-in-Self-Directed-Learning
> - **Key-Term**: [[Scaffolding-Fading-in-Self-Directed-Learning]]
> - **Definition**: A pedagogical approach where initial support is gradually removed to encourage learners to take increasing responsibility for their own learning, fostering self-directedness and independence.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Scaffolding-Fading-in-Self-Directed-Learning involves providing structured guidance or 'scaffolds' that are progressively reduced as the learner gains confidence and competence. This process helps students internalize knowledge and skills, eventually enabling them to learn independently.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this method is applied by educators who start with direct instruction, then transition to guided discovery, and finally encourage independent problem-solving. The scaffolding can be in the form of prompts, questions, or resources that are tailored to the learner's needs and gradually withdrawn as they become more proficient.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the importance of timing and appropriateness of fading; too early or too late can hinder learning. Sub-variants might involve different types of scaffolds (e.g., cognitive, affective) and varying rates of fading based on individual differences.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
