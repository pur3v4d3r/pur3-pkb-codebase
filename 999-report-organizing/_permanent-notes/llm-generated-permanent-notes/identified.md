# identified

> [!definition] identified
> - **Key-Term**: [[identified]]
> - **Definition**: Identified refers to the process by which an individual recognizes and accepts external expectations, norms, or values as their own, integrating them into their self-concept and guiding their behavior accordingly.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> In the context of Self-Determination Theory (SDT), identified regulation is one of four types of motivation. It involves aligning with an external goal because it is seen as personally meaningful or important, rather than just complying out of obligation.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> Individuals who are identifiedly motivated tend to engage in behaviors that align with societal expectations or personal values they have internalized, even when these do not directly benefit them individually.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> This concept is rooted in the idea that people seek to fulfill their basic psychological needs for autonomy, competence, and relatedness.
