# SR Seed 5 — Process

> [!definition] SR Seed 5 — Process
> SR Seed 5 — Process refers to a series of mechanisms within personal knowledge base (PKB) note-making that scaffold comprehension monitoring, including articulation, structured annotation, and linking.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] SR Seed 5 — Process
> **Q:** What three mechanisms allow PKB note-making to scaffold comprehension monitoring?
> **A:** (1) Articulation — writing in one's own words reveals gaps that passive reading conceals; (2) Structured annotation — classifying content with callouts forces paragraph-level comprehension judgments; (3) Linking — connecting new notes to existing notes tests integration and reveals isolation.
