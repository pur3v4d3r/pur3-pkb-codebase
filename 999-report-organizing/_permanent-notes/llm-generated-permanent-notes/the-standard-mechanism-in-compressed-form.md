# The Standard Mechanism in Compressed Form

> [!definition] The Standard Mechanism in Compressed Form
> The Standard Mechanism in Compressed Form represents a simplified model illustrating how external environmental factors support or thwart three basic psychological needs (functional significance), leading to need satisfaction or frustration, which in turn influences regulatory styles and behavioral patterns.
