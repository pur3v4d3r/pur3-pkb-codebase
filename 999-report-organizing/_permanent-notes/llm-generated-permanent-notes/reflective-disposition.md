## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Reflective-disposition is foundational in cognitive psychology and decision-making theory. It involves a conscious effort to analyze situations thoroughly before making decisions or forming opinions.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, people with a reflective-disposition are more likely to consider multiple perspectives, gather information, and weigh evidence before acting or deciding. This approach contrasts with heuristic-based thinking, which often relies on mental shortcuts or heuristics.
