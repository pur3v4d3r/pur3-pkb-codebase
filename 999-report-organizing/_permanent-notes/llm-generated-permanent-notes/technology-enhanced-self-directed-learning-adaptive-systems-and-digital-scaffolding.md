# Technology-Enhanced Self-Directed Learning: Adaptive Systems and Digital Scaffolding

> [!definition] Technology-Enhanced Self-Directed Learning: Adaptive Systems and Digital Scaffolding
> Self-directed learning enhanced by technology, including adaptive systems and digital scaffolding.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Technology-Enhanced Self-Directed Learning: Adaptive Systems and Digital Scaffolding
> **Description:** A comprehensive investigation of how intelligent tutoring systems, learning analytics, adaptive learning platforms, and open educational resources reshape the possibilities for scaffolding-fading in self-directed learning curriculum design — including both the opportunities (personalised adaptive scaffolding at scale) and the risks (filter bubbles, passive consumption, algorithmic dependency).
> **Connection to this report:** Section 8 identified digital technology as a rapidly evolving frontier that this report could only gesture toward. The Scaffolding-Sovereignty Progression's requirement for individually calibrated scaffolding-fading may be most achievable through adaptive technology.
