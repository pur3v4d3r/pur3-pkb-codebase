# relatedness

> [!definition] relatedness
> - **Key-Term**: [[relatedness]]
> - **Definition**: Relatedness refers to the perception of connection and support from others, which is crucial for psychological well-being and motivation according to Self-Determination Theory (SDT).

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> In SDT, relatedness is one of three basic psychological needs that individuals strive to fulfill. It involves feeling valued, cared for, and connected to significant people in one's life.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> This need is met through positive social interactions and relationships, which can enhance motivation by providing a sense of belonging and support.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> The concept of relatedness has been explored across various cultures, showing both universality and cultural specificity.
