## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Pressley's research focuses on understanding how readers use strategies to comprehend text and how these strategies can be improved through instruction. He emphasizes the importance of activating prior knowledge and engaging in self-explanation as key components of effective reading comprehension.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, Pressley's theories have been applied in educational settings to develop instructional methods that enhance students' ability to understand and retain information from texts by encouraging them to explain their thoughts and connections to existing knowledge.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between calibration (the accuracy of one's metacognitive judgments) and sensitivity (the ability to detect differences in text difficulty), which Pressley has explored through various experimental designs.
