# Definition: Grow's SSDL Model

> [!definition] Definition: Grow's SSDL Model
> Grow's SSDL Model is a four-stage framework for self-directed learning that categorizes learners into Dependent, Interested, Involved, and Self-Directed stages based on their level of autonomy and guidance needs.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Definition: Grow's SSDL Model
> **Q:** What are the four stages of Grow's Staged Self-Directed Learning model?
> **A:** Stage 1: Dependent (needs authority/coach). Stage 2: Interested (responds to motivator/guide). Stage 3: Involved (benefits from facilitator). Stage 4: Self-Directed (uses consultant/delegator). Critical insight: stage-teaching style mismatches produce dysfunction.
