> **Identified Limitations:**
> 1. **Cross-cultural depth:** The report acknowledges but does not deeply explore SDL research from non-Western traditions. Collectivist conceptions of self-direction are noted but not given the detailed treatment they deserve.
> 2. **Measurement instrument coverage:** Beyond the SDLRS, alternative instruments (Oddi's CLI, Fisher's scale, Williamson's multidimensional instrument) are mentioned but not analyzed in detail.
> 3. **Digital/AI-augmented SDL:** This rapidly evolving dimension receives conceptual treatment but lacks the empirical grounding available for more established aspects of SDL theory.
> 4. **Neuroscientific substrates:** The cognitive architecture of metacognition is discussed at the psychological level but not grounded in neuroscience (prefrontal cortex function, executive network dynamics).
> 5. **Original contributions require validation:** The Metacognitive Sovereignty Framework and SDL-SRL Grain-Size Continuum are integrative proposals, not empirically validated models.
>
> **Recommendations for Future Revision:**
> 1. Add dedicated subsection on cross-cultural SDL research, particularly from East Asian and indigenous learning traditions.
> 2. Expand treatment of SDL measurement beyond SDLRS to include detailed analysis of alternative instruments and their psychometric properties.
> 3. As AI-augmented learning research matures, update Section 7 with empirical findings about how AI tools affect SDL processes.
> 4. Consider adding neuroscientific grounding for the metacognitive mechanisms described in Section 2.
