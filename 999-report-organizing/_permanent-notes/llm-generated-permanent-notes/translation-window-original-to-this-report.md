# Translation Window** *(original to this report)*

> [!definition] Translation Window** *(original to this report)*
> The region of conditions within which the activities that produce goal achievement coincide with the activities one actually wants to be reinforced; defined operationally by the four conditions of proxy fidelity, honest attainability, measurement adequacy, and failure tolerance.
>
> **Boundary:** Width is a property of the joint goal-measurement-reward-ability-strategy system rather than of the goal alone; engineering practices that preserve the window are precisely those the dark-side literature recommends.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** Provides a unified diagnostic for distinguishing applications likely to produce benefits from those likely to produce pathology; offered as a well-motivated integrative synthesis rather than a validated empirical construct.
>
> **See also:** [[Goal Commitment]], [[self-efficacy]]
