## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> In the context of cognitive psychology, a schema-hierarchy refers to a structured organization of mental frameworks (schemas) that are used to interpret and process information. At its core, this hierarchy allows individuals to categorize and integrate new experiences with existing knowledge, thereby enhancing memory and comprehension.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> The lower levels of the hierarchy typically consist of basic schemas related to fundamental concepts or objects, while higher levels incorporate more complex schemas that represent broader categories or principles. This hierarchical organization enables efficient information processing by allowing quick access to relevant schemas when encountering new stimuli.
