# Transfer to Organizational Practice: Standard Operating Procedures

> [!definition] Transfer to Organizational Practice: Standard Operating Procedures
> Transfer to organizational practice: Standard operating procedures are pre-defined actions encoded in advance for specific triggering conditions, designed to ensure consistent, reliable responses without requiring fresh deliberation, thereby enhancing performance in high-reliability organizations.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Transfer to Organizational Practice: Standard Operating Procedures
> The widespread use of standard operating procedures in high-reliability organizations — aviation, nuclear power, surgical practice — represents an organizational-level application of the same underlying principle. The procedure encodes, in advance and in cool deliberation, what the organization will do when a particular triggering condition arises, and the procedure subsequently operates as a cue-triggered response that does not require fresh deliberation under conditions where deliberation would be slow, error-prone, or impossible. The success of high-reliability organizations depends substantially on the quality of these procedures and on the discipline of actually following them, which is itself an organizational-level instance of the goal-commitment maintenance problem that individual implementation intentions face.
