# Supportive Information (van Merriënboer & Kirschner, 2018)

> [!definition] Supportive Information (van Merriënboer & Kirschner, 2018)
> The conceptual frameworks, domain models, cognitive strategies, and systematic approaches to problem-solving that support the non-recurrent (variable, reasoning-intensive) aspects of complex skill performance. Presented before or during each new task class, remaining available throughout. Bridges what learners already know to what they need to understand at the current level of complexity.
>
> **Boundary:** Supportive information is not a synonym for "all instructional content." It specifically targets non-recurrent skill aspects — those requiring flexible reasoning rather than routine execution. Information for recurrent skill aspects is classified as procedural information.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** Serves the schema construction mechanism through elaborative encoding, connecting to [[elaboration|elaboration]] and [[meaningful-learning|meaningful learning]] research.
>
> **See also:** [[schema-formation]], [[mental-model]], [[cognitive-constructivism]], [[elaboration]]
