> - **Definition**: A target framework is a structured approach used to organize and guide the analysis, design, and implementation of educational interventions, therapeutic strategies, or performance development plans by aligning them with specific goals and objectives.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> In educational settings, a target framework helps educators identify learning outcomes and tailor instructional methods to meet those outcomes. In clinical contexts, it assists therapists in setting realistic recovery goals for patients based on their individual needs and progress.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> For example, in athletic training, a target framework might include phases of conditioning, skill development, and performance enhancement, each with specific milestones and assessments to track progress towards the athlete's ultimate goal.
