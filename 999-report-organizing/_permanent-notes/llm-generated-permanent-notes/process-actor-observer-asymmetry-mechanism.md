# Process: Actor-Observer Asymmetry Mechanism

> [!definition] Process: Actor-Observer Asymmetry Mechanism
> Process: Actor-Observer Asymmetry Mechanism refers to the tendency for actors to attribute their own behavior to situational factors while observers attribute the same behavior to dispositional factors, arising from informational, perceptual, and motivational differences.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Process: Actor-Observer Asymmetry Mechanism
> **Q:** What produces the actor-observer asymmetry in attribution, and through what mechanisms?
> **A:** Actors attribute their own behaviour more to situational factors; observers attribute the same behaviour more to dispositional factors. This arises from: (1) informational differences — actors have access to their own history and context, observers do not; (2) perceptual differences — actors' attention is directed outward toward the situation, observers' attention is directed at the actor; (3) motivational differences — actors may be motivated to explain away negative behaviour situationally.
