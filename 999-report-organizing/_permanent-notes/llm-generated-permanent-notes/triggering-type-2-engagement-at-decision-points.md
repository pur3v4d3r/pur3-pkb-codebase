# Triggering Type 2 Engagement at Decision Points

> [!definition] Triggering Type 2 Engagement at Decision Points
> Triggering Type 2 Engagement at Decision Points involves a series of cognitive steps to ensure thorough scrutiny, including articulating the question, generating multiple answers, considering opposing arguments, identifying evidence that would change your mind, and delaying judgment.

## Additional Material

> [!protocol] Triggering Type 2 Engagement at Decision Points
> When facing a decision whose stakes warrant scrutiny:
> 1. **Articulate the actual question.** Write down (or say aloud) the question you are actually trying to answer. This prevents Type 1 substitution by externalising the target.
> 2. **Generate three alternative answers.** Force the cognitive system out of single-default mode by requiring at least three candidate responses with the evidence for each.
> 3. **State the strongest argument against your preferred answer.** This engages the conflict-detection mechanism and disrupts confirmation-driven processing.
> 4. **Identify what evidence would change your mind.** A judgment for which no possible evidence would change your mind is a judgment that has not been subjected to Type 2 scrutiny.
> 5. **Wait if possible.** Time pressure biases toward endorsement; even a brief delay engages metacognitive processes that immediate response forecloses.
