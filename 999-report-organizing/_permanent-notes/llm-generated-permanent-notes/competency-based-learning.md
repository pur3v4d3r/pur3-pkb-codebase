# competency-based-learning

> [!definition] competency-based-learning
> - **Key-Term**: [[competency-based-learning]]
> - **Definition**: Competency-based learning is an educational approach where instruction and assessment are centered on the skills, knowledge, and behaviors that students must demonstrate to achieve specific learning outcomes, rather than on the amount of time spent in traditional classroom settings.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> In competency-based learning, the focus shifts from covering a fixed curriculum within a set timeframe to ensuring that learners can apply what they have learned effectively. This approach emphasizes mastery of skills and knowledge through performance tasks, assessments, and real-world applications.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> It works by setting clear competencies or learning objectives for each course or module. Students progress based on their demonstrated ability to meet these competencies, often with personalized pacing and support tailored to individual needs.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the use of formative assessment to guide instruction and continuous feedback to help students understand what they need to do next. Sub-variants might include mastery learning, where students must achieve a certain level of proficiency before moving on, or competency-based progression, which allows for flexible pacing.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In practice, this approach can lead to more efficient use of instructional time and better alignment between teaching and assessment.

> [!example] Application
> It also supports differentiated instruction by allowing students to work at their own pace and receive additional support when needed.
