# evidence-based-practice

> [!definition] evidence-based-practice
> - **Key-Term**: [[evidence-based-practice]]
> - **Definition**: Evidence-based practice is an approach to decision-making that integrates the best available research evidence, clinical expertise, and patient values to deliver high-quality care.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Evidence-based practice (EBP) emerged from a recognition that healthcare decisions should be informed by rigorous scientific evidence. It involves systematically identifying, critically appraising, and applying relevant research findings to clinical decision-making processes.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, EBP requires clinicians to ask specific questions about patient care, search for the best available evidence through systematic reviews or meta-analyses, assess the quality of that evidence, apply it in a way that is practical and feasible within their setting, and evaluate its effectiveness. This process ensures that clinical decisions are based on the most current and reliable research.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> EBP encompasses various sub-variants such as evidence-based medicine (EBM), which focuses primarily on healthcare interventions, and evidence-based teaching or learning, which applies similar principles to educational settings.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In clinical settings, EBP can lead to improved patient outcomes by ensuring that treatments are based on the best available research.

> [!example] Application
> In education, EBP helps educators implement instructional strategies that have been shown to be effective through rigorous research studies.
