# metacognitive-scaffolding

> [!definition] metacognitive-scaffolding
> - **Key-Term**: [[metacognitive-scaffolding]]
> - **Definition**: Metacognitive-scaffolding is a pedagogical approach that supports learners by providing structured guidance to enhance their metacognitive skills, enabling them to better understand and regulate their own learning processes.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Metacognitive-scaffolding involves the use of instructional strategies designed to help students become more aware of their thinking processes and develop effective learning habits. This is achieved through structured guidance that gradually reduces as learners gain independence.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, metacognitive-scaffolding can be implemented in various ways, such as using forethought templates, providing feedback on cognitive processes, and encouraging self-assessment and reflection.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the importance of tailoring scaffolds to individual learner needs and ensuring that they are gradually removed as learners become more competent.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
