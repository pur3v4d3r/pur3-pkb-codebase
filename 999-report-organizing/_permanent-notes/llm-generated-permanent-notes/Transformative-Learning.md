# Transformative-Learning

> [!definition] Transformative-Learning
> - **Key-Term**: [[Transformative-Learning]]
> - **Definition**: Transformative learning is a process by which individuals critically reflect on their experiences, leading to new understandings and changes in perspective that can alter their beliefs, values, and behaviors.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Transformative learning involves deep cognitive and affective processes where learners engage with their existing knowledge and experiences to challenge and reframe them. This process often requires critical reflection and the willingness to question one's assumptions and biases.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, transformative learning can be facilitated through various educational methods such as case studies, group discussions, and experiential learning activities that encourage learners to confront contradictions in their beliefs and values, leading to personal growth and development.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the role of social contexts and interactions in facilitating transformation. Theorists like Jack Mezirow have emphasized the importance of critical reflection on the impact of social structures and discourses on individual perspectives.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In education, transformative learning can lead to more effective teaching strategies that promote deep understanding and personal growth among students.

> [!example] Application
> In professional development, it supports continuous improvement by encouraging practitioners to critically evaluate their practices and integrate new insights into their work.
