## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Obsidian is a naturally occurring volcanic glass that forms when lava cools rapidly without crystallization. It is known for its uniform composition and isotropic properties, which give it a distinctive conchoidal fracture pattern, making it ideal for producing sharp edges in tools or cutting implements.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, obsidian was widely used by prehistoric cultures for crafting tools such as blades, arrowheads, and scrapers due to the sharpness of its fractured surfaces. The material's hardness and ability to produce fine edges made it a valuable resource for early human societies.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include variations in color and texture based on the presence of impurities or trace elements, which can affect the quality and usability of obsidian artifacts.

## Practical Implications
