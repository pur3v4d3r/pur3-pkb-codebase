# The Coordination Thesis for Schema Construction

> [!definition] The Coordination Thesis for Schema Construction
> The Coordination Thesis for Schema Construction posits that effective schema building necessitates the integrated use of supportive information to provide mental models and cognitive strategies, alongside learning tasks offering authentic application opportunities.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Coordination Thesis for Schema Construction
> Effective schema construction for complex, non-recurrent skills requires the systematic coordination of two instructional elements: (a) supportive information that provides mental models and cognitive strategies appropriate to the current level of task complexity, and (b) learning tasks that provide authentic, variable opportunities to apply, test, and refine those models and strategies. Neither element alone is sufficient; their coordinated interaction is the mechanism through which flexible, transferable schemas are built.
> *— [[four-component-instructional-design-model-4cid-foundational-report-2026-04-18]]*
