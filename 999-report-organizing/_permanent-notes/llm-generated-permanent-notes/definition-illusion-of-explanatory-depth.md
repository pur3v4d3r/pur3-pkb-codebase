# Definition: Illusion of Explanatory Depth

> [!definition] Definition: Illusion of Explanatory Depth
> The illusion of explanatory depth is the belief that one understands a topic well enough to explain it, which is often revealed as false when attempting an actual explanation.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Definition: Illusion of Explanatory Depth
> **Q:** What is the illusion of explanatory depth, and why does it matter for metacognitive reading?
> **A:** The illusion of explanatory depth is the systematic finding that people believe they understand topics well enough to explain them and discover, when actually attempting the explanation, that their understanding was a felt sense of comprehension supported by recognizable fragments rather than an integrated representation. It matters for metacognitive reading because it is the calibration problem in acute form, demonstrating that internal monitoring routinely fails to flag gaps that explanation reveals.
