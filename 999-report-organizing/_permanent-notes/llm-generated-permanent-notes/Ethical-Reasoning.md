## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Ethical-reasoning involves evaluating situations based on a set of moral principles or values. It requires individuals to consider the potential consequences of their actions and how these align with societal norms, personal beliefs, and ethical standards.

> This process often includes identifying relevant ethical principles, considering different perspectives, and weighing various options before making a decision.
