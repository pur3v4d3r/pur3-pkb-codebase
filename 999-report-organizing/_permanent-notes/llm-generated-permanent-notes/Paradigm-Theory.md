# Paradigm-Theory

> [!definition] Paradigm-Theory
> - **Key-Term**: [[Paradigm-Theory]]
> - **Definition**: Paradigm-Theory refers to the framework within which scientific communities operate, characterized by shared assumptions, practices, and theories that guide their research and understanding of phenomena.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> In science, a paradigm is a set of fundamental beliefs, values, and methods that shape how researchers interpret data and solve problems. It serves as the foundation for scientific inquiry within a specific field.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> Paradigms influence what questions are considered valid, which methodologies are used to investigate these questions, and how results are interpreted. They guide scientists in their research and help maintain coherence within the community.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the fact that paradigms can shift over time as new evidence challenges existing assumptions, leading to paradigm shifts such as those described by Thomas Kuhn.
