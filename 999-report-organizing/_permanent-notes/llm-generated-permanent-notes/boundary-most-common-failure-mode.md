# Boundary: Most Common Failure Mode

> [!definition] Boundary: Most Common Failure Mode
> Boundary: Most Common Failure Mode refers to the quality of cue specification in implementation intentions, where vague or context-dependent cues fail to acquire necessary perceptual discriminability for reliable detection.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Boundary: Most Common Failure Mode
> **Q:** According to the cue-encoding bottleneck hypothesis surfaced in this report, what is the most common single point of failure for implementation intentions in practice?
> **A:** The quality of cue specification. Vague, abstract, or context-dependent cues fail to acquire the perceptual discriminability required for reliable detection, regardless of how strong the underlying goal commitment is.
