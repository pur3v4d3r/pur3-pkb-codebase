# 4C/ID and Individual Differences: Beyond One-Size-Fits-All Sequencing

> [!definition] 4C/ID and Individual Differences: Beyond One-Size-Fits-All Sequencing
> 4C/ID and Individual Differences refers to the need for tailoring the four-component instructional design model (4C/ID) to account for variations in learners' prior knowledge, cognitive abilities, self-regulation, and motivational orientations, moving beyond a one-size-fits-all approach.

## Open Threads

> [!open-question] 4C/ID and Individual Differences: Beyond One-Size-Fits-All Sequencing
> To what extent should 4C/ID blueprints be differentiated based on learner characteristics — prior knowledge, cognitive ability, self-regulation capacity, motivational orientation? The [[Expertise Reversal Effect (Kalyuga, Ayres, Chandler, Sweller, 2003)|expertise reversal effect]] demonstrates that optimal instruction varies with expertise. But the current 4C/ID framework provides limited guidance on how to accommodate individual differences beyond general recommendations for adaptive complexity adjustment.
