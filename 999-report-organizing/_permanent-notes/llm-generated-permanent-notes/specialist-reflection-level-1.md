# Specialist Reflection — Level 1

> [!definition] Specialist Reflection — Level 1
> Specialist Reflection — Level 1 involves self-assessment of behaviors that are autonomously regulated versus those that require external prompting, and recognizing conditions leading to a shift from autonomous to controlled regulation.

## Reflections

> [!reflection] Specialist Reflection — Level 1
> *Before proceeding to Level 2, hold these questions in mind:*
> - Of the behaviors you currently perform without external prompting, which would survive a six-month disruption to your life and which would not? What does the answer tell you about where on the continuum each behavior sits?
> - Can you identify a behavior in your own life that has regressed from a more autonomous regulatory style to a more controlled one? What were the conditions under which the regression occurred?
> - When you observe another person performing a behavior, what surface markers do you actually use to estimate whether they have internalized the regulation, and how reliable are those markers when you check them against later behavior under disruption?
