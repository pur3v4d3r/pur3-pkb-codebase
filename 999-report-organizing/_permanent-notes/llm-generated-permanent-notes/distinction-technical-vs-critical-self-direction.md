# Distinction: Technical vs. Critical Self-Direction

> [!definition] Distinction: Technical vs. Critical Self-Direction
> Technical self-direction involves managing the practical aspects of learning, such as goal-setting and time management, whereas critical self-direction entails questioning the underlying assumptions and power dynamics that influence what is considered legitimate knowledge.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Distinction: Technical vs. Critical Self-Direction
> **Q:** What is Brookfield's distinction between technical and critical self-direction?
> **A:** Technical self-direction: the ability to manage the logistics of one's own learning (goal-setting, resource-finding, time management). Critical self-direction: the ability to question the assumptions, values, and power relations that shape what counts as legitimate knowledge and worthy learning. A learner can be technically self-directed while being critically uncritical.
