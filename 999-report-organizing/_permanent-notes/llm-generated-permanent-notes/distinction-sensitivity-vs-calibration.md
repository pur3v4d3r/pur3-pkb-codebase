# Distinction: Sensitivity vs. Calibration

> [!definition] Distinction: Sensitivity vs. Calibration
> Sensitivity refers to the ability to discriminate between levels of comprehension, while calibration measures the accuracy of one's confidence in that discrimination relative to actual performance.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Distinction: Sensitivity vs. Calibration
> **Q:** What is the formal distinction between metacognitive sensitivity and calibration, and why does it matter?
> **A:** Sensitivity is discriminative — the ability to assign higher confidence to better-comprehended material. Calibration is absolute — the alignment between confidence level and actual performance level. They dissociate: a reader can have good sensitivity but poor (inflated) calibration. The distinction matters because instructional interventions tend to affect one without the other, and the two failure modes have different regulatory consequences.
