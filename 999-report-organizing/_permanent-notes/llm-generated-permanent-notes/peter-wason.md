## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The Wason selection task involves identifying necessary conditions through conditional reasoning. Participants are often misled by their intuitive responses, demonstrating how easily people can be led astray from logical rules.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this task is used to illustrate cognitive biases and heuristics in decision-making processes, particularly the tendency to focus on confirmatory evidence rather than disconfirming it.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the role of confirmation bias and the difficulty in recognizing necessary conditions without explicit instruction.
