# Source Information Is Cognitively Constructed, Not Mechanically Recorded

> [!definition] Source Information Is Cognitively Constructed, Not Mechanically Recorded
> Source information is cognitively constructed, not mechanically recorded, meaning that individuals process and interpret external experiences through their cognitive operations, leading to varied efficacy outcomes despite similar inputs.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Source Information Is Cognitively Constructed, Not Mechanically Recorded
> The four sources of efficacy information do not deposit beliefs into the mind in the manner that sensors deposit data into a computer; they supply raw material that is filtered, weighted, integrated, and interpreted through cognitive operations whose patterns vary across individuals and across time. The same objective experience can produce divergent efficacy outcomes because the cognitive processing of that experience is what does the actual constructive work.
> *— [[self-efficacy-theory-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*
