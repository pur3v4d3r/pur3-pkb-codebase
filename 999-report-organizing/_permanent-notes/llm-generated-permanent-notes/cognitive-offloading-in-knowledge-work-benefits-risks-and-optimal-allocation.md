# Cognitive Offloading in Knowledge Work: Benefits, Risks, and Optimal Allocation

> [!definition] Cognitive Offloading in Knowledge Work: Benefits, Risks, and Optimal Allocation
> Cognitive offloading in knowledge work involves transferring tasks to external tools or systems to enhance performance, but it can also lead to risks such as reduced cognitive development; optimal allocation depends on the context.

## Core Explanation
