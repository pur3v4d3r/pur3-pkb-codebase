# The Specificity-Difficulty Conjunction

> [!definition] The Specificity-Difficulty Conjunction
> The Specificity-Difficulty Conjunction refers to the combination of specific and difficult goals, which according to Goal Setting Theory, leads to the greatest performance improvements by providing a clear criterion for evaluation and mobilizing required resources.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] The Specificity-Difficulty Conjunction
> The central empirical claim of Goal Setting Theory is not that hard goals work, nor that specific goals work, but that the conjunction of specificity and difficulty produces the largest, most reliable performance gains — because specificity provides the discriminative criterion against which performance can be evaluated, while difficulty provides the energetic gradient that mobilizes the resources required to meet the criterion.
> *— [[goal-setting-theory-locke-latham-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*
