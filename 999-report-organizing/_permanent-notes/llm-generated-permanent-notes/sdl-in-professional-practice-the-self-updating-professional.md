# SDL in Professional Practice: The Self-Updating Professional

> [!definition] SDL in Professional Practice: The Self-Updating Professional
> SDL in Professional Practice: The Self-Updating Professional involves continuously diagnosing learning needs, formulating goals, identifying resources, implementing strategies, and evaluating outcomes to maintain and update expertise within a community of practice.

## Examples

> [!example] SDL in Professional Practice: The Self-Updating Professional
> The concept of [[deliberate-practice|deliberate practice]] intersects with self-directed learning in professional contexts where expertise must be continuously maintained and updated. A physician who reads current research, identifies gaps between their existing clinical practice and emerging evidence, designs focused learning activities to close those gaps, and evaluates whether their clinical decision-making has actually improved as a result is engaging in precisely the kind of self-directed learning that Knowles described — diagnosing needs, formulating goals, identifying resources, implementing strategies, and evaluating outcomes. The difference is that this professional's self-direction is embedded within a community of practice that provides both resources and calibration, illustrating that self-direction and social support are complementary rather than contradictory.
> *— [[self-directed-learning-foundational-report-2026-04-19]]*
