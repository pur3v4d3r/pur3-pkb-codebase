# spreading-activation

> [!definition] spreading-activation
> - **Key-Term**: [[spreading-activation]]
> - **Definition**: Spreading-activation is a cognitive process where activation of one concept or memory spreads to related concepts, enhancing their accessibility and facilitating retrieval from memory.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Spreading-activation operates within the framework of associative networks in the brain. When an individual encounters a specific piece of information, it activates nodes (concepts) that are closely associated with it, thereby making these related nodes more accessible for further processing or recall.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> This mechanism is crucial in various cognitive processes such as memory retrieval and decision-making. For instance, when someone thinks about 'dog', the spreading-activation process might also activate concepts like 'bark', 'paw', 'leash', etc., depending on their existing knowledge network.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> The strength of connections between nodes determines how far and how strongly activation spreads. This can vary based on individual differences in prior experiences, learning history, and current context.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application

> [!example] Application
