# self-efficacy-for-learning-and-performance

> [!definition] self-efficacy-for-learning-and-performance
> - **Key-Term**: [[self-efficacy-for-learning-and-performance]]
> - **Definition**: Self-efficacy for learning and performance refers to an individual's belief in their capability to execute behaviors necessary to produce specific performance attainments, particularly in the context of educational settings where it influences how well they learn and perform tasks or solve problems.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Self-efficacy is a construct developed by Albert Bandura that posits individuals' beliefs about their own efficacy as critical determinants of their motivation, affect, and behavior. In the context of learning and performance, it refers to an individual's confidence in their ability to succeed in specific academic or professional tasks.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> This belief can significantly impact how students approach challenges, persist through difficulties, and ultimately achieve their goals. For instance, high self-efficacy can lead to more effective study strategies, better engagement with course material, and higher performance outcomes.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between general self-efficacy (confidence in one's ability across a wide range of tasks) and specific self-efficacy (confidence in one's ability for particular tasks). Additionally, self-efficacy can be influenced by various factors such as past experiences, vicarious experiences, verbal persuasion, and emotional states.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In educational settings, fostering students' self-efficacy can lead to improved academic performance through better study habits and increased motivation.

> [!example] Application
> In professional development, enhancing employees' self-efficacy for specific tasks can improve job satisfaction and productivity.
