# Conditions-for-Valid-Intuition-Kahneman-Klein-Synthesis

> [!definition] Conditions-for-Valid-Intuition-Kahneman-Klein-Synthesis
> Conditions-for-Valid-Intuition-Kahneman-Klein-Synthesis refers to the framework outlining specific environmental, experiential, and feedback criteria under which intuitive judgments are most reliable, as developed by Daniel Kahneman and Gary Klein.

> [!evidence] Conditions-for-Valid-Intuition-Kahneman-Klein-Synthesis
