## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Tulving's research focuses on understanding how different types of memories are stored and retrieved in the brain. He introduced the concept of 'episodic memory', which refers to personal experiences and events, and 'semantic memory', which involves factual knowledge.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, Tulving's work has implications for education, clinical psychology, and cognitive load theory, as it helps explain how different types of information are processed and retained by individuals.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between conscious recollection (episodic) and unconscious retrieval (semantic), which has been further explored in subsequent research.
