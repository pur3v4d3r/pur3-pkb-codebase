# Curriculum Audit for Self-Direction Development

> [!definition] Curriculum Audit for Self-Direction Development
> A Curriculum Audit for Self-Direction Development is a structured evaluation process designed to assess and enhance an educational curriculum's effectiveness in fostering self-directed learning skills across various dimensions such as scaffolding progression, metacognitive development, motivational architecture, and assessment practices.

## Additional Material

> [!protocol] Curriculum Audit for Self-Direction Development
> **Purpose:** Evaluate an existing curriculum's capacity to develop self-directed learning abilities.
>
> **Instructions:** For each dimension, assess the current state on a 1-5 scale and identify specific action items for improvement.
>
> **Dimension 1 — Scaffolding Progression:**
> 1. Does the curriculum provide explicit scaffolding for novice learners?
> 2. Is there a deliberate plan for fading scaffolding as learners develop?
> 3. Are different levels of scaffolding available for learners at different developmental stages?
> 4. Does the curriculum avoid the expertise reversal effect (maintaining scaffolding beyond necessity)?
> 5. Is there evidence that learners are more self-directed at the end than at the beginning?
>
> **Dimension 2 — Metacognitive Development:**
> 1. Does the curriculum include explicit instruction in self-regulated learning strategies?
> 2. Are learners given opportunities to plan, monitor, and evaluate their own learning?
> 3. Is metacognitive modelling (think-alouds, strategy demonstrations) incorporated?
> 4. Do assessment practices develop self-evaluative capacity?
> 5. Is metacognitive development tracked and assessed?
>
> **Dimension 3 — Motivational Architecture:**
> 1. Does the curriculum satisfy autonomy, competence, and relatedness needs?
> 2. Are multiple sources of motivation cultivated (interest, identity, utility, social)?
> 3. Is the curriculum designed to develop intrinsic motivation over time?
> 4. Are choice and agency progressively increased?
> 5. Does the motivational design account for long-term sustainability?
>
> **Dimension 4 — Assessment for Autonomy:**
> 1. Is formative feedback prioritised over summative grading?
> 2. Are self-assessment and peer assessment incorporated?
> 3. Is self-assessment calibrated against expert evaluation?
> 4. Do assessment practices develop the learner's own evaluative capacity?
> 5. Is portfolio or reflective assessment used for longitudinal development?
>
> **Scoring:** Average across dimensions. ≥4.0 = strong alignment; 3.0-3.9 = moderate with improvement areas; <3.0 = significant redesign needed.
