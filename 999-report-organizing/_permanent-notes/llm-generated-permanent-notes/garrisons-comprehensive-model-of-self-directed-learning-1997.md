# Garrison's Comprehensive Model of Self-Directed Learning (1997)

> [!definition] Garrison's Comprehensive Model of Self-Directed Learning (1997)
> Garrison proposed that self-directed learning involves the integration of three overlapping dimensions: self-management (the external management of learning activities and resources, including the social setting), self-monitoring (the cognitive processes of metacognitive monitoring and reflection), and motivation (the decision to enter and persist in learning, including task value and self-efficacy). The model's critical contribution was its insistence that all three dimensions must be addressed simultaneously — self-direction cannot be reduced to any single dimension alone.
>
> **Boundary:** Garrison's model is not a model of self-regulated learning (which focuses primarily on the self-monitoring dimension) nor a model of autonomous learning (which focuses primarily on the self-management dimension). It is specifically a model of self-directed learning that integrates external management, internal monitoring, and motivational persistence.
>
