# Jeroen J.G. van Merriënboer (b. 1959)

> [!definition] Jeroen J.G. van Merriënboer (b. 1959)
> Jeroen J.G. van Merriënboer is a Dutch instructional designer known for developing the 4C/ID model, which integrates schema construction and automation within a comprehensive approach to training complex cognitive skills.

## Additional Material

> [!person] Jeroen J.G. van Merriënboer (b. 1959)
> *Maastricht University, Netherlands*
>
> **Core contribution:** Primary architect of the 4C/ID model, developed through doctoral research on complex cognitive skill training in the 1990s and refined through three decades of collaborative research and application. Established the theoretical integration of schema construction and schema automation within a holistic instructional design framework.
>
> **Relationships:** Built upon the cognitive load theory of [[john-sweller|John Sweller]], collaborated extensively with [[Fred Paas|Fred Paas]] on cognitive load measurement, and partnered with Paul Kirschner on the definitive practitioner-oriented exposition of the model (*Ten Steps to Complex Learning*, multiple editions).
>
> **Key works:** *Training Complex Cognitive Skills* (1997); *Ten Steps to Complex Learning* (2007, 2018 with Kirschner)
