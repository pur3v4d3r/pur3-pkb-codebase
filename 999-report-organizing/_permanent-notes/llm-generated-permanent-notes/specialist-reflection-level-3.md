# Specialist Reflection — Level 3

> [!definition] Specialist Reflection — Level 3
> Specialist Reflection — Level 3 involves tracing specific need-satisfaction conditions for observed behaviors in relational contexts and operationalizing concepts like autonomy to address cross-cultural debates in self-determination theory.

## Reflections

> [!reflection] Specialist Reflection — Level 3
> *Before proceeding to Level 4:*
> - For a behavior whose internalization you have observed succeeding or failing, can you trace the specific need-satisfaction conditions that were present or absent for that particular behavior in that particular relational context?
> - The need-frustration vs need-deprivation distinction has clinical implications. Where in your work might this distinction change a recommendation you would make?
> - The autonomy/independence conflation has produced confusion in cross-cultural SDT debates. How would you operationalize "autonomy" in a study designed to address that confusion directly?
