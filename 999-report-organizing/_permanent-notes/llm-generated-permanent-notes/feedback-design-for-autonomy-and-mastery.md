# feedback-design-for-autonomy-and-mastery

> [!definition] feedback-design-for-autonomy-and-mastery
> - **Key-Term**: [[feedback-design-for-autonomy-and-mastery]]
> - **Definition**: Feedback design for autonomy and mastery involves creating educational feedback that supports learners in taking control of their learning process while fostering a sense of accomplishment and skill development, aligning with self-determination theory and achievement goal theory.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> This concept is rooted in the idea that effective feedback should empower students to make informed decisions about their learning journey. It emphasizes providing learners with information that helps them understand their progress and how they can improve, thereby enhancing their autonomy and mastery over time.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this involves designing assessments and feedback mechanisms that are specific, actionable, and aligned with the learner's goals. For example, teachers might use rubrics or self-assessment tools to help students set personal learning objectives and track their progress towards these goals.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include differentiating between performance-based and mastery-based feedback, where the former focuses on comparing learners against each other, while the latter emphasizes individual improvement and understanding.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> Instructors can use this approach to create more engaging and effective learning environments by tailoring feedback to meet individual student needs.

> [!example] Application
> This method also helps in promoting a growth mindset among students, encouraging them to view challenges as opportunities for learning rather than obstacles.
