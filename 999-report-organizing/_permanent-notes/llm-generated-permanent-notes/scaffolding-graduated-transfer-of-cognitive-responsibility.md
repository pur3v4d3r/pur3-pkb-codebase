# scaffolding — Graduated Transfer of Cognitive Responsibility

> [!definition] scaffolding — Graduated Transfer of Cognitive Responsibility
> Scaffolding is a pedagogical approach that gradually transfers cognitive responsibility from teacher to learner, facilitating self-directed learning within the zone of proximal development.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] scaffolding — Graduated Transfer of Cognitive Responsibility
> **Description:** An examination of scaffolding as a pedagogical and cognitive concept — its Vygotskian origins, contemporary manifestations in education and technology, relationship to the zone of proximal development, and application to the development of self-directed learning capacity.
> **Connection to this report:** The readiness paradox (Section 6) establishes that self-direction cannot develop through self-direction alone — scaffolding is the mechanism that resolves this paradox.
