# mastery-goal

> [!definition] mastery-goal
> - **Key-Term**: [[mastery-goal]]
> - **Definition**: A mastery goal is an educational and psychological concept that refers to the pursuit of learning, understanding, and improving one's skills for their own sake rather than for external rewards such as grades or recognition.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Mastery goals are rooted in self-determination theory (SDT) and focus on personal growth and intrinsic motivation. Individuals with a mastery orientation seek to enhance their abilities and knowledge, often engaging in deep processing strategies like elaboration and organization of information.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, mastery goals can be applied in various educational settings by encouraging students to set learning objectives that are personally meaningful and challenging but achievable. This approach fosters a growth mindset and enhances long-term academic performance and well-being.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between mastery and performance goals, where performance goals emphasize demonstrating competence relative to others, often leading to anxiety and avoidance behaviors.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In educational settings, fostering a mastery orientation can lead to higher levels of engagement and persistence in learning activities, as students are more likely to persist through challenges when the focus is on personal growth.

> [!example] Application
> Teachers and educators can promote mastery goals by providing formative assessments, encouraging self-reflection, and creating a supportive classroom environment that values effort and improvement over grades.
