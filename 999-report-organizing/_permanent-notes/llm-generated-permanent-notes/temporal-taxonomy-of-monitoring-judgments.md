# Temporal Taxonomy of Monitoring Judgments

> [!definition] Temporal Taxonomy of Monitoring Judgments
> Temporal Taxonomy of Monitoring Judgments is a framework that categorizes metacognitive judgments into different stages—before, during/after study, after retrieval failure or success—based on factors like prior knowledge, current accessibility, fluency, and retrieval fluency.
