# The Translation Window: An Original Synthesis

> [!definition] The Translation Window: An Original Synthesis
> The translation window is the range of conditions where goal-directed activities align with desired outcomes, ensuring that achieving a goal also reinforces underlying values.

## Reflections

> [!claude-insight] The Translation Window: An Original Synthesis
> What both the dark-side critique and the performance-vs-mastery tension share is a structural feature one might name the **translation window** — the region of conditions within which the activities that produce goal achievement coincide with the activities one actually wants to be reinforced. Inside the translation window, the goal serves as a faithful proxy for the underlying value; outside it, the goal becomes a distorting proxy whose pursuit generates measurable success on the target while degrading the unmeasured outcomes the target was supposed to indicate. The width of the translation window is not a property of the goal but of the joint system of goal, measurement, reward structure, ability, and strategic adequacy; engineering practices that preserve the translation window — multi-dimensional measurement, learning goals where strategy is unsettled, ethical guardrails on attainability, cooperative structures for interdependent work — are precisely the practices that the dark-side literature recommends and that an integrated reading of the theory itself can support.
> *— [[goal-setting-theory-locke-latham-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*
