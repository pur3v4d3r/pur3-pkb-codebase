## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> The core mechanism of Richard Anderson's framework involves understanding how cognitive load influences the acquisition of motor skills. It emphasizes the role of prior knowledge in facilitating new learning and the importance of managing intrinsic, extraneous, and germane cognitive loads during skill development.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, this framework is applied by educators to design training programs that optimize learning efficiency. By reducing extraneous cognitive load through well-structured tasks and increasing germane load through meaningful practice, learners can more effectively encode new skills into long-term memory.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between different types of cognitive loads and how they interact during skill acquisition. Anderson's framework also highlights the importance of bridging prior knowledge to new learning contexts.
