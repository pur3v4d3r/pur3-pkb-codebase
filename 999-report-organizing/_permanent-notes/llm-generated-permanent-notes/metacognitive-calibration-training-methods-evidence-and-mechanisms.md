# Metacognitive Calibration Training: Methods, Evidence, and Mechanisms

> [!definition] Metacognitive Calibration Training: Methods, Evidence, and Mechanisms
> Metacognitive Calibration Training involves a set of systematic interventions aimed at improving individuals' ability to accurately assess their own cognitive performance, including methods such as feedback-based calibration, generation tasks, delayed judgment-of-learning protocols, and retrieval practice.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Metacognitive Calibration Training: Methods, Evidence, and Mechanisms
> **Description:** A systematic examination of interventions designed to improve metacognitive calibration, including feedback-based calibration training, generation-based interventions, delayed JOL protocols, retrieval practice as calibration tool, and the transfer of calibration gains across tasks and domains.
> **Connection to this report:** Section 6 identifies calibration as the criterion of monitoring quality and discusses the mechanisms of miscalibration, but the full landscape of calibration interventions — their effectiveness, boundary conditions, and mechanisms of action — warrants comprehensive dedicated treatment.
