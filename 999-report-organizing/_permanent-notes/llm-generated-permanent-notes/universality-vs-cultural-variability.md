# Universality vs Cultural Variability

> [!definition] Universality vs Cultural Variability
> Universality vs Cultural Variability refers to the debate within cultural studies about whether certain psychological needs are universally present across cultures or if their expression and satisfaction vary significantly between different cultural contexts.

## Open Threads

> [!tension-identified] Universality vs Cultural Variability
> The mainstream SDT position holds that the three basic needs are universal while their satisfying contents are culturally variable. The tension persists because the operationalization of the needs themselves can smuggle in cultural assumptions, and the question of whether the universality has been established or has been definitionally secured remains live at the boundary.
