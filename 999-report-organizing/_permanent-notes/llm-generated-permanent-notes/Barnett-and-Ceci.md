> - **Definition**: Barnett and Ceci refer to the concept of collective efficacy, which is the belief among members of a group that their combined efforts will lead to successful outcomes in a specific domain or context.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Collective efficacy was first introduced by Bandura (1997) as a key component of social cognitive theory. It involves the shared beliefs and expectations within a group about their ability to achieve goals through collective action.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, collective efficacy can be activated or enhanced through various strategies such as goal setting, feedback, and collaborative problem-solving. These strategies help members of a group to develop a sense of confidence in their joint capabilities.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the distinction between individual self-efficacy (confidence in one's own abilities) and collective efficacy (confidence in the group's combined efforts). The concept has been applied across various domains, including education, sports, and clinical practice.
