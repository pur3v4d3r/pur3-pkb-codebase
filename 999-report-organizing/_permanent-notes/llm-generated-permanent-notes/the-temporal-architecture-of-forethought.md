# The Temporal Architecture of Forethought

> [!definition] The Temporal Architecture of Forethought
> The Temporal Architecture of Forethought is a structured approach to planning that spans micro (session-level), meso (weekly), and macro (quarterly or semester) timescales, ensuring seamless integration between different levels of forethought for effective goal achievement.

## Reflections

> [!claude-insight] The Temporal Architecture of Forethought
> Effective forethought operates across multiple timescales, each requiring distinct scaffolding structures. **Micro-forethought** (beginning of a study session: "What am I studying and how?") requires session-level templates. **Meso-forethought** (weekly planning: "What topics will I cover this week and in what sequence?") requires weekly review templates with forward-planning sections. **Macro-forethought** (quarterly or semester planning: "What are my learning priorities and how do they sequence?") requires project-level dashboards and goal-tracking systems. A PKB designed for comprehensive forethought scaffolding must provide structures at all three timescales — and, critically, must connect them, so that session-level goals are derived from weekly plans, which are derived from quarterly priorities. The [[pkb-goal-scaffolding-architecture|PKB goal scaffolding architecture]] is one existing framework for this multi-level integration.
