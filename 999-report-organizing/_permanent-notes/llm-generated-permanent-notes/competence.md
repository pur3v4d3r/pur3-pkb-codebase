# competence

> [!definition] competence
> - **Key-Term**: [[competence]]
> - **Definition**: Competence refers to the ability to perform tasks effectively and efficiently, often associated with feelings of mastery and success in an activity or domain.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Competence is a fundamental aspect of self-determination theory (SDT), which posits that individuals are motivated by three basic psychological needs: autonomy, competence, and relatedness. When these needs are satisfied, individuals experience intrinsic motivation and well-being.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> In practice, competence involves developing skills and knowledge to achieve goals and solve problems effectively. It is often assessed through performance metrics or self-efficacy beliefs, which influence an individual's confidence in their ability to succeed.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Competence can be further nuanced by cultural context, as different societies may value different types of competencies (e.g., academic vs. social). The concept also has sub-variants such as perceived competence and actual competence.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In education, fostering students' sense of competence is crucial for promoting intrinsic motivation and academic achievement.

> [!example] Application
> In workplace settings, managers can enhance employee performance by providing opportunities for skill development and recognizing achievements.
