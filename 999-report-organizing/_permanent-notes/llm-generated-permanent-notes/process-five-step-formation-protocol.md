# Process: Five-Step Formation Protocol

> [!definition] Process: Five-Step Formation Protocol
> Process: Five-Step Formation Protocol is a method for creating effective implementation intentions involving verifying commitment to a goal, specifying discriminable cues and executable behaviors, rehearsing cue-behavior links, and scheduling periodic reviews.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Process: Five-Step Formation Protocol
> **Q:** What are the five steps in forming an effective implementation intention?
> **A:** (1) Verify upstream commitment to the underlying goal; (2) Specify the cue with discriminability; (3) Specify the behavior with executability; (4) Rehearse the cue-behavior link; (5) Schedule a periodic review.
