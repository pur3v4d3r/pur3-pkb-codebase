# Mechanism: Three Operational Signatures

> [!definition] Mechanism: Three Operational Signatures
> Mechanism: Three Operational Signatures refers to a set of characteristics including immediacy (faster initiation), efficiency (lower cognitive resource demand), and interference shielding (resistance to disruption from competing tasks or distracting information) that distinguish strategic automaticity in implementation-intention enactment from deliberate behavior.
