# The Scaffolding-Sovereignty Progression: Structural Overview

> [!definition] The Scaffolding-Sovereignty Progression: Structural Overview
> The Scaffolding-Sovereignty Progression is a curriculum design framework that transforms learners' dependence into autonomy through structured phases, from guided dependence to negotiated exploration, supported independence, and finally, autonomous sovereignty.
