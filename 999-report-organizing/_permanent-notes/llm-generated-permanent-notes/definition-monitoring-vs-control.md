# Definition: Monitoring vs. Control

> [!definition] Definition: Monitoring vs. Control
> Monitoring involves the upward flow of information from the object-level to the meta-level, while control involves the downward flow of directives from the meta-level to the object-level.

## Flashcards

> [!flashcard] Definition: Monitoring vs. Control
> **Q:** What distinguishes monitoring from control in the Nelson-Narens framework?
> **A:** Monitoring is the upward flow of information from the object-level to the meta-level (informing the meta-level about the object-level's state). Control is the downward flow of directives from the meta-level to the object-level (modifying the object-level's operations based on the meta-level's model).
