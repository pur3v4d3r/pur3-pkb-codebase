> [!definition] Computational Models of Metacognition: Architectures, Implementations, and Predictions
> Computational Models of Metacognition: Architectures, Implementations, and Predictions refers to the use of computational frameworks such as ACT-R, SOAR, and neural networks to represent and simulate metacognitive processes, including their meta-level control mechanisms, and generate novel empirical predictions.

## Core Explanation
