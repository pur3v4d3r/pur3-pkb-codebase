# goal-reframing

> [!definition] goal-reframing
> - **Key-Term**: [[goal-reframing]]
> - **Definition**: Goal-reframing is the process of redefining one's goals to better align with personal values, resources, and circumstances, often leading to more effective and sustainable progress towards desired outcomes.
> - **Domain**: other
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Goal-reframing involves reassessing initial goals in light of new information or changing conditions. This process allows individuals to adapt their objectives to be more realistic and meaningful.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> It works by identifying the underlying values, motivations, and constraints that influence goal setting, then adjusting these goals accordingly. For example, if an individual initially sets a goal to run a marathon but realizes they have limited time for training, they might reframe this as participating in a shorter race or focusing on improving their overall fitness.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances include the importance of self-awareness and flexibility. Sub-variants may involve cognitive restructuring techniques or motivational interviewing approaches.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In education, goal-reframing can help students set more achievable academic goals that align with their long-term aspirations, leading to better learning outcomes.

> [!example] Application
> In personal development, it enables individuals to maintain motivation by adjusting their goals based on feedback and changing life circumstances.
