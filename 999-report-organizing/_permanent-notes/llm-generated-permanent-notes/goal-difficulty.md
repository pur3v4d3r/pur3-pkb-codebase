# Goal Difficulty

> [!definition] Goal Difficulty
> The degree to which a goal exceeds what the performer would otherwise produce in its absence; operationalized in research as the percentage of subjects able to achieve the goal.
>
> **Boundary:** Difficulty is bounded above by perceived attainability — a goal exceeding what the performer believes possible collapses commitment rather than intensifying effort.
>
> **Report-Specific Significance:** Difficulty supplies the energetic gradient that mobilizes resources beyond default operating level; its calibration relative to ability and self-efficacy is the most consequential setting in goal design.
>
> **See also:** [[self-efficacy]], [[goal-setting-theory]]
