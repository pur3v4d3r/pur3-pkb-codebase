# Reflective Questions — Section 9

> [!definition] Reflective Questions — Section 9
> Reflective Questions — Section 9 are probing inquiries within Motivational Psychology that challenge the robustness and applicability of Self-Determination Theory (SDT), particularly concerning its autonomy-as-endorsement response, potential mechanistic revisions, and balance between theoretical breadth and tautology risk.

## Reflections

> [!reflection] Reflective Questions — Section 9
> 1. Does the autonomy-as-endorsement response to cultural critics fully address the concern, or does it risk rendering the concept of autonomy so flexible that it becomes unfalsifiable?
> 2. If the undermining effect requires revision at the mechanistic level, what consequences would follow for CET specifically and for SDT's broader architecture?
> 3. How should SDT researchers balance the theory's breadth (which enables cross-domain application) against the tautology risk (which breadth creates)?
