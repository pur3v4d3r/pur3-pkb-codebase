# Why Architectural Transfer Is More Tractable than Strategy Transfer

> [!definition] Why Architectural Transfer Is More Tractable than Strategy Transfer
> Architectural transfer refers to the process of transferring the underlying structure or framework of a cognitive strategy across different domains, rather than directly transferring specific procedural content.

## Reflections

> [!claude-insight] Why Architectural Transfer Is More Tractable than Strategy Transfer
> The transfer-of-learning literature has been preoccupied with the difficulty of transferring *strategies* — concrete procedural moves trained in one domain to another — and the empirical record on such transfer is sobering. What may transfer more readily, and what the architectural framing developed here implicitly suggests, is transfer at the level of *architecture itself*: the recognition that another domain involves the same monitoring–control loop, the same need for an explicit standard, the same calibration vulnerabilities, and the same regulatory repertoire problem. Practitioners who carry the architecture itself across domains can develop domain-specific implementations of it that work for the new domain, rather than trying to transfer the implementations directly. This is one possible reason why metacognitive expertise generalizes better than other forms of expertise — what generalizes is the architectural insight, not the specific procedural content.
> *— [[metacognition-and-reading-foundational-report-2026-04-20]]*
