# Pause and Connect

> [!definition] Pause and Connect
> Pause and Connect involves pausing during knowledge review to assess actual retrievability rather than relying on processing fluency, thereby enhancing metacognitive control.

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Pause and Connect
> Consider how the concepts of calibration, fluency illusion, and desirable difficulties apply to your own knowledge management practices. When you reread notes in your PKB, are you monitoring actual retrievability or merely processing fluency? How might you restructure your review practices to provide your monitoring system with more diagnostic cues?
