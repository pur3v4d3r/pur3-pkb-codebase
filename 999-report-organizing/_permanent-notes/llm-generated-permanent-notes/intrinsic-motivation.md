# intrinsic-motivation

> [!definition] intrinsic-motivation
> - **Key-Term**: [[intrinsic-motivation]]
> - **Definition**: Intrinsic motivation refers to the drive to engage in an activity for the inherent satisfaction and enjoyment of the task itself, rather than for some separable outcome such as rewards or recognition.
> - **Domain**: educational-psychology
> - **Status**: 🌱 budding | Confidence: speculative

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Intrinsic motivation is a fundamental concept in educational psychology that drives individuals to participate in activities because they find them inherently interesting, enjoyable, or personally fulfilling. This type of motivation contrasts with extrinsic motivation, which involves engaging in an activity for external rewards or outcomes.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> The core mechanism behind intrinsic motivation involves the activation of personal interests and values, leading to a sense of autonomy, competence, and relatedness. These psychological needs are often met through activities that align with one's passions and goals, fostering a deeper engagement and enjoyment of the task at hand.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Key nuances in understanding intrinsic motivation include its relationship to self-determination theory, which posits that individuals have three basic psychological needs: autonomy (feeling in control), competence (feeling capable), and relatedness (feeling connected). When these needs are satisfied, it enhances intrinsic motivation.

## Practical Implications

> [!example] Application
> In educational settings, fostering intrinsic motivation can lead to increased student engagement and better learning outcomes. Teachers can design activities that align with students' interests and provide opportunities for autonomy and mastery.

> [!example] Application
> In workplace environments, promoting intrinsic motivation through meaningful work and recognition of personal growth can enhance job satisfaction and productivity.
