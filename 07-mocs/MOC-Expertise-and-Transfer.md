# 🏆 Map of Content — Expertise & Transfer

> [!abstract] Domain Overview
> How does competence develop from novice to expert? What separates routine from adaptive expertise? How does learning in one context transfer (or fail to transfer) to another? This MOC integrates the expertise-development literature, deliberate practice research, and transfer theory into a unified domain.

[**Expertise-Core-Insight**:: Expert performance is not accumulated experience — it is the product of decades of structured, feedback-rich, deliberate practice that progressively restructures the long-term memory substrate so that working memory constraints effectively disappear within the domain of expertise.]
