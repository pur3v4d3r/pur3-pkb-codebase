# 🎯 Map of Content — Achievement Goals, Attribution & Mindset

> [!abstract] Domain Overview
> This MOC integrates three closely related theoretical frameworks — **Achievement Goal Theory** (what learners pursue), **Attribution Theory** (how they interpret outcomes), and **Mindset Theory** (what they believe about ability). Together these account for how learners construct meaning from achievement contexts and how those meanings shape engagement, persistence, and response to failure.

[**Integrated-Framework-Logic**:: Goal orientations shape the standards against which outcomes are judged; attributions explain why those standards were/weren't met; mindsets determine what attributional patterns are available — making these three frameworks a tightly coupled meaning-making architecture.]
