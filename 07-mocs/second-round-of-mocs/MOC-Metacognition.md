# 🪞 Map of Content — Metacognition

> [!abstract] Domain Overview
> Metacognition — the architecture of thinking about thinking — underlies nearly every sophisticated cognitive capability: learning, reasoning, problem-solving, and self-regulation. This MOC organizes the full empirical and theoretical landscape of metacognition, from Flavell's founding taxonomy through modern computational accounts.

[**Metacognition-Core-Definition**:: The cognitive system's capacity to monitor, evaluate, and control its own processes — operating through a two-level architecture where a meta-level represents and modifies an object-level, generating judgments, feelings, and regulatory actions.]

> [!key-claim] The Two Pillars of Metacognition
> Following [[Flavell's-Metacognitive-Framework|Flavell]] and [[Nelson-and-Narens|Nelson-Narens]], metacognition decomposes into **(1) knowledge about cognition** and **(2) regulation of cognition** — with monitoring and control as the two directional processes linking meta-level and object-level.
