# 📚 Map of Content — Learning Strategies (Evidence-Based)

> [!abstract] Domain Overview
> This MOC organizes the evidence-based learning strategies that cognitive psychology has identified and validated over decades of rigorous research. These strategies — retrieval practice, spaced repetition, interleaving, elaboration, dual coding, and desirable difficulties — form the empirical core of "the science of learning."

[**Strategy-Selection-Principle**:: Evidence-based learning strategies operate by deliberately introducing **desirable difficulties** — conditions that impair short-term performance but enhance long-term retention and transfer by engaging deeper processing and schema construction.]

## 🔄 Retrieval Practice & Testing Effect

> [!key-claim] The Testing Effect Is the Most Replicated Finding in Learning Research
> Testing produces better long-term retention than restudying — across ages, domains, materials, and test formats. This robust effect now forms the cornerstone of evidence-based study advice.

## 🚫 Common Pitfalls & Ineffective Strategies

> [!warning] Commonly Used, Rarely Effective
> Many popular study techniques — highlighting, rereading, summarizing without reflection — show weak evidence. Many evidence-based strategies feel harder (because they are), leading learners to abandon them for intuitive-but-weaker alternatives.
