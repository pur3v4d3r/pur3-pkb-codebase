# 💪 Map of Content — Self-Efficacy & Social Cognitive Theory

> [!abstract] Domain Overview
> Albert Bandura's Social Cognitive Theory (SCT) established human agency as a core psychological capability — with self-efficacy as the mechanism through which people exercise control over their functioning. This MOC covers SCT's triadic reciprocal architecture, the four sources of efficacy, calibration mechanisms, and the extension of agency from personal to collective levels.

[**Self-Efficacy-Definition**:: Bandura's construct — task-specific and domain-specific beliefs about one's capabilities to organize and execute courses of action required to produce given attainments — distinguished from global self-concept by its forward-looking, specific, and agency-focused nature.]

## 🔁 The Triadic Reciprocal Architecture

> [!structure] Triadic Reciprocal Determinism
> SCT's foundational architecture: person, behavior, and environment operate as **mutually determining** causal forces, none reducible to the others. Self-efficacy is the agentic lever within this triadic system.

## 📏 Self-Efficacy Calibration

> [!key-claim] Calibration Is Often More Important Than Level
> A well-calibrated moderate efficacy belief often outperforms a miscalibrated high efficacy belief — because calibration determines whether efficacy guides adaptive action or generates overconfidence and failure.
