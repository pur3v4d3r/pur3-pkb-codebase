# ⚡🐢 Map of Content — Dual-Process Theory, Reasoning & Heuristics

> [!abstract] Domain Overview
> Human reasoning runs on two qualitatively different processing systems: fast, automatic, intuitive Type 1 processing and slow, effortful, reflective Type 2 processing. This MOC integrates the dual-process literature, the heuristics-and-biases research program, and applied reasoning in clinical and legal domains.

[**Dual-Process-Core-Claim**:: Type 1 and Type 2 are not two homunculi but two **styles of processing** that differ in speed, effort, conscious accessibility, and working-memory demands — with Type 2 serving as the capacity-limited override mechanism that can correct, override, or endorse Type 1 output.]
