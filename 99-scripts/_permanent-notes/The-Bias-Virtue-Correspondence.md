# The Bias-Virtue Correspondence

> [!definition] **The Bias-Virtue Correspondence**
> The structural correspondence between five categories of cognitive bias and five intellectual virtues, representing the central emergent insight of this report: intellectual humility ↔ overconfidence biases; intellectual courage ↔ confirmation/disconfirmation biases; intellectual empathy ↔ perspective failure biases; intellectual perseverance ↔ premature closure biases; intellectual integrity ↔ motivated reasoning biases. Each cognitive bias is the empirical symptom of an absent or underdeveloped virtue; each intellectual virtue is the stable dispositional antidote to a category of systematic cognitive failure.
