### 1. Reader-Model

> [!definition] Reader-Model
> The intended audience is an intelligent adult with general education but no assumed expertise in the specific domain. The report treats readers with respect — never dumbing down content, but always providing sufficient context and scaffolding to make specialized knowledge accessible. The goal is accessibility without oversimplification: explaining complex ideas clearly while preserving their genuine complexity and nuance.

*Source: Line 1354 in `topics-prompt.md`*

### 2. Depth-First-Principle

> [!definition] Depth-First-Principle
> Every report represents a permanent intellectual asset in the user's professional knowledge base. Superficial treatment constitutes a critical failure. When uncertain whether to elaborate further, ALWAYS choose elaboration. When choosing between adequate and comprehensive coverage, choose comprehensive. A report that requires follow-up questions to understand the topic is incomplete.

*Source: Line 1358 in `topics-prompt.md`*

### 3. Prose-Primacy-Rule

> [!definition] Prose-Primacy-Rule
> The report reads as scholarly prose — continuous, well-crafted paragraphs that develop ideas through argumentation, evidence, and narrative. Callouts ENRICH the prose; they do not replace it. Lists and bullet points are used sparingly and only when the content genuinely requires enumeration (e.g., a set of discrete steps or criteria). The default format is always paragraphs.

*Source: Line 1812 in `topics-prompt.md`*

### 4. Artifact-Mandate

> [!definition] Artifact-Mandate
> The report MUST be output as a markdown artifact — a downloadable `.md` file — NOT as inline text in the chat. This is non-negotiable. The user needs to download the file directly into their PKB.

*Source: Line 2070 in `topics-prompt.md`*

### 5. Reader-Model

> [!definition] Reader-Model
> The intended audience is a thoughtful, intellectually engaged adult who has either read a foundational treatment of the topic or has enough background to engage with focused analysis. The report treats readers as graduate-level thinkers — capable of handling complexity, ambiguity, and intellectual challenge. It respects the reader by providing genuine analytical depth rather than simplified overviews. The report's voice is that of a knowledgeable colleague sharing their analysis — authoritative but not condescending, confident but intellectually honest about uncertainty.

*Source: Line 2487 in `topics-prompt.md`*

### 6. Depth-First-Principle

> [!definition] Depth-First-Principle
> Every report represents a permanent intellectual asset. The focused analysis format demands even greater depth than the foundational format because it covers less territory — depth compensates for reduced breadth. A Focused Analysis Report that reads like a surface-level summary of its topic has fundamentally failed its purpose.

*Source: Line 2505 in `topics-prompt.md`*

### 8. Cognitive-Engagement-Architecture

> [!definition] Cognitive-Engagement-Architecture
> An advanced metacognitive scaffolding system that moves beyond formulaic reflective questions to create genuine cognitive dialogue between the report and the reader. Built on six engagement modalities: Predictive Engagement, Conceptual Checkpoints, Calibration Exercises, Knowledge State Tracking, Progressive Disclosure Signals, and Reflective Integration. Together, these modalities transform passive reading into active intellectual partnership.

*Source: Line 2657 in `topics-prompt.md`*

### 9. Progressive-Depth-Architecture

> [!definition] Progressive-Depth-Architecture
> A four-layer density protocol ensuring that every major concept receives progressive elaboration through Orientation (what it is), Mechanism (how it works), Implication (why it matters), and Frontier (where the edges are). This replaces simple three-layer depth with a more sophisticated system that includes a "return-and-deepen" pattern where concepts introduced early are revisited with accumulated context.

*Source: Line 2783 in `topics-prompt.md`*
