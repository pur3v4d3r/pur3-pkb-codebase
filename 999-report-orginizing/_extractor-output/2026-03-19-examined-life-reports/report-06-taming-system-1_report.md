#### 4. [DEFINITION] 📖 Type 1 Processing (System 1) *(Line 206)*

> [!definition] 📖 Type 1 Processing (System 1)
> The autonomous, rapid, parallel cognitive processes that operate without deliberate attention, do not compete for [[Working-Memory|Working Memory]] resources, and produce responses automatically in the presence of familiar triggering conditions. Type 1 processing is the defining feature of all learned automaticity — from reading to face recognition to expert intuition — and it executes below the threshold of deliberate awareness. Its defining property, in Evans and Stanovich's precise formulation, is *autonomy*: it cannot be withheld by the thinker and does not require the thinker's conscious initiation.

#### 5. [DEFINITION] 📖 Type 2 Processing (System 2) *(Line 209)*

> [!definition] 📖 Type 2 Processing (System 2)
> The controlled, slow, serial cognitive processes that require [[Working-Memory|Working Memory]] resources, compete with other cognitive demands for cognitive capacity, and involve what Stanovich calls *[[Cognitive-Decoupling|cognitive decoupling]]* — the capacity to hold representations of potential states of affairs in mind while keeping them separated from one's current representations of the actual state of affairs. Type 2 is the machinery of hypothetical thinking: the capacity to reason about what might be, or what was, without confusing those simulations with what is. Its defining property is this decoupling requirement, which is why sustained Type 2 engagement is metabolically and cognitively expensive.

#### 7. [DEFINITION] 📖 Synkatathesis (Assent) and the Stoic Gap *(Line 229)*

> [!definition] 📖 Synkatathesis (Assent) and the Stoic Gap
> In Stoic epistemology, *synkatathesis* (συγκατάθεσις) — assent — is the cognitive act by which the mind endorses an impression and allows it to govern subsequent thought and action. The Stoic insight, developed most extensively by [[Chrysippus]] and transmitted through Epictetus, is that assent is not automatic: it is a voluntary act, and it is the primary site of rational agency. The impression arrives without our choosing. Whether we assent to it — whether we treat it as an accurate guide to reality and allow it to shape our responses — is, within limits, up to us. This is the Stoic [[Dichotomy-of-Control|locus of freedom]]: not freedom from impressions, but freedom in responding to them.

#### 8. [INSIGHT] 💡 The Stoic Insight About Impressions *(Line 238)*

> [!insight] 💡 The Stoic Insight About Impressions
> What the Stoics recognized, centuries before cognitive science developed the language to describe it, is that the most dangerous cognitive events are not the obviously unreasonable thoughts — "I should leap off this building" — but the subtle, plausible-seeming impressions that arrive with the authority of direct perception: "This person dislikes me," "This situation requires that I defend my reputation," "This risk is not as serious as others claim," "My first impulse here is probably correct." These impressions feel like observations, not inferences. They feel like facts, not interpretations. Prosoche — the practice of treating every impression as an impression, never as an unquestionable perception of reality — is the cognitive technology that introduces the necessary epistemic distance.

#### 9. [DEFINITION] 📖 Metacognitive Self-Regulation *(Line 247)*

> [!definition] 📖 Metacognitive Self-Regulation
> The executive oversight system that monitors, evaluates, and redirects one's own ongoing cognitive processes. Schraw and Dennison's Metacognitive Awareness Inventory (1994) operationalizes this across five regulatory components: planning (anticipating the cognitive demands of a task), information management (allocating attention strategically to important information), monitoring (tracking comprehension and progress), debugging (correcting strategies when errors are detected), and evaluation (assessing outcomes and the adequacy of the regulatory process itself). Taken together, these constitute not an occasional intervention but an ongoing executive loop that governs the quality of cognitive processing.

#### 32. [DEFINITION] 📖 Prosoche (προσοχή) *(Line 423)*

> [!definition] 📖 Prosoche (προσοχή)
> The Stoic practice of continuous, vigilant attention to one's own impressions, desires, and actions as they arise — the foundational "spiritual attitude" (Hadot) of Stoic cognitive life. Prosoche operates before assent (synkatathesis) is given to an impression, creating the deliberate evaluation space in which rational agency exercises itself. Distinguished from modern mindfulness by its specifically cognitive-evaluative rather than relaxation-oriented character, and from mere introspection by its connection to specific Stoic standards of evaluation. Key sources: Epictetus, Discourses IV.12; Marcus Aurelius, Meditations II.5, VII.54; Pierre Hadot, *Philosophy as a Way of Life* (1995).

#### 33. [DEFINITION] 📖 Cognitive Decoupling *(Line 426)*

> [!definition] 📖 Cognitive Decoupling
> In Stanovich and Evans's dual process theory, the defining feature of Type 2 processing: the capacity to hold representations of potential states of affairs in mind while keeping them functionally separated from one's current representations of actual states. Decoupling enables hypothetical reasoning (reasoning about what might be true without confusing that reasoning with one's current beliefs), counterfactual thinking, and the evaluation of alternative courses of action. It is cognitively expensive, requiring working memory resources, and it can be trained through practice. The Governed Attentional Gap is the experiential phenomenology of decoupling successfully enacted.

#### 34. [DEFINITION] 📖 Metacognitive Self-Regulation *(Line 429)*

> [!definition] 📖 Metacognitive Self-Regulation
> The executive oversight system, formalized by Flavell (1979) and elaborated by Schraw (1994, 1998), that monitors and governs ongoing cognitive processes. Comprises two major components: knowledge of cognition (declarative, procedural, and conditional knowledge about how cognition works) and regulation of cognition (planning, information management, monitoring, debugging, and evaluation). Distinguished from general intelligence by its executive rather than computational character: a high-IQ thinker who does not monitor their own reasoning may be more systematically biased than a moderate-IQ thinker with robust metacognitive self-regulation.

#### 36. [DEFINITION] 📖 Synkatathesis (Assent) *(Line 435)*

> [!definition] 📖 Synkatathesis (Assent)
> In Stoic epistemology, the voluntary cognitive act by which the mind endorses an impression and allows it to govern subsequent thought and action. The Stoic claim that assent is voluntary — that it is within our power, at least in principle, to withhold assent from even compelling impressions — is the philosophical foundation of prosoche: if assent were automatic, there would be no space for governance. Synkatathesis is the precise Stoic correlate of what cognitive scientists call the point of Type 2 override: the moment at which deliberate processing either affirms or corrects the Type 1 default response.

#### 37. [DEFINITION] 📖 Mindware *(Line 438)*

> [!definition] 📖 Mindware
> Keith Stanovich's term for the cognitive software — the rules, strategies, beliefs, and reasoning frameworks — that Type 2 processing deploys in evaluating and potentially overriding Type 1 responses. Mindware can be "contaminated" (including biased frameworks that produce systematic errors even under deliberate reflection) or "missing" (lacking the relevant frameworks to evaluate a class of impressions). Effective metacognitive governance requires both good mindware (the right frameworks for evaluation) and the regulated attentional gap in which that mindware can be deployed.

> [!methodology-and-sources] 🔬 Research Grounding and Methodological Transparency
> This report synthesizes findings from four distinct research traditions with substantially different methodological foundations. The dual process theory literature draws primarily on experimental cognitive psychology, with strong convergent evidence from behavioral tasks, individual difference studies, and neuroimaging. The Stoic prosoche literature is philosophical and historical, drawing on primary texts (Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius) and secondary scholarship (Hadot, Robertson, Pigliucci) — it is not empirical in the experimental sense, and claims about prosoche's effectiveness rest primarily on philosophical argument and practical testimony. The metacognitive self-regulation literature is a mature empirical domain with established measurement instruments (MAI, think-aloud protocols) and substantial developmental and educational research. The debiasing literature is the most directly empirical component, with the 2025 Swaryandini et al. meta-analysis providing the most comprehensive current synthesis. The emergent concept of the Governed Attentional Gap synthesizes across all four traditions and therefore goes beyond what any single tradition establishes; it represents the report's original theoretical contribution and should be understood as a hypothesis awaiting direct empirical investigation rather than an established finding.
