# Report Idea

I have an idea for a report on Attribution, Self Efficacy, and Motivational Inner Speech Vocabulary. I would like to explore how the inner dialogue that occurs during silent reading of academic texts [like-the-ones-generated-by-this-claude-project] can influence motivation, self-efficacy, goal setting, and ultimately comprehension and critical thinking. Specifically, I want to focus on how the vocabulary used during inner dialogue can impact the outcome and comprehension in deliberately practicing Self Regulated Learning [more-on-why-below].

The report should draw on relevant theories and research findings, such as Attribution Theory, Self Determination Theory, Achievement Goal Theory and others, to provide a comprehensive in depth understanding of how inner speech vocabulary can influence motivation and self-efficacy in the context of reading academic texts.
The report should also provide practical strategies and techniques for developing a more constructive and supportive inner dialogue while reading academic texts. This could include tips for identifying and challenging negative self-talk, as well as suggestions for cultivating a more growth-oriented mindset.

Another apsect to cover is the vocabulary used in constructing different types of goals, such as hiearchal goals [used-in-my-slr-project], and how the inner dialogue can influence the type of goals that are set and pursued. For example, how the use of certain vocabulary can lead to setting more specific, challenging, and meaningful goals, which can in turn enhance motivation and self-efficacy, and how the use of other vocabulary can lead to setting more vague, easy, or less meaningful goals, which can undermine motivation, self-efficacy, and self-regulation.

I would also like for you to addin prompts, vacabular resources, templates, and/or other tools that can be used to me develop a more constructive and supportive inner dialogue while reading academic texts. This could include prompts for identifying and challenging negative self-talk, as well as templates for setting more specific, challenging, and meaningful goals. 

The main purpose of this report is to help me in building the vocabulary, mental models, strategies, and techniques that can be be used to develop a more constructive and supportive vocabulary for reading academic texts, which can ultimately enhance motivation, self-efficacy, comprehension, and critical thinking.

# Some Background

- Why Now?

I have just finished with a mini project of sorts, that deals with Self Regulated Learning. Specifically the Forethought and Self Reflection Phases of Zimmermans Model. I have produced multiple reports on the various aspect of constructing a workflow on the mechanism associated with Self Regulated Learning and the cyclical model. They cover the various strategies, techniques, and tools that can be used to enhance self-regulated learning in the context of reading academic texts. Such as Achievement Goal Theory, Self Determination Theory, Attribution Theory, and others. 

Ive already known, [due-to-my-work-with-Self-Determination-Theory] how important vocabulary usage is in the context of motivation and building self-efficacy. However, during the reading of those reports it became apperently clear how much more of an impact that the useage of certain vocabulary can determins motivation/self-efficacy, specifacally in attributing causes of faliure or progress/success [of-goals] to certain inferences [Attribution-Theory] can either promote or undermine the work being practiced.
