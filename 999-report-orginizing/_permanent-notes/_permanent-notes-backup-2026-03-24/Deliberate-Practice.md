# Deliberate Practice

> [!definition] **Deliberate Practice**
> A highly structured form of practice specifically designed to improve performance, characterized by: activities at the edge of current ability (requiring full concentration), immediate and accurate feedback on performance, the opportunity to correct errors, and the guidance of a coach or teacher who designs practice activities targeting specific weaknesses. Deliberate practice is distinguished from mere experience or play by its explicit metacognitive structure: it requires ongoing monitoring of performance against a clear standard and strategic modification of practice based on identified gaps.
