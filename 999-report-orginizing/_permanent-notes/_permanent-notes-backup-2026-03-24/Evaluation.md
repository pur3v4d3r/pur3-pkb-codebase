# Evaluation

> [!definition] **Evaluation**
> Evaluation is the cognitive act of assessing the credibility, accuracy, logical validity, and contextual adequacy of claims, arguments, sources, and reasoning processes. Evaluation requires the application of intellectual standards — asking whether a claim is clear, accurate, precise, relevant, deep, broad, and logically sound. Importantly, evaluation applies to one's own reasoning as much as to external sources.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Key Insight
> Peter Facione's Delphi Report (1990) identified seven core dispositions of the ideal critical thinker: inquisitiveness, open-mindedness, systematicity, analyticity, truth-seeking, self-confidence in reasoning, and maturity of judgment. These dispositions are not personality traits one either has or lacks at birth — they are cultivatable through educational environments that model, reward, and practise them.
