# The Undermining Effect

> [!definition] **The Undermining Effect**
> [[Undermining-Effect|The undermining effect]] (also called the [[Overjustification-Effect|overjustification effect]]) refers to the phenomenon whereby introducing external rewards for an intrinsically motivating activity reduces subsequent intrinsic motivation. It is predicted by [[Cognitive-Evaluation-Theory]] through the mechanism of the [[Perceived-Locus-of-Causality]]: when a person attributes their engagement to the reward rather than to themselves, the experience of volition is diminished, reducing intrinsic motivation. This is among SDT's most policy-consequential claims.
