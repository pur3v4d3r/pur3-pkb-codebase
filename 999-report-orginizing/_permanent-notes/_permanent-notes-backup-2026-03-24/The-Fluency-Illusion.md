# The Fluency Illusion

> [!definition] **The Fluency Illusion**
> The systematic tendency of learners to misread processing fluency — how easily material is read or processed — as evidence of comprehension and retention. High fluency during study predicts overconfidence but not retention; effortful, disfluent processing predicts better retention despite feeling less productive.
