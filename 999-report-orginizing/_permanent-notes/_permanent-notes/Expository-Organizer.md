# Expository Organizer

> [!definition] **Expository Organizer**
> A type of advance organizer used when learners lack relevant prior knowledge. Provides new, superordinate conceptual scaffolding at a higher level of abstraction than the material to follow, giving the learner a framework into which new specific material can be organized. Compare: [[Comparative-Organizer]].

## Core Explanation

> [!evidence] Supporting Evidence
> Richard Mayer's systematic research on advance organizers consistently found that organizers produced little or no benefit for verbatim retention of presented facts (what he called "reproductive" learning) but produced meaningful benefits for the ability to apply knowledge to novel problems (what he called "productive" or transfer learning).
