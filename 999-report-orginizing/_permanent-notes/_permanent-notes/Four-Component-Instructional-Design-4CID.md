# Four-Component Instructional Design / 4C/ID

> [!definition] **Four-Component Instructional Design / 4C/ID**
> An instructional design model for complex skills, grounded in CLT, specifying four components: (1) whole-task learning tasks; (2) supportive information (schema-building); (3) procedural information (just-in-time guidance for routine procedures); (4) part-task practice (for automating critical components). The model operationalizes CLT principles for complex vocational and professional training domains and represents the most elaborated CLT-derived design framework.

> [!analytical-insight] Key Insight
> Effective instruction is instruction that manages the demands placed on [[Working Memory]] such that cognitive resources are available for the processes that lead to [[Schema Theory|schema]] formation and automation in [[Long-Term Memory]]. Instructional formats that impose unnecessary working memory demands (extraneous load) obstruct learning; formats that support the integration of essential information facilitate it.
