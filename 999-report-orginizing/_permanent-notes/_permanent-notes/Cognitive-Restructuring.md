# Cognitive Restructuring

> [!definition] **Cognitive Restructuring**
> A core technique within cognitive-behavioral approaches in which maladaptive automatic thoughts, dysfunctional assumptions, and core schemas are systematically identified, examined using Socratic questioning, evaluated against available evidence, and replaced or modified through the development of more adaptive alternative beliefs. Cognitive restructuring treats cognitions as hypotheses subject to empirical testing rather than as facts, and involves both cognitive interventions (examining evidence, generating alternatives) and behavioral experiments (testing predictions through action). Not to be confused with simple positive thinking, which merely substitutes affirmations without critical examination of the original belief.
