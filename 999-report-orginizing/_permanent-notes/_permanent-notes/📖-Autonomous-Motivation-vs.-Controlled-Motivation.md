# 📖 Autonomous Motivation vs. Controlled Motivation

> [!definition] **📖 Autonomous Motivation vs. Controlled Motivation**
> In SDT's framework, **autonomous motivation** encompasses intrinsic motivation (doing something because it is inherently interesting and satisfying) and identified regulation (doing something because one genuinely values and endorses it). **Controlled motivation** encompasses introjected regulation (doing something to avoid guilt or protect self-esteem) and external regulation (doing something for reward or to avoid punishment). The distinction is not between internal and external locus of causality but between self-authored and other-authored engagement — a critical nuance with profound implications for intellectual development.
