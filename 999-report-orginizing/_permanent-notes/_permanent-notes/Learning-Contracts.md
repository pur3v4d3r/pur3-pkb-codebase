# Learning Contracts

> [!definition] **Learning Contracts**
> Negotiated agreements between learner and facilitator specifying learning goals, strategies and resources, evidence of learning, and evaluation criteria. The primary pedagogical instrument for operationalising SDL process in formal educational settings, learning contracts externalise and make visible the planning and goal-setting activities that natural SDL performs implicitly. Effective learning contracts are genuinely negotiated rather than merely approved; require iterative revision as learning proceeds; and are evaluated against jointly agreed criteria rather than normative benchmarks. Associated with Knowles's andragogical practice tradition.

> [!analytical-insight] Key Insight
> Process and person are logically independent. SDL-structured environments do not automatically produce self-directed learners, and self-directed learners can learn effectively in non-SDL environments. This means that designing an SDL environment is not the same as developing learner autonomy, and measuring learner self-directedness is not the same as measuring SDL process engagement. Conflating the two produces interventions that fail and research that produces apparently contradictory findings.
