# Declarative/Procedural/Conditional Metacognitive Knowledge

> [!definition] **Declarative/Procedural/Conditional Metacognitive Knowledge**
> **Definition:** Three types of metacognitive knowledge: *Declarative* (knowing *about* oneself, tasks, and strategies), *Procedural* (knowing *how* to implement strategies), and *Conditional* (knowing *when* and *why* to use specific strategies). All three must be developed for expert self-regulation.
> **Operational Indicators:** A learner with strong declarative knowledge knows that elaborative interrogation promotes deeper understanding. A learner with strong procedural knowledge can actually implement it effectively during reading. A learner with strong conditional knowledge knows when to switch from elaborative interrogation to self-explanation based on the nature of the comprehension challenge.
