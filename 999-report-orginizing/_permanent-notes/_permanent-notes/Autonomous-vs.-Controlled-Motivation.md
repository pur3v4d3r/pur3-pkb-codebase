# Autonomous vs. Controlled Motivation

> [!definition] **Autonomous vs. Controlled Motivation**
> [[Autonomous-Motivation|Autonomous motivation]] encompasses intrinsic motivation and identified/integrated regulation — any form of motivation where the person experiences themselves as the origin of their action. [[Controlled-Motivation|Controlled motivation]] encompasses external and introjected regulation — any form where action is experienced as compelled by external pressure or internal compulsion. The [[Relative-Autonomy-Index]] measures the balance between autonomous and controlled motivation. SDT's core empirical claim is that autonomous motivation consistently predicts better outcomes for complex tasks — deeper learning, greater creativity, stronger persistence, higher well-being — than controlled motivation.
