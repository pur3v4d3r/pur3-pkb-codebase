# Functional Integration

> [!definition] **Functional Integration** *(from [[what-does-it-mean-to-truley-understand-something-2026-03-10]])*
> The organisation of domain knowledge in ways that make it productively usable — not merely stored but structured so as to enable inference, prediction, error-detection, and analogical extension. Functional integration is contrasted with information storage: the same facts can be stored without integration (rote memorisation) or integrated in ways that generate new outputs. Understanding, on the functional integration account, is about the *organisation* of knowledge rather than its *content*.

## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Key Insight *(from [[what-does-it-mean-to-truley-understand-something-2026-03-10]])*
> Understanding is the capacity to provide structural explanation — an account of mechanism, causation, or logical necessity — that accurately represents *why* a phenomenon is the way it is, and that survives robust follow-up questioning. This distinguishes genuine understanding from mere familiarity (passive recognition without explanatory depth) and from rote knowledge (accurate reproduction without structural grasp).
