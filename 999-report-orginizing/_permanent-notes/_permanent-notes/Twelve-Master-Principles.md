# Twelve Master Principles

> [!definition] **Twelve Master Principles**
> The unified design framework derived from the five convergence zones and the twenty-six preceding reports, organized in three tiers: four foundational principles (Cognitive Isomorphism, Active Construction, Regulatory Embedding, Motivational Alignment), five derived principles (Note Architecture, Linking Philosophy, Review Architecture, Active Processing Workflows, Calibration Systems), and three refinement principles (Evolutionary Architecture, Dialectical Deepening, Integration Metabolism).
