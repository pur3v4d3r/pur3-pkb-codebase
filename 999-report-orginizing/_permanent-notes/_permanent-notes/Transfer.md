# Transfer

> [!definition] **Transfer**
> The application of knowledge, skills, or strategies learned in one context to a different context. Near transfer involves application to closely similar situations; far transfer involves application across significantly different domains or contexts. Research on critical thinking transfer indicates that it is not automatic and requires deliberate instructional support — including explicit attention to the general principles underlying domain-specific instances of reasoning.
