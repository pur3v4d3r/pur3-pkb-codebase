# Self-Regulated Learning / SRL

> [!definition] **Self-Regulated Learning / SRL**
> The proactive, metacognitively-directed management of one's own cognitive, motivational, behavioural, and contextual processes in service of self-set learning goals. SRL is distinguished from simple learning effort by its cyclical, self-directed quality — learners monitor and regulate their own processes rather than simply responding to external instruction. *Note: SRL frameworks describe the cognitive architecture of regulation; SDT describes the motivational quality of its enactment.*
