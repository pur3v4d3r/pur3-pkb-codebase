# Self-Regulated Reading

> [!definition] **Self-Regulated Reading**
> Reading as a cyclical, goal-directed self-regulatory enterprise in which the reader engages in pre-reading forethought (goal-setting, prior knowledge activation, strategy planning), performance-phase monitoring and control (comprehension monitoring, strategy deployment, attention regulation), and post-reading self-reflection (outcome evaluation, attribution, strategy adaptation). Self-regulated reading is distinguished from externally regulated reading (strategy use in response to teacher prompts alone) and from automatized habitual reading (reading without metacognitive engagement).
