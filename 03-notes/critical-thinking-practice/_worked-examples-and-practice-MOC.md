# 🗺 Worked Examples & Practice Problems — MOC

> [!abstract] Purpose
> A curated index of the **worked examples** (WE-NN) and **practice problems** (PP-NN) for each of the four critical thinking frameworks I deliberately practice with. Worked examples are *reference exemplars* — fully completed analyses to study. Practice problems are *active drills* — prompts to apply the framework yourself, with collapsed solution sketches for after you attempt them.

---

## 📐 Worked Examples vs Practice Problems

> [!info] How the two artifact types differ
> | | Worked Example | Practice Problem |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Purpose** | Study a fully completed analysis | Drill the framework yourself |
> | **Reading mode** | Passive: read end-to-end | Active: fill in blanks, then compare |
> | **When to use** | First exposure to a framework; revisiting after a long gap | Maintaining and deepening fluency |
> | **Effort to consume** | 15-30 min reading | 10-60 min doing |
> | **Output** | Mental model of "what good looks like" | An actual completed analysis you wrote |
> | **Cognitive load** | Lower (germane focus on the *pattern*) | Higher (germane focus on the *execution*) |

> [!helpful-tip] Pedagogically: worked examples first, then practice problems
> The [[Worked Example Effect]] in [[Cognitive Load Theory]] shows that for novices, studying worked examples produces faster, deeper learning than immediate problem-solving — *if* the examples are then paired with active practice on isomorphic problems. That's the structure of this MOC.

## 🧭 Suggested Learning Path

> [!workflow] Recommended sequence (~3 weeks)
> | Week | Focus | Activities |
> |---|---|---|
> | 1 | **Acquaint** | Read all 4 worked examples (one per framework). Annotate questions in margins. |
> | 2 | **Drill the easy problems** | Complete PP-01, PP-03, PP-05, PP-07 (one per framework). Compare to solution sketches. |
> | 3 | **Stretch into the hard problems** | Complete PP-02, PP-04, PP-06, PP-08. Expect lower rigor scores; that is correct. |
> | 4+ | **Promote to live objects** | Run each framework on **real** objects of analysis from your week, using the master templates. |

## 🔵 Paul-Elder Framework

> [!definition] When to use
> Full-rigor analysis (~30-60 min) of decisions, complex claims, and policy proposals. The most comprehensive framework: 8 elements + 9 standards.

### Worked Example
- [[WE-01_paul-elder_should-i-switch-from-obsidian-to-logseq|WE-01 — Should I switch from Obsidian to Logseq?]]
   *Object type: decision · Demonstrates: full 8-elements + 9-standards walkthrough; pre/post confidence shift 4→2; steelmanning; surfacing motivated reasoning.*

### Practice Problems
- [[PP-01_paul-elder_daily-exercise-longevity-claim|PP-01 (Easy) — "Daily exercise is the most important habit for longevity"]]
   *~30 min · Object: simple claim · Drill: surfacing assumptions hidden in the word "most."*
- [[PP-02_paul-elder_ai-literacy-university-requirement|PP-02 (Hard) — "Universities should require AI literacy"]]
   *~60 min · Object: policy proposal · Drill: stakeholder standpoints, concept disambiguation, curriculum displacement.*

## 🟢 FRISCO Model

> [!definition] When to use
> **Rapid triage** (~5-15 min) of a single argument, claim, news article, or op-ed. Use when you need a verdict (sound / questionable / unsound) fast.

### Worked Example
- [[WE-02_frisco_remote-work-productivity-claim|WE-02 — "Remote work reduces overall worker productivity"]]
   *Object type: op-ed argument · Demonstrates: 5-letter rapid triage; explicit fallacy naming (appeal to authority, cherry picking, is-ought).*

### Practice Problems
- [[PP-03_frisco_screen-time-op-ed|PP-03 (Easy) — Screen-time op-ed]]
   *~10 min · Drill: correlation-vs-causation, undefined terms, slippery slope detection.*
- [[PP-04_frisco_minimum-wage-economic-claim|PP-04 (Hard) — Minimum-wage economic argument]]
   *~15 min · Drill: separating real evidence from rhetorical framing in a politically charged multi-premise argument; symmetry test for own-side bias.*

## 🟣 SEE-I Method

> [!definition] When to use
> Concept elaboration (~10-20 min). Use to test whether you actually understand a concept (vs merely recognize it) before relying on it in higher-order analysis.

### Worked Example
- [[WE-03_see-i_confirmation-bias|WE-03 — Confirmation Bias]]
   *Demonstrates: 4-move structure (State / Elaborate / Exemplify / Illustrate); transfer test across psychology + software dev; counter-example via Popperian science; analogy + mermaid diagram; mastery self-check.*

### Practice Problems
- [[PP-05_see-i_cognitive-load|PP-05 (Easy) — Cognitive Load]]
   *~12 min · Drill: the three-type distinction (intrinsic / extraneous / germane); counter-example via difficulty ≠ load.*
- [[PP-06_see-i_epistemic-vigilance|PP-06 (Hard) — Epistemic Vigilance]]
   *~18 min · Drill: source-vs-content components; evolved-vs-learned distinction; counter-example via miscalibrated distrust.*

## 🟡 Metacognitive Reflection

> [!definition] When to use
> Periodic (weekly / monthly / quarterly) second-order review of your own practice. Surfaces the patterns that no single session can reveal.

### Worked Example
- [[WE-04_metacognitive_weekly-reflection|WE-04 — Weekly reflection on a fictional 7-session sample]]
   *Demonstrates: 8-section reflection; standards-trend analysis identifying Depth + Fairness as bottlenecks; virtues inventory; 5 dated falsifiable commitments.*

### Practice Problems
- [[PP-07_metacognitive_scenario-past-month|PP-07 (Easy) — Reflect on a fictional past month]]
   *~25 min · Drill: descriptive-first reading of session data; bottleneck-standard identification; structural (not aspirational) regulation moves.*
- [[PP-08_metacognitive_scenario-stuck-thinker|PP-08 (Hard) — Diagnose "The Stuck Thinker"]]
   *~35 min · Drill: 3-month log diagnosis; second-order failure detection (reflection process itself failing); confirmation-bias-machine pattern.*

## 🧪 Diagnostic Self-Check

> [!question] Use these to decide what to practice next
> - Have I read all 4 worked examples? → If no, read those first.
> - Can I, *without referring back to the worked example*, name the 8 elements + 9 standards of [[Paul-Elder Framework]]? → If no, re-read [[WE-01_paul-elder_should-i-switch-from-obsidian-to-logseq|WE-01]].
> - Can I do FRISCO from memory in under 10 minutes? → If no, drill [[PP-03_frisco_screen-time-op-ed|PP-03]].
> - When I last did a SEE-I, did I produce both a transfer-domain example *and* a counter-example *and* an analogy? → If no, drill [[PP-05_see-i_cognitive-load|PP-05]] or [[PP-06_see-i_epistemic-vigilance|PP-06]].
> - Have I done a Metacognitive Reflection in the past 4 weeks? → If no, run the [[_master-metacognitive-reflection-v1.0.0|live template]] on your own recent sessions.
