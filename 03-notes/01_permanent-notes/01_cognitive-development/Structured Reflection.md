## Core Explanation

> [!analytical-insight] Core Explanation
> Paragraph 1: Foundational context and core mechanism. Structured reflection involves setting specific goals or questions for the reflection process, using a predefined framework or set of prompts to guide the examination, and documenting findings in a structured format such as a journal entry or reflective essay.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 2
> Paragraph 2: How it works or is applied in practice. This method can be used in various contexts, including personal development, professional training, therapy sessions, and educational settings. It typically involves identifying areas for improvement, analyzing patterns of behavior, and formulating strategies to address identified issues.

> [!analytical-insight] Explanation 3
> Paragraph 3: Key nuances, sub-variants, or theoretical roots. There are different approaches to structured reflection, such as the Reflective Practice model by Donald Schon, which emphasizes learning from practice through reflective dialogue with others, and the Critical Reflection framework developed by Paulo Freire, which focuses on social justice and transformative change.
